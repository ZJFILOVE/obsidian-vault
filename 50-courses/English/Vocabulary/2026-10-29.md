# Vocabulary — 2026-10-29：把预测强度写清楚

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-10-29|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-10-29|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-10-29|词汇]]

## 1. a degree of uncertainty — 一定程度的不确定性

- **核心义：** an amount of uncertainty that should be acknowledged rather than erased
- **常用搭配：** involve / retain / communicate a degree of uncertainty
- **例句：** Any estimate made before the final survey will retain a degree of uncertainty.
- **词族：** uncertain；uncertainty；certain；certainty。
- **对比：** **some uncertainty** 更口语；a degree of uncertainty 稍正式，强调不确定性可以有不同程度。
- **边界：** 有不确定性不等于所有结果同样可能。

## 2. in-built uncertainty — 内生的／固有的不确定性

- **核心义：** uncertainty arising from the nature or design of a process
- **常用搭配：** in-built uncertainty in a model / projection / sampling process
- **例句：** Long-term demand projections contain in-built uncertainty because future prices and preferences may change.
- **对比：** **measurement error** 是测量误差；in-built uncertainty 可来自系统本身，不一定是操作错误。
- **变体：** **built-in** 在美式英语中更常见。

## 3. a one-off result — 一次性的结果

- **核心义：** a result observed once and not yet shown to be repeatable
- **常用搭配：** a one-off event / payment / experiment / model run
- **例句：** The team treated the sudden increase as a one-off result until it appeared in three later samples.
- **对比：** **one-time** 强调只发生一次；one-off 常暗示不应据此推出稳定模式。
- **语法：** 作形容词时写 one-off；名词可说 **a one-off**。

## 4. be dominated by — 主要受……支配／影响

- **常用搭配：** be dominated by one supplier / seasonal demand / a circulation pattern
- **例句：** The early discussion was dominated by cost, while accessibility received little attention.
- **词族：** dominate；dominant；dominance。
- **对比：** **be influenced by** 强度较弱；dominated by 表示某因素占主导，但不必是唯一因素。
- **边界：** 使用 dominated 时要有证据说明相对强度。

## 5. capture the nuance — 捕捉细微差别

- **核心义：** represent important distinctions that a simpler account would miss
- **常用搭配：** capture the nuance / complexity / variation / lived experience
- **例句：** A single satisfaction score may fail to capture the nuance in residents' comments.
- **词族：** nuance；nuanced（细致而有层次的）。
- **对比：** **add detail** 是增加细节；capture the nuance 强调细节改变理解或判断。
- **易错点：** nuance 的发音重心不等于 new answer；写作时常用不可数泛指。

## 6. issue a forecast — 发布预报

- **常用搭配：** issue a forecast / warning / statement / permit
- **例句：** The agency issued an updated flood forecast after receiving new river-level data.
- **词族：** issue 可作动词“发布”，也可作名词“问题／议题／期号”。
- **对比：** **make a forecast** 强调形成预测；issue a forecast 强调正式对外发布。
- **语法：** 发布者作主语，不写 the forecast issued unless using passive voice。

## 7. the spread of scenarios — 情景的分散程度

- **核心义：** how widely possible outcomes differ from one another
- **常用搭配：** a wide / narrow spread of outcomes; scenarios converge / diverge
- **例句：** The wide spread of cost scenarios made a precise budget commitment premature.
- **对比：** **range** 常指最高与最低之间的范围；spread 还强调结果在范围中的分散。
- **边界：** 情景很集中可以提高置信度，但不保证模型没有共同偏差。

## 8. with absolute certainty — 以绝对确定的方式

- **常用结构：** say / predict / know with absolute certainty
- **例句：** The inspection cannot determine with absolute certainty when the crack first appeared.
- **词族：** absolute；absolutely；certainty。
- **对比：** **with confidence** 表示有较强依据；with absolute certainty 是更高、通常更难满足的证据门槛。
- **语用：** 常与 cannot 连用，用来限制结论；不要因此把结论降成“完全不可知”。

## 词块联结练习

围绕一项活动报名人数预测写四句：

**one-off result → spread of scenarios → a degree of uncertainty → issue an updated forecast**

要求先说明为什么单次数字不足，再说明情景分散度，随后给出有边界的判断，最后写何时更新预测。

> [!answer]- 原创示例
> Last year's late surge may have been a one-off result. The current models show a wide spread of registration scenarios, so the final attendance estimate still carries a degree of uncertainty. The organizers will issue an updated forecast after the early-bird deadline.

## 近义辨析：possible、likely、confident、certain

- **possible**：未被排除，不表示概率最高。
- **likely**：比替代结果更有可能，但仍非确定。
- **confident**：说话者认为证据较强；最好补充依据。
- **certain**：几乎没有合理怀疑，证据门槛最高。

把 *I am confident* 当成结论不够；继续问：**confident because the scenarios converge, because the data are recent, or merely because one chart looks dramatic?**

## 闭卷检索测验

1. 补全：a degree ___ uncertainty；be dominated ___；issue ___ forecast；___ absolute certainty。
2. 用英语解释 a one-off result 为什么不能自动代表趋势。
3. 把 *All models agree* 改成允许小幅差异但仍表示较高置信度的句子。
4. 用 capture the nuance 写一句，说明单一平均值遗漏了什么。
5. 解释 scenario spread 很窄仍不能排除哪一种风险。
6. 分别用 likely 和 possible 改写 “Demand will double.”。

> [!answer]- 检索答案
> 1. of；by；a；with。
> 2. It has been observed only once, so its repeatability and underlying cause have not yet been established.
> 3. The model scenarios are closely clustered, which supports relatively high confidence in the central estimate.
> 4. The overall average fails to capture the nuance between first-time users and long-term users.
> 5. Different models may share the same assumptions or bias, so agreement alone does not guarantee accuracy.
> 6. Demand is likely to double. / A doubling of demand is one possible scenario.

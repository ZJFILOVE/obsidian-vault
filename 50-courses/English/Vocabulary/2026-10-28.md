# Vocabulary — 2026-10-28：把“信任”写成可观察的条件

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-10-28|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-10-28|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-10-28|词汇]]

## 1. rely on — 依赖；以……为依据

- **核心义：** depend on someone or something for support, action or a result
- **常用搭配：** rely on public transport / reliable data / a promise / one source alone
- **例句：** The emergency plan relies on local teams being able to share updates when mobile networks fail.
- **词族：** rely（动词）；reliable（可靠的）；reliability（可靠性）；unreliable（不可靠的）。
- **对比：** **trust** 可指相信诚实或能力；rely on 更强调实际把结果交托给某人或某物。
- **语法：** rely on + 名词／动名词；不要写 rely somebody。

## 2. fulfil a promise — 履行承诺

- **核心义：** do what one has promised to do
- **常用搭配：** fulfil a promise / commitment / obligation / requirement
- **例句：** The supplier fulfilled its promise by replacing the damaged equipment before the workshop.
- **词族：** fulfilment（履行；满足感，语境决定）；美式常拼 **fulfill / fulfillment**。
- **对比：** **keep a promise** 更日常；fulfil a promise 稍正式，强调承诺内容得到完成。
- **边界：** attempt to fulfil 不等于已经完成。

## 3. on a small scale — 小规模地

- **核心义：** involving a limited number, area or amount
- **常用搭配：** test on a small scale / operate on a regional scale / scale up a pilot
- **例句：** The clinic tested evening appointments on a small scale before extending them to all departments.
- **词族：** scalable（可扩展的）；scalability（可扩展性）；scale up/down（扩大／缩小）。
- **对比：** **a small sample** 只说样本小；on a small scale 可以描述整个项目的范围。
- **边界：** 小规模成功不自动保证扩大后仍有效。

## 4. end up doing — 最终做了；结果成为

- **核心义：** reach an outcome, often unintended
- **常用结构：** end up doing / end up with + noun / end up in + place or state
- **例句：** Without a shared timetable, volunteers ended up visiting the same households twice.
- **语法：** 后接动名词，不接 to do；过去式 **ended up**。
- **对比：** **eventually** 只表示最终；end up 常暗示过程出乎意料。

## 5. be accepted as — 被接受为；被视作

- **常用结构：** be accepted as payment / evidence / a valid form of identification
- **例句：** A digital copy is accepted as evidence of registration but not as proof of identity.
- **词族：** accept（接受）；acceptable（可接受的）；acceptance（接受、认可）。
- **对比：** **be recognized as** 强调正式承认或识别；be accepted as 更关注实际规则或共同惯例。
- **易错点：** accepted by 指接受者；accepted as 指被当作什么。

## 6. mistake A for B — 把 A 误认成 B

- **常用搭配：** mistake correlation for causation / activity for progress / a copy for the original
- **例句：** Decision-makers should not mistake a rise in app downloads for sustained use.
- **词族：** mistake（名词／动词）；mistaken（错误的）；mistakenly（错误地）。
- **语法：** A 是被误认的对象，B 是误以为的身份；顺序不要颠倒。
- **对比：** **confuse A with B** 强调混淆两者；mistake A for B 强调把 A 当成 B。

## 7. erode trust — 逐步削弱信任

- **核心义：** gradually weaken confidence or trust
- **常用搭配：** erode public trust / confidence / institutional legitimacy / a competitive advantage
- **例句：** Repeated unexplained delays eroded residents' trust in the reporting system.
- **词族：** erosion（侵蚀、削弱）；erosive（有侵蚀性的，较少见）。
- **对比：** **destroy trust** 强度更高、结果更彻底；erode 强调逐渐累积。
- **边界：** 写 erode 时最好说明反复发生的机制，而不是只列一次事故。

## 8. remain broadly stable — 保持大体稳定

- **核心义：** stay relatively steady despite limited variation
- **常用搭配：** remain broadly stable / consistent / unchanged / in line with expectations
- **例句：** Attendance remained broadly stable, although the share of first-time visitors increased.
- **词族：** stable（稳定的）；stability（稳定性）；stabilize（使稳定）；unstable（不稳定的）。
- **对比：** **remain constant** 暗示几乎不变；broadly stable 允许小幅波动。
- **语用：** broadly 是精度词，不是模糊填充词；它明确降低绝对程度。

## 词块联结练习

围绕一个公共系统完成四句：

**rely on a rule → be accepted as valid → mistake activity for success → repeated failures erode trust**

要求：第一句写依赖关系，第二句写接受标准，第三句纠正一个错误指标，第四句说明信任怎样被逐步削弱。

> [!answer]- 原创示例
> The booking system relies on clinics updating available slots every morning. A confirmation code is accepted as proof of an appointment, but not as proof of payment. Administrators should not mistake a rise in searches for improved access. If confirmed appointments repeatedly disappear, those failures will erode trust in the service.

## 近义辨析：stable、constant 与 reliable

1. 数据在一个范围内小幅波动 → **remain broadly stable**。
2. 数值完全不变 → **remain constant**，需要更强证据。
3. 系统按预期持续工作 → **remain reliable / demonstrate reliability**。
4. 人们据此安排实际行动 → **rely on the system**。
5. 人们愿意把某物当作有效凭证 → **accept it as valid**。

## 闭卷检索测验

1. 补全：rely ___；accepted ___ payment；mistake A ___ B；end up ___ the task twice。
2. 用英文解释 trust 与 rely on 的区别。
3. 改写：The number did not change at all.（改成允许小幅波动的谨慎表达）
4. 用 erode trust 写一句包含“反复发生”机制的句子。
5. 解释为什么 a successful small-scale pilot 不等于 scalability 已被证明。
6. 用 fulfil a promise 和 be accepted as 写两句，分别说明履约与规则认可。

> [!answer]- 检索答案
> 1. on；as；for；doing。
> 2. Trust concerns belief in honesty, ability or validity; rely on means that an action or outcome actually depends on that person, rule or system.
> 3. The figure remained broadly stable.
> 4. Repeated changes without notice eroded users' trust in the timetable.
> 5. Conditions, coordination costs and failure modes may change when a project expands.
> 6. 示例：The contractor fulfilled its promise to publish weekly updates. A signed digital receipt was accepted as evidence of submission.

# Vocabulary — 2026-11-03：谈学习过程、迁移与证据强度

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-11-03|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-11-03|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-11-03|词汇]]

以下 8 个词块由今日澳大利亚科学院文章的真实用法引出；释义、搭配、例句、对比和测验均为原创。重点不是背神经科学术语，而是学会把“过程、效果和边界”说准确。

## 1. increase the chances of — 提高……发生的概率

- **核心义：** make an outcome more likely without guaranteeing it
- **常用搭配：** increase the chances of success / recovery / long-term retention
- **原创例句：** A clear checklist increases the chances of detecting an error before publication.
- **词族：** chance；chancy（较少用于正式写作）。
- **对比：** ensure / guarantee 表示保证；increase the chances of 只表示概率提高。
- **语法：** of 后接名词或动名词：increase the chances of remembering。

## 2. make something meaningful — 使某事具有意义或与已有知识产生联系

- **核心义：** connect information with a purpose, pattern or personal context
- **常用搭配：** make a concept / task / experience meaningful to learners
- **原创例句：** Asking students to compare two familiar routes made the map scale meaningful.
- **词族：** meaning；meaningful；meaningfully；meaningless。
- **对比：** make something clear 强调理解；make it meaningful 强调联系与价值。
- **使用边界：** personally meaningful 不等于 objectively true。

## 3. a shallow level of processing — 浅层加工

- **核心义：** attention to surface features rather than meaning or relationships
- **常用搭配：** process information at a shallow / deeper level
- **原创例句：** Copying the color of each heading may involve only a shallow level of processing.
- **词族：** process；processing；processed。
- **对比：** deep processing 关注含义、关系和推理；shallow 不一定“错误”，但可提取线索通常较少。
- **搭配提醒：** processing 在此是认知加工，不是行政“办理”。

## 4. form multiple representations of — 为……形成多种表征

- **核心义：** encode the same idea in several forms, such as words, images, sounds or spatial relations
- **常用搭配：** form visual / verbal / spatial representations of a concept
- **原创例句：** The class formed multiple representations of the water cycle through a diagram, a paragraph and a physical model.
- **词族：** represent；representation；representative。
- **对比：** repeat information 再次呈现同一形式；form multiple representations 改变呈现与联系的方式。
- **语义提醒：** representation 在这里不是“代表”或“代理人”。

## 5. consolidate a memory — 巩固记忆

- **核心义：** stabilize newly learned information so it can be retained and retrieved later
- **常用搭配：** memory consolidation；consolidate learning / gains / evidence
- **原创例句：** A short review the next day helped consolidate the sequence of steps.
- **词族：** consolidate；consolidation；consolidated。
- **对比：** encode 侧重新信息最初如何记录；consolidate 侧重记录之后如何稳定。
- **边界：** consolidation 不等于永不遗忘。

## 6. transfer to — 迁移到；适用于另一任务或情境

- **核心义：** carry a skill, effect or principle from one context into another
- **常用搭配：** transfer to a new task / workplace / unfamiliar context
- **原创例句：** Speed on familiar exercises did not fully transfer to questions with a different structure.
- **词族：** transfer；transferable；transferability。
- **对比：** apply A to B 强调主动应用；transfer to 常用来检验效果是否自然延伸。
- **搭配提醒：** 名词可写 transfer of learning，动词用 transfer to。

## 7. focused practice — 聚焦练习；针对特定能力的练习

- **核心义：** sustained practice directed at a clearly defined skill
- **常用搭配：** deliberate / focused / repeated practice
- **原创例句：** Focused practice on transitions made her presentation easier to follow.
- **词族：** focus；focused；refocus。
- **对比：** repetition 只说明重复；focused practice 还说明练习目标。
- **边界：** 练得更多不自动意味着能迁移到所有相关能力。

## 8. be associated with — 与……相关

- **核心义：** occur together or show a statistical relationship, without automatically establishing causation
- **常用搭配：** be positively / strongly / consistently associated with
- **原创例句：** Longer commuting times were associated with lower attendance in the survey.
- **词族：** associate；association；associated。
- **对比：** be caused by 明确因果；be associated with 保留替代解释。
- **语法：** 主语通常是观察到的特征或结果，不写 associate to。

## 词块联结练习

用 6 句话设计一项学习建议，至少包含五个词块：

1. 怎样 make the material meaningful；
2. 怎样 form multiple representations；
3. 哪一步用于 consolidate the memory；
4. 你要测试效果是否 transfer to 新任务；
5. 为什么结果只能写 be associated with；
6. 哪些条件可能 increase the chances of 成功。

> [!answer]- 示例（原创）
> Learners first connect a new term with a familiar decision to make it meaningful. They then form multiple representations of the idea by writing a definition and drawing a diagram. A brief review the next day is used to consolidate the memory. The final test asks whether the idea transfers to an unfamiliar case rather than repeating the original example. If participants choose their own study schedule, better performance can only be described as being associated with the method. A comparison group would increase the chances of identifying a real effect.

## 易混对比

| 表达 | 重点 | 示例判断 |

## 闭卷检索测验

1. 写出“提高长期保持概率”的英文词块。
2. make a concept clear 与 make it meaningful 有什么区别？
3. 改错：Students processed the rule in a shallow processing level.
4. 用 form multiple representations of 写一句包含 diagram 的句子。
5. encode 与 consolidate 分别处在学习过程的哪个阶段？
6. 为什么 improvement on one task may not transfer to another？
7. focused practice 比 repetition 多表达了什么？
8. 改写为不宣称因果：Long commutes caused lower attendance.

> [!answer]- 检索答案
> 1. increase the chances of long-term retention
> 2. clear 强调容易理解；meaningful 强调与目的、模式或已有知识建立联系。
> 3. Students processed the rule **at a shallow level**.
> 4. Students formed multiple representations of the process by combining a diagram with a verbal explanation.
> 5. Encoding describes the initial recording of information; consolidation describes its later stabilization.
> 6. The two tasks may rely on different cues, operations or contexts.
> 7. It identifies a specific target for practice rather than repetition alone.
> 8. Longer commutes were associated with lower attendance.

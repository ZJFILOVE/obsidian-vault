# English Vocabulary — 2026-11-10：谈设计、门槛与取舍的 8 个词块

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-11-10|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-11-10|今日翻译]]

## 1. prioritize A over B

- **意思**：在决策中把 A 放在 B 之前；强调资源或目标的排序。
- **常见搭配**：prioritize usability over feature count; prioritize long-term maintenance; competing priorities
- **例句**：The redesign prioritizes clear feedback over decorative animation.
- **词族**：priority (n.); prioritization (n.)
- **对比**：*prefer A to B* 可表示个人偏好；*prioritize* 更常用于有限资源下的制度或设计选择。

## 2. bring together

- **意思**：把原本分散的人、方法或功能整合到一起。
- **常见搭配**：bring together existing technologies; bring researchers and users together; bring together evidence from several sources
- **例句**：The platform brings together booking, payment, and progress tracking in one interface.
- **近义对比**：*combine* 只说明合并；*bring together* 常暗示这些部分原先分散，整合后开始协同。

## 3. lower the barrier to

- **意思**：降低做某事所需的知识、金钱、时间或制度成本。
- **常见搭配**：lower the barrier to entry; lower barriers to participation; reduce financial barriers
- **例句**：Plain-language instructions can lower the barrier to using public data.
- **词族**：barrier (n.); barrier-free (adj.)
- **对比**：*make something easy* 太笼统；该词块要求说明具体门槛和具体行为。

## 4. a standard look and feel

- **意思**：不同页面或工具共享的视觉样式与交互方式。
- **常见搭配**：maintain a consistent look and feel; standard interface elements; visual consistency
- **例句**：A standard look and feel lets users transfer what they learned from one screen to another.
- **对比**：*appearance* 只偏视觉；*look and feel* 同时覆盖视觉和操作体验。

## 5. flatten the learning curve

- **意思**：让初学者更快达到可用水平；在一般英语中表示降低学习难度。
- **常见搭配**：a steep learning curve; shorten onboarding; reduce initial complexity
- **例句**：Templates flatten the learning curve, although experts may still need advanced controls.
- **使用提醒**：*learning curve* 在技术语境里有时有严格数学含义；日常写作中通常指学习难度或上手速度。

## 6. be intended for

- **意思**：某物在设计或计划时面向某类人或用途。
- **常见搭配**：intended for nonspecialists; intended use; intended audience; unintended consequence
- **例句**：The summary is intended for readers without formal training in economics.
- **词族**：intend (v.); intention (n.); intentional (adj.); unintended (adj.)
- **对比**：*be used by* 描述实际用户，*be intended for* 描述设计目标，两者可能不一致。

## 7. cumbersome

- **意思**：笨重、繁琐，导致操作缓慢或不便。
- **常见搭配**：a cumbersome registration process; cumbersome equipment; administratively cumbersome
- **例句**：The verification step is useful, but the current process is too cumbersome for occasional users.
- **词族／对比**：*cumbersome* 强调负担和步骤；*complex* 只表示结构复杂，不一定难用。

## 8. trade-off

- **意思**：改善一项目标时牺牲另一项目标的取舍关系。
- **常见搭配**：a trade-off between simplicity and control; make a trade-off; an acceptable trade-off
- **例句**：Removing advanced settings creates a trade-off between ease of use and flexibility.
- **对比**：*disadvantage* 是单独的缺点；*trade-off* 强调两个目标相互牵制。

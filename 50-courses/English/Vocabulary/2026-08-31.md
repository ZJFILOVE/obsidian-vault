# Vocabulary — Day 13

## Core language blocks
- **be accustomed to doing sth.**
- **be exposed to information/content**
- **develop a habit of doing sth.**
- **association / correlation**
- **causation**
- **prove that...**
- **at intervals**

## Logic
**association/correlation ≠ causation**.
`indicate/suggest` are weaker than `prove`.

# Vocabulary — Day 10

## Core language blocks
- **be distracted by**
- **distract sb. from sth.**
- **take on additional tasks**
- **What really matters is not A, but B.**
- **provide sb. with sth.**
- **enable sb. to do sth.**

## Trap
Say `students are easily distracted`, not `students are easy to be distracted`.

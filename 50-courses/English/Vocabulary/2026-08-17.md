# Vocabulary — Day 3

## Core collocations
- **distinguish A from B**
- **affect sth.** / **have an effect on sth.**
- **ensure that...** / **assure sb. that...**
- **tend to do sth.**
- **raise concerns about**

## Practice
- distinguish reliable information **from** misleading content
- Lack of sleep may **affect** concentration.
- Repeat the experiment to **ensure that** the result is reliable.
- Students **tend to** memorize facts.
- AI has **raised concerns about** academic honesty.

## Trap
`affect` is usually a verb; `effect` is usually a noun. `raise` is transitive; `rise` is not.

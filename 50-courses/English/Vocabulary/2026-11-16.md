# English Vocabulary — 2026-11-16：描述尺度、基准与长期模式的 8 个词块

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-11-16|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-11-16|今日翻译]]

## 1. over the short / long term

- **含义：** 从短期／长期来看；*term* 指分析时间范围。
- **常见搭配：** change over the short term; remain stable over the long term; short-term variation; long-term trend
- **例句：** *A policy may reduce waiting times over the short term without solving the staffing problem over the long term.*
- **词族：** short-term / long-term（形容词，置于名词前通常加连字符）
- **辨析：** *in the long run* 更口语、更概括；*over a 30-year period* 给出具体观察窗。

## 2. at a particular location

- **含义：** 在某一特定地点；用来限定观测的空间范围。
- **常见搭配：** conditions at a particular location; measurements taken at multiple locations; location-specific effects
- **例句：** *A sensor reading at a particular location should not be treated as a citywide average.*
- **词族：** locate; location; local; locally
- **辨析：** *particular* 强调“这一具体对象”；*peculiar* 常表示“奇怪的”或“特有的”，不能随意替换。

## 3. emerge from

- **含义：** 从大量过程或证据中逐渐显现、形成。
- **常见搭配：** a pattern emerges from the data; consensus emerges from discussion; problems emerge from implementation
- **例句：** *A consistent pattern emerged from several years of observations.*
- **词族：** emerge; emergence; emerging
- **辨析：** *result from* 强调因果结果；*emerge from* 可以只表示“逐渐显现”，不一定证明单一原因。

## 4. establish a baseline

- **含义：** 建立基准线，供后续比较变化。
- **常见搭配：** collect baseline data; establish a baseline before the intervention; compare results with the baseline
- **例句：** *The team recorded energy use for six months to establish a baseline before installing new equipment.*
- **词族：** base; baseline; base-level（较少用）
- **辨析：** baseline 是比较起点；*benchmark* 往往是目标、行业标准或参照对象，两者有时重叠但不等同。

## 5. be averaged over

- **含义：** 在某段时间、某组对象或某个范围内取平均。
- **常见搭配：** be averaged over 30 years; values averaged across sites; annual average
- **例句：** *The reported figure was averaged over all participating schools, so it hides differences between them.*
- **词族：** average（名词／形容词／动词）; averaging
- **辨析：** *average over time* 指跨时间聚合；*typical* 表示“典型”，不一定由算术平均得到。

## 6. on its own

- **含义：** 单独地、仅凭自身；常用于限制证据强度。
- **常见搭配：** cannot explain the change on its own; insufficient on its own; valuable on its own
- **例句：** *One unusually busy week is not enough on its own to justify hiring a permanent employee.*
- **近义表达：** by itself; in isolation
- **辨析：** *alone* 既可指“独自”，也可指“仅仅”；*on its own* 在证据句中歧义更少。

## 7. be associated with

- **含义：** 与……相关；只陈述共同变化，不自动宣称因果。
- **常见搭配：** be positively associated with; an association between A and B; remain associated after adjustment
- **例句：** *Longer commute times were associated with lower reported satisfaction, but the survey did not identify the cause.*
- **词族：** associate; association; associated
- **辨析：** *cause / lead to* 表示因果，证据要求更高；*correlate with* 更偏统计表达。

## 8. generalize from A to B

- **含义：** 把从 A 得到的结论推广到 B。
- **常见搭配：** generalize from a small sample to the population; findings are not generalizable; limited external validity
- **例句：** *We should not generalize from one examination week to the entire academic year.*
- **词族：** general; generally; generalize; generalization; generalizable
- **辨析：** *summarize* 是概括已有内容；*generalize* 涉及把结论扩展到更广范围。

## 组合使用｜把结论放回正确尺度

过强：

> One cold day disproves a long-term warming trend.

更准确：

> A cold day describes conditions **at a particular location** over a short period. **On its own**, it does not overturn a trend **averaged over** many years and locations.

学术版：

> Although the observation may be unusual relative to an established baseline, it is not appropriate to generalize from a single event to a long-term climate pattern.

# Vocabulary — 2026-11-04：把测量值、感知与比较条件说准确

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-11-04|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-11-04|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-11-04|词汇]]

以下 8 个词块由 NPS 今日文章的真实语境引出；释义、搭配、例句、对比与测验均为原创。重点是准确表达数量关系和观察条件，而不是背光学术语。

## 1. represent a narrow band of — 只占……中的窄小范围

- **核心义：** form only a small, limited part of a wider range
- **常用搭配：** represent a narrow band of frequencies / wavelengths / possible outcomes
- **原创例句：** The temperatures recorded during one afternoon represent a narrow band of the conditions the material may face.
- **词族：** represent；representation；representative。
- **对比：** represent 在这里是“构成／占据”，不是“作为某人的代表”。
- **边界：** small sample 和 narrow band 都提示覆盖有限，但前者偏样本数量，后者偏连续范围。

## 2. be more sensitive to — 对……更敏感

- **核心义：** respond more strongly to a given change or signal
- **常用搭配：** be sensitive to light / temperature / wording / small changes
- **原创例句：** The second sensor is more sensitive to small pressure changes near the lower end of the range.
- **词族：** sense；sensitive；sensitivity；sensitise。
- **对比：** sensitive 不等于 accurate；设备可能很敏感但存在系统偏差。
- **搭配提醒：** 用 sensitive to，不写 sensitive for。

## 3. be perceived as — 被感知或理解为

- **核心义：** be experienced, noticed or interpreted in a particular way
- **常用搭配：** be perceived as brighter / unfair / difficult / a threat
- **原创例句：** The same waiting time may be perceived as longer when no progress information is provided.
- **词族：** perceive；perception；perceptible；imperceptible。
- **对比：** be measured as 报告测量结果；be perceived as 报告体验或解释。
- **语法：** 后接形容词或名词，不写 perceive like。

## 4. be proportional to — 与……成比例

- **核心义：** vary according to a constant ratio
- **常用搭配：** directly / inversely proportional to；roughly proportional to
- **原创例句：** In this simplified model, processing time is roughly proportional to the number of records.
- **词族：** proportion；proportional；proportionally；disproportionate。
- **对比：** be associated with 只说明共同变化；be proportional to 对函数关系要求更强。
- **边界：** roughly proportional 是近似关系，不可替代精确拟合。

## 5. a wide range of — 范围广泛的

- **核心义：** many different values, types or conditions between broad limits
- **常用搭配：** a wide range of temperatures / responses / applications / backgrounds
- **原创例句：** The coating remained stable across a wide range of humidity levels.
- **词族：** range；ranging from A to B。
- **对比：** a large number of 强调数量；a wide range of 强调差异与跨度。
- **搭配提醒：** range 通常接 of；若给端点，用 range from ... to ...。

## 6. be dependent on — 取决于

- **核心义：** change according to a condition, variable or context
- **常用搭配：** be highly / partly / strongly dependent on
- **原创例句：** The usefulness of the warning is dependent on when it reaches the user.
- **词族：** depend；dependent；dependence；independent。
- **对比：** be caused by 声称因果来源；be dependent on 强调条件关系，未必说明唯一原因。
- **语法：** dependent on，不写 dependent of。

## 7. impair someone’s ability to — 损害某人做……的能力

- **核心义：** make a function or task more difficult or less effective
- **常用搭配：** impair vision / judgement / memory / the ability to detect
- **原创例句：** Repeated interruptions can impair a reader’s ability to follow a long argument.
- **词族：** impair；impairment；impaired。
- **对比：** prevent 表示阻止；impair 表示削弱，仍可能完成。
- **语气：** 正式写作中比 make worse 更具体。

## 8. put a value in perspective — 把数值放回合适参照

- **核心义：** explain what a number means by comparing it with relevant baselines
- **常用搭配：** put the figure / cost / risk / increase in perspective
- **原创例句：** Comparing the annual cost with one day of operation puts the figure in perspective.
- **词族：** perspective；from the perspective of。
- **对比：** provide context 可加入各种背景；put in perspective 常通过比较修正过大或过小的直觉。
- **使用边界：** 参照必须相关；用无关的大数字作陪衬不算有效比较。

## 词块联结练习

用五句英文解释一个测量结果，至少使用六个今日词块：

1. 说明样本只覆盖一个 narrow band；
2. 指出仪器对某类变化 more sensitive；
3. 区分 measured value 与 perceived effect；
4. 说明某关系是否 proportional；
5. 写出结果 dependent on 的条件；
6. 用相关基线 put the value in perspective。

> [!answer]- 示例（原创）
> The morning measurements represent a narrow band of the building’s operating conditions. The sensor is more sensitive to rapid changes than to slow drift. A small numerical difference may still be perceived as noticeable by occupants. However, perceived comfort is not proportional to temperature alone. It is also dependent on humidity, airflow and clothing. Comparing the change with normal day-to-day variation puts the value in perspective.

## 易混对比

| 表达 | 重点 | 不应偷换成 |
|---|---|---|
| be measured as | 仪器或规则给出的值 | be perceived as |
| be perceived as | 人的体验或解释 | be objectively equal to |
| be proportional to | 固定比例关系 | merely be associated with |
| be dependent on | 随条件变化 | have one single cause |
| impair an ability | 削弱能力 | completely prevent |
| a wide range of | 类型或数值跨度大 | simply a large number of |

## 语境选择

选择最准确的表达：

1. The device can detect tiny changes, so it is very **(sensitive / proportional)** to pressure.
2. The queue felt longer without updates; it was **(measured as / perceived as)** more frustrating.
3. Accuracy changes with lighting conditions, so it is **(dependent on / representative of)** the environment.
4. The interruption made the task harder but not impossible; it **(impaired / prevented)** performance.
5. Compare the risk with the baseline rate to **(put it in perspective / represent it)**.

> [!answer]- 选择答案
> 1. sensitive
> 2. perceived as
> 3. dependent on
> 4. impaired
> 5. put it in perspective

## 闭卷检索测验

1. 写出“可见光只占电磁波谱中的窄小范围”。
2. sensitive 与 accurate 为什么不能互换？
3. 用 be perceived as 写一句含 waiting time 的句子。
4. associated with 与 proportional to 的证据要求有何不同？
5. 改错：The result is dependent of the background.
6. a wide range of 与 a large number of 的重点有何不同？
7. impair 与 prevent 的强度差异是什么？
8. 什么样的参照不能真正 put a value in perspective？

> [!answer]- 检索答案
> 1. Visible light represents a narrow band of the electromagnetic spectrum.
> 2. Sensitivity is responsiveness to small changes; accuracy is closeness to the correct value.
> 3. The same waiting time may be perceived as longer when no progress information is available.
> 4. Association only requires variables to vary together, whereas proportionality requires a consistent ratio.
> 5. The result is dependent **on** the background.
> 6. A wide range emphasises diversity or span; a large number emphasises quantity.
> 7. Impair means weaken; prevent means stop something from occurring or being completed.
> 8. An unrelated, emotionally striking or differently defined comparison.

# Vocabulary — 2026-10-27：从注意力写到设计反馈

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-10-27|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-10-27|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-10-27|词汇]]

## 1. capitalize on — 利用……获得优势

- **核心义：** use a situation or opportunity to gain an advantage
- **常用搭配：** capitalize on growing demand / public interest / an unexpected opportunity
- **例句：** The local museum capitalized on renewed interest in crafts by extending its weekend workshops.
- **词族：** capitalization 在金融和书写语境另有含义，不要由短语机械推出。
- **对比：** **take advantage of** 更日常，也可能暗含占便宜；capitalize on 常用于商业、政策或策略说明。
- **边界：** 说明利用了机会，不自动证明策略成功。

## 2. be attuned to — 敏锐察觉并适应

- **核心义：** be aware of and responsive to a condition, preference or change
- **常用搭配：** be attuned to user needs / local concerns / subtle changes
- **例句：** Good facilitators are attuned to signs that quieter participants are being excluded.
- **词族：** attune（使适应、使协调）；attunement（协调／觉察，较正式）。
- **对比：** **be aware of** 只表示知道；be attuned to 更强调持续感知和回应。

## 3. make or break — 决定成败

- **核心义：** have a decisive effect on success or failure
- **常用搭配：** make or break a campaign / a small business / a first impression
- **例句：** Reliable delivery during the first month can make or break a subscription service.
- **语域：** 带强调色彩，适合评论和口语；学术写作中若证据不足，可降为 **substantially affect**。
- **易错点：** 不要写 make or broke；固定形式用 break。

## 4. wind up — 最终处于；结果做了

- **常用结构：** wind up doing / wind up in a place / wind up with a result
- **例句：** Without a clear archive, several teams wound up collecting the same data twice.
- **语法：** 过去式和过去分词是 **wound up**；后接动名词，不接 to do。
- **对比：** **end up** 更常见；wind up 稍口语，常带非预期结果。
- **歧义：** wind up a company 也可表示结束／清算公司，要靠宾语判断。

## 5. get in on — 参与并分享机会或收益

- **常用搭配：** get in on the trend / the deal / the action
- **例句：** Independent shops got in on the repair trend by offering evening workshops.
- **语域：** 非正式，适合口语和新闻特写；正式写作可换 **participate in / enter**。
- **对比：** **join** 只表示加入；get in on 往往暗示机会已形成，行动者希望分得收益。

## 6. render — 使成为；呈现；渲染

- **核心义：** cause something to be in a state, or represent it in a particular form
- **常用搭配：** render a rule ineffective / render an image digitally / render assistance
- **例句：** Poor contrast can render an otherwise useful chart unreadable on a phone.
- **语法：** render + object + adjective：render the device unusable；不要加 as。
- **词族／对比：** rendering 可指图像渲染；**make** 更日常，render 更正式且常用于结果状态。

## 7. homogeneity — 同质性；缺少差异

- **核心义：** the quality of being similar throughout
- **常用搭配：** cultural homogeneity / visual homogeneity / increasing homogeneity
- **例句：** A shared template improved navigation but also produced visual homogeneity across the sites.
- **词族：** homogeneous（同质的）；heterogeneous（异质的）；heterogeneity（异质性）。
- **易错点：** homogeneous 读音中没有 extra “i”；拼写也不是 homogenous（后者另有较少见含义）。
- **边界：** 相似不一定有害；要说明在哪个维度相似，以及损失了什么差异。

## 8. crave — 强烈渴望

- **常用结构：** crave attention / variety / a sense of connection；crave doing 较少见
- **例句：** After a week of scheduled calls, the team craved an afternoon of uninterrupted work.
- **词族：** craving（强烈欲望）。
- **对比：** **want** 中性；crave 强度更高，常与情感、食物或体验相连，不适合每个正式场景。
- **语用：** 可写矛盾需求：Users may crave both public recognition and private, unmeasured time.

## 词块联结练习

围绕一个产品或公共服务，完成四句：

**be attuned to a need → capitalize on an opportunity → wind up changing behavior → examine whether homogeneity increased**

要求：第一句写行动者，第二句写激励，第三句写非预期结果，第四句写待验证问题。

> [!answer]- 原创示例
> The transit agency became more attuned to passengers who checked arrival times on their phones. A contractor capitalized on this demand by adding prominent countdown screens. Some commuters wound up watching the display rather than platform announcements. Researchers should still examine whether standardized screens improved clarity without creating excessive visual homogeneity across stations.

## 近义辨析：观察现象，还是宣称结果

1. 知道需求并持续回应 → **be attuned to**，比 be aware of 更主动。
2. 借已有机会获益 → **capitalize on**，不等于创造了机会。
3. 出现非预期终点 → **wind up**，语气比 finally 中性叙事更强。
4. 使系统无法使用 → **render the system unusable**，结构是宾语加形容词。
5. 在某一维度越来越相似 → **increasing homogeneity**，仍需定义比较维度。

## 闭卷检索测验

1. 补全：capitalize ___；be attuned ___；get in ___ the trend；wind up ___ the same choice。
2. 用英文解释 be aware of 与 be attuned to 的差别。
3. 改写：Poor labeling made the guide unable to be used.（用 render）
4. 为 make or break 写一个较谨慎的正式替代表达。
5. 用 homogeneity 和 heterogeneous 各写一句，明确比较维度。
6. 用至少四个今日词块解释一个“指标改变行为”的例子，并补一句证据限制。

> [!answer]- 检索答案
> 1. on；to；on；making。
> 2. Being aware means knowing that something exists; being attuned also suggests sensitivity and a readiness to respond.
> 3. Poor labeling rendered the guide unusable.
> 4. Delivery reliability may substantially affect the service's early adoption.
> 5. 示例：A single template increased visual homogeneity. The users remained heterogeneous in age, language and technical experience.
> 6. 合格答案应写出行动者如何觉察激励、利用机会并最终改变行为，同时说明观察不能自动证明长期效果。

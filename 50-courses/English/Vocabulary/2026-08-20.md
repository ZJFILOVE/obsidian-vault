# Vocabulary — Day 6

## Core collocations
- **meet the demand / meet the needs**
- **assume that...**
- **identify a problem / weakness**
- **prevent sb. from doing sth.**
- **increasing / sufficient evidence**
- **consist of**

## Trap
`assume that...` = 假定；`assure sb. that...` = 向某人保证。

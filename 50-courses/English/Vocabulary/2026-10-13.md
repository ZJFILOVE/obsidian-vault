## 1. identical in mass

- **含义：** 质量完全相同
- **例句：** *The two particles are identical in mass but carry opposite electric charges.*

## 2. be confined to

- **含义：** 被限制在……范围内
- **例句：** *Particle confinement is limited to a few milliseconds under standard conditions.*

## 3. rule out

- **含义：** 排除（某种可能性）
- **例句：** *Further trials are needed to rule out systematic measurement errors.*

## 4. give rise to

- **含义：** 引起；导致
- **例句：** *Small asymmetries in the early universe may have given rise to the matter we observe today.*

## 5. account for

- **含义：** 解释；占……比例
- **例句：** *Standard models cannot yet account for the observed imbalance.*

## 6. in equal amounts

- **含义：** 等量地
- **例句：** *Theory suggests matter and antimatter should have been produced in equal amounts.*

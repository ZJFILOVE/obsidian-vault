# Vocabulary Practice — 2026-09-07

## 1. infer /ɪnˈfɜːr/
**v. 推断；从证据得出结论**

### Use
- infer A from B
- infer a relationship from the data
- it can be inferred that...

### Word origin
来自拉丁语 *inferre*，由 **in-**（into）和 **ferre**（carry, bear）构成。可以把它理解为把观察到的证据“带入”推理，最终得到一个结论。

### Word family
- inference n. 推断；推论
- inferential adj. 推理的

### Example
> Researchers inferred the reaction mechanism from several indirect measurements.

---

## 2. circumference /sərˈkʌmfərəns/
**n. 圆周；周长**

### Word origin
来自拉丁语中表示 **around** 的 *circum-* 和表示 **carry/bear** 的词根 *fer-*。**circum-** 也出现在 *circumnavigate*（环航）等词中。

### Collocations
- Earth's circumference
- measure the circumference
- circumference of a circle

### Example
> Eratosthenes estimated Earth's circumference using shadows measured in two locations.

---

## 3. observable /əbˈzɜːrvəbəl/
**adj. 可观察到的；可观测的**

### Word family
- observe v.
- observation n.
- observable adj./n.
- observer n.

### Collocations
- observable evidence
- observable change
- directly observable

### Example
> A useful model should make predictions about observable phenomena.

---

## 4. estimate /ˈestɪmeɪt/ (v.), /ˈestɪmət/ (n.)
**v. 估计；估算  n. 估计值**

### Use
- estimate the size/cost/value
- a rough estimate
- an accurate estimate
- be estimated at...

### Example
> We can estimate the lifetime from the fitted decay constant.

### Nuance
**estimate** 通常强调基于已有信息得到近似数值；**guess** 可以几乎没有充分依据，因此科研表达里通常优先用 *estimate*。

---

## 5. ingenious /ɪnˈdʒiːniəs/
**adj. 巧妙的；有创造力的**

### Collocations
- an ingenious solution
- an ingenious experiment
- an ingenious method

### Memory note
不要和 **ingenuous**（坦率的、天真的）混淆。

### Example
> The experiment was ingenious because it converted a difficult measurement into a much simpler one.

---

## 6. deduce /dɪˈdjuːs/
**v. 推论；演绎出**

### Word origin
来自拉丁语 *deducere*：**de-**（away/down）+ **ducere**（lead）。同一个 *duc/duct* 词根还能见于 **conduct, introduce, produce** 等词。

### Use
- deduce A from B
- deduce a conclusion

### Infer vs. deduce
日常和科研英语中二者常有重叠。**deduce** 更容易让人联想到依据原则或证据进行逻辑推演；**infer** 是更通用的“从证据得到结论”。

### Example
> From the spectral changes, we can deduce that a new intermediate has formed.

---

## 7. compelling /kəmˈpelɪŋ/
**adj. 令人信服的；强有力的；非常吸引人的**

### Collocations
- compelling evidence
- a compelling argument
- a compelling explanation

### Word family
- compel v. 迫使；使不得不
- compelling adj.

### Example
> A single measurement is rarely compelling evidence for a complicated mechanism.

---

## 8. account for
**phr. v. 解释；说明；占（比例）**

### Two high-value meanings
1. **explain:** This model cannot account for the observed shift.
2. **constitute a proportion:** One component accounts for 60% of the signal.

### Example
> Any proposed mechanism should account for all major experimental observations.

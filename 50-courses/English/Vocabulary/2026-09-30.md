# Vocabulary Practice — 2026-09-30

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-09-30|Today's input]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-09-30|Today's translation]]

Today's vocabulary partly connects to the NIH article, but the target is broader: reusable English for discussing **signals, interactions, mechanisms, evidence, and cautious inference**.

## 1. encode information

**Meaning:** 编码信息；用某种可传递或可识别的模式来表示信息。

### Collocations
- encode sensory information
- encode a signal
- encode data
- information is encoded in...
- neural coding / data encoding

### Example
> The pattern of activity may encode information about both the identity and intensity of a stimulus.

### Contrast
- **store information** = 保存信息
- **transmit information** = 传递信息
- **encode information** = 把信息转换为某种模式或表示形式

In biology, “encode” can describe how activity patterns represent information. In computing, it can describe converting data into a defined representation.

## 2. modulate a response

**Meaning:** 调节反应；使反应变强、变弱或以其他方式发生改变，而不一定彻底开启或关闭。

### Collocations
- modulate neural activity
- modulate a response
- modulate expression
- modulate the effect of
- modulation of signaling

### Example
> Background noise can modulate how strongly listeners respond to a faint sound.

### Word family
- **modulate** (v.)
- **modulation** (n.)
- **modulatory** (adj., technical)

### Contrast
**Control** is broader and may imply direct regulation. **Modulate** usually emphasizes graded adjustment.

## 3. enhance

**Meaning:** 增强、提高；让某种效果、性能或反应更强。

### Collocations
- enhance performance
- enhance a response
- enhance sensitivity
- enhance the effect of
- enhance visibility

### Example
> Clear labeling can enhance the usability of a complex interface.

### Word family
- **enhancement** (n.)

### Contrast
- **increase** = 最一般的“增加”
- **enhance** = 强调改善、增强某种已有能力或效果

## 4. suppress

**Meaning:** 抑制、压低；减少一个反应、信号、过程或表达的强度。

### Collocations
- suppress a response
- suppress activity
- suppress expression
- suppress a signal
- suppress symptoms

### Example
> One background signal may suppress the response to another, making the second harder to detect.

### Contrast
- **eliminate** = 完全消除
- **suppress** = 压低、抑制，但不一定降到零

This distinction is useful in biology, statistics, engineering, and everyday analytical writing.

## 5. interact with

**Meaning:** 与……相互作用；A 的效果会因为 B 的存在而发生变化。

### Collocations
- interact with another factor
- interact with the environment
- two variables interact
- interaction effect
- drug interaction

### Example
> The effect of sleep duration may interact with stress level rather than operate independently.

### Contrast
**Co-occur** only means two things appear together. **Interact** means the behavior or effect of one depends on the other.

## 6. disentangle A from B

**Meaning:** 把两个混在一起、难以区分的因素拆开分析。

### Collocations
- disentangle cause from correlation
- disentangle one effect from another
- difficult to disentangle
- disentangle genetic and environmental influences

### Example
> The study design makes it difficult to disentangle the effect of the program from differences in who chose to participate.

### Contrast
- **separate** = 一般性的分开
- **disentangle** = 强调两个因素已经交织在一起，需要分析才能区分

A very useful academic verb when discussing confounding or overlapping mechanisms.

## 7. infer X from Y

**Meaning:** 根据 Y 推断 X；强调 X 不是直接观察到，而是由证据推出。

### Collocations
- infer a mechanism from the data
- infer intent from behavior
- infer causality from correlation
- cannot be inferred from...
- reasonable to infer that...

### Example
> We can observe the change in response directly, but the underlying mechanism must be inferred from the pattern of evidence.

### Word family
- **inference** (n.)
- **inferential** (adj.)

### Contrast
- **observe** = 直接看到或测到
- **infer** = 根据观察推断一个未被直接测量的结论

This distinction is central to academic writing.

## 8. be consistent with

**Meaning:** 与……一致；某个结果符合某种解释，但通常不表示该解释已经被唯一证明。

### Collocations
- be consistent with the hypothesis
- be consistent with previous findings
- results are consistent with...
- evidence is consistent with...

### Example
> The pattern is consistent with a learning effect, but other explanations remain possible.

### Contrast
**Prove** is much stronger. If several explanations could produce the same result, **be consistent with** is often the safer phrase.

## 9. more than the sum of its parts

**Meaning:** 整体表现不能仅由各部分单独表现简单相加来解释。

### Collocations
- be more than the sum of its parts
- the whole is more than the sum of its parts
- a non-additive effect
- combined effect

### Example
> A team can be more than the sum of its parts when members' skills complement one another.

### Academic precision
This expression is idiomatic and useful, but in formal scientific writing you may prefer:

> The combined effect was **non-additive**.

or

> The effect of one factor depended on the presence of another.

## Reusable mini-patterns

> Neural activity can **encode information** about a stimulus.

> A second factor may **modulate the response** rather than produce a separate effect.

> The treatment **enhanced** one response but **suppressed** another.

> Two variables may **interact with** each other instead of acting independently.

> The design cannot fully **disentangle** selection effects **from** treatment effects.

> We should not **infer causality from** correlation alone.

> The result is **consistent with** the proposed mechanism, but it does not uniquely establish it.

> In an interactive system, the combined outcome may be **more than the sum of its parts**.

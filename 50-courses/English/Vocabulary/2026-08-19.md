# Vocabulary — Day 5

## Core collocations
- **provide sb. with sth.**
- **evaluate reliability/evidence**
- **depend on** / **be dependent on**
- **enable sb. to do sth.**
- **draw a conclusion**
- **have access to**

## Practice
Online learning provides students **with** flexibility and enables them **to access** resources.

## Trap
Use **draw a conclusion**, not `make a conclusion`.

# English Vocabulary — 2026-11-11：描述推动、引导、碰撞与尺度

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-11-11|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-11-11|今日翻译]]

以下 8 个词与词块来自今日阅读任务，但都可以迁移到工程、政策、研究方法和日常系统说明。

## 1. propel A forward

**含义**：推动 A 向前；也可比喻某事快速发展。

- **搭配**：propel a vehicle / propel a project forward / be propelled by pressure
- **例句**：A clear deadline propelled the stalled project forward.
- **词族**：*propel* (v.)；*propulsion* (n.)；*propellant* (n.)
- **对比**：*push* 最普通；*propel* 强调持续动力或机制。

## 2. steer A toward / away from B

**含义**：引导方向；既可指物理转向，也可指把讨论、政策或选择导向某处。

- **搭配**：steer a beam / steer the conversation / steer users away from unsafe choices
- **例句**：The moderator steered the discussion back toward the evidence.
- **词族**：*steer* (v./n.)；*steering* (n./adj.)
- **对比**：*guide* 较中性；*steer* 更强调控制方向。

## 3. collide with

**含义**：与……碰撞；比喻计划、利益或观点正面冲突时也可使用。

- **搭配**：particles collide with a target / priorities collide / collide head-on
- **例句**：The expansion plan collided with strict conservation rules.
- **词族**：*collision* (n.)；*collisional* (adj., 技术用语)
- **对比**：*crash into* 更口语且常暗示损坏；*collide with* 更中性。

## 4. be transformed into

**含义**：被转化为另一种形式、状态或用途。

- **搭配**：energy is transformed into heat / data are transformed into indicators
- **例句**：Raw survey responses were transformed into three comparable measures.
- **词族**：*transform* (v.)；*transformation* (n.)；*transformative* (adj.)
- **对比**：*change into* 只说变化；*transform into* 常强调结构或功能发生显著改变。

## 5. probe

**含义**：仔细探查、追问；名词也可指探测器或探针。

- **搭配**：probe the structure of / probe a claim / a space probe
- **例句**：The follow-up interview probed why several participants left the programme.
- **词族**：*probe* (v./n.)；*probing* (adj.)
- **对比**：*examine* 是广义检查；*probe* 暗含深入未知部分。

## 6. concentrate A at / in B

**含义**：把 A 集中在某个位置、群体或尺度。

- **搭配**：concentrate energy in a small area / risk is concentrated among / concentrated effort
- **例句**：The average looks modest, but the burden is concentrated among night-shift workers.
- **词族**：*concentrate* (v./n.)；*concentration* (n.)；*concentrated* (adj.)
- **对比**：*focus on* 表示注意力或重点；*concentrate A in B* 明确 A 的分布。

## 7. provided that

**含义**：只要；表示结论成立所需的明确条件。

- **搭配**：can proceed provided that / effective provided that / safe provided that
- **例句**：The dataset can be shared, provided that identifying details are removed.
- **对比**：*if* 最宽泛；*provided that* 更强调条件是许可或结果的前提。

## 8. supply A with B

**含义**：向 A 提供 B；注意宾语顺序。

- **搭配**：supply a machine with power / supply researchers with data / a stable supply of
- **例句**：The archive supplies researchers with images and detailed provenance records.
- **词族**：*supply* (v./n.)；*supplier* (n.)
- **对比**：*provide B for A* 与 *provide A with B* 均可；*supply* 常强调持续、成批或系统性的提供。

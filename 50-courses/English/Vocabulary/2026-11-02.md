# Vocabulary — 2026-11-02：谈证据、修正与解释边界

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-11-02|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-11-02|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-11-02|词汇]]

以下 8 个词块由今日英国图书馆文章的真实用法引出；释义、搭配、例句、对比和测验均为原创。今天不追求生僻词，而是练习怎样说明材料位置、研究动作和解释边界。

## 1. behind-the-scenes insight — 幕后视角；对运作过程的深入了解

- **核心义：** information that reveals how a process or institution works away from public view
- **常用搭配：** offer / provide behind-the-scenes insight into a project
- **原创例句：** Staff interviews provided behind-the-scenes insight into how emergency decisions were recorded.
- **词族：** insight；insightful。
- **对比：** an overview 给总体轮廓；behind-the-scenes insight 强调普通受众平时看不到的过程。
- **使用边界：** insight 不自动等于完整或无偏的记录。

## 2. stand on the fringes of — 处于……的边缘

- **核心义：** remain outside the central or most accepted part of a field, group or industry
- **常用搭配：** stand on the fringes of academic debate / the industry / public life
- **原创例句：** Citizen archives once stood on the fringes of formal historical research.
- **词族：** fringe 可作名词或形容词，如 fringe group。
- **对比：** be excluded from 强调被排斥；stand on the fringes of 只描述位置，不必然说明原因。
- **语体提醒：** 不要把 fringes 直译成衣服的“流苏”。

## 3. a line of questioning — 一组连贯的提问思路

- **核心义：** a connected sequence of questions designed to explore a topic
- **常用搭配：** pursue / change / return to a line of questioning
- **原创例句：** The interviewer changed her line of questioning when the participant raised a safety concern.
- **词族：** question；questioning；questionnaire。
- **对比：** a questionnaire 是预先设计的问卷；a line of questioning 可以在访谈中灵活调整。
- **边界：** 它描述提问路径，不说明问题一定中立。

## 4. deviate from — 偏离；不完全遵循

- **核心义：** move away from a plan, standard, route or expected pattern
- **常用搭配：** deviate from the script / protocol / original plan / average
- **原创例句：** Researchers may deviate from the interview guide when a new theme requires clarification.
- **词族：** deviation；deviant（语义更强，慎用）。
- **对比：** depart from 常用于较正式的规则或传统；deviate from 常强调相对既定路径的变化。
- **搭配提醒：** 用 from，不用 deviate the plan。

## 5. be met with — 遭遇；受到某种反应

- **核心义：** receive a particular response when an idea, proposal or action is presented
- **常用搭配：** be met with resistance / skepticism / enthusiasm / silence
- **原创例句：** The proposed schedule was met with cautious support rather than immediate approval.
- **词族：** meet；meeting；注意这里不是“会见”。
- **对比：** cause resistance 直接宣称因果；be met with resistance 只报告随后的反应。
- **语法：** 被动结构后接反应名词，通常不接 by people unless needed。

## 6. be subject to review — 需要接受审查

- **核心义：** be open to formal examination and possible revision
- **常用搭配：** be subject to independent / annual / peer review
- **原创例句：** Claims based on newly digitized records should remain subject to review.
- **词族：** review；reviewer；reviewable。
- **对比：** under review 表示当前正在审查；subject to review 表示制度上可能或必须审查。
- **边界：** 经过 review 不等于已经被证明毫无错误。

## 7. seep into — 逐渐渗入；不明显地影响

- **核心义：** gradually enter a place, process or account, often without a clear boundary
- **常用搭配：** assumptions / emotions / commercial pressures seep into a decision
- **原创例句：** Personal expectations can seep into a supposedly neutral summary.
- **词族：** seep；seepage 多用于液体渗漏。
- **对比：** insert 是有意放入；seep into 常暗示缓慢、难察觉。
- **语体提醒：** 用于抽象影响时带比喻色彩，适合分析性写作。

## 8. draw on — 利用；借鉴；以……为资源

- **核心义：** use knowledge, experience, evidence or resources as a basis
- **常用搭配：** draw on archival evidence / personal experience / several disciplines
- **原创例句：** The final report drew on interviews, maintenance records and passenger surveys.
- **词族：** drawing 在此不作“绘画”理解。
- **对比：** rely on 可能强调依赖；draw on 强调把某项资源调动进当前任务。
- **边界：** draw on personal experience 不等于把经验当成普遍事实。

## 词块联结练习

用 5–6 句描述一次访谈研究，至少包含四个词块：

1. 访谈提供了什么 behind-the-scenes insight；
2. 研究者原先采用什么 line of questioning；
3. 为什么需要 deviate from 预设问题；
4. 哪些个人假设可能 seep into 解释；
5. 结论为何仍应 be subject to review。

> [!answer]- 示例（原创）
> The interviews provided behind-the-scenes insight into how the service handled missed deliveries. The researchers began with a fixed line of questioning but deviated from it when several participants raised privacy concerns. They drew on complaint records to compare those accounts with documented events. Even so, the team acknowledged that staff assumptions could seep into its interpretation. The resulting recommendations therefore remained subject to independent review.

## 易混对比

| 表达 | 重点 | 示例判断 |
|---|---|---|
| under review | 目前正在审查 | The proposal is under review this week. |
| subject to review | 制度上可／须审查 | Every safety claim is subject to review. |
| draw on | 调用一种资源 | The report draws on interviews. |
| rely on | 依赖某资源 | The estimate relies on self-reported data. |
| on the fringes of | 处在边缘位置 | The practice remained on the fringes of the field. |
| excluded from | 被排除在外 | Temporary workers were excluded from the survey. |

## 闭卷检索测验

1. 写出“为项目提供幕后视角”的英文词块。
2. 用一个句子区分 questionnaire 与 a line of questioning。
3. 改错：The interviewer deviated the original script.
4. “提案受到谨慎支持”怎样用 be met with 表达？
5. under review 与 subject to review 的时间含义有什么不同？
6. 用 seep into 写一句“个人偏好逐渐影响选择”。
7. draw on personal experience 为什么不等于把个人经验当普遍规律？
8. 用 stand on the fringes of 写一句不含负面原因推断的句子。

> [!answer]- 检索答案
> 1. provide behind-the-scenes insight into the project
> 2. A questionnaire fixes the questions in advance, whereas a line of questioning may develop during an interview.
> 3. The interviewer deviated **from** the original script.
> 4. The proposal was met with cautious support.
> 5. under review 表示当前正在审查；subject to review 表示制度上可能或必须接受审查。
> 6. Personal preferences can seep into a selection process unless the criteria are made explicit.
> 7. It identifies a resource used in the account; the writer must still state the scope and compare it with other evidence.
> 8. The method stood on the fringes of the field for decades before receiving wider attention.

## 间隔复习

- **明天：** 闭卷写出 deviate from、be met with、be subject to review 的搭配。
- **三天后：** 用 draw on、seep into 写两句，分别说明证据来源和偏差风险。
- **一周后：** 用 behind-the-scenes insight、line of questioning 和 on the fringes of 概述一个你熟悉的访谈或纪录片。

# Vocabulary — Day 2

## Core collocations
- **encourage sb. to do sth.**
- **maintain concentration**
- **consider the evidence**
- **contribute to**
- **be dependent on**
- **rather than**

## Practice
1. The university introduced measures to ___ students to exercise. **encourage**
2. Phone notifications make it difficult to ___ concentration. **maintain**
3. Researchers should ___ all available evidence. **consider**
4. Regular exercise can ___ to better health. **contribute**
5. Students should not become excessively ___ on AI. **dependent**

## Trap
`contribute to` / `lead to` / `result in` use different prepositions.

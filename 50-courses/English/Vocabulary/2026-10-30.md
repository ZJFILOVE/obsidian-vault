# Vocabulary — 2026-10-30：把行为差异和研究范围说准确

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-10-30|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-10-30|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-10-30|词汇]]

以下词块由今日 NPS 研究简报的真实用法引出；释义、搭配、例句、对比和测验均为原创。重点是范围、变化和机制，不编造词源故事。

## 1. human-made structure — 人造结构／人工设施

- **核心义：** a physical structure built by people rather than a temporary human presence
- **常用搭配：** avoid human-made structures；build near a human-made structure；the density of human-made structures
- **原创例句：** The survey distinguished temporary crowds from permanent human-made structures such as roads and shelters.
- **词族：** structure；structural；infrastructure。
- **对比：** **human presence** 是人当时是否在场；human-made structure 即使无人使用也仍存在。
- **边界：** structure 不只指建筑物，也可包括道路、围栏或其他固定设施。

## 2. stay away from — 远离；避免接近

- **核心义：** deliberately or habitually keep a distance from a person, place or thing
- **常用搭配：** stay away from crowded areas / exposed food / unstable ground
- **原创例句：** During the nesting season, visitors are asked to stay away from the marked shore.
- **词族与语体：** **avoid** 更紧凑、稍正式；keep away from 常见于警示。
- **对比：** **move away from** 强调已经靠近后离开；stay away from 强调保持距离。

## 3. vary by — 因类别不同而变化

- **核心义：** differ according to a category such as species, age group or location
- **常用搭配：** vary by species / region / age / measurement method
- **原创例句：** The preferred pickup time varied by neighborhood, even though the service hours were identical.
- **词族：** vary；variation；variable；variability。
- **对比：** **vary with temperature** 常指随连续变量变化；**vary from site to site** 强调地点之间的范围。
- **边界：** vary by 指出异质性，但本身不解释原因。

## 4. track — 追踪；连续记录

- **核心义：** follow the location, condition or progress of something over time
- **常用搭配：** track animal movement / expenses / recovery / changes over time
- **原创例句：** The clinic tracked missed appointments for six months before changing its reminder system.
- **词族：** tracking；tracker；trace。
- **对比：** **monitor** 强调持续观察状态；track 常强调沿时间或路径记录变化。
- **边界：** tracking data can reveal patterns，但不自动解释这些模式为何出现。

## 5. before and during — 在……之前及期间

- **核心义：** compare a baseline period with a later period while an event or intervention is occurring
- **常用搭配：** before and during the pilot / closure / heat wave
- **原创例句：** Researchers measured platform crowding before and during the revised timetable.
- **对比：** **before and after** 包括事后时期；before and during 不告诉你事件结束后是否持续。
- **迁移提示：** 两个时期的季节、样本组成和测量方式也要尽量可比。

## 6. stick around — 留在附近；继续停留

- **核心义：** remain in a place for longer than expected or after circumstances change
- **常用搭配：** stick around after the event / for another hour / despite the noise
- **原创例句：** Several visitors stuck around after the lecture to examine the demonstration.
- **语体：** 口语色彩明显；正式写作可换成 **remain / continue to use the area**。
- **对比：** **stay** 中性；stick around 往往带有“没有马上离开”的语用意味。
- **边界：** 留在某地不一定说明偏好，也可能受食物、路线或障碍影响。

## 7. long-lasting effect — 持久影响

- **核心义：** an effect that continues beyond the immediate event
- **常用搭配：** have a long-lasting effect on behavior / health / public trust
- **原创例句：** A short disruption can have a long-lasting effect if it changes how users perceive reliability.
- **词族：** last；lasting；long-lasting。
- **对比：** **permanent** 表示不会消失；long-lasting 只表示持续较久，不保证永久。
- **边界：** 若观察期很短，应避免轻易把持续到研究末期写成 long-lasting。

## 8. balance the risks and benefits — 权衡风险与收益

- **核心义：** consider possible harms and gains together when making a choice
- **常用搭配：** balance the risks and benefits of treatment / proximity / a policy change
- **原创例句：** The committee balanced the risks and benefits of opening the archive to unsupervised visitors.
- **词族：** balance；balanced；imbalance。
- **对比：** **weigh the risks against the benefits** 更突出两方对照；balance 强调同时考虑。
- **边界：** 说某个行为符合风险收益权衡，是机制解释；若没有直接测量动机，应保留 may / could。

## 词块组合：从方法写到边界

用八个词块搭一段四句微型摘要：

1. Researchers **tracked** movement **before and during** a period of reduced human activity.
2. Responses **varied by** species, population and individual.
3. Some animals continued to **stay away from human-made structures**, while others **stuck around** in developed areas.
4. The pattern may reflect how animals **balance the risks and benefits** of proximity, but a **long-lasting effect** requires longer observation.

注意第四句用 may：前三句描述数据模式，第四句提出机制解释。

## 易混对比速查

| 想表达 | 更合适 | 需要避免 |
|---|---|---|
| 人当时是否出现 | human presence | 用 structure 代替人流 |
| 固定的人工环境 | human-made structures | 把所有结构都写成 buildings |
| 组别间不同 | vary by group | 直接写 the group caused |
| 连续记录位置 | track movement | 把 track 写成证明原因 |
| 事件期间的比较 | before and during | 擅自补出事后持续性 |
| 持续较久 | long-lasting | 在短观察期内写 permanent |

## 检索测验

1. 填空：The response varied ___ age group but changed ___ temperature.
2. 用正式英语改写：Several participants stuck around after the workshop.
3. human presence 与 human-made structure 的区别是什么？
4. 为什么 track a pattern 不能自动改写为 explain the pattern？
5. 用 before and during 写一句，同时指出比较的一个潜在限制。
6. 把 “The effect was permanent” 降低到现有短期数据能支持的强度。
7. 用 balance the risks and benefits 写一句，并用 may 标明它是解释。
8. 选词：The animals (stayed away from / moved away from) the road throughout the observation period. 哪个更贴切？为什么？

> [!answer]- 检索答案
> 1. **by；with**。
> 2. Several participants remained after the workshop to continue the discussion.
> 3. human presence 指人在某时某地的出现或活动；human-made structure 指道路、建筑、围栏等较持久的物理特征。
> 4. 追踪可以显示位置或状态随时间的模式，但原因可能需要额外变量、比较组或实验设计。
> 5. We measured noise before and during the festival, although the two periods differed in weather as well as visitor numbers.
> 6. The effect persisted until the end of the eight-week observation period.
> 7. The behavior may reflect how individuals balance the risks and benefits of entering a crowded space.
> 8. **stayed away from**，因为句子强调在整个观察期保持距离；moved away from 会暗示它们原先靠近，后来离开。

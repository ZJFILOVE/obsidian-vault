# English Vocabulary — 2026-11-13：谈规则、规避与执行的 8 个词块

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-11-13|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-11-13|今日翻译]]

这些词块可用于历史、公共政策、商业监管和学术论证。学习重点不是逐词背诵，而是掌握常见宾语、语气强度和近义表达之间的差别。

## 1. ration scarce goods

**含义：** 对稀缺商品实行定量配给。

- **常见搭配：** `ration food / fuel / medical supplies`; `a rationing system`; `ration coupons`
- **例句：** During an emergency, authorities may ration scarce goods to prevent a small group from buying up the entire supply.
- **词族：** `ration`（动词/定额），`rationing`（配给制度），`rationed`（受配给限制的）
- **对比：** `allocate` 强调分配资源，不一定限制个人购买；`ration` 通常暗含稀缺和数量上限。

## 2. circumvent restrictions

**含义：** 绕过限制，通常指利用漏洞而不正面遵守规则。

- **常见搭配：** `circumvent a ban / rule / safeguard`; `find ways to circumvent`
- **例句：** A rule may look strict on paper but remain ineffective if firms can easily circumvent its reporting requirements.
- **词族：** `circumvent`，`circumvention`
- **对比：** `violate` 是直接违反；`evade` 强调逃避义务或侦查；`circumvent` 常强调迂回绕开。

## 3. impose a ceiling price

**含义：** 设定最高限价。

- **常见搭配：** `impose / lift a price ceiling`; `sell above the ceiling price`
- **例句：** The city imposed a ceiling price while it monitored whether the shortage was temporary.
- **词族：** `impose`，`imposition`; `ceiling` 在这里是上限
- **对比：** `cap` 可作名词或动词，语体更简洁；`ceiling price` 更明确地指最高合法价格。

## 4. forge coupons or documents

**含义：** 伪造票券或文件。

- **常见搭配：** `forge a signature / certificate / permit`; `a forged document`; `counterfeit coupons`
- **例句：** Investigators found that some permits had been forged, while others were genuine but stolen.
- **词族：** `forge`，`forgery`，`forged`，`forger`
- **对比：** `counterfeit` 常指仿造货币或可流通票证；`forge` 常指签名、文件或证明。

## 5. divert goods from official channels

**含义：** 把商品从正式渠道转移出去。

- **常见搭配：** `divert funds / supplies / traffic`; `be diverted to`; `distribution channel`
- **例句：** Weak inventory controls made it easier to divert medical supplies from official channels.
- **词族：** `divert`，`diversion`
- **对比：** `redirect` 可以是公开、合法的重新安排；`divert` 在监管语境中常暗示偏离原定用途。

## 6. an under-the-counter deal

**含义：** 私下进行、未申报或不合法的交易。

- **常见搭配：** `an under-the-counter sale / payment`; `a backroom deal`
- **例句：** Small under-the-counter deals were difficult to detect because neither side wanted to report them.
- **词形：** 作前置复合形容词时保留连字符。
- **对比：** `informal transaction` 不一定违法；`under-the-counter` 明显带有隐蔽或违规色彩。

## 7. crack down on violations

**含义：** 严厉打击违规行为。

- **常见搭配：** `crack down on fraud / illegal trade / tax evasion`; `a regulatory crackdown`
- **例句：** Regulators cracked down on large-scale violations but lacked staff to investigate every complaint.
- **词族：** `crack down`（动词短语），`crackdown`（名词）
- **对比：** `enforce a rule` 是一般执法；`crack down on` 强调突然加强或更严厉的行动。

## 8. pull cash out of circulation

**含义：** 减少流通中的现金或购买力。

- **常见搭配：** `money in circulation`; `withdraw currency from circulation`; `absorb excess liquidity`
- **例句：** Policymakers may try to pull purchasing power out of circulation when excess demand is driving inflation.
- **词族：** `circulate`，`circulation`，`circulating`
- **对比：** `raise revenue` 强调政府获得收入；`pull cash out of circulation` 强调降低市场中的可支配资金。

- **含义：** 维系在一起；使不解体

## 2. escape detection

- **含义：** 逃脱探测；未被检出
- **例句：** *Weakly interacting particles would escape through conventional detectors unnoticed.*

## 3. carry away energy

- **含义：** 带走能量
- **例句：** *Invisible particles can still be inferred from the energy and momentum they carry away.*

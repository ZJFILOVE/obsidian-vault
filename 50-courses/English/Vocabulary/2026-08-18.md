# Vocabulary — Day 4

## Core collocations
- **take responsibility for**
- **indicate that...** / **suggest that...**
- **confirm whether...**
- **ensure that...**
- **be essential to**
- **acquire knowledge**
- **make a judgment**

## Trap
`indicate/suggest` = 表明/暗示，证据强度弱于 `prove`.

`make progress` is correct; `progress` is uncountable.

# Vocabulary — 2026-10-26：从工具功能写到制度后果

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-10-26|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-10-26|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-10-26|词汇]]

## 1. roll out — 推出；分阶段部署

- **核心义：** introduce a product, service or policy for use, often gradually
- **常用搭配：** roll out a new system / a national program / an update
- **例句：** The library will roll out the lending service in three branches before expanding it citywide.
- **词族／对比：** **a rollout** 是名词；**launch** 强调正式开始，**roll out** 更容易带出分阶段实施。
- **易错点：** 不要把所有“发布”都写成 publish；软件或服务上线常用 launch / roll out。

## 2. en masse — 大批地；集体地

- **核心义：** all together or in very large numbers
- **常用搭配：** arrive en masse / move en masse / adopt a tool en masse
- **例句：** Users did not adopt the platform en masse until mobile access became reliable.
- **语域：** 比 in large numbers 更凝练，常见于新闻与评论；不要在同一段反复使用。
- **对比：** **collectively** 可强调共同决定；en masse 只说明规模或同时发生，不证明协调行动。

## 3. circulate — 传播；传阅；流通

- **核心义：** move among people, places or systems
- **常用搭配：** circulate a memo / circulate among staff / currency in circulation
- **例句：** The draft was circulated among community groups before the final vote.
- **词族：** circulation（流通／发行量／血液循环）；circulatory（循环系统的）。
- **对比：** **distribute** 强调主动分发，**circulate** 更强调材料在网络中继续移动。

## 4. lower the barrier to — 降低……的门槛

- **常用搭配：** lower the barrier to entry / participation / access
- **例句：** Plain-language instructions may lower the barrier to using public data.
- **结构：** barrier 后的 to 是介词，接名词或动名词：the barrier to participating。
- **边界：** 门槛降低不等于公平已经实现；还要检查设备、时间、费用和信任等限制。
- **对比：** **remove the barrier** 表示障碍被消除，证据要求更强。

## 5. give rise to — 引起；促成

- **常用搭配：** give rise to concern / new practices / unintended effects
- **例句：** Cheap storage gave rise to new habits of keeping data that no one intended to review.
- **语法：** 主语通常是条件、变化或事件；rise 在短语中不变。
- **对比：** **cause** 更直接；give rise to 适合复杂机制，但仍不应替代因果证据。

## 6. catalyze — 促成并加速

- **核心义：** cause or accelerate a process or change
- **常用搭配：** catalyze innovation / debate / institutional change
- **例句：** A small grant catalyzed cooperation among organizations that had rarely shared data.
- **词族：** catalyst（催化剂／促进因素）；catalytic（催化的）。
- **对比：** **trigger** 常指明显起点，**catalyze** 强调加速已经具备条件的过程。
- **易错点：** 不要因为事件发生在前，就自动说它 catalyzed 后续变化。

## 7. at the behest of — 应……的要求／推动

- **核心义：** because a person or group asked for or directed something
- **常用搭配：** act at the behest of a regulator / conduct a review at the board’s behest
- **例句：** The audit was commissioned at the behest of residents who questioned the procurement process.
- **语域：** 正式，常用于制度、法律和新闻语境；日常表达可用 at the request of。
- **边界：** 它说明谁推动行动，不自动暗示不当控制。

## 8. withhold judgment — 暂不下判断

- **常用搭配：** withhold judgment until / reserve judgment on
- **例句：** The committee withheld judgment until the full safety data were available.
- **词族：** withhold（扣留／保留）；withholding（扣留行为）。
- **对比：** **reject** 是否定，**remain neutral** 是保持中立；withhold judgment 表示证据尚不足。
- **语用：** 学术讨论中，它能把“我不知道”改写成有条件的判断：还缺什么证据、何时再判断。

## 词块联结练习

用以下链条写四句话，主题自选：

**roll out a tool → lower the barrier to use → give rise to an unexpected practice → withhold judgment until evidence is available**

要求：至少一句写收益，一句写代价，一句明确证据缺口。不要把四个短语硬塞进同一句。

> [!answer]- 原创示例
> The city rolled out a multilingual reporting app in six districts. It lowered the barrier to reporting damaged streetlights, but it also gave rise to duplicate requests when users could not see existing cases. Officials are withholding judgment on the program’s overall effectiveness until response times and neighborhood coverage can be compared.

## 近义辨析：技术能够什么，技术造成什么

选择更准确的动词：

1. The update was introduced gradually across five regions. → **was rolled out**
2. The file continued to move among departments after the first email. → **circulated**
3. The new rule was one factor that accelerated an existing shift. → **catalyzed**
4. The current evidence is insufficient for a conclusion. → **withhold judgment**
5. Participation became easier, but not universally accessible. → **lowered the barrier to participation**

## 闭卷检索测验

1. 补全：roll ___ a new service；___ masse；give rise ___；at the behest ___。
2. 用英文解释 circulate 与 distribute 的差别。
3. 把过强句改弱：The app removed all barriers to public participation.
4. 选择：A review launched because the board formally requested it was conducted (at / on / by) the behest of the board.
5. 用 catalyze 写一句话，并补充一条你仍需验证的替代解释。
6. 用四个词块复述今日文章的核心机制，但不要复述具体数据。

> [!answer]- 检索答案
> 1. out；en；to；of。
> 2. Distribute emphasizes an act of sending material to recipients; circulate emphasizes its continued movement through a group or system.
> 3. The app lowered some practical barriers to public participation, although access still depended on devices, connectivity and awareness.
> 4. at。
> 5. 示例：The pilot may have catalyzed cooperation, but the teams were also responding to a new funding requirement.
> 6. 合格答案应写清：工具被推出、复制门槛降低、材料开始广泛流通、由此产生新行为；并保留评价边界。

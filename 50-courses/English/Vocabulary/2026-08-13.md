# Vocabulary — Day 1

## Core collocations
- **balance A with B** — 平衡 A 与 B
- **have an impact on** — 对……产生影响
- **sufficient evidence** — 充分的证据
- **consistent effort** — 持续稳定的努力
- **take advantage of** — 利用

## Practice
1. Many college students are under increasing pressure to ___ academic success with a healthy social life. **balance**
2. The new policy is expected to have a significant ___ on the way students use AI. **impact**
3. Scientists have not yet found sufficient ___ to support this explanation. **evidence**
4. Learning a language requires ___ effort rather than a few days of intensive study. **consistent**
5. The professor encouraged students to ___ advantage of university resources. **take**

## Trap
**evidence** is uncountable: `a piece of evidence`, not `an evidence / evidences`.

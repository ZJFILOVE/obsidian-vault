# Vocabulary — Day 14

## Core language blocks
- **make it difficult to do sth.**
- **be accustomed to doing sth.**
- **maintain concentration**
- **tend to do sth.**
- **overlook an important detail**
- **only when... + inversion** (advanced)

## Reading logic
- `may / can / tend to / suggest` are weaker than `cause / prove / always`.
- Familiarity does not necessarily mean mastery.

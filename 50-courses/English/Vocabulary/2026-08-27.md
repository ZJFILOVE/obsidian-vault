# Vocabulary — Day 11

## Core language blocks
- **be distracted by** / **distract sb. from**
- **affect sth.** / **have an effect on sth.**
- **attribute A to B**
- **contribute to an outcome**
- **the choices available**

## Causal strength
`be associated with` < `contribute to` < `cause`

Do not strengthen a weak association into a causal claim in reading questions.

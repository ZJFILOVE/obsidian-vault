# Vocabulary Practice — 2026-09-15

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-09-15|Today's input]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-09-15|Today's translation]]

Today's set focuses on **measurement, matching, stability, and technical explanation**. Several items come naturally from the NIST article, but the aim is to learn language that transfers to experiments, engineering, statistics, and ordinary discussion.

## 1. oscillate / oscillation

**Meaning:** 往复变化；振荡；在两个状态或数值之间反复移动

### Collocations
- oscillate at a frequency of...
- periodic oscillation
- electrical / mechanical oscillation
- oscillate between two values / positions

### Word family
- **oscillate** (v.)
- **oscillation** (n.)
- **oscillator** (n.) — 产生周期性信号或运动的装置

### Example
> A clock needs some process that oscillates in a sufficiently regular way to provide repeatable ticks.

### Contrast
**oscillate** emphasizes repeated movement or variation around a pattern. **fluctuate** is broader and often describes irregular changes in prices, temperature, demand, or measurements.

> The signal **oscillates** at a fixed frequency.  
> Energy prices **fluctuate** from month to month.

## 2. drift

**Meaning:** 逐渐偏移；漂移；在没有明显突变的情况下慢慢离开原来的值或位置

### Collocations
- drift over time
- measurement drift
- clock drift
- drift away from a target

### Example
> Even a small amount of clock drift can become significant when many systems must remain synchronized.

### Usage note
In technical English, **drift** is useful because it describes a **gradual systematic change**, not just random noise.

## 3. be in sync with / synchronize

**Meaning:** 与……同步；使多个过程按照协调的时间或状态运行

### Collocations
- be in sync with a reference clock
- keep devices in sync
- synchronize data / clocks / operations
- synchronized network

### Word family
- **synchronize** (v.)
- **synchronization** (n.)
- **synchronous** (adj.)

### Example
> Distributed computer systems need reliable methods to keep their clocks in sync.

### Contrast
- **simultaneous** = 恰好同时发生
- **synchronized** = 经过协调，使时间或过程保持一致

Two events can be simultaneous by coincidence; systems are usually synchronized deliberately.

## 4. tune A to B / be tuned to

**Meaning:** 调节 A，使其匹配 B；把设备或参数调到某个目标状态

### Collocations
- tune a laser to a frequency
- tune a receiver to a signal
- be finely tuned to...
- tune a model / parameter

### Example
> The instrument is tuned to a narrow frequency range before the measurement begins.

### Transfer value
This is not limited to physics. In computing, you can **tune a model**, **tune a database**, or **tune system parameters** for a particular workload.

## 5. resonance / resonant

**Meaning:** 共振；当外部驱动频率与系统的特征频率匹配时出现的强响应。比喻义可表示“引起强烈共鸣”。

### Collocations
- resonant frequency
- reach resonance
- a resonant system
- resonate with an audience / idea

### Word family
- **resonate** (v.)
- **resonance** (n.)
- **resonant** (adj.)

### Reliable memory note
The word ultimately comes from Latin **resonare**, “to sound again,” built from **re-** (“again/back”) + **sonare** (“to sound”). That history fits both the physical sense of a strong response and the later metaphorical sense of an idea “echoing” with someone.

### Example
> A system responds especially strongly when the driving frequency approaches its resonant frequency.

## 6. intrinsic

**Meaning:** 内在的；本身固有的；不是主要由外部条件赋予的

### Collocations
- intrinsic property
- intrinsic value
- intrinsic limitation
- intrinsic motivation

### Example
> Researchers try to separate an intrinsic property of the system from effects introduced by the measuring instrument.

### Contrast
- **intrinsic** — comes from the thing itself
- **extrinsic** — comes from outside conditions, rewards, or influences

This pair is useful in science, psychology, economics, and philosophy.

## 7. accuracy vs. precision

These two words are often blurred in everyday speech, but technical writing benefits from keeping them separate.

### accuracy
**Meaning:** 测量结果离真实值或参考值有多接近

**Collocations:** measurement accuracy, improve accuracy, highly accurate estimate

### precision
**Meaning:** 重复测量彼此有多接近，或测量分辨得有多细

**Collocations:** high precision, precision measurement, improve measurement precision

### Example
> A set of measurements can be highly precise but still inaccurate if they are all shifted by the same systematic error.

### Word family
- accurate / accurately / accuracy
- precise / precisely / precision

## 8. calibrate / calibration

**Meaning:** 校准；把仪器的输出与已知参考标准进行比较并调整或表征误差

### Collocations
- calibrate an instrument
- calibration standard
- calibration procedure
- regularly calibrated equipment

### Example
> The sensor must be calibrated against a reliable reference before quantitative measurements are trusted.

### Common error
**calibrate** is not simply “check.” Calibration specifically involves comparison with a reference and, depending on context, adjustment or characterization.

## 9. convert A into B

**Meaning:** 把 A 转换为 B；强调形式、单位、用途或状态发生变化

### Collocations
- convert energy into motion
- convert data into information
- convert a signal into a digital value
- convert counts into units of time

### Example
> Software converts the detector's raw signal into a value that users can interpret.

### Contrast
- **convert A into B** — A changes form/state/function
- **translate A into B** — often language, or turning an idea/result into another usable form
- **transform A into B** — stronger, often broader or more substantial change

## Useful mini-patterns for technical explanation

### Method → inference
> **By measuring** X, researchers can **estimate/detect/infer** Y.

### Condition → response
> **When** A approaches B, the system **responds by** C.

### Limitation → cautious claim
> The method is highly precise, **but its accuracy still depends on** calibration and environmental control.

These patterns are worth learning as complete chunks because they help you explain experiments without overclaiming.

# Vocabulary — 2026-10-09

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-10-09|Daily]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-10-09|Translation]]

Learn each chunk as a meaning–collocation–example unit. Some are inspired by the map reading; others are deliberately transferable to university life and writing.

## 1. depict / representation
**Meaning:** show or describe something in an image, diagram, or words. **Collocations:** depict a region; accurately depict; visual representation. **Family:** depiction (n.), representative (adj./n.). **Example:** The diagram depicts the main stages of the process but omits minor variations. **Contrast:** *depict* emphasizes portrayal; *prove* implies evidence.

## 2. distortion / distort
**Meaning:** a change that makes an image, number, or account differ from the original. **Collocations:** spatial distortion; distort proportions; a distorted picture. **Family:** distorted (adj.). **Example:** A map projection may distort area even when it preserves direction. **Contrast:** distortion need not imply deliberate deception.

## 3. at the expense of
**Meaning:** gaining one advantage while sacrificing another. **Collocations:** speed at the expense of accuracy; growth at the expense of quality. **Example:** The new system increased speed at the expense of flexibility. **Note:** The phrase marks a trade-off, not necessarily a financial cost.

## 4. be suited to / suitable for
**Meaning:** fit a particular purpose or context. **Collocations:** well suited to; suited to a task; suitable for beginners. **Family:** suitability (n.). **Example:** This visualization is suited to comparing trends rather than exact values. **Contrast:** *suited to* stresses fit; *capable of* stresses ability.

## 5. approximate / approximation
**Meaning:** come close to a value or representation without matching it exactly. **Collocations:** approximate a curve; rough approximation; approximate value. **Family:** approximately (adv.). **Example:** The model approximates real behavior under a limited set of conditions. **Contrast:** *approximate* is not the same as *fabricate*.

## 6. account for
**Meaning:** explain a result or constitute a proportion. **Collocations:** account for variation; account for differences; account for 30 percent. **Example:** Differences in sampling may account for part of the observed trend. **Contrast:** *account for* can describe explanation or arithmetic share; infer meaning from context.

## 7. be contingent on
**Meaning:** depend on a condition. **Collocations:** contingent on approval; contingent on circumstances; contingent upon availability. **Family:** contingency (n.). **Example:** Whether the policy works is contingent on consistent implementation. **Register:** formal; everyday alternative: *depend on*.

## 8. trade-off
**Meaning:** a balance between competing advantages and disadvantages. **Collocations:** make a trade-off; unavoidable trade-off; trade-off between A and B. **Example:** Designers face a trade-off between simplicity and completeness. **Contrast:** A trade-off is not always a zero-sum exchange, but it involves competing priorities.

## 9. qualify a claim
**Meaning:** make a statement more precise by specifying limits or conditions. **Collocations:** qualify a conclusion; carefully qualified statement; qualification. **Example:** The authors qualified their claim by noting that the sample was small. **Family:** qualification (n.), qualified (adj.). **Contrast:** Here *qualify* means limit or refine, not earn a credential.

> [!answer]- Retrieval answers
> 1. at; of.
> 2. depict.
> 3. The result depends on the sample size / is contingent on the sample size.
> 4. An approximation is an intentionally inexact representation; distortion is a change in shape, proportion, or meaning that can arise from representation.
> 5. This design is better suited to comparing trends than exact values.
> 6. on.
> 7. Researchers must qualify this conclusion.
> 8. Explain something; make up a specified proportion.
> 9. Example: There is a trade-off between rapid delivery and thorough testing.

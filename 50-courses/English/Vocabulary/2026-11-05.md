# Vocabulary — 2026-11-05：用材料证据支持谨慎推断

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-11-05|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-11-05|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-11-05|词汇]]

以下 8 个词块由今日大都会艺术博物馆文章的真实语境引出；释义、搭配、例句、对比和测验均为原创。重点是把对象、方法、结果与结论分开。

## 1. identify the material — 鉴定／识别材料

- **核心义：** determine what substance or material something is made from
- **常用搭配：** identify a pigment / binder / fibre / unknown compound
- **原创例句：** The colour of the residue was not enough to identify the material, so the team collected a spectrum.
- **词族：** identify；identification；identity；identifiable。
- **对比：** identify 强调确定身份；detect 只表示发现某物存在。
- **边界：** A signal may detect a component without uniquely identifying it.

## 2. be dedicated to doing something — 致力于做某事

- **核心义：** devote sustained effort to a purpose
- **常用搭配：** be dedicated to preserving / studying / improving / documenting
- **原创例句：** The archive is dedicated to preserving records that would otherwise be difficult to access.
- **词族：** dedicate；dedicated；dedication。
- **对比：** be intended to do 描述设计目的；be dedicated to doing 强调人或机构的长期投入。
- **语法：** to 是介词，后接名词或动名词，不写 dedicated to preserve。

## 3. lengthen the life of — 延长……的使用寿命或保存期

- **核心义：** make an object, system or material remain usable for longer
- **常用搭配：** lengthen the life of an artwork / device / battery / component
- **原创例句：** Lower storage temperatures can lengthen the life of the reagent, provided that freezing is avoided.
- **词族：** long；length；lengthen；longevity。
- **对比：** extend a deadline 是延长期限；lengthen the life of 更强调对象持续可用。
- **精度提醒：** 对人通常说 extend life expectancy / prolong life，避免机械套用。

## 4. serve as a support for — 作为……的支撑体

- **核心义：** function as the physical base on which something is placed or built
- **常用搭配：** serve as a support for paint / a platform for discussion / a reference
- **原创例句：** A thin glass slide served as a support for the sample during imaging.
- **词族：** serve；service；support；supportive。
- **对比：** support 作可数技术名词时可指支撑体；support 作不可数名词时也可指帮助或证据。
- **搭配提醒：** serve as 已含“充当”，不写 serve as to be。

## 5. become warped — 逐渐翘曲、变形

- **核心义：** change from a flat or intended shape, often because of heat or moisture
- **常用搭配：** wood / a panel / a frame becomes warped；warped by humidity
- **原创例句：** The lid became warped after repeated heating and no longer formed a tight seal.
- **词族：** warp；warped；warping。
- **对比：** bend 可指主动或暂时弯曲；warp 常暗示不均匀的永久形变。
- **语法：** become + 形容词描述状态变化；也可写 the panel warped。

## 6. reveal an underdrawing — 显示绘画表层下的底稿

- **核心义：** make a preliminary drawing beneath paint visible
- **常用搭配：** reveal an earlier design / hidden layer / structural feature
- **原创例句：** The scan revealed an earlier outline beneath the final layer, but it did not show why the change was made.
- **词族：** reveal；revealing；revelation。
- **对比：** reveal 表示使原本不可见的信息可见；prove 表示证据足以确立命题。两者不能互换。
- **术语：** underdrawing 是绘画技术词；在其他场景用 preliminary sketch / underlying structure。

## 7. be consistent with — 与……相符／一致

- **核心义：** agree with an explanation without necessarily proving it
- **常用搭配：** be consistent with the data / a proposed mechanism / earlier observations
- **原创例句：** The delayed response is consistent with a transport limitation, although an instrument lag has not been ruled out.
- **词族：** consist；consistent；consistency；consistently。
- **对比：** consistent with 比 prove 弱；它允许多个解释同时与数据相符。
- **搭配提醒：** consistent with，不写 consistent to。

## 8. rule out an alternative explanation — 排除另一种解释

- **核心义：** show that a competing explanation is unlikely or impossible
- **常用搭配：** rule out contamination / bias / measurement error / an alternative cause
- **原创例句：** A control sample is needed to rule out contamination during preparation.
- **词族：** alternative；alternatively；explain；explanation。
- **对比：** fail to find evidence for X 不等于 rule out X；“没发现”可能只是检测能力不足。
- **语体：** rule out 常见于科学和医学写作；正式替换可用 exclude。

## 词块关系图

| 阅读动作 | 推荐词块 | 不要过度声称 |
|---|---|---|
| 确认对象是什么 | identify the material | 仅凭颜色就确定成分 |
| 描述物理角色 | serve as a support for | 把 support 一律译成“支持观点” |
| 描述时间变化 | become warped / lengthen the life of | 把共现写成因果 |
| 报告新可见信息 | reveal an underdrawing | 把 reveal 自动升级为 prove |
| 表达谨慎解释 | be consistent with | 暗示只有一个可能机制 |
| 加强因果判断 | rule out an alternative explanation | 把“没观察到”当成“已排除” |

## 检索测验

先遮住上文再作答。

1. 填空：The spectrum helped the team ______ the unknown coating.
2. 改错：The laboratory is dedicated to preserve fragile samples.
3. 翻译：降低湿度可能延长纸张的保存期，但也必须避免材料过度干燥。
4. 选词：The image **reveals / proves** a hidden line, but it does not explain why the artist drew it.
5. 填空：The wooden frame ______ ______ after several wet–dry cycles.
6. 改写，降低强度：The pattern proves that the sample was contaminated.
7. 翻译：仅有这一项测量还不能排除另一种解释。
8. 区分 identify 与 detect：写两句，第一句只说发现信号，第二句说完成身份确认。

> [!answer]- 参考答案
> 1. identify
> 2. The laboratory is dedicated to preserving fragile samples.
> 3. Lower humidity may lengthen the life of paper, but excessive drying must also be avoided.
> 4. reveals
> 5. became warped
> 6. The pattern is consistent with contamination, but it does not prove it.
> 7. This measurement alone cannot rule out an alternative explanation.
> 8. The detector detected a weak signal near the surface. Additional analysis identified the compound as a common plasticiser.

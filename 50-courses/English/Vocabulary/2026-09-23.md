# Vocabulary Practice — 2026-09-23

Today's set partly connects to the NASA reading, but the goal is broader: reusable language for **possible explanations, verification, competing hypotheses, and evidence strength**. Focus on collocations and contrasts rather than memorizing isolated Chinese meanings.

## 1. candidate

**Meaning:** 候选者；候选对象。除了“候选人”，在科学和技术语境中也常表示“一个值得进一步检验、但尚未最终确认的可能对象或解释”。

### Collocations
- a candidate planet
- a candidate explanation
- a candidate gene
- a promising candidate
- identify a candidate
- screen candidates

### Example
> The researchers identified several candidate explanations before deciding which ones deserved further testing.

### Contrast
- **candidate** = 仍处于待筛选、待验证阶段
- **confirmed result** = 已经过足够验证、证据更强

A candidate is not a weak word. It simply marks a **stage in the evidence process**.

## 2. detect

**Meaning:** 检测到；发现某种通常不容易直接观察的信号、物质、变化或异常。

### Collocations
- detect a signal
- detect a change
- detect a trace amount
- detect an anomaly
- early detection
- detection limit

### Example
> The instrument can detect very small changes that would be difficult to observe directly.

### Word family
- **detection** (n.)
- **detectable** (adj.)
- **undetectable** (adj.)

### Contrast
- **notice** = 日常“注意到”
- **detect** = 更常见于仪器、系统、医学、科学或正式分析语境

## 3. false positive

**Meaning:** 假阳性；系统或测试给出“有”的结果，但实际目标并不存在。这个词最常见于医学检测、机器学习、安防系统和科学筛选。

### Collocations
- produce a false positive
- reduce false positives
- false-positive rate
- distinguish true signals from false positives

### Example
> A screening method can be useful even if it produces some false positives, provided that later tests can filter them out.

### Contrast
- **false positive** = 报告“存在”，实际不存在
- **false negative** = 报告“不存在”，实际存在

Do not confuse a false positive with a **mistake in interpretation**. A false positive often refers specifically to the output of a test or detection system.

## 4. rule out

**Meaning:** 排除某种可能性；通过证据表明某个解释、诊断或原因不太可能成立。

### Collocations
- rule out an explanation
- rule out a diagnosis
- rule out contamination
- rule out other causes
- cannot yet be ruled out

### Example
> Before drawing a causal conclusion, researchers should rule out obvious alternative explanations where possible.

### Contrast
- **ignore** = 不考虑，可能没有依据
- **rule out** = 经过检查或证据后排除

### Grammar note
Common passive form:
> Contamination **cannot be ruled out** completely.

## 5. corroborate

**Meaning:** 用独立证据证实、印证已有说法或发现。常用于研究、调查、历史证据、证词和数据分析。

### Collocations
- corroborate a finding
- corroborate an account
- corroborating evidence
- independently corroborate
- evidence corroborates the claim

### Example
> Measurements from a second instrument helped corroborate the original finding.

### Contrast
- **confirm** can be broader and may imply a stronger final judgment.
- **corroborate** emphasizes that **additional evidence supports something already suggested by other evidence**.

This makes it especially useful in academic writing.

## 6. artifact

**Meaning:** 今天重点掌握科学和数据语境中的用法：由测量方法、仪器、处理流程或实验条件产生的“伪影 / 假象”，并不代表真正的目标现象。

### Collocations
- measurement artifact
- imaging artifact
- data-processing artifact
- an artifact of the method
- eliminate artifacts

### Example
> The unexpected peak might represent a real effect, but it could also be an artifact of the measurement procedure.

### Contrast
- **error** = 一般性的错误
- **artifact** = 特别强调某种看似真实的模式是由方法、设备或处理过程产生

### Other meaning
**Artifact** 也可以表示“人工制品、文物”。语境决定意思。

## 7. tentative

**Meaning:** 暂定的；初步的；由于证据仍不充分而保留修改可能性的。

### Collocations
- tentative conclusion
- tentative explanation
- tentative identification
- tentative agreement
- remain tentative

### Example
> The conclusion should remain tentative until the result has been tested with an independent sample.

### Contrast
- **uncertain** = 不确定，范围很广
- **tentative** = 已经有一个初步结论或方案，但明确保留修正空间

### Word family
- **tentatively** (adv.)

## 8. robust

**Meaning:** 在学术和技术语境中常表示“稳健的；在不同条件、假设或数据扰动下仍然成立的”。

### Collocations
- robust evidence
- robust result
- robust method
- robust to changes in...
- statistically robust

### Example
> A result is more convincing if it remains robust when researchers change reasonable assumptions in the analysis.

### Contrast
- **strong** = 很广泛，可以表示程度强
- **robust** = 更强调结果或方法在不同条件下不容易崩溃

A single dramatic result is not automatically a robust result.

## 9. converge on

**Meaning:** 多条独立信息、方法或讨论逐渐指向同一个结论。

### Collocations
- multiple lines of evidence converge on...
- independent methods converge on the same estimate
- researchers converge on an explanation
- gradually converge on a solution

### Example
> Confidence increases when several independent methods converge on the same conclusion.

### Contrast
- **agree with** = 普通的“同意 / 一致”
- **converge on** = 强调从不同起点逐渐汇聚到同一个结果或解释

### Word family
- **converge** (v.)
- **convergence** (n.)
- **convergent** (adj.)

## Reusable mini-patterns

### From possibility to testing
> Researchers identified a promising **candidate** and then designed a test to **rule out** competing explanations.

### Signal quality
> A detected pattern may be a genuine signal, a **false positive**, or an experimental **artifact**.

### Independent support
> Evidence from a second method can **corroborate** the original observation.

### Appropriate caution
> The interpretation should remain **tentative** until more evidence is available.

### Stronger inference
> A result becomes more convincing when it is **robust** and multiple lines of evidence **converge on** the same explanation.

## Contrast drill

Choose the more precise option and explain why.

1. The sensor **detected / noticed** a trace amount of the compound.
2. The unexpected image feature may be an **artifact / opinion** caused by the reconstruction algorithm.
3. Researchers used a second assay to **corroborate / repeat verbally** the first result.
4. The finding is still **tentative / permanent** because the sample is small.
5. The team tried to **rule out / forget** contamination as an alternative explanation.
6. The model was considered **robust / loud** because its conclusion changed little across several reasonable parameter settings.
7. Three independent estimates **converged on / collided with** a similar value.
8. A screening test can produce a **false positive / false agreement** even when the target condition is absent.

> [!answer]- Answers
> 1. **detected** — instrument-based scientific context
> 2. **artifact** — the feature may have been created by the method rather than by the underlying object
> 3. **corroborate** — independent evidence supports an earlier result
> 4. **tentative** — a provisional conclusion that may change with more evidence
> 5. **rule out** — evidence is used to exclude an explanation
> 6. **robust** — the result remains stable under reasonable changes
> 7. **converged on** — separate approaches point toward the same result
> 8. **false positive** — the test indicates presence when the target is actually absent

## Retrieval quiz

Try without looking back.

1. Which noun can describe an object or explanation that is promising but not yet confirmed?
2. Complete: The instrument can ______ very small changes in fluorescence intensity.
3. What is the difference between a **false positive** and a **false negative**?
4. Complete: Researchers tried to ______ out contamination before interpreting the signal.
5. Which verb means “support an existing finding with additional evidence”?
6. In experimental science, what does **artifact** mean?
7. Complete: The conclusion should remain ______ until the sample has been independently tested.
8. What makes a result **robust** rather than merely dramatic?
9. Complete: Several independent measurements ______ on approximately the same value.
10. Why is **corroborate** often more precise than **prove** in academic writing?

> [!answer]- Answers
> 1. candidate
> 2. detect
> 3. A **false positive** reports a target that is not actually present; a **false negative** fails to report a target that is present.
> 4. rule
> 5. corroborate
> 6. A pattern or feature created by the measurement, processing, or experimental method rather than by the phenomenon being studied.
> 7. tentative
> 8. It remains broadly stable under reasonable changes in assumptions, methods, samples, or analysis choices.
> 9. converged
> 10. **Corroborate** says that additional evidence supports a claim without implying that all uncertainty has been eliminated.

## 1. articulate / articulation
**Meaning:** 清楚表达；发出（语音）；发音动作  
**Collocations:** articulate a sound, articulate an idea, place/manner of articulation  
**Word family:** articulate, articulation, articulatory  
**Example:** Different consonants require different places and manners of articulation.  
**Etymology:** from Latin *articulus*, “joint” or “small division”; the idea of dividing something into clear parts later developed into the senses of distinct speech and clear expression.

## 2. phoneme
**Meaning:** 音位；在某种语言中能够区别词义的最小语音单位之一  
**Collocations:** phoneme inventory, phonemic contrast, distinguish two phonemes  
**Example:** Two sounds may be physically different without functioning as separate phonemes in a language.

## 3. distinguish A from B
**Meaning:** 区分 A 与 B  
**Collocations:** distinguish one sound from another; distinguish correlation from causation  
**Example:** Learners may initially find it difficult to distinguish two unfamiliar consonants from each other.

## 4. constraint
**Meaning:** 限制；约束条件  
**Collocations:** physical constraint, articulatory constraint, impose a constraint on  
**Word family:** constrain, constrained  
**Example:** The structure of the vocal tract places physical constraints on the sounds humans can produce.

## 5. inventory
**Meaning:** 清单；（语言学）某语言所具有的一组音位/语音单位  
**Collocations:** consonant inventory, vowel inventory, phoneme inventory  
**Example:** Languages can have very different consonant inventories even when their speakers share the same basic anatomy.

## 6. perceive / perception
**Meaning:** 感知；察觉  
**Collocations:** perceive a difference, speech perception, auditory perception  
**Word family:** perceive, perception, perceptual  
**Example:** Native-language categories can influence how listeners perceive unfamiliar speech sounds.  
**Etymology:** from Latin *percipere*, roughly “to take in” or “grasp completely,” which is a useful image for information being taken in by the senses and interpreted.

## 7. plausible
**Meaning:** 看起来合理的；有可信度的  
**Collocations:** plausible explanation, plausible hypothesis, seem plausible  
**Example:** A plausible explanation is not necessarily a proven one.

## 8. reproduce
**Meaning:** 重现；复现；模仿产生  
**Collocations:** reproduce a sound, reproduce an experiment, reproduce a result  
**Word family:** reproduction, reproducible  
**Example:** Being able to hear a sound does not guarantee that a learner can reproduce it accurately.

## 9. theoretically
**Meaning:** 理论上；按理论而言  
**Collocations:** theoretically possible, theoretically predicted  
**Word family:** theory, theoretical, theoretically  
**Example:** A theoretically possible sound may still be absent from the languages studied so far.

## Useful contrasts

- **sound** — 最宽泛的“声音/语音”
- **phone** — 实际发出的语音单位（较技术）
- **phoneme** — 在特定语言中承担区别意义功能的抽象单位
- **pronunciation** — 一个词/表达实际怎样发音
- **articulation** — 发音器官如何形成声音

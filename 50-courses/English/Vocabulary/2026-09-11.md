# Vocabulary Practice — 2026-09-11

## 1. extreme weather
**Meaning:** 极端天气  
**Collocations:** extreme-weather event, extreme weather conditions  
**Example:** Scientists are studying whether climate change is increasing the frequency of extreme-weather events.

## 2. likelihood
**Meaning:** 可能性，概率  
**Collocations:** increase/reduce the likelihood of; the likelihood that...  
**Word family:** likely, unlikely  
**Memory note:** *likely* is the adjective; *likelihood* names the degree to which something is likely.

## 3. attribute A to B
**Meaning:** 把 A 归因于 B  
**Example:** It is too early to attribute every flood to climate change.  
**Transfer warning:** 不要写成 *attribute B for A*。

## 4. intensify
**Meaning:** 加剧，使增强  
**Collocations:** intensify a storm, intensify pressure, intensify the impact  
**Word family:** intense, intensity, intensification  
**Etymology:** from Latin *intensus*, “stretched tight”; the modern sense developed toward “made stronger.”

## 5. vulnerability
**Meaning:** 脆弱性，易受影响的程度  
**Collocations:** infrastructure vulnerability, social vulnerability, vulnerability to flooding  
**Word family:** vulnerable, vulnerably  
**Etymology:** from Latin *vulnus*, “wound”; literally related to being open to injury.

## 6. exposure
**Meaning:** 暴露程度；接触  
**Collocations:** exposure to risk, degree of exposure, prolonged exposure  
**Word family:** expose, exposed  
**Example:** Coastal cities face greater exposure to storm surges.

## 7. adaptive capacity
**Meaning:** 适应能力，尤其指系统应对变化的能力  
**Collocations:** build/enhance adaptive capacity; limited adaptive capacity  
**Example:** Wealthier regions often have greater adaptive capacity because they can invest in resilient infrastructure.

## 8. early-warning system
**Meaning:** 预警系统  
**Collocations:** strengthen/improve an early-warning system; issue an early warning  
**Example:** An effective early-warning system can reduce casualties even when a storm cannot be prevented.

## 9. resilient / resilience
**Meaning:** 有韧性的；恢复力  
**Collocations:** resilient infrastructure, community resilience, build resilience  
**Word family:** resilience, resiliently  
**Etymology:** from Latin *resilire*, “to spring back”; useful image: a system that can recover after being pushed.

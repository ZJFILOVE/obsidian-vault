# English Vocabulary — 2026-11-09：从观测结果走到谨慎结论的 8 个词块

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-11-09|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-11-09|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-11-09|词汇]]

今天学习八个能迁移到实验、新闻和政策评估中的表达。目标不是把植物声学术语背下来，而是控制“观测—比较—机制—功能”之间的结论强度。

## 1. emit a sound / signal

**含义：** 发出声音、光、热或其他可测信号。

- **常见搭配：** emit ultrasonic sounds; emit light / radiation / a signal; be emitted by
- **例句：** The sensor emits a short signal every ten seconds, but the signal carries no identifying information.
- **词族：** emit → emission；名词重音和拼写都要注意双 s：**emission**。
- **辨析：** emit 只说明“产生并释放”；它不自动包含 send intentionally。设备可有意发射信号，物理系统也可无意辐射能量。

## 2. in response to

**含义：** 作为对某刺激、事件或变化的反应。

- **常见搭配：** change in response to demand; respond to stress / feedback; a response to treatment
- **例句：** The library extended its opening hours in response to repeated student requests.
- **词族：** respond (v.) → response (n.) → responsive (adj.)；**responsible** 是“负责的”，不要混淆。
- **辨析：** after 只表达先后；in response to 暗示反应关系，但仍不一定证明唯一因果机制。

## 3. distinguish between A and B

**含义：** 区分 A 与 B；识别两类事物之间的差别。

- **常见搭配：** distinguish between conditions; distinguish A from B; a distinguishing feature
- **例句：** The model could distinguish between the two recording conditions, although its performance in a noisy street was not tested.
- **词族：** distinct → distinction；distinguish → distinguishable / indistinguishable。
- **辨析：** **distinguish between A and B** 与 **distinguish A from B** 都正确；不要写 *distinguish A and B*。

## 4. be detectable up to ...

**含义：** 在最远某距离、最长某时间或最低某浓度下仍可被检测到。

- **常见搭配：** detectable up to five metres away; remain detectable for hours; below the detection limit
- **例句：** The marker remained detectable for two days, but its concentration steadily declined.
- **词族：** detect → detection → detectable；对比 **noticeable**（人能明显察觉）与 **measurable**（可测量）。
- **辨析：** detectable 描述仪器或方法能发现，不代表普通感官可察觉，也不等于影响具有实际重要性。

## 5. be comparable to

**含义：** 在指定维度上可以相比，或大致相当。

- **常见搭配：** comparable in size / cost / volume; broadly comparable to; a comparable group
- **例句：** The two districts were comparable in population, but they differed in access to public transport.
- **词族：** compare → comparison → comparable；**comparative** 常指“比较性的”或“相对的”。
- **辨析：** comparable 不等于 identical。写作时最好补出比较维度：comparable **in volume**。

## 6. peak after / peak at

**含义：** 在某段时间后或某个数值处达到峰值。

- **常见搭配：** peak after five days; peak at noon / at 40 percent; reach a peak
- **例句：** Demand peaked at 8 p.m. and then declined gradually.
- **词族：** peak 可作名词、动词和形容词：peak demand / demand peaked / a peak in demand。
- **辨析：** **peak after five days** 强调经过多久；**peak on the fifth day** 指第五天；**peak at 50** 指数值。

## 7. there is no evidence suggesting that ...

**含义：** 目前没有证据支持某个较强主张。

- **常见搭配：** no direct evidence; limited evidence; evidence suggesting / indicating that
- **例句：** There is no evidence suggesting that the lower response rate was caused by the new form.
- **结构：** evidence 通常不可数；**evidence that ...** 比 *evidence to prove ...* 更自然。
- **辨析：** no evidence 不等于 evidence of absence。它可能表示未找到、未测量或研究设计不能回答。

## 8. remain unclear

**含义：** 经过现有研究后仍不清楚、尚未确定。

- **常见搭配：** the mechanism remains unclear; it remains unclear whether / how / why; remain uncertain
- **例句：** It remains unclear whether the effect would persist once the reminders were removed.
- **词族：** clear → clarify → clarification；unclear 比 unknown 稍弱，常表示已有线索但尚未解决。
- **辨析：** unclear 可以是信息表达不清，也可以是科学问题未解决；上下文要说明是哪一种。

## 组合使用｜把标题降到证据允许的强度

过强：

> Plants intentionally warn one another when they are thirsty.

更准确：

> Some stressed plants emitted ultrasonic sounds that differed across experimental conditions. There is no evidence suggesting that the sounds were produced intentionally, and their ecological function remains unclear.

这里同时使用了 **emit、differ、no evidence、remain unclear**，把观测和解释分开。

## 检索测验｜先遮住上文

### A. 中译英

1. 传感器在温度升高时会发出短脉冲。
2. 该模型能够区分真实交易与模拟记录。
3. 这种标记在处理结束两小时后仍可检测到。
4. 两组在年龄上大致可比，但收入差异明显。
5. 投诉数量在第三周达到峰值，随后下降。
6. 目前没有证据表明提醒功能提高了长期参与度。
7. 这种变化的精确机制仍不清楚。
8. 学校根据使用者反馈调整了表格。

### B. 选词填空

从 **emit / response / distinguish / detectable / comparable / peaked / evidence / unclear** 中选择并变形。

1. The signal remained ______ for several minutes after the device was turned off.
2. The two samples are not directly ______ because they were collected in different seasons.
3. The number of requests ______ at 240 on Monday morning.
4. The classifier failed to ______ between background noise and the target sound.
5. The office changed the rule in ______ to repeated complaints.
6. The exact source of the error remains ______.
7. The material can ______ light at several wavelengths.
8. There is not enough ______ to support a causal claim.

### C. 纠错

1. The two groups are comparable with size.
2. The device emitted a signal in respond to movement.
3. There are no evidences to prove the plants communicate.
4. The value peaked in 50 percent.

> [!answer]- 参考答案
> **A**
> 1. The sensor emits a short pulse in response to a rise in temperature.
> 2. The model could distinguish between real transactions and simulated records.
> 3. The marker remained detectable two hours after the treatment ended.
> 4. The two groups were broadly comparable in age, but they differed substantially in income.
> 5. The number of complaints peaked in the third week and then declined.
> 6. There is currently no evidence suggesting that the reminder feature improves long-term participation.
> 7. The precise mechanism behind the change remains unclear.
> 8. The university revised the form in response to user feedback.
>
> **B** 1. detectable；2. comparable；3. peaked；4. distinguish；5. response；6. unclear；7. emit；8. evidence。
>
> **C**
> 1. The two groups are comparable **in** size. / The group is comparable **to** the other group in size.
> 2. The device emitted a signal in **response** to movement.
> 3. There is no **evidence suggesting that** the plants communicate intentionally.
> 4. The value peaked **at** 50 percent.

# Vocabulary Practice — 2026-09-04

## 1. procrastinate /prəˈkræstəneɪt/
**v. 拖延，推迟去做**

### Word origin
来自拉丁语 *procrastinare*：**pro-**（向前）+ **crastinus**（属于明天的，来自 *cras* = tomorrow）。

可以直接把它记成：**把事情“往明天推” = procrastinate**。

### Collocations
- procrastinate on a task
- stop procrastinating
- chronic procrastination

### Example
> I kept procrastinating on the report until the deadline was only a few hours away.

---

## 2. immediate /ɪˈmiːdiət/
**adj. 立即的；眼前的；直接的**

### Word origin
来自拉丁语 *immediatus*：**in-**（not）+ **mediatus**（in the middle）。原始画面是“中间没有东西隔着”，所以产生 **direct / without delay** 的意思。

### Collocations
- immediate effect
- immediate response
- immediate discomfort

### Memory link
**mediate** = 居中、调解；**immediate** = 中间没有东西 → 直接的、立即的。

---

## 3. regulate /ˈreɡjəleɪt/
**v. 调节；控制；规范**

### Word origin
来自拉丁语 *regula* = **rule / straight guide**。同一词根可以联想到 **regular, regulation**。

### Collocations
- regulate emotions
- regulate body temperature
- government regulation

### Example
> Learning to regulate negative emotions can make difficult tasks easier to begin.

---

## 4. postpone /poʊstˈpoʊn/
**v. 推迟，延期**

### Word origin
拉丁语 **post-**（after）+ **ponere**（put/place）。字面就是 **put after → 往后放**。

同一个 *ponere* 词根还可以联想到：
- position：放置的位置
- postpone：往后放

### Difference
- **postpone**：某件事被正式或具体地推迟到以后
- **procrastinate**：因为不想开始而不断拖延，通常带有行为习惯色彩

---

## 5. trigger
**v. 引发，触发  n. 扳机；诱因**

### Memory link
最初指枪械的 **trigger（扳机）**。扣一下扳机就引起后续反应，因此发展出：

> trigger a reaction / trigger anxiety / trigger a response

### Example
> A difficult task may trigger an immediate stress response.

---

## 6. rational /ˈræʃənəl/
**adj. 理性的；基于推理的**

来自拉丁语 *ratio*，与 **reason / calculation** 有关。

### Word family
- rational adj.
- irrational adj.
- rationale n. 基本原理、理由
- rationalize v. 合理化

### Example
> We may know rationally that delaying the task will make things worse.

---

## 7. temptation /tempˈteɪʃən/
**n. 诱惑；诱因**

### Collocations
- resist temptation
- give in to temptation
- the temptation to check your phone

### Example
> Turning off notifications reduces the temptation to check your phone.

---

## 8. prioritize /praɪˈɔːrətaɪz/
**v. 优先处理；确定优先级**

来自 **priority + -ize**。

### Structure
- prioritize A over B
- prioritize important tasks

### Example
> People sometimes prioritize immediate comfort over long-term goals.

## Quick review
填空：

1. People often ______ difficult tasks until the last minute.
2. A notification can ______ the urge to check your phone.
3. We sometimes ______ immediate comfort over long-term benefits.
4. Learning to ______ emotions is an important self-management skill.
5. The meeting was ______ until Friday.

> [!answer]- Answers
> 1. procrastinate on
> 2. trigger
> 3. prioritize
> 4. regulate
> 5. postponed

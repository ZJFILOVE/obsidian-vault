# English Vocabulary — 2026-11-06：分类、边界与可视化的 8 个词块

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-11-06|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-11-06|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-11-06|词汇]]

今天不背地图专名，而是学习能迁移到论文、报告和数据图表中的表达。每个词块都要同时掌握“它能说什么”和“它不能替你证明什么”。

## 1. spatial distribution

**含义：** 空间分布；某事物在地理空间中如何出现。

- **常见搭配：** map the spatial distribution of; uneven spatial distribution; changes in spatial distribution
- **例句：** The figure summarizes the spatial distribution of reported cases, but it does not explain why they cluster in certain areas.
- **词族：** space → spatial；distribute → distribution → distributed
- **辨析：** distribution 可以是任何“分布”；spatial distribution 明确强调位置关系。不要把它自动等同于 population density。

## 2. a clear-cut distinction

**含义：** 泾渭分明的区别、边界清楚的二分。常用于否定：**not a clear-cut distinction**。

- **常见搭配：** draw / make a distinction; a sharp distinction; the distinction between A and B
- **例句：** The distinction between a temporary change and a lasting trend is not always clear-cut.
- **词族：** distinct → distinction → distinctive；注意 distinct from，不是 distinct with。
- **辨析：** clear-cut 强调界线明确；clear 也可以只表示“表达容易理解”。A clear explanation 不等于 a clear-cut category。

## 3. account for

**含义一：** 把某因素纳入解释、设计或计算；**含义二：** 解释某结果；**含义三：** 占某比例。

- **常见搭配：** account for uncertainty / seasonal variation / missing data; account for 30 percent
- **例句：** A useful classification should account for overlap without making the legend impossible to read.
- **对比：** explain why 强调给出原因；account for 往往强调解释得足够完整或把因素考虑进去。
- **易错：** 在 “A accounts for 20%” 中不是“解释了 20%”，而是“占 20%”。

## 4. conform to

**含义：** 符合、遵从某规则、形状或预期。

- **常见搭配：** conform to a standard / pattern / rule / expectation
- **例句：** Administrative boundaries rarely conform perfectly to the areas in which people share the same daily practices.
- **词族：** conform → conformity / nonconformity；conforming / nonconforming
- **辨析：** comply with 常用于遵守法律或正式要求；conform to 也可描述形状或模式相符。

## 5. overlap with / overlap between

**含义：** 重叠、交叉；可指空间、时间、职责或类别。

- **常见搭配：** substantial overlap; partially overlap; areas of overlap
- **例句：** There is considerable overlap between the two categories, so forcing every case into only one group would be misleading.
- **语法：** A overlaps **with** B；there is overlap **between** A and B。
- **对比：** intersect 常强调交叉点或集合关系；overlap 更强调共享一部分范围。

## 6. dominant

**含义：** 占主导的、最有影响力或数量最多的；不等于“唯一”。

- **常见搭配：** dominant language / factor / pattern / market position
- **例句：** No single explanation was dominant across all six sites.
- **词族：** dominate（动词）→ dominant（形容词）→ dominance（名词）
- **辨析：** dominant 说明相对位置；common 只说明常见。A dominant category may still leave a large minority outside it.

## 7. represent

**含义：** 表示、呈现、代表。用于图表时要问“通过什么编码规则表示”。

- **常见搭配：** represent data / uncertainty / a group; be represented by color
- **例句：** Darker colors represent higher reported values; they do not represent greater certainty.
- **词族：** represent → representation → representative / misrepresent
- **辨析：** represent 不等于 reproduce。地图可以表示现实的某些方面，但不会复制现实全部细节。

## 8. depending on

**含义：** 取决于、随着条件不同而变化。适合在结论中主动标出条件。

- **常见搭配：** depending on the purpose / sample / time period / definition used
- **例句：** The same observation can support different classifications depending on the question being asked.
- **结构扩展：** depending on who collected the data, when it was collected, and why it was needed
- **辨析：** because of 指原因；depending on 指结果随条件变化，不一定说明完整因果机制。

## 词块组合训练

把两个以上词块放进同一句，避免只会孤立填空：

1. **Because the categories overlap, the map uses stripes to represent areas where no single language is dominant.**
2. **Whether the distinction appears clear-cut depends on how the data were collected and which variation the model accounts for.**
3. **The spatial distribution of a practice may not conform to administrative boundaries.**

### 从强结论退一步

| 过强表达 | 更精确的表达 |
|---|---|
| The colors prove that the groups are separate. | The colors represent separate categories chosen by the mapmaker. |
| This factor explains the pattern. | This factor may account for part of the pattern. |
| The blue region is uniform. | Blue is the dominant category, although local overlap may remain. |

## 检索测验｜先合上上文

### A. 中译英

1. 这张图显示的是报告病例的空间分布，而不是疾病的真实流行率。
2. 两类之间并没有泾渭分明的界线。
3. 模型需要把季节变化考虑进去。
4. 语言使用范围不一定与行政边界一致。
5. 这两个职责有相当大的重叠。
6. “占主导”并不等于“唯一存在”。
7. 颜色表示分类，不表示证据的确定性。
8. 结论会随使用的定义而变化。

### B. 选词并解释

从 **represent / prove / account for / conform to** 中选最合适的词：

1. The symbols ___ uncertainty in the estimate.
2. The analysis should ___ differences in sample size.
3. The observed association does not ___ a causal relationship.
4. Local practices do not always ___ national categories.

### C. 纠错

1. The red zone is dominant, so everyone there belongs to the same group.
2. The map accounts 40 percent of the report.
3. The two definitions overlap each other in several cases.

> [!answer]- 参考答案
> **A**
> 1. The figure shows the spatial distribution of reported cases rather than the true prevalence of the disease.
> 2. There is no clear-cut distinction between the two categories.
> 3. The model needs to account for seasonal variation.
> 4. Patterns of language use do not necessarily conform to administrative boundaries.
> 5. There is substantial overlap between the two responsibilities.
> 6. Being dominant does not mean being the only one present.
> 7. The colors represent categories, not the certainty of the evidence.
> 8. The conclusion varies depending on the definition used.
>
> **B** 1. represent；2. account for；3. prove；4. conform to。
>
> **C**
> 1. The red category is dominant, but the area may still contain substantial variation.
> 2. The map accounts **for** 40 percent of the report.（若想说“解释差异”：The factor accounts for part of the variation.）
> 3. The two definitions overlap in several cases. / There is overlap between the two definitions.

# Vocabulary Practice — 2026-09-09

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-09-09|Daily]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-09-09|Translation]]

## 1. coordinate
**v. 协调，使配合**

**Useful collocations:** coordinate movement; coordinate multiple tasks; hand-eye coordination  
**Word family:** coordinate, coordination, coordinator

**Memory:** *co-* means “together”; *ordinate* is related to order/arrangement. Think: put several processes into an organized relationship.

> Playing an instrument requires the brain to coordinate perception and movement.

## 2. engage
**v. 使参与；吸引；调动**

**Collocations:** engage the brain; engage in an activity; engage an audience  
**Word family:** engagement, engaged

> A difficult explanation can engage several kinds of attention at once.

## 3. integrate
**v. 整合；使成为整体**

**Collocations:** integrate information; integrate A with B; sensory integration  
**Word family:** integration, integrated, integrity

**Memory:** Latin *integer* meant “whole, intact.” To integrate is to bring parts into a whole.

> Skilled performance integrates auditory, visual, and motor information.

## 4. simultaneous
**adj. 同时发生的**

**Collocations:** simultaneous processing; simultaneous events; occur simultaneously  
**Word family:** simultaneously

**Memory:** from Latin *simul*, “at the same time.” This also helps with **simulate** only superficially in spelling—do not treat them as the same modern meaning.

> Musicians often handle several simultaneous streams of information.

## 5. consolidate
**v. 巩固；使加强；合并**

**Collocations:** consolidate knowledge; consolidate gains; memory consolidation  
**Word family:** consolidation

**Memory:** Latin roots convey the idea of making something firm/solid. Link it mentally to **solid**.

> Sleep and repeated practice can help consolidate newly learned material.

## 6. refine
**v. 改进；精炼，使更精确**

**Collocations:** refine a skill; refine a method; refine an argument  
**Word family:** refinement, refined

> Feedback allows learners to refine their technique rather than repeat the same mistake.

## 7. cognitive
**adj. 认知的**

**Collocations:** cognitive ability; cognitive load; cognitive process  
**Word family:** cognition, cognitive

**Memory:** ultimately related to Latin *cognoscere*, “to get to know.” Connect **cognition** with knowing, perceiving, remembering, and reasoning.

> The task becomes harder when cognitive load exceeds what you can manage effectively.

## 8. transfer
**n./v. 迁移；转移**

**Collocations:** transfer a skill; knowledge transfer; transfer to a new context  
**Word family:** transferable, transference

**Memory:** *trans-* = across + a root meaning carry/bear: carry something across from one context to another.

> A useful question is whether a skill learned in one domain transfers to another.

## 9. in response to
**chunk: 对……作出反应；作为对……的回应**

**Collocations:** change in response to feedback; react in response to stress; adjust in response to evidence

> Good learners revise their strategy in response to evidence rather than defending the first idea they had.

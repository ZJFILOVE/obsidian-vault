# Vocabulary — Day 9

## Core language blocks
- **dependence on / depend on / be dependent on**
- **weaken one's ability to do sth.**
- **deserve attention**
- **the real challenge is not A, but B**
- **evaluate the quality of information**
- **draw a conclusion based on sufficient evidence**

## Trap
`information`, `evidence`, and `progress` are uncountable in normal academic use.

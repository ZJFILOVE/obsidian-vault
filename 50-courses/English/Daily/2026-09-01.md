# English Practice — Day 14

## Mini Reading
People often judge the effectiveness of studying by how easy it feels. Rereading the same material can create a strong sense of familiarity. Because the words and ideas become easier to recognize, learners may assume that they have mastered the material. However, recognition is not the same as being able to retrieve information without help. Tests that require learners to recall information from memory may feel more difficult, but the effort involved can reveal weaknesses and strengthen long-term learning.

### Questions
1. Why can rereading create an illusion of mastery? **The material becomes familiar and easier to recognize.**
2. Advantage of recall from memory: **it can expose weaknesses**.
3. Best summary: **familiarity does not necessarily mean mastery**.

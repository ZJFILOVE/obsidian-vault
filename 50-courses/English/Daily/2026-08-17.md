# English Practice — Day 3

## Reading
Many university students sacrifice sleep when they have exams or assignments to complete. Staying awake for several extra hours may appear to provide more time for study. However, this strategy can sometimes have the opposite effect.

Sleep plays an important role in learning. During sleep, the brain processes information acquired during the day and helps strengthen newly formed memories. Therefore, reducing sleep in order to study longer may make it more difficult to remember what has been learned.

Sleep deprivation can also affect attention and decision-making. A tired student may spend two hours reading a chapter but absorb less information than a well-rested student could learn in a much shorter period. More importantly, people who lack sleep do not always recognize how much their performance has declined.

Sleep should therefore be considered part of an effective learning strategy. Better time management may reduce the need to choose between adequate sleep and adequate preparation.

### Questions
1. Main purpose: explain **why sleep is part of effective learning**.
2. Sleep helps the brain **process information and strengthen memories**.
3. Sleep-deprived students may **fail to notice their own decline**.
4. Better time management can reduce the conflict between **sleep and study**.

# English Practice — Day 13

## Mini Reading
Students sometimes believe that remembering information immediately after studying means that they have learned it successfully. However, short-term performance can be misleading. Information that seems easy to recall immediately may be forgotten several days later. By contrast, retrieving information from memory after some time has passed can feel more difficult, but this effort may strengthen long-term learning. Researchers therefore often recommend reviewing material at intervals rather than repeating it many times in a single study session.

### Questions
1. Main point: **spaced review can be more useful for long-term learning**.
2. Short-term performance can mislead because information remembered now may later be forgotten.
3. `By contrast` introduces an **opposite/different situation**.

## Logic note
**association/correlation ≠ causation**.

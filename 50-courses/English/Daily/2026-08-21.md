# English Practice — Day 7

## Reading
When students receive a poor grade, their first reaction is often to focus on the result itself. Yet a grade provides only limited information about why a student performed poorly.

Feedback is more useful when it identifies specific causes of a mistake: misunderstanding a concept, overlooking a detail, or using an ineffective method. Each problem requires a different response.

Simply spending more time studying may not address the real weakness. Effective learners therefore treat mistakes as information and ask not only “How can I get a higher score?” but also “Why did I make this mistake?”

### Questions
1. Main idea: analyze the **causes of mistakes** to improve more effectively.
2. Examples show that **different mistakes require different solutions**.
3. `real weakness` = the **underlying reason** for poor performance.
4. Understanding why an error occurred helps choose a better response.

# English Practice — Day 9

## Translation traps
1. 取得显著进步 → **make significant progress**
2. 这并不一定意味着…… → **This does not necessarily mean...**
3. 使学生能够获取信息 → **enable students to access information**
4. 防止学生过度依赖 AI → **prevent students from depending too heavily on AI**
5. 根据充分的证据作出结论 → **draw a conclusion based on sufficient evidence**

## Spaced review
- Rather than blindly increasing their study time, students should focus on improving learning efficiency.
- Success depends not only on ability but also on consistent effort.
- Students should distinguish reliable information from misleading information.
- Simply being busy does not necessarily mean being productive.

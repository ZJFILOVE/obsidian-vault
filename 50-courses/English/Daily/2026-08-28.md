# English Practice — Day 12

## Mini Reading
People tend to seek information that supports what they already believe. This tendency can make decision-making easier because conflicting information requires additional mental effort to evaluate. However, ignoring such information has a cost. When people consider only evidence supporting their existing views, they may become increasingly confident without becoming more accurate. Good judgment therefore requires not only collecting information but also actively examining evidence that challenges one's assumptions.

### Questions
1. `this tendency` = **seeking information that supports existing beliefs**.
2. People may become more confident without becoming more **accurate**.
3. Good judgment requires **examining evidence that challenges one's beliefs**.

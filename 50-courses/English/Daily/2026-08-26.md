# English Practice — Day 10

## Error hunting
Correct the sentences:
1. `The Internet provides students many useful informations.` → **provides students with much useful information**.
2. `More study time does not necessary lead...` → **does not necessarily lead...**
3. `distinguish reliable information with...` → **distinguish A from B**.
4. `enables people completing...` → **enables people to complete...**
5. `students are easy to be distracted...` → **students are easily distracted...**

- prevent sb. from participating in...
- depend not only on talent but also on consistent effort

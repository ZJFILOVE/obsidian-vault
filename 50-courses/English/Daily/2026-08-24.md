# English Practice — Day 8

## Collocation check
1. Researchers need more evidence before they can **draw** a reliable conclusion.
2. Lack of sleep can seriously **affect** students' ability to concentrate.
3. The program was introduced **in response to** growing concerns.
4. Students should **take responsibility for** evaluating information.
5. Regular exercise can **contribute to** better sleep.

## Spaced review
Translate:
- 熟悉一个概念并不一定意味着真正掌握了它。
- 经济困难可能使一些学生无法参加无薪实习。
- 技术应该支持独立思考，而不是取代它。
- 仅仅投入更多时间并不能保证取得进步。

### Reference
- Being familiar with a concept does **not necessarily** mean that you have truly mastered it.
- Financial difficulties may **prevent some students from participating in** unpaid internships.
- Technology should support independent thinking **rather than** replace it.
- Simply spending more time cannot **guarantee progress**.

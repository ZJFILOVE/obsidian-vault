## 1. CET-6 level

先独立翻译，再展开答案：

**近年来，极端天气事件造成的损失越来越大。虽然单个暴风雨不能简单地归因于气候变化，但不断升高的气温可能会增加某些灾害发生的概率，并使其影响更加严重。因此，公共部门需要同时加强预警系统和长期的减排措施。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> In recent years, extreme-weather events have caused increasingly serious damage. Although no single storm can be simply attributed to climate change, rising temperatures may increase the likelihood of certain disasters and make their impacts more severe. Therefore, public authorities need to strengthen both early-warning systems and long-term emissions-reduction measures.

### Useful structures
- cause increasingly serious damage
- attribute A to B
- increase the likelihood of...
- make the impact more severe
- strengthen both A and B

### Common transfer errors
- 不要把“概率增加”译成 *the possibility is bigger*；优先使用 **increase the likelihood/probability of**。
- “减排措施”通常是 **emissions-reduction measures** 或 **measures to reduce emissions**。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level

**评估气候风险时，研究人员不能只关注灾害是否发生，还必须分析暴露程度、基础设施脆弱性以及社会应对能力。相同强度的风暴在不同地区造成的后果可能完全不同，因为风险不仅取决于自然事件本身，也取决于人类系统是否具备足够的适应能力。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> When assessing climate risk, researchers must examine not only whether a disaster occurs but also the degree of exposure, the vulnerability of infrastructure, and the capacity of society to respond. Storms of the same intensity can produce completely different consequences in different regions, because risk depends not only on the natural event itself but also on whether human systems possess sufficient adaptive capacity.

### Useful structures
- when assessing...
- examine not only A but also B
- the vulnerability of infrastructure
- produce different consequences
- depend on whether...
- possess sufficient adaptive capacity

### Common transfer errors
- “暴露程度”在风险语境下通常是 **degree of exposure**，不是 *exposure level* in every context。
- “适应能力”可用 **adaptive capacity**；不要机械翻成 *ability of adaptation*。

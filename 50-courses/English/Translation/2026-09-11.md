## 1. CET-6 level

先独立翻译，再展开答案：

**人类的发音器官能够产生的声音远多于任何一种语言实际使用的声音。一个声音在生理上可以发出，并不意味着它会自然地成为某种语言的一部分。学习外语时，人们往往不是“听不见”陌生声音，而是习惯用自己母语已有的分类方式去理解它们。因此，训练发音也需要训练听觉上的区分能力。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> The human vocal tract can produce far more sounds than any single language actually uses. The fact that a sound is physiologically possible does not mean that it will naturally become part of a language. When learning a foreign language, people often do not literally fail to hear unfamiliar sounds; rather, they tend to interpret them through categories already established by their native language. Therefore, pronunciation training also requires practice in auditory discrimination.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **the human vocal tract** — 人类发音器官/声道
- **far more ... than ...** — 远多于……
- **the fact that A does not mean that B** — A 这一事实并不意味着 B
- **interpret A through B** — 通过 B 的框架理解 A
- **auditory discrimination** — 听觉辨别能力

### Common transfer errors

- “能发出某个声音”不要机械写成 *can speak a sound*；通常用 **produce/articulate a sound**。
- “并不意味着”优先用 **does not mean that...**，不要把逻辑关系写成绝对否定。
- “用母语已有的分类方式理解”不是 *understand by mother-tongue categories*；可用 **interpret ... through categories established by one's native language**。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level

**语言中的声音系统既受到人体结构的限制，也受到历史演变以及语言内部对立关系的塑造。某种声音即使可以被稳定地发出，如果它没有帮助说话者区分词义，或在长期变化中被其他声音取代，它也可能不会在某种语言中形成独立的音位。因此，研究语音不能只列举人类能够产生哪些声音，还需要分析哪些声音差异在特定语言中真正承担信息功能。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> The sound system of a language is shaped both by constraints imposed by human anatomy and by historical change and contrastive relationships within the language. Even if a sound can be produced consistently, it may fail to develop into an independent phoneme if it does not help speakers distinguish meanings, or if it is replaced by another sound over time. Therefore, the study of speech sounds involves not only cataloguing what humans can produce but also analyzing which distinctions actually carry information in a particular language.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **be shaped by...** — 受到……塑造
- **constraints imposed by...** — 由……施加的限制
- **contrastive relationship/distinction** — 对立关系 / 区别性差异
- **develop into an independent phoneme** — 形成独立音位
- **distinguish meanings** — 区分词义
- **carry information** — 承担/携带信息功能
- **not only A but also B** — 不仅……而且……

### Common transfer errors

- “音位”是 **phoneme**；不要和一般意义上的 **sound** 混用。
- “对立关系”在语言学里更自然的是 **contrastive relationship/distinction**，不是直译成 *opposite relationship*。
- “承担信息功能”可用 **carry information / serve a contrastive function**，避免中式表达 *undertake an information function*。

## 3. Precision rewrite

把下面两个较弱的表达改成更自然、精确的英语：

1. *People cannot hear some foreign sounds.*  
2. *A language chooses some sounds from all possible sounds.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Speakers may perceive unfamiliar sounds through phonological categories from their native language.
> 2. Languages make use of only a subset of the speech sounds that humans can physically produce.

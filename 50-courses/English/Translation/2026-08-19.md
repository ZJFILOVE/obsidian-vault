# Translation — Day 5

## Chinese → English
与过去相比，如今大学生获得学习资源更加方便。互联网使他们能够随时查找资料，并与来自不同地区的人交流。然而，丰富的信息并不一定都具有相同的价值。因此，学生需要培养判断信息质量的能力，而不是简单地接受他们在网上看到的一切。

## Reference
Compared with the past, university students today have easier access to learning resources. The Internet enables them to search for information at any time and communicate with people from different regions. However, the large amount of information available does not necessarily have the same value. Therefore, students need to develop the ability to evaluate the quality of information rather than simply accept everything they see online.

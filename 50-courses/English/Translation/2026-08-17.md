# Translation — Day 3

## Chinese → English
近年来，越来越多的大学生开始意识到睡眠的重要性。充足的睡眠不仅有助于保持身体健康，还能提高注意力和学习效率。然而，由于学习任务繁重，一些学生仍然习惯熬夜。因此，合理安排时间对于大学生来说十分重要。

## Reference
In recent years, an increasing number of university students have begun to realize the importance of sleep. Adequate sleep not only helps maintain physical health but also improves concentration and learning efficiency. However, due to heavy academic workloads, some students still tend to stay up late. Therefore, managing time properly is particularly important for university students.

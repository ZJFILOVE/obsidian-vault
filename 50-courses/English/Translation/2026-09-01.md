# Translation — Day 14

## A · CET-4+
许多大学生在学习时习惯把手机放在身边，以便随时查看消息。虽然每次查看手机可能只需要几秒钟，但频繁的打断会使人难以长时间保持注意力。因此，为了提高学习效率，学生可以在学习期间关闭不必要的通知，并把手机放在不容易拿到的地方。

### Reference
Many university students are accustomed to keeping their phones nearby while studying so that they can check messages at any time. Although checking a phone may take only a few seconds each time, frequent interruptions can make it difficult to maintain concentration for long periods. Therefore, to improve learning efficiency, students can turn off unnecessary notifications and keep their phones somewhere that is not easily accessible while studying.

## B · CET-6
人们在评价自己的学习效果时，往往过于关注投入了多少时间，而忽视了这些时间是如何使用的。事实上，两个人即使学习相同的时间，最终取得的效果也可能有很大差异。只有当学习者主动检验自己的理解、发现错误并及时调整学习方法时，投入的时间才更有可能转化为真正的进步。

### Reference
When evaluating their learning, people tend to focus too much on how much time they have spent studying while overlooking how that time has been used. In fact, even if two people spend the same amount of time studying, their learning outcomes may differ considerably. Only when learners actively test their understanding, identify mistakes, and adjust their learning methods in time is the time they invest more likely to result in genuine progress.

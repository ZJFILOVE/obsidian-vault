## 练习一：CET-6——社区维修咖啡馆（15–18 分钟）

某社区中心每月举办两次维修咖啡馆，居民可以带来坏掉的小家电、自行车或衣物，并在志愿者指导下尝试修理。活动免费，但参加者需要提前说明物品类型和故障，以便中心安排合适的工具。志愿者会讲解安全步骤，却不承诺每件物品都能修好。涉及高压电池、燃气设备或严重结构损坏的物品不会在现场处理。参加者应在维修过程中留在场内，并决定是否接受建议更换的零件。中心会记录物品是否恢复使用、使用了哪些零件以及三个月后物品是否仍在使用，但不会把没有修好的物品简单算作活动失败，因为诊断问题和学习基本技能也是服务目标。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A community center holds a repair café twice a month. Residents may bring broken small appliances, bicycles or clothing and try to repair them with guidance from volunteers. The service is free, but participants must describe the type of item and the problem in advance so that the center can prepare suitable tools. Volunteers will explain the safety steps, but they do not guarantee that every item can be repaired. Items involving high-voltage batteries, gas equipment or serious structural damage will not be handled on site. Participants are expected to remain present during the repair and decide whether to accept any suggested replacement parts. The center will record whether an item is returned to use, which parts are used and whether the item is still in use three months later. However, it will not simply count every unrepaired item as a failure, because diagnosing a problem and learning basic skills are also goals of the service.

### 结构与搭配

- **hold ... twice a month**：每月两次；不要把 twice a month 写成 every two months。
- **with guidance from volunteers**：在志愿者指导下；guidance 通常不可数。
- **describe ... in advance**：提前说明，让后面的目的从句有明确基础。
- **do not guarantee that**：不作结果保证，比 cannot repair every item 更准确。
- **be handled on site**：在现场处理；适合服务与维修语境。
- **return an item to use**：使物品恢复使用，避免只写 fix。
- **count A as B**：把 A 计作／视作 B。

### 常见中英迁移错误

1. “举办活动”用 hold / run an event，不是 open an activity。
2. “安排工具”不是 arrange tools in a line；此处可用 prepare suitable tools。
3. “留在场内”是 remain present，不是 keep in the room。
4. “是否仍在使用”要保留时间点：whether the item is still in use three months later。
5. “不简单算作失败”要体现评价标准，不要改成 every repair is successful。

## 练习二：TOEFL／academic——弹性截止日期试点（20–25 分钟）

某大学在四门大一课程中试行一次弹性截止日期：学生可以把一项作业延期最多七天，且无需提供医疗证明。另四门规模和考核方式相近的课程继续使用原规则。研究团队比较了作业提交率、迟交天数、最终成绩、退课率和教师用于处理延期申请的时间，并在学期初记录学生的通勤时间、兼职工作时长和既往成绩。试点课程的按时或获准延期后提交率较高，教师处理个别申请的时间减少；然而，最后一周提交的作业明显增多。由于学生并非随机分配到课程，且教师可以自行设计提醒方式，研究者没有把所有差异归因于新规则。后续研究需要判断，该政策是减少了不必要的行政负担，还是把学习压力推迟到了学期末，并考察不同背景的学生是否同等受益。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A university piloted a flexible deadline in four first-year courses. Students could postpone one assignment for up to seven days without providing medical documentation. Four other courses with similar enrolment and assessment formats retained the existing rules. The research team compared submission rates, the number of days assignments were late, final grades, course withdrawal rates and the time instructors spent processing extension requests. It also recorded students' commuting time, hours of paid work and prior academic performance at the beginning of the semester. The pilot courses had a higher rate of assignments submitted either on time or within an approved extension, and instructors spent less time dealing with individual requests. However, substantially more assignments were submitted in the final week. Because students were not randomly assigned to courses and instructors could design their own reminder systems, the researchers did not attribute every difference to the new rule. Further research should determine whether the policy reduced unnecessary administrative burdens or merely shifted study pressure to the end of the semester, and whether students from different backgrounds benefited equally.

### 结构与搭配

- **pilot a policy**：试行政策；pilot 可直接作动词。
- **for up to seven days**：最多七天，包含更短时间。
- **retain the existing rules**：保留原规则，比 keep old rules 更正式。
- **process extension requests**：处理延期申请；process 强调行政流程。
- **either on time or within an approved extension**：把两种合规提交方式并列清楚。
- **be randomly assigned to**：被随机分配到；没有随机分配时要控制因果结论。
- **attribute every difference to**：把所有差异归因于；否定式可保留多个解释。
- **shift pressure to**：把压力转移到另一个时间或群体。

### 结论强度阶梯

| 过强 | 更准确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| Flexible deadlines improved learning. | Submission rates were higher in the pilot courses. | 提交增加不等于学习质量提高。 |
| The policy reduced stress. | The policy may have changed when pressure occurred. | 学期末提交集中可能代表压力后移。 |
| Teachers saved time because of the rule. | Instructors in the pilot courses spent less time processing individual requests. | 教师提醒方式和课程差异仍存在。 |
| All students benefited. | The available results do not show whether benefits were evenly distributed. | 总体结果不能回答群体差异。 |

## 精确改写

将下列句子改成证据强度合适的英文，再展开折叠答案：

1. 维修咖啡馆证明免费服务能解决所有浪费问题。
2. 弹性截止日期让学生学得更好。
3. 教师少花了时间，所以新政策一定是唯一原因。

> [!answer]- 精确改写参考
> 1. The repair café may keep some items in use for longer, but the pilot cannot show that a free service will eliminate every source of waste.
> 2. The pilot courses had higher submission rates, although the available measures do not establish that students learned more.
> 3. Instructors in the pilot courses spent less time processing individual requests, but course design and reminder practices may also have contributed.

## 中英迁移提醒

- “指导”常用 guidance，不要随意加复数。
- “提前说明故障”需要把信息、时间和目的分别写清。
- “无需证明”可用 without providing documentation，避免 no need evidence 的口语拼接。
- “最终成绩”是 final grades，不是 final scores in the semester。
- “并非随机分配”是研究设计边界，应紧跟因果结论。
- “同等受益”讨论分配效果，不能由总体平均值回答。

## 间隔复习句

1. 志愿者会说明安全步骤，但不保证每件物品都能修好。
2. 提交率上升与政策效果一致，但不能排除课程与提醒方式的差异。

> [!answer]- 间隔复习参考
> 1. Volunteers will explain the safety steps, but they do not guarantee that every item can be repaired.
> 2. The higher submission rate is consistent with a policy effect, but differences in courses and reminder practices cannot be ruled out.

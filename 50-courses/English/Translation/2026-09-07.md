# Translation Practice — 2026-09-07

## 1. CET-6 level
先自己翻译，尽量不用逐字对应的方法：

**科学发现并不总是依赖昂贵的设备。有时，一个设计巧妙的实验只需要测量几个容易观察的量，就能帮助研究者推断出无法直接测量的信息。真正困难的地方往往不是收集更多数据，而是提出一个能够把观察结果与科学问题联系起来的好方法。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Scientific discoveries do not always depend on expensive equipment. Sometimes, a cleverly designed experiment needs to measure only a few easily observable quantities to help researchers infer information that cannot be measured directly. The real difficulty often lies not in collecting more data, but in developing a good method that connects observations with the scientific question being investigated.

### Useful structures
- depend on expensive equipment
- a cleverly designed experiment
- observable quantities
- infer A from B
- the difficulty lies not in A, but in B
- connect observations with a research question

### Common transfer errors
- **“推断出”** 不要机械翻成 *guess out*；学术语境常用 **infer / deduce**。
- **“真正困难的地方是……”** 可用 **the difficulty lies in...**，比 *the difficult place is...* 自然。
- **“只需要……”** 常用 **need only + verb** 或 **only needs to + verb**，注意 only 的位置。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level

**在学习复杂概念时，人们很容易把“能够认出一个解释”误认为“能够独立解释这个概念”。然而，真正的理解通常要求学习者在没有提示的情况下重建推理过程，并说明证据为什么支持某个结论。因此，与其反复阅读同一段材料，不如尝试主动回忆、提出问题，并用自己的语言解释关键步骤。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> When learning complex concepts, people can easily mistake being able to recognize an explanation for being able to explain the concept independently. Genuine understanding, however, usually requires learners to reconstruct the reasoning process without prompts and explain why the evidence supports a particular conclusion. Therefore, rather than repeatedly rereading the same material, it is often more effective to practice active recall, ask questions, and explain the key steps in one's own words.

### Useful structures
- mistake A for B
- genuine understanding
- reconstruct the reasoning process
- without prompts
- evidence supports a conclusion
- rather than doing A, ...
- in one's own words

### Common transfer errors
- **“把 A 误认为 B”**：**mistake A for B**，不要写成 *mistake A as B*。
- **“证据支持结论”**：英语自然搭配是 **evidence supports a conclusion/claim**。
- **“用自己的语言”**：固定表达 **in your own words**，不是 *use your own language*。

## 3. Spaced review
回顾上一组表达，不看答案翻译：

1. **更快获得答案并不一定会带来更深入的理解。**
2. **与其等待动力，不如优先处理一个可以立即开始的小步骤。**

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. Faster access to answers does not necessarily lead to deeper understanding.
> 2. Rather than waiting for motivation, prioritize one small step that you can start immediately.

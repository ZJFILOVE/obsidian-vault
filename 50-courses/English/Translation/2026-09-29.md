## 1. CET-6 level — campus parcel lockers

**许多大学在宿舍区设置快递柜，希望减少学生排队取件的时间，也减轻驿站在晚间高峰期的压力。快递柜确实能让部分包裹实现自助领取，但如果柜门数量不足、包裹长时间无人取走，新的瓶颈就会从“人工发放速度”转移到“可用柜格数量”。此外，并不是所有包裹都适合放入快递柜：体积较大的物品、需要冷藏的食品以及需要当面核验身份的快件仍然需要其他处理方式。因此，学校在评价快递柜是否有效时，不能只统计每天有多少包裹通过柜子领取，还应观察高峰期等待时间、柜格周转速度、超时占用比例，以及学生是否因为柜子已满而不得不前往更远的取件点。一个好的系统不一定消除所有排队，而是应该把有限的人工和空间资源用在最需要它们的环节。**

Translate it into natural English. Try to use **shift the bottleneck**, **turnover**, and **does not necessarily**.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Many universities have installed parcel lockers near student dormitories in an effort to reduce the time students spend waiting for deliveries and to ease pressure on staffed pickup stations during the evening rush. Lockers can certainly allow some parcels to be collected without staff assistance, but if there are too few compartments or packages remain unclaimed for long periods, the bottleneck may simply shift from the speed of manual distribution to the availability of locker space. Moreover, not every parcel is suitable for a locker. Large items, food that requires refrigeration, and deliveries that require in-person identity checks still need to be handled in other ways. Universities should therefore evaluate the system by more than the number of parcels collected from lockers each day. They should also examine peak-hour waiting time, compartment turnover, the proportion of lockers occupied beyond the intended period, and whether students are forced to travel to more distant pickup points when nearby lockers are full. A good system does not necessarily eliminate every queue; it uses limited staff time and physical space where they are most needed.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **install parcel lockers** — 设置快递柜
- **staffed pickup station** — 有工作人员的取件点
- **ease pressure during peak hours** — 缓解高峰期压力
- **shift the bottleneck from A to B** — 把瓶颈从 A 转移到 B
- **remain unclaimed** — 长时间无人领取
- **locker / compartment turnover** — 柜格周转
- **require an in-person identity check** — 需要当面核验身份
- **be suitable for...** — 适合……
- **evaluate a system by more than...** — 评价系统不能只看……
- **use limited resources where they are most needed** — 把有限资源用于最需要的环节

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“取快递”**通常写 **pick up / collect a parcel**，不是 *take an express*。
2. **“柜格”**可写 **locker compartment / compartment**，不要机械写成 *small cabinet*。
3. **“包裹无人取走”**用 **remain unclaimed / remain uncollected** 很自然。
4. **“周转速度”**可以用 **turnover**；这里强调一个柜格被释放后再次使用的速度。
5. **“缓解压力”**常用 **ease / relieve pressure on...**，不要写 *reduce the pressure of the station*。
6. **“不一定消除”**优先用 **does not necessarily eliminate...**，比绝对的 *cannot eliminate* 更精确。

## 2. TOEFL / academic level — evaluating urban heat adaptation

**在夏季高温越来越频繁的城市中，种植行道树、建设遮阳设施和使用高反射率屋顶经常被视为降低热暴露的办法。不过，“城市平均气温下降了多少”并不是评价这些措施的唯一指标。对行人而言，下午两点在公交站是否有阴影，可能比全市日平均气温下降零点几度更重要；对老年人和户外劳动者而言，措施是否覆盖他们实际活动的地点和时间，也比城市总体绿化率更能反映实际收益。与此同时，树木需要多年才能形成足够树冠，维护和灌溉也需要资源，而高反射率屋顶主要改变建筑表面的能量吸收，并不直接为街道上的行人提供遮阴。因此，研究者在比较不同降温方案时，应明确评价对象、时间尺度和空间尺度，并区分“模型预测的平均效果”与“特定人群真正减少了多少热暴露”。只有在这些条件被说明之后，城市之间的比较才更有意义。**

Translate it into academic English. Try to preserve the distinction between **citywide averages** and **human exposure**.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> In cities where periods of extreme summer heat are becoming more frequent, street trees, shade structures, and highly reflective roofs are often proposed as ways to reduce heat exposure. However, the change in a city's average temperature is not the only relevant measure of whether these interventions are effective. For a pedestrian, the presence of shade at a bus stop at two o'clock in the afternoon may matter more than a small reduction in the citywide daily mean temperature. For older adults and outdoor workers, whether an intervention reaches the places and times in which they are actually exposed may be more informative than the city's overall share of green space. At the same time, trees may take years to develop sufficient canopy and require resources for maintenance and irrigation, while reflective roofs mainly alter the amount of energy absorbed by building surfaces and do not directly provide shade to pedestrians at street level. Researchers comparing cooling strategies should therefore specify the population of interest, the time scale, and the spatial scale of evaluation, and they should distinguish modeled average effects from reductions in heat exposure actually experienced by particular groups. Comparisons across cities become more meaningful only when these conditions are made explicit.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **heat exposure** — 热暴露
- **street trees / shade structures** — 行道树 / 遮阳设施
- **reflective roof** — 高反射率屋顶
- **citywide average / daily mean temperature** — 全市平均值 / 日平均气温
- **population of interest** — 研究关注的人群
- **time scale / spatial scale** — 时间尺度 / 空间尺度
- **develop sufficient canopy** — 形成足够树冠
- **be more informative than...** — 比……更能提供有效信息
- **modeled average effect** — 模型预测的平均效果
- **make conditions explicit** — 把条件明确写出

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“高温越来越频繁”**不要写 *high temperature becomes more and more frequent*；用 **periods of extreme heat are becoming more frequent**。
2. **“降低热暴露”**是 **reduce heat exposure**，不是 *reduce exposure of heat*。
3. **“覆盖人群”**在干预语境中常用 **reach / cover the population**；不要只想到物理上的 *cover*。
4. **“全市平均气温”**可写 **citywide average temperature**，而 **average city temperature** 容易产生歧义。
5. **“实际收益”**按语境可写 **real-world benefit / benefit actually experienced**。
6. **“有意义的比较”**常用 **meaningful comparison**，比 *significant comparison* 更自然；**significant** 在学术语境中还可能被理解为统计显著。

## 3. Precision rewrite — make the claim testable

Rewrite each claim so that it becomes narrower and easier to evaluate.

1. *Parcel lockers solve delivery congestion on campus.*
2. *More trees make cities cooler.*
3. *Reflective roofs protect residents from heat.*
4. *A system is successful if more people use it.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Parcel lockers may reduce waiting at staffed pickup points when enough compartments are available and parcels are collected promptly.
> 2. Increasing tree canopy can reduce heat exposure in some outdoor locations, although the size and timing of the effect depend on canopy development, local geometry, and weather conditions.
> 3. Reflective roofs can reduce heat absorbed by building surfaces and may lower cooling demand, but they do not directly provide shade to pedestrians at street level.
> 4. Higher use can indicate that a system is accessible or convenient, but it does not by itself establish that the system improves the outcome it was designed to address.

## 4. Micro-drill — translate the relationship, not the word order

1. 新技术有时不是消除瓶颈，而是把瓶颈转移到系统的另一个环节。
2. 一个全市平均值可能掩盖不同社区之间很大的差异。
3. 如果措施在最需要它的人群中很少被使用，那么总体使用量很高也可能具有误导性。
4. 在评价项目之前，应该先说明“成功”具体指什么结果。

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. New technology sometimes shifts a bottleneck to another part of the system rather than eliminating it.
> 2. A citywide average can conceal substantial differences between neighborhoods.
> 3. A high overall level of use can still be misleading if the intervention is rarely used by the people who need it most.
> 4. Before evaluating a program, we should specify the outcome that would count as success.

## 6. One idea, three structures

Express the idea **“评价一个系统不能只看最容易统计的指标”** in three ways.

### A. Direct
Use **should not be evaluated solely by...**

### B. Contrast
Use **easy to measure does not mean...**

### C. Academic
Use **a valid evaluation requires...**

> [!answer]- Possible answers
> A. A system should not be evaluated solely by the indicator that is easiest to count.
> B. The fact that an outcome is easy to measure does not mean that it best captures whether the system is working.
> C. A valid evaluation requires measures that correspond to the system's intended outcomes rather than merely to the data that are easiest to collect.

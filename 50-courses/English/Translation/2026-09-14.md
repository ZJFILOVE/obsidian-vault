## 1. CET-6 level — repair instead of replacement

先独立翻译。尽量不要逐词对应中文，而是先判断每句话的逻辑关系。

**过去，人们常常会把坏掉的日用品拿去修理，而现在，许多产品一旦出现问题就会被直接替换。造成这种变化的原因并不只有消费者“更浪费”这么简单。维修费用较高、零件难以获得、产品设计不便拆卸，以及新产品价格不断下降，都可能影响人们的选择。如果企业能够提供更长时间的维修支持，而消费者也愿意比较“修”和“换”的长期成本，一些原本会被丢弃的物品就有可能继续使用。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> In the past, people often had broken household items repaired, whereas today many products are simply replaced once something goes wrong. This shift cannot be explained merely by saying that consumers have become more wasteful. High repair costs, limited access to spare parts, designs that are difficult to disassemble, and the falling prices of new products can all influence people's choices. If companies provide longer-term repair support and consumers are willing to compare the long-term costs of repairing and replacing an item, some products that would otherwise be discarded may remain in use for much longer.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **have sth. repaired** — 找人把某物修好；比 *repair something by oneself* 更适合“拿去修”
- **once something goes wrong** — 一旦出现问题
- **cannot be explained merely by...** — 不能仅仅用……解释
- **access to spare parts** — 获得零件的渠道/可获得性
- **be difficult to disassemble** — 难以拆卸
- **the long-term cost of doing sth.** — 做某事的长期成本
- **would otherwise be discarded** — 否则本来会被丢弃
- **remain in use** — 继续被使用

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“坏掉的日用品”**不要写成 *broken daily necessities*。英语里更自然的是 **broken household items / everyday products**。
2. **“维修费用较高”**可以用 **high repair costs**，不需要写成 *the fee of repair is high*。
3. **“影响人们的选择”**用 **influence/shape people's choices**，通常比 *affect people's selection* 自然。
4. **“继续使用”**如果主语是产品，可用 **remain in use**；如果主语是人，则可用 **continue using it**。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level — evaluating a policy by more than one metric

**公共政策常常会设定一个容易量化的目标，例如提高参与率、缩短等待时间或降低某项成本。然而，一个指标得到改善，并不自动意味着政策整体上取得了成功。参与人数增加，可能伴随着服务质量下降；平均等待时间缩短，也可能掩盖少数群体等待更久的问题。因此，在评估一项政策时，研究者不仅要问“目标是否达成”，还要考察收益和成本如何分布、是否出现了意料之外的行为变化，以及短期改善能否持续。只有把多个指标和不同群体的结果结合起来，才能对政策效果作出更可靠的判断。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Public policies often set targets that are easy to quantify, such as increasing participation, reducing waiting times, or lowering a particular cost. However, improvement in a single indicator does not automatically mean that a policy has succeeded overall. Higher participation may be accompanied by lower service quality, while a shorter average waiting time may conceal the fact that some groups are waiting longer. Therefore, policy evaluation should ask not only whether a target has been met, but also how benefits and costs are distributed, whether unintended behavioral changes have emerged, and whether short-term improvements can be sustained. A more reliable judgment requires combining multiple indicators with evidence about outcomes across different groups.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **set a measurable/quantifiable target** — 设定可量化目标
- **improvement in a single indicator** — 单一指标的改善
- **succeed overall** — 整体上取得成功
- **be accompanied by...** — 同时伴随着……
- **conceal the fact that...** — 掩盖……这一事实
- **meet a target** — 达成目标
- **unintended behavioral changes / unintended consequences** — 非预期的行为变化 / 后果
- **be sustained over time** — 随时间持续
- **outcomes across different groups** — 不同群体中的结果

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“指标得到改善”**不要写 *the indicator becomes better*；更自然的是 **the indicator improves / improvement in the indicator**。
2. **“政策取得成功”**通常用 **a policy succeeds / is successful**，而不是 *the policy gets success*。
3. **“掩盖问题”**可用 **conceal/mask a problem**；学术写作中 *hide* 往往语气过于口语。
4. **“考察”**不要固定翻成 *investigate*。根据语境可用 **examine, assess, evaluate, consider**。
5. **“可靠的判断”**可以用 **a reliable/robust judgment or assessment**，避免 *a correct judgment* 这种过强表达。

## 3. Precision rewrite

把下面三句改得更自然、更准确。

1. *If one number becomes better, the policy is successful.*
2. *Companies should give repair service for more long time.*
3. *This result proves that the new system is useful for everyone.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Improvement in a single indicator does not necessarily mean that the policy has succeeded overall.
> 2. Companies should provide repair support for a longer period.
> 3. This result suggests that the new system may be beneficial, but it does not establish that every group benefits equally.

## 4. Spaced review

这些句子复习前几天的高频表达。先自己翻，再展开答案。

1. **一个解释听起来合理，并不意味着它已经被证据证实。**
2. **我们需要区分“能够产生某种结果”和“在真实环境中稳定地产生这种结果”。**

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. The fact that an explanation sounds plausible does not mean that it has been established by evidence.
> 2. We need to distinguish being able to produce a result from producing it consistently in real-world conditions.

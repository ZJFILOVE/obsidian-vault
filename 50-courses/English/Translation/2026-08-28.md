# Translation — Day 12

## A · CET-4+
对于大学生来说，保持规律的学习习惯往往比考试前集中复习更加有效。虽然短时间内投入大量精力可能带来一定效果，但如果缺乏长期的积累，学到的知识很容易被遗忘。因此，学生应该根据自己的实际情况制定合理的学习计划，并定期检查自己的进步。

### Reference
For university students, maintaining regular study habits is often more effective than intensive review shortly before an examination. Although putting a great deal of effort into studying within a short period may produce some results, what has been learned can easily be forgotten without long-term accumulation. Therefore, students should develop reasonable study plans according to their own circumstances and regularly check their progress.

## B · CET-6
人们有时认为，只要能够获得足够的信息，就能作出正确的决定。然而，信息本身并不能保证良好的判断。如果我们只寻找支持自己原有观点的证据，而忽视与之矛盾的信息，即使掌握了大量资料，也可能得出错误的结论。因此，作出合理判断不仅取决于我们知道多少，还取决于我们如何评价所获得的信息。

### Reference
People sometimes assume that having access to sufficient information will enable them to make correct decisions. However, information alone cannot guarantee good judgment. If we look only for evidence that supports our existing views while ignoring information that contradicts them, we may draw incorrect conclusions even if we have a large amount of information. Therefore, sound judgment depends not only on how much we know but also on how we evaluate the information we obtain.

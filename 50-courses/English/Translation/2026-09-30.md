## 1. CET-6 level — campus second-hand book exchange

**一些大学会在学期结束前组织二手教材交换活动，希望降低学生购买教材的成本，也减少仍可使用的书被直接丢弃。这样的活动看起来很简单，但要真正方便学生，需要解决几个实际问题。首先，不同课程使用的版本可能不同，旧版教材即使内容大体相同，也未必适合下一届学生。其次，如果所有人都在最后一周集中交书，工作人员可能没有足够时间检查书籍状态、分类并更新库存。学校还需要说明哪些书可以回收、如何标价，以及无人领取的书最终如何处理。因此，评价活动是否成功，不能只看收到了多少本书，还应观察真正被再次使用的比例、学生平均节省了多少钱，以及活动是否减少了不必要的浪费。**

Translate it into natural English. Try to use **make it easier for**, **be suitable for**, and **the proportion of**.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Some universities organize second-hand textbook exchanges near the end of each semester in order to reduce the cost of buying course materials and prevent usable books from being discarded. Although such programs may appear straightforward, several practical issues need to be addressed if they are to make it genuinely easier for students to reuse books. First, different editions may be assigned in different years, and an older edition may not be suitable for the next group of students even if most of the content is similar. Second, if everyone turns in books during the final week of the semester, staff may not have enough time to inspect their condition, sort them, and update the inventory. The university also needs to state clearly which books will be accepted, how prices are determined, and what happens to books that no one claims. Success should therefore not be judged only by the number of books collected. It should also be evaluated by the proportion that are actually reused, the amount students save on average, and whether the program reduces unnecessary waste.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **organize a second-hand textbook exchange** — 组织二手教材交换活动
- **reduce the cost of buying course materials** — 降低购买教材的成本
- **prevent usable books from being discarded** — 避免仍可使用的书被丢弃
- **make it easier for someone to do something** — 让某人更容易做某事
- **be suitable for the next group of students** — 适合下一届学生
- **inspect the condition of a book** — 检查书籍状态
- **update the inventory** — 更新库存
- **the proportion that are actually reused** — 真正被再次使用的比例
- **be judged only by...** — 仅依据……来评价
- **reduce unnecessary waste** — 减少不必要的浪费

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“二手书”**通常写 **second-hand books / used books**；不要写 *second books*。
2. **“教材版本”**用 **edition**，不是 *version* when referring to a published textbook edition.
3. **“回收书”**在这个语境中更自然的是 **accept / collect used books**；**recycle** 容易让人理解成把书作为废纸处理。
4. **“无人领取”**可写 **remain unclaimed / no one claims them**。
5. **“减少浪费”**用 **reduce waste**；不要机械写成 *decrease the waste phenomenon*。
6. **“成功不能只看……”**可写 **success should not be judged only by...**，比 *cannot only see...* 自然得多。

## 2. TOEFL / academic level — evaluating hybrid learning

**在比较线上课程、线下课程和混合式课程时，仅仅比较三组学生的期末成绩，往往不足以判断哪种教学形式更有效。学生并不总是被随机分配到不同课程中：时间安排更灵活、自我管理能力更强，或本来就更熟悉数字工具的学生，可能更愿意选择线上课程。如果这些差异没有被考虑，成绩差异就可能部分反映学生原本的特点，而不是教学形式本身的效果。与此同时，期末成绩也不能完整代表学习质量。研究者还可以考察课程完成率、知识保留时间、学生参与度以及不同基础学生之间的差距。更可靠的研究应尽可能控制初始差异，明确哪些结果指标最重要，并说明结论适用于什么样的学生和课程。即使某种教学形式在平均成绩上表现更好，也不能由此推出它对每一类学生、每一门课程都更有效。**

Translate it into academic English. Preserve the distinction between **selection effects**, **outcome measures**, and **scope of inference**.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> When online, in-person, and hybrid courses are compared, differences in final grades alone are often insufficient to determine which format is more effective. Students are not always randomly assigned to different types of courses. Those who need greater scheduling flexibility, are better at managing their own time, or are already more comfortable with digital tools may be more likely to choose online classes. If such differences are not taken into account, part of the observed gap in grades may reflect pre-existing student characteristics rather than the effect of the instructional format itself. Final grades also do not capture every dimension of learning quality. Researchers may additionally examine course completion, long-term retention of knowledge, student engagement, and gaps in outcomes between students with different levels of prior preparation. A more reliable study should control for baseline differences where possible, specify which outcomes matter most, and state clearly the populations and course types to which the conclusions apply. Even if one format produces higher average grades, that result does not establish that it is more effective for every kind of student or every subject.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **be randomly assigned to...** — 被随机分配到……
- **be more likely to choose...** — 更可能选择……
- **take differences into account** — 把差异考虑进去
- **pre-existing characteristics** — 原本就存在的特征
- **the effect of X itself** — X 本身的效果
- **capture every dimension of...** — 完整反映……的每个维度
- **course completion / knowledge retention / engagement** — 课程完成 / 知识保留 / 参与度
- **control for baseline differences** — 控制初始差异
- **state the population to which a conclusion applies** — 说明结论适用于什么人群
- **does not establish that...** — 并不能证明……

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“线上课 / 线下课”**常用 **online / in-person courses**；*offline course* 虽能理解，但不如 **in-person** 自然。
2. **“随机分配”**是 **randomly assign**，不是 *randomly distribute students*。
3. **“考虑差异”**用 **take differences into account / account for differences**。
4. **“原本的特点”**可以写 **pre-existing characteristics / baseline characteristics**。
5. **“知识保留”**在教育研究中常用 **retention of knowledge / knowledge retention**，不是 *knowledge keeping*。
6. **“适用于”**常用 **apply to**；结论的适用范围可以写 **scope / generalizability**。
7. **“不能推出”**按学术语境常写 **does not establish / does not imply**，而不是一律写 *cannot prove*。

## 3. Precision rewrite — weaken the claim when the design is weak

Rewrite each sentence so that the wording matches the evidence more carefully.

1. *Online learning causes students to get lower grades.*
2. *Students who choose hybrid courses are more independent.*
3. *Higher completion rates prove that the course is better.*
4. *A result from one introductory course applies to university teaching in general.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Students in the online group received lower grades in this comparison, but the difference may partly reflect pre-existing differences between the groups.
> 2. Students who selected hybrid courses may differ in self-management or scheduling needs, although the comparison does not establish why they chose that format.
> 3. A higher completion rate is one useful outcome, but it does not by itself establish that the course produces better learning.
> 4. A finding from one introductory course may be informative, but its generalizability to other subjects, institutions, or student populations must be tested rather than assumed.

## 4. Micro-drill — translate the relationship, not the Chinese word order

1. 观察到两个变量一起变化，并不自动意味着其中一个导致了另一个。
2. 如果两个组在研究开始前就不同，那么后来的差异可能有多种解释。
3. 一个容易统计的指标未必最能代表我们真正关心的结果。
4. 在把结果推广到其他人群之前，应该先说明样本包含哪些人。

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. The fact that two variables change together does not automatically mean that one causes the other.
> 2. If the groups differed before the study began, later differences may have more than one explanation.
> 3. The outcome that is easiest to measure is not necessarily the one that best represents what we actually care about.
> 4. Before generalizing the result to other populations, researchers should state clearly who was included in the sample.

## 6. One idea, three structures

Express the idea **“观察到的差异可能来自分组方式，而不是干预本身”** in three ways.

### A. Direct
Use **may reflect... rather than...**

### B. Academic
Use **cannot be attributed solely to...**

### C. Methodological
Use **selection into the groups may...**

> [!answer]- Possible answers
> A. The observed difference may reflect how participants entered the groups rather than the intervention itself.
> B. The outcome cannot be attributed solely to the intervention because the groups differed at baseline.
> C. Selection into the groups may account for part of the observed difference in outcomes.

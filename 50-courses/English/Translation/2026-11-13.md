## 练习一｜CET-6：社区智能取件柜试点

**建议时间：12–15 分钟**

某社区计划在三个出入口安装智能取件柜，试运行六个月。支持者认为，集中存放包裹可以减少快递员反复进入小区，也能降低住户错过配送的概率。不过，一些老年住户担心操作流程过于复杂，另一些居民则担心柜机占用公共空间。居委会因此决定保留上门配送选项，并在试点期间每月收集使用率、投诉类型和平均取件时间。只有在多数住户实际受益、设备故障率保持较低且无障碍服务得到改善的情况下，项目才会扩大。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> A neighborhood plans to install smart parcel lockers at three entrances for a six-month trial. Supporters argue that centralized storage could reduce repeated entry by couriers and make residents less likely to miss deliveries. However, some older residents worry that the process may be too complicated, while others are concerned that the lockers will take up shared space. The residents' committee has therefore decided to retain home delivery and to collect monthly data on usage, complaint types, and average pickup time throughout the trial. The project will be expanded only if most residents benefit in practice, the equipment failure rate remains low, and accessibility services improve.

### 结构与搭配

- `install ... at three entrances`：地点是具体入口时用 `at` 很自然。
- `a six-month trial`：作前置定语时 `month` 用单数，并加连字符。
- `make residents less likely to miss deliveries`：比直译成 `reduce the possibility of residents missing` 更自然。
- `take up shared space`：描述占用空间，不要写成 `occupy the public resources`。
- `retain home delivery`：强调试点并未取消原有选项。
- `only if ...`：把扩大的三个必要条件放在同一平行结构中。

### 常见中式英语迁移

1. **“试运行六个月”** 不宜写成 `try to run for six months`；这会变成“尝试运行”。用 `run a six-month trial`。
2. **“错过配送”** 是 `miss a delivery`，不是 `lose the delivery`。
3. **“实际受益”** 用 `benefit in practice`，避免笼统的 `get real benefits`。
4. `data` 在正式学术写作中通常按复数处理，但现代一般写作中单数用法也常见；同一篇保持一致即可。

### 精确改写

把下面过强的结论改得与试点证据相称：

- 原句：`The lockers will solve the neighborhood's delivery problem.`
- 建议：`The trial will test whether the lockers reduce missed deliveries without creating unacceptable access or space problems.`

## 练习二｜TOEFL / Academic：预注册与选择性报告

**建议时间：18–22 分钟**

为了提高研究透明度，一所大学鼓励研究团队在收集数据之前登记主要假设、样本量和分析方法。预注册并不能自动消除偏差，也不能保证研究结论正确；研究人员仍可能遇到设备故障、招募不足或未预见的数据问题。然而，预先说明分析计划，可以帮助读者区分事先提出的检验与观察数据后才形成的探索性分析。评估这项政策时，不应只统计登记项目的数量，还应考察研究团队是否解释了对原计划的偏离，以及这些偏离是否改变了主要结论。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> To improve research transparency, a university encourages research teams to register their primary hypotheses, sample sizes, and analytical methods before collecting data. Preregistration does not automatically eliminate bias or guarantee that a study's conclusions are correct; researchers may still encounter equipment failures, recruitment shortfalls, or unforeseen problems with the data. Nevertheless, specifying an analysis plan in advance can help readers distinguish between tests proposed beforehand and exploratory analyses developed only after the data have been examined. The policy should not be evaluated merely by counting registered projects. Reviewers should also examine whether research teams explain departures from their original plans and whether those departures alter the main conclusions.

### 结构与搭配

- `register ... before collecting data`：主句与时间状语的逻辑主语一致。
- `eliminate bias / guarantee that ...`：`eliminate` 与 `guarantee` 语义强度不同，分别搭配名词和从句。
- `specify an analysis plan in advance`：`specify` 强调明确写出细节。
- `distinguish between A and B`：A、B 保持名词短语平行。
- `departures from the original plan`：学术语境中比 `changes of the plan` 更准确。
- `alter the main conclusions`：表示变化影响结论；若只是轻微调整，可用 `affect`。

### 常见中式英语迁移

1. **“提高透明度”** 是 `improve/increase transparency`，不是 `raise the transparent degree`。
2. **“事先提出”** 可用 `proposed beforehand`，不要堆成 `put forward before in advance`。
3. **“遇到问题”** 用 `encounter problems`；`meet problems` 不自然。
4. 不要把预注册写成因果保证：`Preregistration makes studies unbiased` 既过强，也忽略执行质量。

### 论证强度重写

将结论按强度从高到低排列，并说明哪一句最符合原文：

1. `Preregistration prevents researchers from changing their methods.`
2. `Preregistration can make deviations from an original plan easier to identify.`
3. `Preregistration proves that a study is reliable.`

最符合原文的是 **2**。`can make ... easier` 表示提高可见性，没有声称行为一定不会发生，也没有把程序透明度等同于结论可靠性。

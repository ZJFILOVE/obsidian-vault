## 练习一｜CET-6：图书馆延长开放时间

### 中文原文

某市图书馆准备在考试季延长晚间开放时间。过去，管理人员通常根据进馆人数判断需求，但这一数字可能低估真实使用情况：有些学生会在座位已满时直接离开，还有一些人只使用自习区，不办理借阅。为更合理地安排人员，图书馆计划结合门禁记录、座位抽样和匿名问卷，而不是依赖单一指标。如果试点显示晚间需求稳定，图书馆将优先增加两个工作日的服务，并在三个月后评估成本、噪声投诉和使用者满意度。延长开放并不意味着所有分馆都必须采用同一方案；人口结构、交通条件和现有空间都会影响结果。

### 写作要求

- 130–170 词；
- 使用 **may underestimate**、**rather than rely on**、**if the pilot shows**；
- 明确区分“观察到的进馆人数”和“真实需求”；
- 最后一句不能把局部试点夸大为普遍结论。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> A city library is considering longer evening hours during the examination season. In the past, managers usually estimated demand from the number of people entering the building. That figure, however, may underestimate actual use: some students leave when all seats are occupied, while others use only the study area and borrow no books. To allocate staff more reasonably, the library plans to combine access records, sample counts of occupied seats, and an anonymous survey rather than rely on a single indicator. If the pilot shows stable evening demand, the library will first add service on two weekdays and then evaluate costs, noise complaints, and user satisfaction after three months. Longer hours do not mean that every branch should adopt an identical schedule. Local population patterns, transport conditions, and available space may all influence whether the policy works.

### 结构与搭配

1. **estimate A from B**：根据 B 估计 A。若证据只是 proxy，不要用 determine。
2. **may underestimate actual use**：may 保留不确定性；underestimate 后直接接宾语。
3. **combine A, B, and C rather than rely on D**：rather than 后与主句动词保持平行。
4. **allocate staff / extend opening hours / conduct a pilot**：比 arrange workers、open longer time、do a test 更自然。
5. **whether the policy works**：当结论取决于条件时，用 whether 避免武断的 it will work。

### 常见迁移错误

- ❌ *according to the entering people number*  
  ✅ **based on the number of visitors** / **from entry counts**
- ❌ *some students directly leave*  
  ✅ **some students leave immediately**；副词位置按英语信息节奏调整。
- ❌ *open time*  
  ✅ **opening hours**
- ❌ *all branch libraries must use one plan*  
  ✅ **every branch should adopt the same arrangement**；原文是在反对一刀切，并非讨论法律义务。

### 精确度重写

把下面句子改得更审慎：

> Entry counts prove that students need longer hours.

参考方向：

> Entry counts provide one measure of demand, but they should be interpreted alongside seat use and survey responses.

## 练习二｜TOEFL / Academic：城市树荫与热暴露研究

### 中文原文

研究者希望判断增加街道树荫是否会降低行人的热暴露。他们比较了六个街区在夏季下午的路面温度、空气温度和步行人数，并记录树冠覆盖率。然而，树木较多的街区往往也拥有较宽的人行道、较少的车流和较新的建筑，因此简单相关性不能单独证明树荫造成了全部差异。更可靠的研究可以在同一街区树木修剪前后重复测量，或把相似路段进行匹配，同时记录云量、风速和测量时段。即使温度下降，政策评估仍需考虑维护成本、用水、过敏风险，以及树荫是否真正覆盖居民最常走的路线。

### 写作要求

- 170–220 词；
- 使用 **is associated with**、**cannot by itself establish**、**control for**、**even if**；
- 不要把 observational comparison 写成 randomized experiment；
- 用一句话说明“温度指标改善”与“政策成功”不是同一件事。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> Researchers want to determine whether additional street shade can reduce pedestrians’ exposure to heat. They compared surface temperature, air temperature, and foot traffic in six neighborhoods on summer afternoons and recorded the proportion of each area covered by tree canopy. Greater canopy cover was associated with lower temperatures in some locations. This relationship, however, cannot by itself establish that shade caused the entire difference. Neighborhoods with more trees also tended to have wider sidewalks, lighter traffic, and newer buildings, all of which could influence local conditions. A stronger design could repeat measurements in the same neighborhood before and after scheduled pruning, or match road segments with similar characteristics while controlling for cloud cover, wind speed, and time of measurement. Even if additional shade lowers measured temperatures, a policy evaluation must still consider maintenance costs, water use, allergy risks, and whether the shade reaches the routes residents use most often. An improved temperature indicator is evidence of one benefit, not a complete definition of policy success.

### 结构与搭配

1. **determine whether ...**：研究问题用 whether 引出，避免预设答案。
2. **be associated with**：报告相关性，不偷换为 lead to / result in。
3. **cannot by itself establish that ...**：by itself 把“证据不足”定位在单一比较上，而不是否定研究价值。
4. **match road segments / control for cloud cover**：match 是设计操作，control for 是分析中处理混杂因素。
5. **Even if X, Y must still ...**：承认一项结果成立，同时指出政策判断还有其他维度。

### 常见迁移错误

- ❌ *researchers want to judge whether*  
  ✅ **researchers want to determine / assess whether**；judge 常带个人裁决色彩。
- ❌ *the road temperature*  
  ✅ **surface temperature**；明确测量对象。
- ❌ *newer buildings disturb the result*  
  ✅ **building age may confound the comparison** / **may influence local conditions**。
- ❌ *shade covers the road people most go*  
  ✅ **shade reaches the routes residents use most often**。

### 论证强度阶梯

将同一结果分别写成三个强度：

1. **描述：** Neighborhoods with more canopy had lower afternoon surface temperatures in this sample.
2. **相关：** Greater canopy cover was associated with lower surface temperatures after measured weather conditions were considered.
3. **有限因果：** The before-and-after pattern is consistent with a cooling effect of shade, although concurrent changes cannot be ruled out.

不要跳到：**Trees always solve urban heat.**

## 间隔复习｜明天先译后看

1. 单一指标可能低估需求，因此试点应同时记录使用量、成本和反馈。
2. 即使两个变量相关，这种关系也不能单独确立因果机制。

> [!answer]- 复习参考
> 1. A single indicator may underestimate demand, so the pilot should record use, costs, and feedback together.
> 2. Even if two variables are associated, the relationship cannot by itself establish a causal mechanism.

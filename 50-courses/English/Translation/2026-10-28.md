# Translation — 2026-10-28：把运行规则和因果证据分开

## 使用方法与原创说明

以下中文材料、情境和参考译文均为独立编写，不翻译或改写今日货币文章。六级篇练系统规则、通知与隐私边界；托福／学术篇练干预、比较组、机制与因果限制。两个项目均为虚构教学材料，不对应真实服务或研究。

先独立翻译，再检查四件事：**谁采取行动、规则何时触发、例外是否写清、观察到的差异能支持多强的结论**。折叠答案是一种可行写法，不是唯一答案。

## 练习一：CET-6——校园失物招领平台（15–18 分钟）

一所大学计划把分散在保卫处、图书馆和宿舍前台的失物信息集中到一个线上平台。捡到物品的人可以上传照片和发现地点，但不得展示身份证、银行卡号码或完整联系方式。物主提交物品特征后，由工作人员核对未公开的细节；核对成功并不意味着物品会邮寄，领取人仍需在七天内到指定地点出示有效证件。系统会在到期前发送一次提醒，逾期物品按照现行规定处理。学校将记录认领用时、误领报告和无人领取比例，并邀请没有成功找回物品的学生说明遇到的障碍。平台的目标是减少信息分散，而不是保证每件失物都能找回。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A university plans to bring together lost-and-found information currently held by campus security, the library and residence-hall reception desks on a single online platform. People who find an item may upload a photo and the place where it was found, but they must not display an identity-card number, bank-card details or complete contact information. After an owner describes the item, staff will check details that have not been made public. Successful verification does not mean the item will be mailed; the claimant must still present valid identification at the designated location within seven days. The system will send one reminder before the deadline, after which unclaimed items will be handled under existing rules. The university will record the time required for successful claims, reports of incorrect collection and the proportion of items left unclaimed. It will also invite students who failed to recover an item to explain the barriers they encountered. The platform is intended to reduce fragmented information, not to guarantee that every lost item will be recovered.

### 结构与搭配

- **bring together information held by...**：集中由不同单位保存的信息；不要用 collect departments。
- **make details public / details that have not been made public**：公开信息／未公开细节。
- **present valid identification**：出示有效证件；identification 在此不可数。
- **within seven days**：七天内；若写 by the seventh day，参照点要明确。
- **be handled under existing rules**：按现行规则处理，不擅自补写销毁或捐赠。
- **the proportion of items left unclaimed**：无人领取物品的比例；注意 proportion 后接 of。
- **be intended to, not to**：准确保留“目标是……而不是保证……”。

### 常见中式英语与精度修正

1. “误领报告”是 **reports of incorrect collection / mistaken claims**，不是 false reports，后者容易变成虚假举报。
2. “核对成功”用 **successful verification**，不要擅自强化为 proof of legal ownership。
3. “邀请失败者说明障碍”不是 blame users；可写 **invite users to describe the barriers they encountered**。
4. 把 *The platform will solve the lost-item problem* 改为：  
   **The platform may reduce information gaps, but recovery will still depend on reporting, verification and timely collection.**

## 练习二：TOEFL／academic——街道树荫与夏季步行（20–25 分钟）

某城市拟评估在公交站周围增加树荫是否会提高夏季步行量。研究团队将选择十个即将种树的站点，并为每个站点匹配一个道路宽度、周边人口和公交班次相近但暂不种树的站点。研究者将在改造前后记录正午地表温度、候车人数和步行经过路口的人数，并在天气相似的工作日重复测量。访谈可帮助解释人们是否因为更凉爽、视觉环境改善或其他原因改变路线。然而，即使种树站点的步行量增长更快，也不能立即把全部差异归因于树荫：同期店铺开业、道路施工或公交时刻变化都可能影响结果。团队应事先说明匹配标准，报告树木成活与冠幅变化，并检验效果是否只出现在最热的时段。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A city plans to evaluate whether adding tree shade around bus stops increases walking during the summer. The research team will select ten stops scheduled for tree planting and match each of them with a stop that has a similar road width, surrounding population and bus frequency but is not yet scheduled for planting. Researchers will record midday surface temperature, the number of people waiting for buses and the number of pedestrians crossing the intersection before and after the intervention. Measurements will be repeated on weekdays with similar weather conditions. Interviews may help explain whether people changed their routes because of cooler conditions, an improved visual environment or other reasons. Even if walking increases more quickly at the planting sites, however, the entire difference cannot immediately be attributed to shade: new shops, road works or changes to bus timetables during the same period may also affect the outcome. The team should specify its matching criteria in advance, report tree survival and changes in canopy width, and test whether the effect appears only during the hottest hours.

### 结构与学术搭配

- **evaluate whether A increases B**：检验是否提高，不预设结果。
- **be scheduled for tree planting**：计划种树；比 will have trees 更精确。
- **match A with B on / by...**：按可比较特征进行匹配。
- **before and after the intervention**：明确时间比较；intervention 不等于实验一定随机。
- **attribute the difference to**：把差异归因于；不能写 contribute the difference to。
- **during the same period**：同期发生，用来提示时间变化混杂。
- **specify criteria in advance**：事先说明标准，减少看完结果后调整规则。

### 因果边界检查

1. 匹配站点提高可比性，但不是随机分配；未测量差异仍可能存在。
2. 地表温度、候车人数和路口步行量是不同结果变量，不能互相替代。
3. 访谈可提出机制解释，却不能单独证明路线变化由树荫造成。
4. 树木需要时间形成冠幅；“种下”不能自动等同于“已有有效树荫”。

## 精度改写

### A. 把绝对承诺降到可验证范围

原句：**The new platform will ensure that students get all lost property back.**

改写要求：保留平台能做的事，也说明无法保证的结果。

> [!answer]- 参考改写
> The platform is expected to make reports easier to find, but it cannot guarantee recovery because success also depends on whether an item is reported, verified and collected on time.

### B. 把相关变化改成谨慎因果表达

原句：**The trees caused more people to walk.**

改写要求：加入比较结果和至少一个替代解释。

> [!answer]- 参考改写
> Walking increased more at the planting sites than at the matched sites, a pattern consistent with a shading effect; however, concurrent changes in shops, construction or bus service would need to be examined before the difference was attributed to the trees.

## 中英迁移提醒

- 中文常省略规则执行者；英文程序说明要补出 **staff / the system / the claimant / the research team**。
- “提高了”不总是 **improved**。数量增加用 **increase**，条件变好用 **improve**。
- “影响因素很多”不要停在 **many factors**；至少命名两项，并说明它们影响哪个结果。
- “证明”在观察性研究中常应降为 **suggest / be consistent with / support**。
- **not yet scheduled** 不等于永远不做，准确保留对照站点的时间状态。

## 间隔复习句

1. 新系统可以减少信息分散，但不能保证每一次申请都能通过核验。
2. 即使干预站点的变化更大，研究者仍应检查同期事件和未测量差异。

> [!answer]- 间隔复习参考
> 1. The new system may reduce fragmented information, but it cannot guarantee that every claim will pass verification.
> 2. Even if the intervention sites show a larger change, researchers should still examine concurrent events and unmeasured differences.

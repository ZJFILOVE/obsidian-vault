# Translation — Day 10

## A · CET-4+
随着在线课程越来越普及，大学生拥有了更多自主安排学习时间的机会。与传统课堂相比，在线学习更加灵活，也使学生能够接触到来自不同地区的优质教育资源。然而，这种便利也要求学生具有更强的自我管理能力。如果缺乏明确的学习计划，他们很容易受到其他事情的干扰。

### Reference
As online courses have become increasingly popular, university students have gained more opportunities to manage their own study time. Compared with traditional classes, online learning offers greater flexibility and enables students to access high-quality educational resources from different regions. However, this convenience also requires students to develop stronger self-management skills. Without a clear study plan, they can easily be distracted by other activities.

## B · CET-6
科技的发展使人们能够比过去更快地完成许多任务，但效率的提高并不一定意味着人们拥有了更多的空闲时间。事实上，当完成工作的速度加快时，人们有时会承担更多任务。因此，真正重要的可能不是我们能够完成多少事情，而是我们能否合理地决定哪些事情值得投入时间和注意力。

### Reference
Technological development has enabled people to complete many tasks more quickly than in the past, but increased efficiency does not necessarily mean that people have more free time. In fact, when work can be completed more quickly, people sometimes take on additional tasks. Therefore, what really matters may not be how many things we can accomplish, but whether we can make reasonable decisions about which activities deserve our time and attention.

# Translation — 2026-10-19：先把比较的单位说清楚

[[50-courses/English/English|English]] · [[50-courses/English/Daily/2026-10-19|今日材料]] · [[50-courses/English/Translation/2026-10-19|翻译]] · [[50-courses/English/Vocabulary/2026-10-19|词汇]]

## 使用方法与原创说明

以下两段中文、情境和参考译文均为独立编写的教学练习，不翻译、不概写今天四个人的工作陈述，也不是对真实校园工程或研究的报道。第一篇练问题、改动与评价标准；第二篇练研究方法、测量对象与结论范围。先独立写，再展开答案；参考译文不是唯一标准。

批改顺序：**信息有没有丢 → 主语和时间是否清楚 → 比较是否同类 → 搭配是否自然**。长句不是必需品，清楚的两句胜过结构失控的一句。

## 练习一：CET-6——自行车停放区的改造（15–18 分钟）

一所大学计划改造教学楼旁的自行车停放区。最初的方案是在原有空地上增加更多车架，但学生调查发现，问题并不只是车位不足。一些车架离出入口太近，停满以后会妨碍行人通行；另一些车架虽然有空位，却因为夜间照明不足而很少被使用。负责人员因此决定先调整布局，补充照明，再根据试行结果决定是否增加车架。新方案还为不同尺寸的自行车预留了空间，并用清楚的标线区分通道和停放区域。项目结束后，学校不应只比较改造前后的车位总数，还应在相同的上课时段记录通道是否畅通，以及空置车位分布在哪里。增加设施数量可以是手段，但不能自动等同于改善使用体验。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A university plans to redesign the bicycle parking area beside a teaching building. The initial proposal was to install more racks in the existing space, but a student survey found that the problem was not simply a shortage of parking spaces. Some racks were too close to the entrances and exits and obstructed pedestrians when full. Others were rarely used, despite having spaces available, because they were poorly lit at night. The staff responsible therefore decided to rearrange the area and improve the lighting before using the trial results to determine whether additional racks were needed. The revised plan also left room for bicycles of different sizes and used clear markings to separate access routes from parking areas. When the project is completed, the university should not merely compare the total number of spaces before and after the redesign. It should also check whether the access routes remain clear and where unused spaces are located, making these observations during comparable class periods. Installing more facilities may be a means to an end, but it does not automatically amount to improving the user experience.

### 结构与搭配

1. **redesign the area / install racks**：改造整体空间与安装某类设施，动作对象不同。不要把所有“改造”都译成 rebuild，后者容易暗示拆建。
2. **a shortage of parking spaces**：shortage 是不足，后接 of；不写 lack of enough spaces，因为不足意义重复。也可以简洁地说 too few spaces。
3. **when full / despite having spaces available**：前者补条件，后者表示让步。两处省略结构的主语都应能清楚指向车架，不让读者误以为“行人满了”。
4. **before using … to determine whether …**：先做什么，再据此判断什么。whether 引导“是否”，不要把评估写成已经决定扩建。
5. **separate A from B**：区分通道与停放区域；不要写 separate A with B。
6. **during comparable class periods**：比较的是相近的使用情境，不是随便挑两个时间点。comparable 不表示两次观测各方面完全相同。
7. **amount to + 名词／动名词**：等同于某种结果。这里不是 amount of 的数量结构，后面不接 to improve。

### 中式英语修正

- “有空位”可以用 spaces are available，不写 there have empty places。
- “妨碍行人”用 obstruct pedestrians 或 block pedestrian access；不要硬写 influence people walk。
- “根据试行结果”可用 on the basis of the trial results，复习昨天的介词结构。
- “使用体验”在此用 the user experience 或 people's experience of using the area，不自动换成 life quality。

### 精度改写

把 **The redesign was successful because it created more parking spaces.** 改成一句不把“数量”当作唯一标准的判断。

> [!answer]- 一个可行版本
> The number of spaces is one relevant measure, but the redesign should also be assessed in terms of access and how effectively the available spaces are used.

## 练习二：TOEFL／学术——树荫与街道温度（18–22 分钟）

一个研究小组希望了解街道树荫与行人所处环境温度之间的关系。他们在同一城区选择了若干有树荫和无树荫的测量点，并在连续几天的固定时段进行观测。为了使数据更容易比较，所有传感器都安装在相同高度，研究人员还记录了路面材料、周围建筑和交通情况。这个虚构研究的初步分析显示，部分有树荫的测量点温度较低，但不能仅凭这一结果计算整个城区植树后的降温幅度。不同地点原本就可能存在差异，而且传感器读数不能直接代表每一位行人的热舒适程度。后续研究应增加不同季节的观测，并明确区分空气温度、表面温度与主观舒适度。如果这些指标被混在一起，一个看起来精确的平均值也可能回答了错误的问题。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A research team aims to investigate the relationship between street-tree shade and the temperature of the environment experienced by pedestrians. The researchers selected several shaded and unshaded measurement sites within the same urban district and collected observations at fixed times over several consecutive days. To make the data more comparable, all sensors were installed at the same height. The team also recorded information about paving materials, surrounding buildings and traffic conditions. In this hypothetical study, preliminary analysis showed lower temperatures at some of the shaded sites. This finding alone, however, cannot be used to estimate how much an entire district would cool after trees were planted. The sites may have differed in other ways from the outset, and sensor readings cannot directly represent every pedestrian's thermal comfort. Further research should include observations in different seasons and clearly distinguish air temperature, surface temperature and subjective comfort. If these measures are conflated, even an apparently precise average may answer the wrong question.

### 信息结构与学术搭配

- **investigate the relationship between A and B**：比较关系，不提前写成 prove that A causes B。原文没有承诺确定因果，翻译也不能替它承诺。
- **shaded and unshaded sites**：用平行形容词限定同类对象。不能一边用树木数量，另一边用温度，直接当成两组地点的对称名称。
- **at fixed times over several consecutive days**：at 说明时点，over 说明观察跨越的时段。consecutive 表示连续，不等于每天无限连续地测量。
- **make the data more comparable**：提高可比性，不代表排除了所有差异。more 不能漏；否则语气更绝对。
- **from the outset**：从一开始。原有差异不是观测之后新产生的影响。
- **distinguish air temperature, surface temperature and subjective comfort**：明确三个指标，不把“温度”与“感觉舒服”当成同义替换。
- **conflate**：把本应区分的东西混为一谈；可用 conflate A with B。它比 compare 更进一步，不要把“比较两者”误译成“混淆两者”。

### 常见迁移错误

1. “在相同高度”用 at the same height，不写 in the same high。height 是名词，high 是形容词或副词。
2. “部分地点较低”不能扩成 all shaded streets were cooler。保留 some 和比较对象。
3. “读数不能直接代表感受”不等于读数无用；不要增加 The sensors were unreliable 这种原文没有的设备判断。
4. “植树后的幅度”是待估计的改变，不能用已观测的两组均值之差直接冒充。译文保持 cannot be used … alone 的边界。

## 精确改写：三个不同的问题

修正下面三句，每句先指出它混淆了什么，再写自己的版本。

1. All bicycles have a parking space, so pedestrians can move freely.
2. The sensor recorded a lower temperature, so every visitor felt more comfortable.
3. Taking measurements at the same height removed every difference between the sites.

> [!answer]- 改写参考
> 1. Providing enough spaces does not by itself ensure that pedestrian access remains clear. 车位数量与通行条件不同。
> 2. A lower sensor reading does not directly establish how every visitor experienced the conditions. 仪器读数与主观体验不同。
> 3. Installing sensors at the same height improves comparability, but differences between the sites may remain. 部分标准化与消除全部差异不同。

## 间隔复习：把周末句型迁移到新对象

1. 我们应依据试行期间的观察作决定，同时为其他解释保留余地。
2. 我喜欢这份指南的地方，在于它把测量值和个人感受区分开了。

> [!answer]- 复习参考
> 1. We should make a decision on the basis of observations from the trial while leaving room for alternative explanations.
> 2. What I like about this guide is that it distinguishes measured values from personal experiences.

## 批改清单

- [ ] 两篇都先独立写，练习中的研究没有当成真实成果传播。
- [ ] whether、some、more 等范围与程度信息没有丢。
- [ ] 比较两边采用相同的量或明确说明量不同。
- [ ] 检查过一个名词／形容词错误和一个介词搭配。

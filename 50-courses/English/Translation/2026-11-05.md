## 练习一：CET-6——减少夜间无效通知（15–18 分钟）

某学院准备调整课程平台的夜间通知设置。晚上十点到次日早上七点之间，系统默认只推送与次日课程取消、教室临时变更和校园安全有关的紧急信息。作业提醒、讨论区回复和普通公告仍会保留在平台中，但要到静默时段结束后才集中发送。教师若认为一条消息必须立即送达，需要选择紧急类别并写明理由；学生也可以为特定课程自行关闭静默模式。学院将在试行六周后比较夜间通知数量、学生打开消息的时间和漏看重要信息的报告。该措施的目标不是阻止师生在夜间使用平台，而是减少无需立即打断他人的提醒。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A college plans to adjust the night-time notification settings on its course platform. Between 10 p.m. and 7 a.m., the system will by default send only urgent messages concerning next-day class cancellations, last-minute room changes and campus safety. Assignment reminders, replies in discussion forums and routine announcements will remain available on the platform, but they will be delivered together after the quiet period ends. If an instructor believes that a message must be delivered immediately, they will need to select an emergency category and state the reason. Students may also turn off quiet mode for a particular course. After a six-week trial, the college will compare the number of night-time notifications, the time at which students open messages and reports of missed important information. The aim is not to prevent teachers and students from using the platform at night, but to reduce alerts that do not require an immediate interruption.

### 结构与搭配

- **by default**：默认情况下，常放在谓语前后。
- **concerning / related to**：与……有关；比 about 更适合规则文本。
- **remain available**：仍可访问；没有推送不等于内容消失。
- **after the quiet period ends**：静默时段结束后，明确时间边界。
- **state the reason**：说明理由；不用 write the reason clearly unless。
- **turn off quiet mode for a particular course**：对特定课程关闭静默模式。
- **after a six-week trial**：试行六周后；trial 在这里是试点，不是审判。
- **not to ..., but to ...**：准确表达“目标不是……而是……”。

### 常见中英迁移错误

1. “到早上七点之间”用 **between 10 p.m. and 7 a.m.**，不要混写 from ... between。
2. “集中发送”可写 **be delivered together / in a batch**，不是 concentratedly send。
3. “漏看信息的报告”是 **reports of missed information**，不是 missing-information reports（易歧义为信息本身缺失）。
4. “关闭静默模式”是允许即时通知；不要译成 close the silence。
5. 最后一句不要把“减少打断”夸大成“禁止夜间发消息”。

### 精确改写

把下面两句合并为一句，保留例外：

- Routine alerts will be delayed.
- Students can opt out for individual courses.

可参考结构：

> Although routine alerts will be delayed by default, students can opt out of the setting for individual courses.

## 练习二：TOEFL／academic——阴性结果与选择性报告（20–25 分钟）

一个研究团队分析了某领域五年内登记的二百项小型实验。登记记录通常包含最初的主要指标和预计样本量，因此研究者可以比较研究开始前的计划与后来公开的论文。结果显示，报告显著差异的实验更可能在两年内发表；未发现显著差异的实验则更常只出现在会议摘要中，或没有公开完整结果。不过，这种关联不能单独证明期刊拒绝了阴性结果，因为研究者也可能因为样本不足、测量失败或研究问题发生变化而停止写作。为了区分这些解释，后续调查需要记录投稿、审稿和作者停止项目的具体阶段。无论原因是什么，若未发表的实验从综述中消失，读者就可能高估某种干预效果的一致性。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A research team analysed two hundred small experiments registered in a particular field over a five-year period. Registration records usually included the original primary outcome and planned sample size, allowing the researchers to compare plans made before data collection with papers released later. The analysis found that experiments reporting statistically significant differences were more likely to be published within two years. Experiments that found no significant difference, by contrast, more often appeared only in conference abstracts or did not make their full results public. This association alone, however, does not prove that journals rejected negative results, because investigators may also have stopped writing after an inadequate sample, a measurement failure or a change in the research question. To distinguish among these explanations, a follow-up study would need to record the specific stage at which submission, review or the project itself ended. Whatever the cause, if unpublished experiments disappear from reviews, readers may overestimate how consistently an intervention works.

### 结构与搭配

- **over a five-year period**：在五年期间；描述观察窗口。
- **the original primary outcome**：最初设定的主要指标。
- **allowing ... to compare ...**：现在分词补充结果或功能，逻辑主语要清楚。
- **be more likely to be published**：更可能发表；不要误写 more possible。
- **by contrast**：引出对照，不自动表示因果。
- **this association alone does not prove ...**：仅凭该关联不能证明……
- **distinguish among explanations**：区分多个解释；两个可用 between。
- **the stage at which ... ended**：……停止的具体阶段。
- **overestimate how consistently ... works**：高估效果的一致性。

### 常见中英迁移错误

1. “登记”在研究语境中是 **registration / preregistration**，不是普通的 sign up。
2. “显著差异”若语境指统计检验，应写 **statistically significant difference**；不要把 significant 一律理解为“重要”。
3. “阴性结果”是 **negative or null results**，并不等于“错误结果”。
4. **not published** 不能自动改写成 **rejected by a journal**；后者加入了原文没有的原因。
5. “无论原因是什么”用 **whatever the cause**，后面的结论仍应保持 may，而不是必然 will。
6. review 在这里是文献综述，不是审稿；需要用上下文消除歧义。

### 结论强度阶梯

按从弱到强排序，并说明哪一级与原文匹配：

1. The pattern is consistent with selective reporting.
2. The pattern is associated with publication status.
3. The pattern proves that journals suppress negative findings.

原文支持 1 和 2；不支持 3。翻译时若把 **更可能发表** 写成 **期刊偏好并拒绝其他结果**，就把观察性关联误译成了确定机制。

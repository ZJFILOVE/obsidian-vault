## 练习一｜CET-6：校园维修角

### 中译英

一所大学在图书馆旁边设立了每周开放一次的维修角，学生可以带来损坏的小家电、衣物或自行车。活动并不承诺所有物品都能修好；志愿者会先判断问题是否安全、是否有合适的零件，以及维修需要多长时间。如果修理成本高于替换成本，负责人会向物主解释原因，而不是为了追求“零废弃”而勉强修复。项目的价值不仅在于减少垃圾，还在于让学生了解物品的使用寿命，并学会在购买前考虑维护成本。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> A university has set up a repair corner next to its library that opens once a week, where students can bring in damaged small appliances, clothing, or bicycles. The programme does not promise that every item can be fixed. Volunteers first assess whether the problem can be addressed safely, whether suitable parts are available, and how much time the repair would take. If the cost of repairing an item exceeds the cost of replacing it, the coordinators explain why rather than forcing a repair in pursuit of a zero-waste ideal. The value of the programme lies not only in reducing waste but also in helping students understand the useful life of everyday objects and factor maintenance costs into their purchasing decisions.

### 结构与搭配

- **set up a repair corner**：set up 表示建立并安排运行；只说 put a corner 容易变成“放一个角落”。
- **bring in damaged items**：bring in 强调把物品带到某个服务点；bring 通常也能表达，但 bring in 更贴近维修场景。
- **assess whether ...**：先评估是否可行，再决定是否行动。whether 后面可以并列多个判断条件。
- **suitable parts are available**：available 表示零件是否能够取得；不要误写成零件“很合适”。
- **the cost of repairing ... exceeds the cost of replacing ...**：用两个平行的名词结构比较成本，逻辑更清楚。
- **factor maintenance costs into a decision**：把维护成本纳入决策，而不是简单说 think about costs。

### 常见中式迁移错误

1. “维修角”不是 *repair place corner*；用 **repair corner / repair station** 即可。
2. “修理成本高于替换成本”不要写成 *repairing cost is higher than replacement*。比较双方最好保持名词结构平行：**the cost of repairing A exceeds the cost of replacing it**。
3. “并不承诺所有物品都能修好”不等于 *all items cannot be repaired*。原句说的是不作保证，不是断言所有物品都不能修。
4. “为了追求……”可以写 **in pursuit of ...**；不要把“为了”一律译成 *for to*。

### 精确改写

把下面这句改得更具体，明确志愿者先检查安全性和零件：

**Volunteers repair things if they can.**

> [!answer]- 一种更精确的改写
> Volunteers first assess whether an item can be repaired safely and whether the necessary parts are available before deciding to take on the job.

## 练习二｜TOEFL / Academic：混合式课程调查

### 中译英

一项关于混合式课程的校级调查显示，选择线上参与的学生提交作业的时间更灵活，但他们在课堂讨论中的发言次数较少。研究者不应仅凭这一差异断言线上学习削弱了参与度，因为课程类型、网络条件和学生是否同时承担照护责任都可能影响选择。后续研究可以比较同一批学生在不同课程中的表现，并记录他们实际参与讨论的方式。只有在控制这些因素后，才能判断教学形式本身是否造成了稳定差异。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> A university-wide survey of hybrid courses found that students who chose to participate online had greater flexibility in when they submitted assignments, but they made fewer contributions to in-class discussions. Researchers should not conclude solely on the basis of this difference that online learning reduces student engagement, because course type, internet access, and whether students also have caregiving responsibilities may all influence that choice. A follow-up study could compare the same students across different courses and record how they actually take part in discussions. Only after these factors have been controlled for can researchers determine whether the mode of instruction itself produces a consistent difference.

### 结构与搭配

- **a university-wide survey of ...**：university-wide 修饰调查范围；不要写成 *a survey in all university*。
- **make contributions to a discussion**：比直译 *say words in the discussion* 更自然；contribution 可以指发言、问题或回应。
- **conclude solely on the basis of ...**：明确指出证据不足以支撑某个结论。
- **influence that choice**：that 指前面的线上参与选择；代词指代要清楚。
- **compare the same students across different courses**：同一批学生跨课程比较，有助于减少个体差异带来的干扰。
- **control for a factor**：在分析中控制某个因素；这里不是“控制学生”，而是统计上处理混杂因素。
- **the mode of instruction itself**：把“教学形式本身”与课程、网络和照护责任等因素区分开。

### 常见中式迁移错误

1. “发言次数较少”不是 *the number of speaking is less*；可写 **made fewer contributions** 或 **spoke less frequently**。
2. “仅凭这一差异”要保留证据边界：**solely on the basis of this difference**，而不是 *because of this difference*。
3. “控制这些因素”在研究设计中通常写 **control for these factors**，不是 *control these factors*。
4. “稳定差异”可译为 **a consistent difference**；不要用 *stable difference* 造成不自然的搭配。

### 论证强度训练

请把下列三个结论按证据强度从弱到强排列，并说明原文最支持哪一个：

1. Online learning reduces student engagement.
2. Online participants made fewer contributions in this survey.
3. The survey found fewer in-class contributions among students who chose online participation, although several factors may explain the difference.

> [!answer]- 判断依据
> 第 2 句最接近单一观测结果；第 3 句补上了研究情境和解释限制，因此最忠实于完整材料。第 1 句把相关观察直接升级成因果结论，超出了题目提供的证据。

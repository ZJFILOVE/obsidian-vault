## 用法与原创范围

下面两个情境及中文段落均为独立编写的教学练习，不翻译、不概写今天的共享经济短文，也不是对真实项目或研究的报道。第一篇练六级叙述与让步，第二篇练托福／学术语境中的研究设计、指标和限制。参考译文不是唯一答案。

先遮住折叠答案，完成自己的版本；批改时先看逻辑和信息是否保留，再看词汇是否自然。不要为了“学术”把每个动词都换成抽象名词。

## 练习一：CET-6——旧厂房里的社区剧场（15–18 分钟）

一家社区剧场由旧厂房改建而成，希望吸引从未看过现场演出的居民。负责人起初认为，只要票价足够低，大家就会愿意参加。然而，走访发现，一些居民不知道演出几点结束，担心回家太晚；另一些人则不确定是否可以带孩子进入剧场。于是，剧场没有急着增加演出数量，而是在购票页面说明结束时间、交通方式和适合的观众年龄，并安排工作人员回答初次观众的问题。新的做法并不意味着所有顾虑都能得到解决，但它承认，第一次走进剧场需要的不只是便宜的票。试行结束后，负责人打算分别了解新观众和老观众的体验，而不是只用售票总数判断项目是否成功。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6
> A community theatre converted from a former factory hopes to attract residents who have never attended a live performance. Its organisers initially assumed that people would be willing to come as long as tickets were cheap enough. However, interviews revealed that some residents did not know when performances would end and were worried about getting home late, while others were unsure whether they could bring their children. Instead of rushing to put on more performances, the theatre therefore added information about finishing times, transport options and recommended audience ages to its booking page. Staff were also assigned to answer questions from first-time visitors. These changes do not mean that every concern can be resolved, but they acknowledge that attending a theatre for the first time requires more than an inexpensive ticket. At the end of the trial, the organisers plan to examine the experiences of new and returning audience members separately, rather than judge the programme solely by total ticket sales.

### 结构拆解

1. **a theatre converted from a former factory**：过去分词作后置定语，theatre 是“被改建成的剧场”，不是剧场主动改建别的东西。也可写 a former factory that has been converted into a theatre，但转换方向要一致。
2. **assumed that … as long as …**：把“最初假设”和假设成立的条件放在同一句。as long as 表示条件；as soon as 表示时间，不能互换。
3. **some … while others …**：写两类人的不同顾虑。这里的 while 是对比，不是“当……的时候”。
4. **Staff were assigned to …**：原文不强调具体安排者时，可以使用被动。也可主动写 The organisers assigned staff to …。
5. **do not mean that every concern can be resolved**：否定“全部都能解决”，不是说“没有任何顾虑能解决”。all/every 与否定词的位置要特别小心。
6. **new and returning audience members**：returning 表示再次来，不是“正在回家”；不要直译成 old audiences 而使“老观众”带上年龄含义。

### 中式英语修正

- “看现场演出”用 attend a live performance，不写 look a performance。
- “票价足够低”可写 tickets are cheap enough；enough 在形容词后。不要写 enough cheap。
- “说明结束时间”用 provide information about when performances end，不能 explain the time 对应所有语境。
- “担心回家太晚”用 be worried about getting home late；be worried 后的介词不能随意改成 for。

### 小修订：把语气改准确

把“Low prices bring everyone to the theatre”改成一句含条件或范围的表达，再补一个低价之外的因素。

> [!answer]- 一个可行版本
> Lower prices may encourage some residents to attend, but clear information about the visit can also matter to first-time audience members.

## 练习二：TOEFL／学术——复习方式与延迟测验（18–22 分钟）

一个研究小组希望比较两种复习方式：反复阅读笔记，以及在不看笔记的情况下写出自己记得的内容。为了减少学习时间差异的影响，研究人员为所有参与者安排了相同长度的复习时段，并将他们随机分配到两个组。测验既包括当天的记忆题，也包括一周后需要把所学概念应用于新情境的问题。练习情境中的初步结果显示，两组在当天测验中的差距较小，但在一周后的部分题目中表现不同。不过，研究者不能据此断定某种方法对每个人、每门课程都更有效。参与者均为自愿报名的大学生，而且部分人此前已经熟悉材料。后续研究应记录先前知识，并将记忆保持与迁移表现分开评价。只有说明比较了什么、在什么条件下比较，研究结论才有可能被合理地应用到教学中。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic
> A research team aims to compare two revision strategies: repeatedly reading notes and writing down what learners can recall without consulting them. To reduce the influence of differences in study time, the researchers gave all participants revision sessions of equal length and randomly assigned them to two groups. The assessments included both memory questions administered on the same day and questions a week later that required learners to apply the concepts to unfamiliar situations. In this hypothetical study, preliminary results indicate that the groups differed little on the immediate test but performed differently on some of the delayed questions. However, the researchers cannot conclude from this that either method is more effective for every learner or in every course. All participants were university students who had volunteered for the study, and some were already familiar with the material. Further research should record prior knowledge and assess retention separately from transfer performance. Research findings can be applied sensibly to teaching only when the basis and conditions of the comparison are made clear.

### 学术表达与信息精度

- **compare two strategies: A and B**：冒号后两项形式保持平行。这里都用 -ing 形式，不写 repeatedly reading … and to write …。
- **without consulting …**：不查阅某材料。consult a source 是及物用法，不需要在 consult 后再加 to。
- **sessions of equal length**：相同的是学习时段长度，不自动意味着所有人投入程度完全相同。翻译不要替作者多做一个控制变量。
- **randomly assign participants to groups**：assign 是分配；select participants 是选择样本。随机分组不等于随机抽样。
- **differed little / performed differently on some …**：保留“较小”“部分”的范围；不能擅自改成没有差异或全面领先。
- **retention / transfer performance**：保持记忆与迁移到新情境是不同的评价维度，不是两个更高级的“分数”同义词。
- **only when …**：参考译文把必要条件放在后面。如果改到句首，应写 Only when the conditions are clear **can the findings be applied** …，注意部分倒装。

### 常见迁移错误

1. “一周后测验”可写 a test a week later；after a week later 重复。
2. “熟悉材料”用 be familiar with the material，不写 familiar to the material。The material is familiar to them 是另一种主语安排。
3. “根据结果得出结论”用 conclude from the results；不要把一个差异直接写成 proves the method works for all students。
4. 研究者“不能得出普遍结论”不等于“研究毫无用处”。翻译限制条件时，不要额外加入否定研究价值的评价。

## 精确改写：先找信息损失

1. The new theatre policy removed all barriers.
2. The experiment proved that one method was better.
3. Random assignment means the sample represents all students.

> [!answer]- 改写示例与理由
> 1. The changes addressed some practical concerns, although other barriers may remain. 保留“部分”，不声称障碍全部消失。
> 2. In the hypothetical study, the groups performed differently on some delayed questions; this does not establish a universal advantage. 保留时间、题目范围与练习情境。
> 3. Random assignment can improve comparability between groups, but it does not by itself make the sample representative of all students. 分组与抽样分开。

## 间隔复习：回忆昨天的两个句型

1. 目前的数据与这一解释相容，但还不能排除另一种可能性。
2. 初步结果提示可能存在联系，而不是已经证实因果。

> [!answer]- 复习参考
> 1. The current data are consistent with this explanation, but they do not yet rule out an alternative.
> 2. Preliminary findings suggest a possible association rather than an established causal relationship.

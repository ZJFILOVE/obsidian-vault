# Translation — Day 4

## Chinese → English
大学教育不仅是为了让学生获得专业知识，也应该培养他们独立解决问题的能力。随着社会的快速发展，人们在工作中可能会遇到许多以前没有接触过的问题。因此，仅仅记住书本上的知识是不够的，学生还需要学会分析信息并作出合理的判断。

## Reference
University education is not only intended to help students acquire professional knowledge but also to develop their ability to solve problems independently. With the rapid development of society, people may encounter many problems at work that they have never dealt with before. Therefore, simply memorizing knowledge from textbooks is not enough. Students also need to learn how to analyze information and make reasonable judgments.

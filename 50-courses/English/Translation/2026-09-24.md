## 1. CET-6 level — making a campus second-hand exchange actually useful

先判断逻辑：**需求存在 → 平台看似简单 → 低使用率的原因 → 具体改进 → 效果取决于执行细节**。

**不少大学都会建立二手物品交流群或线上平台，希望学生能够转卖教材、小家电和宿舍用品。这样的做法看起来既省钱又能减少浪费，但平台真正能否长期运行，并不只取决于有没有人发布商品。如果信息分类混乱、卖家长时间不回复，或者交易地点和时间很难协调，学生即使看到合适的物品，也可能放弃联系。相比单纯增加发布数量，更重要的是降低交易过程中的时间成本和不确定性。例如，平台可以要求卖家标注物品状态、价格、可交易时间和大致地点，并在物品售出后及时更新状态。学校还可以在毕业季设置固定的线下交换点，让不方便单独约时间的学生集中完成交易。这些措施未必能让所有闲置物品都被重新利用，但可以提高有效信息的比例，并减少双方反复确认细节所花的时间。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Many universities set up second-hand exchange groups or online platforms so that students can resell textbooks, small appliances, and dormitory supplies. In principle, such systems can save money and reduce waste, but their long-term usefulness depends on more than the number of items posted. If listings are poorly organized, sellers take a long time to respond, or the time and location of a transaction are difficult to coordinate, students may give up even after finding something they want. Rather than simply increasing the number of listings, it is more important to reduce the time cost and uncertainty involved in completing a transaction. For example, sellers can be required to state an item's condition, price, available pickup times, and approximate location, and to update the listing promptly once the item has been sold. Universities can also set up fixed exchange points during graduation season so that students who cannot easily arrange individual meetings can complete transactions in one place. Such measures may not ensure that every unused item finds a new owner, but they can increase the proportion of useful listings and reduce the time both sides spend confirming basic details.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **set up a second-hand exchange platform** — 建立二手交换平台
- **resell textbooks / appliances** — 转卖教材 / 小家电
- **reduce waste** — 减少浪费
- **depend on more than...** — 不只取决于……
- **be poorly organized** — 分类 / 组织混乱
- **coordinate a time and location** — 协调时间和地点
- **give up even after...** — 即使……也可能放弃
- **reduce the time cost involved in...** — 降低……过程中的时间成本
- **state an item's condition** — 标明物品状态
- **update a listing promptly** — 及时更新商品信息
- **complete a transaction** — 完成交易
- **increase the proportion of useful listings** — 提高有效信息的比例

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“二手物品”**通常写 **second-hand items / used items**。不要写 *second items*。
2. **“闲置物品”**可根据语境写 **unused items / items no longer needed**，而不是机械翻译成 *idle goods*。
3. **“信息分类混乱”**不要写 *information classification is chaotic*。更自然的是 **listings are poorly organized / categories are unclear**。
4. **“协调交易时间”**常用 **coordinate / arrange a time for the transaction**；*negotiate a time* 通常不是首选。
5. **“提高有效信息比例”**可写 **increase the proportion of useful / active listings**，不要硬译成 *raise the rate of effective information*。
6. **“未必能让所有……”**是很常见的让步结构：**may not ensure that every...**, **will not necessarily make every...**。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level — evaluating open-plan offices

先判断逻辑：**设计目标 → 目标不等于实际效果 → 不同任务的需求冲突 → 指标设计 → 情境化结论**。

**开放式办公室通常被认为能够减少部门之间的物理隔离，并创造更多偶遇和非正式交流，因此一些机构把它视为促进协作的设计方式。然而，“更容易看到同事”并不自动意味着高质量合作会增加。对于需要频繁讨论的团队，较短的沟通距离可能确实有帮助；但对于需要长时间阅读、写作或分析数据的员工，持续的谈话声、人员走动和视觉干扰也可能增加注意力切换的成本。评价开放式办公环境时，如果只统计员工每天与多少人交谈，可能会高估它对工作的积极影响，因为交流次数无法说明一次交流是否必要、是否解决了问题，也无法反映被打断之后重新进入复杂任务所需的时间。更有解释力的研究应该同时考察协作频率、任务完成质量、主观专注程度、非计划中断次数以及不同岗位的工作性质。最终的问题不应只是“开放式办公室是否有效”，而应是“它在什么任务、团队和组织条件下能够带来哪些收益，又会产生哪些成本”。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Open-plan offices are often expected to reduce physical separation between departments and create more opportunities for spontaneous, informal interaction, which is why some organizations adopt them as a way to encourage collaboration. However, being able to see colleagues more easily does not automatically lead to more productive cooperation. For teams that need frequent discussion, shorter communication distances may indeed be useful. For employees who spend long periods reading, writing, or analyzing data, however, ongoing conversation, foot traffic, and visual distractions may increase the cost of switching attention. If an evaluation measures only how many people an employee talks to each day, it may overstate the benefits of an open office because the frequency of interaction says little about whether a conversation was necessary, whether it solved a problem, or how much time was required to regain focus after an interruption. A more informative study would examine collaboration frequency alongside task quality, perceived ability to concentrate, the number of unplanned interruptions, and the nature of different roles. The more useful question, therefore, is not simply whether open-plan offices work, but under what tasks, team structures, and organizational conditions they create benefits and what costs accompany those benefits.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **reduce physical separation between...** — 减少……之间的物理隔离
- **create opportunities for spontaneous interaction** — 创造偶发 / 自发交流机会
- **adopt A as a way to...** — 把 A 作为……的一种方式
- **does not automatically lead to...** — 并不自动导致……
- **need frequent discussion** — 需要频繁讨论
- **increase the cost of switching attention** — 增加注意力切换的成本
- **overstate the benefits of...** — 高估……的好处
- **the frequency of interaction** — 交流频率
- **regain focus after an interruption** — 被打断后重新集中注意力
- **examine A alongside B** — 将 A 与 B 同时考察
- **the nature of different roles** — 不同岗位的工作性质
- **under what conditions...** — 在什么条件下……
- **what costs accompany those benefits** — 这些收益伴随哪些成本

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“促进协作”**可写 **encourage / facilitate collaboration**。*promote cooperation* 并非错误，但在组织设计语境中 **collaboration** 更自然。
2. **“偶遇和非正式交流”**可以写 **spontaneous encounters and informal interaction**，不要写 *random meetings*，后者容易像“随机安排的会议”。
3. **“注意力切换成本”**是抽象概念，可写 **the cost of switching attention / task-switching costs**，不必逐字写 *attention conversion cost*。
4. **“高质量合作”**不要机械写 *high-quality cooperation*。根据语境可写 **productive collaboration / effective collaboration**。
5. **“交流次数无法说明……”**可以用 **the frequency of interaction says little about...**，比 *cannot explain* 更贴近“指标不足以反映”的意思。
6. **“是否有效”**在学术写作中最好进一步拆开：**effective for what outcome, for whom, and under what conditions?**

## 3. Precision rewrite — make the claim narrower and more defensible

把下面句子改得更精确。不要只是换同义词，要缩小因果强度或补充条件。

1. *Second-hand platforms reduce waste on campus.*
2. *Open offices improve teamwork because employees talk more often.*
3. *More interactions mean better collaboration.*
4. *Students do not use exchange platforms because the interface is inconvenient.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. A well-used second-hand platform can reduce some waste by making it easier for students to pass usable items to new owners.
> 2. Open offices may facilitate some forms of teamwork when employees need frequent, informal communication, but the same layout can also create distractions for concentration-heavy tasks.
> 3. A higher frequency of interaction does not by itself establish that collaboration has become more effective; the content, necessity, and outcome of those interactions also matter.
> 4. An inconvenient interface may discourage use, but response time, trust, item quality, and the difficulty of arranging transactions may also affect participation.

## 4. Micro-drill — translate the relationship, not the Chinese word order

Translate each sentence into natural English.

1. 这个方案的优势不在于它完全消除了等待，而在于它减少了不必要的反复确认。
2. 一个指标容易测量，并不意味着它最能反映真正重要的结果。
3. 对某类任务有帮助的设计，对另一类任务可能反而增加负担。
4. 如果一个结论只在非常特定的条件下成立，就应该把这些条件写进结论本身。

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. The advantage of the system is not that it eliminates waiting completely, but that it reduces unnecessary back-and-forth confirmation.
> 2. The fact that a measure is easy to collect does not mean that it best captures the outcome that actually matters.
> 3. A design that helps with one type of task may create additional burdens for another.
> 4. If a conclusion holds only under highly specific conditions, those conditions should be stated as part of the conclusion itself.

## 5. Spaced review — earlier language

2. **如果某个指标无法反映长期效果，就不应该只凭这个指标判断项目是否成功。**

## 6. Short precision exercise — one idea, three structures

把“不同岗位需要不同的办公环境”分别改写成三种英文结构。

### A. Direct statement

使用 **different roles require...**。

### B. Conditional formulation

使用 **what works for... may not...**。

### C. Academic formulation

使用 **the effect depends on...**。

> [!answer]- Possible answers
> A. Different roles require different working environments.
> B. What works well for employees who collaborate constantly may not work as well for those who need long periods of uninterrupted concentration.
> C. The effect of an office layout depends partly on the nature of the tasks employees are expected to perform.

## 1. CET-6 level — improving library seat-reservation systems

先看逻辑：**为什么预约制有价值 → 实际运行中为什么会失效 → 不能只靠处罚 → 如何把规则设计得更可执行**。

**在考试周，大学图书馆的座位往往供不应求，因此许多学校引入了线上预约系统，希望减少“占座但长期不使用”的情况。这样的系统可以提高座位利用率，但如果规则设计得过于复杂，也可能带来新的问题。例如，有些学生预约后临时改变计划，却忘记取消；另一些学生虽然按时到达，却因为签到时间过短而被系统自动释放座位。如果管理方只是一味提高违规惩罚，而不改善提醒方式和取消流程，学生可能会觉得系统不公平，实际执行效果也未必更好。更合理的做法是提供清晰的到场时间窗口、在预约开始前发送提醒，并允许用户通过简单操作快速取消。这样既能减少空置座位，也能降低无意违规的比例。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> During exam periods, seats in university libraries are often in short supply, so many institutions introduce online reservation systems in an effort to reduce the problem of students occupying seats without actually using them for long periods. Such systems can improve seat utilization, but overly complicated rules may create new problems. Some students change their plans after making a reservation but forget to cancel it, while others arrive on time yet lose their seats because the check-in window is too short. If administrators simply increase penalties for violations without improving reminders and the cancellation process, students may perceive the system as unfair, and compliance may not improve. A more reasonable approach is to provide a clear arrival window, send reminders shortly before the reservation begins, and allow users to cancel quickly with minimal effort. This can reduce the number of unused reserved seats while also lowering the share of accidental violations.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **be in short supply** — 供不应求；供应紧张
- **introduce a reservation system** — 引入预约系统
- **seat utilization** — 座位利用率
- **change one’s plans** — 改变计划
- **check-in window** — 签到时间窗口
- **increase penalties for violations** — 提高违规处罚
- **perceive a system as unfair** — 认为一个制度不公平
- **improve compliance** — 提高规则遵守程度
- **with minimal effort** — 只需很少操作 / 成本
- **accidental violations** — 非故意违规

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“占座”**不宜机械写成 *occupy a seat* in every context. 如果强调“预约后不使用”，可以写 **hold a seat without using it**, **leave a reserved seat unused**, 或根据上下文描述具体行为。
2. **“供不应求”**常见表达是 **be in short supply**, **demand exceeds supply**, 或 **there are not enough seats to meet demand**，而不是 *supply cannot satisfy need*。
3. **“签到时间过短”**写成 **the check-in window is too short** 比 *the sign-in time is too short* 更自然，因为这里强调的是允许签到的一段时间。
4. **“一味提高处罚”**可以写 **simply keep increasing penalties** 或 **rely primarily on stricter penalties**，不要逐字写成 *blindly raise punishment*。
5. **“执行效果”**在制度语境中通常不要写 *execution effect*. 根据含义可写 **compliance, effectiveness, how well the system works in practice**。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level — evaluating whether remote work improves productivity

先判断逻辑：**一个看似简单的问题 → 指标选择造成偏差 → 自我选择和任务差异 → 评价设计应该怎么改进**。

**远程办公是否能够提高生产率，看起来像是一个可以通过比较“在家办公”和“到办公室办公”两组员工的工作产出来回答的问题。然而，这种比较往往比想象中复杂。首先，不同岗位的产出并不容易用同一种指标衡量：客服人员可以按处理工单数量统计，研究人员或设计人员的工作质量却很难用短期数字表示。其次，能够选择远程办公的员工可能本来就在资历、自律程度、家庭条件或岗位性质上与其他员工不同，因此两组人的差异不能自动归因于办公地点本身。此外，短期产出提高也可能伴随着沟通成本上升、培训新人更困难或团队知识共享减少等长期代价。较严格的研究应尽可能比较相似岗位和相似员工，在调整办公方式前记录基线表现，并同时追踪产出质量、完成速度、离职率以及团队协作等指标。只有这样，研究者才更有可能判断远程办公在什么条件下有效，而不是得出“远程一定更高效”或“办公室一定更好”这样的绝对结论。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Whether remote work improves productivity may seem like a question that can be answered simply by comparing the output of employees who work from home with that of employees who work in the office. In practice, however, the comparison is considerably more complicated. First, different jobs cannot necessarily be evaluated using the same productivity measure. Customer-service work may be tracked through the number of cases handled, whereas the quality of research or design work is much harder to capture with a short-term numerical indicator. Second, employees who are able to choose remote work may already differ from other employees in seniority, self-discipline, home circumstances, or job characteristics. Differences between the two groups therefore cannot automatically be attributed to work location itself. In addition, higher short-term output may come with longer-term costs, including greater communication overhead, more difficult onboarding, or weaker knowledge sharing within teams. A more rigorous study should compare similar employees performing similar tasks where possible, record baseline performance before work arrangements change, and track several outcomes at the same time, including output quality, completion speed, turnover, and team collaboration. Only then can researchers more confidently identify the conditions under which remote work is effective rather than concluding that remote work is always more productive or that office work is inherently superior.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **evaluate productivity using...** — 用……评价生产率
- **a short-term numerical indicator** — 短期数值指标
- **capture the quality of...** — 衡量 / 反映……的质量
- **differ in seniority / job characteristics** — 在资历 / 岗位特征上不同
- **cannot automatically be attributed to...** — 不能自动归因于……
- **come with longer-term costs** — 伴随长期成本
- **communication overhead** — 沟通额外成本
- **onboarding new employees** — 新员工入职与融入
- **record baseline performance** — 记录基线表现
- **track multiple outcomes** — 同时追踪多个结果指标
- **under the conditions in which...** — 在……条件下
- **inherently superior** — 本质上更优

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“工作产出”**不是所有场景都适合写 *work output*. 可以根据语境使用 **output, productivity, performance, completed work**。
2. **“用同一种指标衡量”**自然表达是 **evaluate using the same measure / metric**，而不是 *measure by the same index*。
3. **“不能自动归因于”**是很重要的学术表达：**cannot automatically be attributed to...**。它比直接写 *is not caused by* 更准确，因为你是在限制因果推断，而不是断言完全没有因果关系。
4. **“长期代价”**通常可用 **long-term costs / trade-offs**。如果是明确负面后果，也可用 **long-term drawbacks**。
5. **“培训新人”**在职场英语中常用 **onboarding new employees / training new hires**；*train new people* 太口语且不够具体。
6. **“绝对结论”**可写 **an absolute conclusion / a blanket conclusion**。**Blanket claim** 尤其适合指不考虑条件差异的一概而论。

## 3. Precision rewrite — make the claim testable

把下面句子改得更具体、更可验证，避免模糊词和过强因果。

1. *The reservation system is bad because many students complain about it.*
2. *Remote work makes employees more productive.*
3. *People who work at home finish more tasks, so working from home is better.*
4. *Strict rules can solve the problem of unused seats.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Frequent complaints may indicate usability or fairness problems, but complaint volume alone does not establish that the reservation system performs poorly on every measure.
> 2. Remote work may improve productivity for some tasks and employees, but the effect depends on how productivity is measured and on the nature of the work.
> 3. Employees working from home completed more tasks in this comparison, but differences in job type, experience, or self-selection may partly account for the result.
> 4. Stricter rules may reduce some forms of misuse, but compliance also depends on whether the rules are clear, practical, and easy to follow.

## 5. Spaced review

先自己翻译，再展开答案。下面复习的是前几天出现过的表达。

1. **一个因素可以强化已经存在的趋势，却不一定是最初造成这一趋势的原因。**
2. **如果研究没有控制两组在起点上的差异，就很难判断观察到的结果究竟来自干预还是原有条件。**

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. A factor can reinforce an existing trend without necessarily being the original cause of that trend.
> 2. If a study does not control for baseline differences between the two groups, it is difficult to determine whether the observed result comes from the intervention or from pre-existing conditions.

## 6. Short precision exercise — one idea, three levels of strength

把中文“新制度减少了空座位”分别翻译成三种证据强度：

1. **仅描述同时发生的变化**
2. **表达可能贡献**
3. **表达较强因果，但加上证据条件**

> [!answer]- Possible answers
> 1. The introduction of the new system was followed by a reduction in the number of unused seats.
> 2. The new system may have contributed to the reduction in unused seats.
> 3. If other relevant factors remained broadly unchanged, the evidence would provide stronger support for the conclusion that the new system reduced the number of unused seats.

## 使用方法与原创说明

以下两篇中文材料、情境和参考译文均为独立编写，不翻译或改写今日英国图书馆文章。六级篇练社区工具借用服务的资格、期限与责任；托福／学术篇练安静车厢试点的分期实施、比较设计和结论限制。两个项目均为虚构教学材料。

## 练习一：CET-6——社区工具库（15–18 分钟）

某社区中心计划建立一个工具库，让居民借用电钻、梯子和园艺设备。年满十八岁的居民完成安全说明后，可以凭住址证明免费注册。每件工具通常可借七天；若没有其他人预约，借用者可在线续借一次。中心会在到期前一天发送提醒，但逾期三天后才暂停账户。工作人员负责检查设备是否安全，借用者则应清洁工具并报告损坏。对于正常磨损，中心不会收费；若设备因明显误用而损坏，工作人员会先说明依据，再决定是否要求赔偿。试行八周后，中心将公布借用次数、按时归还率、维修成本和未能满足的预约量，以判断服务是否值得扩大。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A community center plans to set up a tool library where residents can borrow electric drills, ladders and gardening equipment. Residents aged 18 or above may register free of charge after completing a safety briefing and providing proof of address. Each tool may normally be borrowed for seven days. If no one else has reserved it, the borrower may renew the loan once online. The center will send a reminder one day before the due date, but it will suspend an account only after an item is three days overdue. Staff members are responsible for checking whether the equipment is safe, while borrowers are expected to clean the tools and report any damage. The center will not charge for ordinary wear and tear. If a tool is damaged through clear misuse, staff will explain the evidence before deciding whether compensation is required. After the eight-week pilot, the center will publish the number of loans, the on-time return rate, repair costs and the number of reservations it could not meet in order to decide whether the service should be expanded.

### 结构与搭配

- **aged 18 or above**：简洁表达资格；不要写成 have 18 years old。
- **complete a safety briefing**：briefing 是简短、针对行动的信息说明。
- **renew the loan**：续借；renew the tool 会误成“更新工具”。
- **be three days overdue**：逾期三天；overdue 已包含“超过期限”。
- **ordinary wear and tear**：正常磨损，常见于租借与保修语境。
- **through clear misuse**：因明显误用；避免把所有损坏都写成使用者过错。
- **decide whether the service should be expanded**：评估后决定，不提前承诺扩张。

### 常见中英迁移错误

1. “凭住址证明”不是 by address proof；可写 by providing proof of address。
2. 中文连续出现“中心”时，英语要区分 the center 与 staff members 的责任。
3. “才暂停”要体现延后条件：only after，而不是 after only。
4. “是否值得扩大”不是 prove it is worth expanding；试点提供决策信息，不自动证明结论。

## 练习二：TOEFL／academic——安静车厢试点（20–25 分钟）

某城市在三条通勤铁路线上分阶段引入安静车厢：第一条线路于九月开始，另外两条在十一月开始。研究团队比较政策实施前后车厢内的平均噪声、乘客投诉、车厢占用率和列车员介入次数，并把尚未实施政策的线路作为同期比较组。初步结果显示，第一条线路的噪声和投诉下降，但高峰期乘客更倾向于挤入普通车厢。由于三条线路的客流结构、站距和延误率不同，研究者没有把差异全部归因于安静车厢政策。团队还指出，告示更新和列车员培训与政策同时发生，可能共同影响乘客行为。后续研究将考察效果是否持续、不同群体是否同等受益，以及车厢重新分配是否改变整列车的拥挤程度。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A city introduced quiet carriages in stages on three commuter rail lines. The first line adopted the policy in September, while the other two followed in November. The research team compared average noise levels, passenger complaints, carriage occupancy and the number of staff interventions before and after implementation, using the lines that had not yet adopted the policy as contemporaneous comparison groups. Preliminary results showed declines in noise and complaints on the first line, but passengers were more likely to crowd into regular carriages during peak hours. Because the three lines differed in passenger composition, distance between stations and delay rates, the researchers did not attribute all observed differences to the quiet-carriage policy. They also noted that updated signs and staff training occurred at the same time and may have jointly influenced passenger behavior. Further research will examine whether the effects persist, whether different groups benefit equally, and whether reallocating carriage space changes crowding across the train as a whole.

### 结构与搭配

- **introduce ... in stages**：分阶段引入，能为比较设计提供时间差。
- **before and after implementation**：实施前后；implementation 比 carry out the policy 更名词化、适合学术语体。
- **a contemporaneous comparison group**：同期比较组，强调处于同一时间窗口。
- **be more likely to**：倾向增加，不等于每个乘客都会这样做。
- **attribute A to B**：把 A 归因于 B；否定式可控制因果强度。
- **occur at the same time**：同时发生，是替代解释线索，不等于已证明混杂程度。
- **across the train as a whole**：把局部车厢效果放回整列车系统考察。

### 结论强度阶梯

| 过强 | 更准确 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| The policy eliminated noise. | Noise levels declined on the first line during the initial period. | 没有降到零，也没有覆盖全部线路和长期。 |
| Quiet carriages caused fewer complaints. | The decline in complaints was consistent with a policy effect. | 告示、培训和线路差异仍可能影响结果。 |
| All passengers benefited. | The available measures do not yet show whether benefits were evenly distributed. | 总体均值不能回答群体差异。 |
| Crowding was reduced. | Crowding may have shifted from quiet to regular carriages. | 局部改善可能转移问题。 |

## 精确改写

将下列句子改成证据强度合适的英文，再展开答案：

1. 试点证明工具库一定能减少每个家庭的消费。
2. 安静车厢让所有乘客的通勤体验都变好了。
3. 投诉减少，所以噪声一定是唯一原因。

> [!answer]- 精确改写参考
> 1. The pilot may show whether shared tools can reduce some purchases, but it cannot establish that every household will buy less.
> 2. The early findings suggest lower noise on one line, but they do not show that every passenger had a better journey.
> 3. Fewer complaints are consistent with lower noise, although updated signs, staff training and changes in passenger distribution may also have contributed.

## 中英迁移提醒

- “免费注册”是 register free of charge，不是 register freely。
- “若没有其他人预约”要写成条件从句，不能省掉执行主体。
- “分阶段”不仅是时间描述，也可能构成比较设计的一部分。
- “下降”应说明指标和范围；decline 本身不等于因果。
- “共同影响”可用 jointly influence，避免 both ... together 的冗余。
- “是否同等受益”是分配问题，不能由总体平均数直接回答。

## 间隔复习句

1. 中心会先解释损坏依据，再决定是否要求赔偿。
2. 同期比较组加强了政策解释，但不能排除所有同时发生的变化。

> [!answer]- 间隔复习参考
> 1. The center will explain the evidence of damage before deciding whether compensation is required.
> 2. A contemporaneous comparison group strengthens the policy explanation, but it cannot rule out every change occurring at the same time.

## 练习一：六级——校园夜间接驳车（建议 15 分钟）

某大学计划开通连接图书馆、宿舍区和校医院的夜间接驳车。学生最初希望车辆越多越好，但试运行调查发现，不同时间段的需求差异很大。闭馆后的半小时内，候车人数迅速增加，而其他时段的车辆经常只有少数乘客。因此，学校没有简单地增加全天车次，而是把部分车辆调整到高峰时段，并公开每周的等候时间。对于需要无障碍服务的学生，新的安排还保留了预约渠道。学校表示，如果只根据平均乘车人数评价服务，就可能忽略那些使用人数不多但确实重要的出行需求。一个月后，他们将结合学生反馈与运行记录，决定是否进一步调整线路。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：六级
> A university plans to introduce a night shuttle service linking its library, residence halls and campus health centre. Students initially hoped for as many buses as possible, but a pilot survey found that demand varied considerably throughout the evening. The number of people waiting rose sharply during the half-hour after the library closed, while buses carried only a few passengers at other times. Instead of simply adding more departures throughout the evening, the university therefore moved some buses to peak periods and began publishing weekly waiting-time data. The new arrangement also retained a booking option for students who needed accessible transport. The university noted that judging the service solely by average passenger numbers could overlook travel needs that were important despite involving relatively few users. After a month, it will use student feedback and operating records to decide whether further route changes are needed.

### 结构与搭配

1. **a service linking A, B and C**：现在分词短语后置，避免把“连接”写成三个松散的短句。也可用 a service that connects …。
2. **demand varied considerably**：demand 在这里是总的需求，不必写复数；vary 表示变化，不是“不稳定”的情绪评价。
3. **the number of people waiting rose**：主语中心词是 number，因此谓语用单数。比较 a number of students were waiting，意思是“不少学生在等”。
4. **instead of simply adding …**：instead of 后接名词或动名词，不能直接接完整陈述句。
5. **judge A solely by B / overlook a need**：只看一个指标，可能漏掉重要需求；保留 could，避免把可能性译成必然结论。

### 容易出现的中式英语

- “开通服务”用 introduce/launch a service，不用 open a service。
- “车次”用 departures 或 scheduled services，不用 car times。
- “公开数据”用 publish/make data available，不用 public the data。
- “根据反馈决定”可写 use feedback to decide，不必总用 according to 来连接所有逻辑。

## 练习二：托福／学术——城市降温研究（建议 20 分钟）

一个研究团队希望比较两种街道改造方式对夏季热环境的影响：增加树木和铺设浅色路面。研究人员没有把城市平均气温作为唯一指标，而是在多条街道记录行人高度的气温、地表温度和遮阴情况。他们还收集了风速、湿度和交通流量，因为这些因素可能影响不同街道之间的比较。初步数据表明，两种改造都可能带来益处，但作用机制和适用条件并不相同。树木的效果可能随树冠规模和供水条件变化，而较低的地表温度也不一定意味着行人感受到同等程度的凉爽。为了避免把季节变化误认为改造效果，团队计划保留未改造的对照街道，并在相近天气条件下重复测量。研究的目标不是宣布一种方法普遍优于另一种，而是明确在什么地点、对哪些人群，以及采用什么评价指标时，某项干预更值得投入。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：托福／学术
> A research team aims to compare the effects of two street redesign strategies on summer heat exposure: planting more trees and installing light-coloured paving. Rather than relying solely on citywide average air temperature, the researchers measured air temperature at pedestrian height, surface temperature and shade along several streets. They also collected data on wind speed, humidity and traffic volume, since these factors could affect comparisons between locations. Preliminary findings suggest that both interventions may offer benefits, although their mechanisms and the conditions under which they work differ. The effects of trees may depend on canopy size and water availability, while a lower surface temperature does not necessarily translate into an equivalent improvement in pedestrian thermal comfort. To avoid mistaking seasonal changes for intervention effects, the team plans to retain untreated comparison streets and repeat measurements under similar weather conditions. The aim is to establish where, for whom and according to which criteria an intervention warrants investment, rather than to declare either approach universally superior.

### 学术表达要点

- **compare the effects of A and B on C**：把对象、干预、结果变量分清，不要只写 compare A with B 就省去研究终点。
- **rather than relying solely on …**：指出指标选择；并不意味着被替代的指标没有任何价值。
- **conditions under which …**：常用于写适用边界。which 指 conditions，不可随意改成 when which。
- **does not necessarily translate into …**：两个指标有关联，也不能把一个指标的改善等同于另一个指标等幅改善。
- **mistake A for B**：把 A 误当作 B。注意顺序，mistake the effect for the season 会颠倒原意。
- **preliminary findings suggest / may depend on**：保留证据强弱。避免 proves 或 guarantees。

### 三个常见错误

1. “影响”作名词是 effect，作动词通常用 affect。The weather may affect the measurements. / The effect remains uncertain.
2. “进行比较”写 make a comparison 或 compare，不能 compare the comparison。
3. “在相近条件下”用 under similar conditions，不是 in same condition；similar 不等于 completely identical。

## 精确改写

把以下三句改得更准确，答案不限。

1. More buses make every student happier.
2. Cooler pavement proves that a street is comfortable.
3. A better average means that all groups benefit equally.

> [!answer]- 改写示例
> 1. More frequent buses may reduce waiting times during busy periods, although the effect on individual students depends on their routes and schedules.
> 2. A lower pavement temperature is one useful measurement, but pedestrian comfort also depends on factors such as shade and humidity.
> 3. An improved average can coexist with unequal outcomes across groups.

## 间隔复习：先写再看

1. 目前的结果与这一解释相容，但还不足以排除其他可能性。
2. 如果主要瓶颈仍然存在，简化申请表未必能缩短整个审批过程。

> [!answer]- 复习参考
> 1. The current results are consistent with this explanation, but they do not yet rule out alternatives.
> 2. Simplifying the application form may not shorten the overall approval process if the main bottleneck remains.

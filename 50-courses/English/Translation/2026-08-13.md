# Translation — Day 1

## Chinese → English
中国大学生越来越重视时间管理。合理安排学习和休息不仅可以提高学习效率，也有助于保持身心健康。因此，许多学生开始使用日程表或手机应用来规划每天的任务。

## Reference
Chinese university students are paying increasing attention to time management. A reasonable balance between study and rest can not only improve learning efficiency but also help maintain physical and mental health. Therefore, many students have begun to use schedules or mobile apps to plan their daily tasks.

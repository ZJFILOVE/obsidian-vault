## 练习一：CET-6——社区共享厨房（15–18 分钟）

某社区中心在工作日晚间开放共享厨房，供居民提前预约制作家庭餐食。每个时段最多接待四组使用者，每组至少有一名成年人在场。中心提供炉灶、基础炊具和清洁用品，但不提供食材，也不替使用者保存剩余食品。首次参加者需要提前十五分钟到达，完成设备与消防安全说明。若使用者在开始前两小时取消，预约次数不会受到影响；无故缺席两次者在接下来的一个月内只能预约候补时段。工作人员会检查设备是否恢复原位，却不会评价菜品是否成功。中心记录预约使用率、取消时间和清洁所需时间，以判断开放时段是否需要调整。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A community center opens a shared kitchen on weekday evenings, which residents can book in advance to prepare meals for their households. Each session can accommodate up to four groups, and every group must have at least one adult present. The center provides stoves, basic cookware and cleaning supplies, but it does not provide ingredients or store leftover food for users. First-time participants must arrive fifteen minutes early to complete an equipment and fire-safety briefing. If users cancel at least two hours before a session begins, the cancellation will not affect their booking record. Anyone who misses two sessions without notice may book only wait-list slots during the following month. Staff check whether equipment has been returned to its proper place, but they do not judge whether a dish is successful. The center records the booking rate, cancellation times and the time required for cleaning in order to decide whether its opening hours should be adjusted.

### 结构与搭配

- **book in advance**：提前预约；book 可直接作动词。
- **accommodate up to four groups**：最多容纳四组；up to 要保留上限含义。
- **have an adult present**：有成年人在场；present 在此作表语。
- **complete a safety briefing**：完成安全说明，不写 finish a safety introduction。
- **miss a session without notice**：未通知而缺席。
- **return equipment to its proper place**：把设备放回适当位置。
- **the time required for cleaning**：清洁所需时间，required 是后置修饰。
- **decide whether ... should be adjusted**：判断是否需要调整。

### 常见中英迁移错误

1. “开放厨房”是 open a shared kitchen，不是 open kitchen（后者也可指开放式厨房布局）。
2. “每组至少一名”要写 every group / each group，避免主语范围含糊。
3. “剩余食品”是 leftover food，不是 remained food。
4. “无故缺席”可写 miss without notice，不要直译 without reason unless。
5. “评价菜品成功”不是 evaluate whether food wins；可用 judge whether a dish is successful。

## 练习二：TOEFL／academic——校园树荫与热暴露试点（20–25 分钟）

某大学在六条步行路线中的三条增设临时遮阳棚，并在另外三条长度和路面材料相近的路线上维持原状。研究团队在连续四周的中午和傍晚记录空气温度、黑球温度、相对湿度、行人数量和平均步行速度，同时邀请自愿参加者报告热舒适度。增设遮阳棚的路线在中午具有较低的黑球温度，行人停留时间也更长；但空气温度的差异很小，傍晚的路线使用量没有稳定变化。由于遮阳棚附近同时增加了座椅，且参与问卷的行人由自己选择是否作答，研究者没有把所有观察到的差异都归因于遮阳本身。后续研究应分别检验遮阳、座椅和路线位置的作用，并观察短期舒适度变化能否转化为更持久的步行行为。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A university installed temporary shade structures along three of six pedestrian routes and left the other three unchanged. The comparison routes were similar in length and pavement material. For four consecutive weeks, the research team recorded air temperature, globe temperature, relative humidity, pedestrian counts and average walking speed at midday and in the evening. It also invited volunteers to report their thermal comfort. At midday, routes with the shade structures had lower globe temperatures, and pedestrians remained there longer. However, differences in air temperature were small, and evening use of the routes showed no consistent change. Because seating was added near the shade structures at the same time and survey respondents chose whether to participate, the researchers did not attribute every observed difference to shade alone. Further research should test the separate effects of shade, seating and route location, and examine whether short-term improvements in comfort translate into more sustained walking behaviour.

### 结构与搭配

- **install ... along a route**：沿路线安装设施。
- **leave the comparison sites unchanged**：让对照地点保持原状。
- **for four consecutive weeks**：连续四周；consecutive 强调没有中断。
- **pedestrian counts**：行人计数；比 number of walking people 更自然。
- **show no consistent change**：没有稳定变化，不等于完全没有任何波动。
- **attribute A to B alone**：把 A 仅归因于 B；alone 保留混杂因素。
- **test the separate effects of**：分别检验多个因素的作用。
- **translate into sustained behaviour**：转化为持续行为；不是字面“翻译”。

### 结论强度阶梯

| 过强写法 | 更准确写法 | 原因 |
|---|---|---|
| Shade cooled the campus. | Shaded routes had lower globe temperatures at midday. | 局部辐射环境不同，不等于全校园空气降温。 |
| Students walked more because of shade. | Pedestrians stayed longer on the routes with shade and new seating. | 座椅与自选路线仍是替代解释。 |
| The intervention had no evening effect. | Evening use showed no consistent change during the four-week study. | “未观察到稳定变化”不等于证明效果为零。 |
| Everyone felt more comfortable. | Volunteer responses suggested a difference in reported thermal comfort. | 自愿作答不能代表所有使用者。 |

## 精确改写

把下列句子改成证据强度合适的英文：

1. 共享厨房减少了社区里的食物浪费。
2. 遮阳棚让学生更愿意步行。
3. 晚上没有显著变化，所以遮阳在晚上完全无效。

> [!answer]- 精确改写参考
> 1. The shared kitchen may make cooking facilities more accessible, but the available booking records do not measure community food waste.
> 2. Pedestrians remained longer on routes with shade structures and additional seating, although the design does not isolate the effect of shade.
> 3. Route use showed no consistent evening change during the four-week observation period; this does not establish that shade has no evening effect under other conditions.

## 句子重组：把条件放到正确位置

将信息组合成一句清楚的英文：

1. 取消不影响记录；条件是至少提前两小时；对象是已预约使用者。
2. 中午黑球温度较低；对象是三条试点路线；空气温度差异很小。
3. 研究不能只归因于遮阳；原因一是同时增加座椅；原因二是问卷自选参加。

> [!answer]- 重组参考
> 1. A cancellation will not affect a user’s booking record if it is made at least two hours before the session.
> 2. The three pilot routes had lower globe temperatures at midday, although differences in air temperature were small.
> 3. The observed differences cannot be attributed to shade alone because seating was added at the same time and survey participation was self-selected.

## 中英迁移提醒

- “最多”用 up to；“至少”用 at least，二者不能交换。
- “保持原状”是 remain / be left unchanged，不是 keep original situation。
- “连续四周”描述观察期；不要误写成 four weeks continuously，仿佛仪器每秒都在测。
- “没有稳定变化”保留时间波动，不写 did not change at all。
- “转化为长期行为”常用 translate into，不要因 translate 的常见义而回避。
- 学术段的限制句不是客套话，而是决定结论能够多强的核心信息。

## 间隔复习句

1. 首次参加者需要提前十五分钟到达并完成安全说明。
2. 中午舒适度的短期改善未必会转化为持续的步行行为。

> [!answer]- 间隔复习参考
> 1. First-time participants must arrive fifteen minutes early and complete a safety briefing.
> 2. A short-term improvement in comfort at midday may not translate into sustained walking behaviour.

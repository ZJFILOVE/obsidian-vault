# Translation — Day 8

## A · CET-4+
随着移动互联网的发展，大学生获取信息变得越来越方便。然而，信息数量的增加并不一定意味着信息质量的提高。面对大量网络内容，学生需要学会区分可靠的信息和误导性的信息，并根据充分的证据作出判断。

### Reference
With the development of the mobile Internet, it has become increasingly convenient for university students to obtain information. However, an increase in the amount of information does not necessarily mean an improvement in its quality. Faced with a large amount of online content, students need to learn to distinguish reliable information from misleading information and make judgments based on sufficient evidence.

## B · CET-6
许多人认为，只要投入更多时间，就一定能够取得更好的学习效果。然而，学习效率不仅取决于学习时间，还受到注意力、学习方法以及休息质量等因素的影响。因此，与其盲目延长学习时间，学生更应该找出自己的薄弱环节，并采取有针对性的方法加以改进。

### Reference
Many people assume that spending more time studying will necessarily lead to better learning outcomes. However, learning efficiency depends not only on the amount of time spent studying but also on factors such as attention, learning methods, and the quality of rest. Therefore, rather than blindly extending their study time, students should identify their weaknesses and take appropriate measures to improve them.

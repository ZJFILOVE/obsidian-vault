## Exercise 1 — CET-6: The neighborhood library
近年来，一些社区图书馆不再仅仅提供借阅服务，而是举办读书会、数字技能培训和面向老年人的智能手机课程。这些活动让图书馆成为不同年龄居民交流的公共空间。不过，增加活动数量并不必然意味着服务质量提高。如果工作人员长期忙于组织活动，普通读者可能更难获得个性化帮助。因此，管理者应定期收集反馈，比较不同群体的需求，并根据实际参与情况调整安排。公共服务的成功不应只用参加人数来衡量，还应考虑它是否真正降低了居民获取信息的门槛。

> [!answer]- Reference translation — CET-6
> In recent years, some community libraries have gone beyond lending books by organizing reading groups, digital-skills workshops, and smartphone classes for older residents. These activities have turned libraries into public spaces where people of different ages can interact. However, offering more activities does not necessarily mean providing better services. If staff spend most of their time organizing events, ordinary readers may find it harder to receive individual assistance. Administrators should therefore collect feedback regularly, compare the needs of different groups, and adjust their schedules in light of actual participation. The success of a public service should be measured not only by attendance but also by whether it genuinely lowers barriers to accessing information.

### CET-6 structure and collocation clinic
**go beyond doing something** is more idiomatic than *not only stay in*. **does not necessarily mean** avoids an unjustified causal claim. **collect feedback**, **adjust a schedule**, and **lower barriers to accessing information** are reusable collocations. Use **older residents**, not *old people*, in neutral institutional prose. “参加人数” is **attendance** or **the number of participants**, not *participating population*.

## Exercise 2 — Academic/TOEFL: Evaluating a university initiative
一所大学准备推出跨学科研究资助计划，希望鼓励来自不同院系的教师共同解决复杂问题。该计划最初拟以合作论文数量作为主要绩效指标，但部分研究人员指出，这一指标可能偏向能够迅速发表成果的项目，而忽视需要长期积累数据的研究。跨学科合作还可能增加沟通成本，因为参与者对基本概念、证据标准和研究方法的理解并不完全一致。为避免将形式上的合作误认为实质性进展，大学应在项目开始时明确研究目标，并在中期评估中同时考察团队如何处理分歧、是否形成共享的方法，以及是否产生可供后续研究使用的数据。即使某些项目暂时没有发表论文，它们也可能通过建立长期合作关系创造价值；但这种可能性同样需要证据，而不能成为免于评估的理由。

> [!answer]- Reference translation — academic English
> A university is preparing to launch an interdisciplinary research funding program intended to encourage faculty from different departments to address complex problems together. The original proposal would use the number of coauthored papers as its principal performance indicator. Some researchers, however, argue that this metric may favor projects capable of producing publishable results quickly while overlooking work that requires long-term data collection. Interdisciplinary collaboration may also increase communication costs, since participants do not necessarily share the same understanding of basic concepts, standards of evidence, or research methods. To avoid mistaking nominal collaboration for substantive progress, the university should define research objectives at the outset and assess, at the midpoint, how teams resolve disagreements, whether they have developed shared methods, and whether they have produced data that subsequent studies can use. Even projects that have not yet resulted in publications may create value by establishing lasting partnerships. That possibility, however, also requires evidence and should not become an excuse to avoid evaluation.

### Academic structures and transfer errors
Use **be intended to + verb** for purpose, **favor A while overlooking B** for biased evaluation, and **mistake A for B** for conceptual confusion. **standards of evidence** is more natural than *evidence standards* in many academic contexts. “暂时没有发表论文” means **have not yet resulted in publications**, not *have no papers forever*. “免于评估” is **avoid evaluation / be exempt from evaluation**, not *escape from estimate*.

## Precision rewrite
Improve the following overstatements without weakening them into empty hedges:
1. More interdisciplinary papers prove that the program is successful.
2. Every community library should offer exactly the same services.
3. If a project produces no publications, it has no value.

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. An increase in interdisciplinary publications may indicate progress, but it should be evaluated alongside research quality and long-term outcomes.
> 2. Libraries should adapt their services to the needs of the communities they serve.
> 3. A project without immediate publications may still create useful data or partnerships, although those benefits also require evaluation.

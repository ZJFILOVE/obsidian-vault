## 使用方法与原创说明

以下两组中文段落、情境和参考译文均为独立编写，不翻译或改写今日复印机文章。六级篇练公共服务的流程、例外与责任；托福／学术篇练观察研究、选择偏差和谨慎外推。两者均为虚构教学材料，不对应真实政策或已发表论文。

## 练习一：CET-6——社区冰箱的食物共享规则（15–18分钟）

一个社区中心计划在大厅设置公共冰箱，附近餐馆和居民可以把仍可安全食用的包装食品放入其中。志愿者每天两次检查保质期、包装是否完整以及冷藏温度，并记录被取走和被丢弃的食物数量。为了降低使用门槛，领取者无需说明收入或家庭状况，也不必登记姓名。不过，冰箱不接收自制熟食、已经开封的乳制品或无法确认制作日期的餐点。项目负责人强调，共享食物可以减少浪费并提供临时帮助，但它不能代替稳定的社会救助。若温度记录连续异常，冰箱将暂停开放，直到设备通过检查。试运行三个月后，社区中心会同时考虑食品安全事件、实际领取量和志愿者工作负担，而不会只用捐赠总重量判断项目是否成功。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A community center plans to install a shared refrigerator in its lobby, where nearby restaurants and residents can leave packaged food that is still safe to eat. Twice a day, volunteers will check expiration dates, make sure the packaging is intact, monitor the refrigeration temperature, and record how much food is collected or discarded. To lower the barrier to access, people who take food will not have to disclose their income or household circumstances, nor will they be required to register their names. However, the refrigerator will not accept homemade cooked food, dairy products that have already been opened, or meals whose preparation dates cannot be confirmed. The project leaders stress that food sharing may reduce waste and provide temporary assistance, but it cannot replace reliable social support. If temperature records remain abnormal, access to the refrigerator will be suspended until the equipment passes an inspection. After a three-month pilot, the center will consider food-safety incidents, actual collection volumes and the workload placed on volunteers instead of judging the project solely by the total weight of donations.

### 结构与搭配

- **install / set up a shared refrigerator**：install 强调设备安置；set up 更强调建立安排。
- **still safe to eat**：比 mechanically 写成 edible 更自然，也保留“仍然安全”的时间含义。
- **lower the barrier to access**：降低使用门槛；access 在这里是名词。
- **disclose income / household circumstances**：披露收入／家庭情况；不要把 explain 与私人信息机械搭配。
- **remain abnormal**：记录持续异常；若只出现一次，用 a reading is outside the safe range 更精确。
- **judge solely by**：只依据……判断；solely 控制范围，避免把一个指标写成全部结果。

### 常见中式英语与精度修正

1. “保质期”按包装情境可用 **expiration date / use-by date**，不要一律写 guarantee period。
2. “领取者”不是 receiver 的机械替换；可写 **people who take/collect food**。
3. “暂停开放”是临时措施，用 **be suspended**，不要擅自强化成 permanently close。
4. “减少浪费”是项目目标，不是已证实结果；用 **may / is intended to**。
5. 把过强句 *The refrigerator solves food insecurity* 改为：  
   **The refrigerator may provide short-term access to food, but its reach and reliability must be evaluated separately from broader social support.**

## 练习二：TOEFL／academic——晚间课程与学生留存（20–25分钟）

一所社区学院拟研究新增晚间课程是否提高在职学生完成证书项目的比例。学院将比较新增课程前后两个学年的选课和结业记录，并通过匿名问卷了解学生的工作时间、通勤距离和照护责任。如果晚间班学生的续读率上升，这一结果与“更灵活的时间安排降低了学习障碍”的解释一致，但不能单独证明课程时间是原因。两个学年的学生构成可能不同；同时选择晚间班的学生也可能具有更强的完成意愿。研究团队计划把课程难度、授课教师和学生此前修读学分纳入分析，并报告缺失问卷的比例。即使结果在多个专业中相似，结论仍应限于有相近交通、学费和学生支持条件的院校。学院还应检查，晚间课程是否只是把资源从白天学生转移出去。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A community college plans to investigate whether adding evening classes increases the proportion of working students who complete certificate programs. The college will compare enrollment and completion records from the two academic years before and after the new classes were introduced, and an anonymous survey will collect information about students’ working hours, commuting distances and caregiving responsibilities. If retention rises among students in evening classes, the finding would be consistent with the explanation that more flexible scheduling reduces barriers to study; by itself, however, it would not establish that class timing caused the change. The student populations may differ across the two periods, and students who choose evening classes may also have stronger intentions to complete their programs. The research team plans to account for course difficulty, instructors and credits previously earned, and to report the proportion of missing survey responses. Even if similar results appear across several fields of study, the conclusion should be limited to institutions with comparable transportation, tuition and student-support conditions. The college should also examine whether evening provision merely shifts resources away from daytime students.

### 结构与学术搭配

- **investigate whether**：研究是否；比 prove that 预设更少。
- **the proportion of students who...**：完成某事的学生比例；主干是 proportion，谓语通常用单数。
- **be consistent with an explanation**：与某种解释一致，不等于排除其他解释。
- **by itself, it would not establish that...**：单凭这一结果不足以证明因果。
- **account for**：在分析中考虑／控制；不保证所有差异都被消除。
- **credits previously earned**：此前获得的学分；earned 修饰 credits。
- **limit the conclusion to**：主动标明外推边界。
- **shift resources away from**：从某群体转走资源；要求后续实证检查。

### 常见迁移错误与精度改写

1. “结业率／续读率”分别可写 **completion rate / retention rate**，不要都写 pass rate。
2. “在职学生”是 **working students / students in employment**，不是 students on duty。
3. “学生构成不同”用 **student populations may differ**，不等于个人发生变化。
4. “选择晚间班的人更有完成意愿”是选择偏差的可能来源，不要把它写成已测量事实。
5. 把过强句 *Evening classes improved graduation* 改为：  
   **Completion rose after evening classes were added, but the before-and-after comparison cannot separate the scheduling change from shifts in the student population or course provision.**

## 精确改写

将下列模糊句各改写一遍：

1. The food program was successful.
2. Flexible classes helped students.

> [!answer]- 精确改写参考
> 1. During the pilot, the program distributed most accepted food without a reported safety incident, although volunteer workload increased on weekends.
> 2. Working students enrolled in evening classes had higher retention during the observed period, but the available data do not establish that scheduling alone produced the difference.

## 间隔复习

明天闭卷翻译：

1. 一个项目降低了进入门槛，并不意味着它已经覆盖最需要帮助的人。
2. 即使不同专业出现相似趋势，研究者仍应报告样本差异和未被观察到的替代解释。

> [!answer]- 间隔复习参考
> 1. Lowering the barrier to entry does not mean that a program has already reached the people who need it most.
> 2. Even if a similar trend appears across different fields, researchers should still report differences in the samples and alternative explanations that were not observed.

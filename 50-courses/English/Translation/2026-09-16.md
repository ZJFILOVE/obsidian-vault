## 1. CET-6 level — reducing digital distraction

先判断段落结构：**常见做法 → 为什么不够 → 更现实的改进方式**。

**很多学生在需要集中注意力时会把手机调成静音，或者暂时关闭社交媒体通知。这些方法确实能减少一部分干扰，但问题并不完全来自通知本身。学习环境中的噪声、频繁切换任务的习惯，以及担心错过消息的心理，都可能让人难以长时间专注。因此，与其只依赖意志力，不如主动设计一个更容易专注的环境。例如，可以把手机放到看不见的地方，提前规定查看消息的时间，并把较大的任务拆成几个能够在短时间内完成的步骤。这样做并不能消除所有分心，但可以减少人们反复作出“要不要看手机”这一决定的次数。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Many students silence their phones or temporarily turn off social-media notifications when they need to concentrate. These measures can certainly reduce some distractions, but the problem does not come from notifications alone. Noise in the study environment, the habit of switching between tasks, and anxiety about missing messages can all make sustained concentration difficult. Rather than relying entirely on willpower, it is often more effective to design an environment that makes focus easier. For example, students can keep their phones out of sight, decide in advance when to check messages, and break larger tasks into steps that can be completed within a manageable period of time. These strategies cannot eliminate every distraction, but they can reduce how often people have to make the repeated decision of whether to check their phones.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **silence one's phone** — 把手机调成静音
- **turn off notifications** — 关闭通知
- **reduce distractions** — 减少干扰
- **sustained concentration / sustained attention** — 持续专注
- **switch between tasks** — 在任务之间切换
- **anxiety about missing...** — 对错过……的焦虑
- **rely entirely on willpower** — 完全依赖意志力
- **decide in advance** — 提前决定
- **break a task into manageable steps** — 把任务拆成可管理的小步骤
- **keep something out of sight** — 把某物放到视线之外

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“把手机调成静音”**不要写 *make the phone silent*；最自然的是 **silence the phone / put the phone on silent**。
2. **“难以长时间专注”**可以写 **make sustained concentration difficult**，而不是 *make people difficult to focus*。英语里通常是 **make it difficult for people to focus** 或直接改变主语。
3. **“依赖意志力”**是 **rely on willpower**，不要写 *depend on one's perseverance*，后者意思不同。
4. **“提前规定查看消息的时间”**不需要机械翻成 *regulate the time of checking messages*；**decide/set specific times to check messages** 更自然。
5. **“减少作出决定的次数”**最好直接表达为 **reduce how often people have to make a decision**，避免冗长的 *reduce the number of times of making decisions*。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level — interpreting evidence in educational research

先识别逻辑：**平均差异 → 潜在混杂因素 → 相关不等于因果 → 更强研究设计**。

**在比较两种教学方法时，研究者有时会发现，采用其中一种方法的学生平均成绩更高。然而，仅凭这一差异并不能判断教学方法本身导致了更好的结果。选择某种课程的学生可能原本就更有动力，也可能拥有更多学习资源；不同班级的教师经验和评分标准也可能不同。如果这些因素没有被充分控制，观察到的成绩差异就可能同时反映教学方法和学生背景的影响。因此，观察性数据可以帮助研究者发现值得进一步研究的关联，但要作出更强的因果推断，通常需要更严格的研究设计、合理的对照组，以及对替代解释的系统检验。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> When comparing two teaching methods, researchers sometimes find that students using one method achieve higher average scores. However, this difference alone does not show that the teaching method itself caused the better outcomes. Students who choose a particular course may already be more motivated or have access to greater learning resources, while teachers may differ in experience and grading standards across classes. If these factors are not adequately controlled for, the observed difference in scores may reflect both the teaching method and differences in student background. Observational data can therefore help researchers identify associations that deserve further investigation, but stronger causal inference usually requires a more rigorous research design, an appropriate comparison group, and systematic testing of alternative explanations.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **compare two approaches / methods** — 比较两种方法
- **achieve higher average scores** — 获得更高的平均成绩
- **this difference alone does not show that...** — 仅凭这一差异不能说明……
- **have access to greater resources** — 拥有更多资源
- **differ in experience / standards** — 在经验/标准上存在差异
- **control for a factor** — 在分析中控制某因素
- **observed difference** — 观察到的差异
- **identify an association** — 发现关联
- **causal inference** — 因果推断
- **comparison/control group** — 对照组
- **alternative explanation** — 替代解释

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“平均成绩更高”**通常是 **higher average scores**，而不是 *the average score is more high*。
2. **“导致了更好的结果”**在证据不足时要注意因果强度。**cause** 很强；如果只是观察性结果，可以用 **be associated with / coincide with**。
3. **“控制因素”**在研究方法语境中通常是 **control for a factor**，不是简单的 *control a factor*。后者更像实际操控它。
4. **“学生背景”**是 **student background / background characteristics**；不要写 *student's background condition*。
5. **“值得进一步研究的关联”**可写 **associations that deserve further investigation**，比 *relations worthy to research* 更自然。
6. **“替代解释”**是 **alternative explanation**，在学术写作中非常高频，指同一结果可能由其他机制解释。

## 3. Precision rewrite — control the strength of the claim

把下面句子改成更自然、证据强度更准确的英文。

1. *Students using the app got higher scores, so the app improved learning.*
2. *Turning off notifications can solve the concentration problem.*
3. *The data prove that this teaching method is the best.*
4. *The researchers controlled many things, so there are no other explanations.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Students using the app achieved higher scores, but the difference does not by itself establish that the app improved learning.
> 2. Turning off notifications can reduce one source of distraction, but it does not address every cause of poor concentration.
> 3. The data suggest that the method may be effective under the conditions studied, but they do not establish that it is universally superior.
> 4. Controlling for several measured factors strengthens the analysis, but unmeasured or alternative explanations may still remain.

## 4. Micro-drill — choose the best academic verb

Fill each blank with **indicate, establish, contribute to, account for,** or **control for**.

1. A single observational study rarely ______ a causal relationship on its own.
2. Several environmental factors may ______ difficulty concentrating.
3. Researchers often ______ prior achievement when comparing two student groups.
4. The results ______ that the intervention deserves further study, but they do not prove that it will work everywhere.
5. Differences in prior experience may partly ______ the gap between the two groups.

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. establish
> 2. contribute to
> 3. control for
> 4. indicate
> 5. account for

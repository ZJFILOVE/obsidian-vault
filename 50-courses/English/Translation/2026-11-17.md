### 中文原文

在日常直觉里，我们常把物体拥有的某种特征看作它与生俱来的内在属性。然而在很多复杂系统里，这种特征实际上源于对象与周围环境之间的持续互动。如果只孤立地检查单个部件，往往找不到产生该特征的源头；只有把环境场与相互作用强度一并纳入分析，观察到的差异才能得到合理解释。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> In everyday intuition, we often treat a property of an object as an intrinsic trait it possesses on its own. In many complex systems, however, that property actually arises from continuous interaction between the object and its surrounding environment. Examining an isolated component alone often fails to reveal the source of the trait; only when both the ambient field and interaction strength are brought into the analysis can the observed differences be satisfactorily explained.

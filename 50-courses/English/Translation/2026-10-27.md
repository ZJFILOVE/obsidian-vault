## 练习一：CET-6——图书馆自助取书柜（15–18 分钟）

市图书馆准备在两个地铁站试运行自助取书柜，使无法在开放时间到馆的读者也能领取预约图书。读者收到通知后有三天时间取书；逾期未取的书将被送回原分馆，并取消本次预约，但不会影响今后的借阅资格。每个柜子的容量有限，因此大型画册、需要恒温保存的资料和馆际互借图书暂不适用。工作人员每天检查设备状态，并在柜门无法打开或系统重复扣除借阅次数时人工处理。图书馆将记录使用量、故障率和图书滞留时间，同时通过简短问卷了解哪些读者仍因交通、数字技能或无障碍需求而无法使用。试点是否扩大，不会只看取书总量，还会考虑服务是否真正覆盖了原本难以到馆的人。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> The city library plans to pilot self-service book lockers at two metro stations so that readers who cannot visit a branch during opening hours can still collect reserved books. Once notified, readers will have three days to pick up their items. Books that are not collected by the deadline will be returned to the original branch and the reservation will be cancelled, but the reader's future borrowing eligibility will not be affected. Because each locker has limited capacity, oversized art books, materials requiring temperature-controlled storage and interlibrary-loan items will not be eligible at first. Staff will check the equipment every day and intervene when a locker door fails to open or the system records the same loan more than once. The library will track usage, fault rates and the time books remain uncollected, while a short survey will explore which readers are still unable to use the service because of transport, digital skills or accessibility needs. A decision on expanding the pilot will consider not only total collections but also whether the service has reached people who previously found it difficult to visit a branch.

### 结构与搭配

- **pilot a service / run a pilot**：前者把 pilot 当动词，后者用名词；不要写 try-run。
- **during opening hours**：在开放时间内；比 in open time 自然。
- **by the deadline**：在截止时间前；逾期可写 after the deadline / overdue，视对象而定。
- **borrowing eligibility**：借阅资格；不要误写 borrowing qualification test。
- **be eligible for / be eligible to do**：物品适用某服务、个人有资格做某事。
- **intervene when**：系统例外出现时人工介入；比 deal it by people 更清楚。
- **not only A but also B**：并列成分保持同一语法层级。

### 常见中式英语与精度修正

1. “领取预约图书”用 **collect reserved books**；reserve 在这里是“预约保留”，不是储备资源。
2. “取消本次预约”是 **cancel this reservation**，不能擅自强化为 suspend the account。
3. “暂不适用”写 **will not be eligible at first / will initially be excluded**，保留未来调整空间。
4. “覆盖某群体”在服务语境可写 **reach a group**，不必机械写 cover people。
5. 把模糊句 *The lockers made access fair* 改为：  
   **The lockers extended collection hours, but the evaluation must still examine who could not use the service and why.**

## 练习二：TOEFL／academic——音频反馈与论文修改质量（20–25 分钟）

一所大学拟研究教师用三分钟音频代替书面评语，是否能提高学生修改论文的质量。研究将在六门写作课中进行，同一位教师会在不同作业中交替使用两种反馈方式。研究者将比较学生修改前后的论证完整性、证据使用和结构清晰度，并记录教师制作反馈所需的时间。学生还可以自愿填写问卷，说明他们是否完整收听音频、是否反复查看书面意见，以及哪种形式更容易理解。如果收到音频反馈的论文进步更明显，这一结果仍不能自动证明声音形式本身更有效：教师可能在音频中提供了更多解释，不同作业的难度也可能不同。问卷参与者还可能比未参与者更重视反馈。研究团队应事先说明评分标准，报告缺失数据，并检验结果是否因课程、教师或学生原有水平而异。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A university plans to investigate whether replacing written comments with three-minute audio feedback improves the quality of students' essay revisions. The study will be conducted in six writing courses, and each instructor will alternate between the two feedback formats across different assignments. Researchers will compare the completeness of the argument, use of evidence and structural clarity before and after revision, while also recording the time instructors spend producing feedback. Students may complete a voluntary survey indicating whether they listened to the entire recording, returned to written comments more than once, and found one format easier to understand. If essays receiving audio feedback show greater improvement, the result would not automatically establish that the audio format itself was more effective: instructors may have provided fuller explanations in recordings, and the assignments may have differed in difficulty. Survey participants may also be more attentive to feedback than non-participants. The research team should specify the scoring criteria in advance, report missing data, and test whether the results vary by course, instructor or students' prior level.

### 结构与学术搭配

- **investigate whether A improves B**：研究 A 是否改善 B；不预设有效。
- **replace A with B**：用 B 替代 A；顺序不要颠倒。
- **alternate between two formats**：在两种形式间交替；若随机分配必须有真实随机过程才能写 randomly assign。
- **before and after revision**：清楚标出比较时点。
- **would not automatically establish that**：观察到差异仍不足以直接建立因果。
- **differ in difficulty**：难度不同；不要写 have different difficulties，后者常指遇到不同困难。
- **specify criteria in advance**：预先规定标准，有助于避免看完结果后改变评价方式。
- **vary by course**：因课程而异；表示分组异质性。

### 常见迁移错误与精度改写

1. “修改质量”是 **quality of revisions / revision quality**，不是 modify quality。
2. “完整收听”用 **listen to the entire recording**，不要用 completely hear。
3. “更重视反馈”可写 **be more attentive to feedback**，不等于实际学得更多。
4. “结果因教师而异”是 **results vary by instructor**；不要把 by 写成 from。
5. 把过强句 *Audio feedback caused better writing* 改为：  
   **Students made larger revisions after receiving audio feedback, but differences in explanation length and assignment difficulty remain plausible alternatives.**

## 精确改写

将下列句子改成可评估的陈述：

1. The locker service was convenient for everyone.
2. Students preferred audio feedback because it was better.

> [!answer]- 精确改写参考
> 1. The lockers extended collection beyond branch hours, although the pilot had not yet established whether readers with limited digital access could use them independently.
> 2. Among survey respondents, more students rated audio feedback as easy to understand; the voluntary survey did not show whether the format improved learning for all enrolled students.

## 1. CET-6 level — reducing food waste on campus

先判断逻辑：**问题 → 常见误区 → 更有效的改进方式 → 为什么这些方式更可持续**。

**大学食堂每天都要准备大量食物，因此完全避免剩饭并不现实。如果食堂为了防止食物不够而长期准备过多，浪费就会逐渐变成一种固定成本。另一方面，仅仅提醒学生“不要浪费”也未必足够，因为份量过大、菜品信息不清楚以及高峰期排队时间过长，都可能让学生作出并不理想的选择。更实际的做法是提供大小不同的份量、清楚标注菜品和价格，并根据不同时间段的销量调整备餐数量。这样既能给学生更多选择，也能减少食堂在需求不确定时一次性准备过多食物的压力。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> University canteens have to prepare large quantities of food every day, so eliminating leftovers completely is unrealistic. If a canteen consistently prepares too much food in order to avoid running short, waste can gradually become a recurring cost. At the same time, simply reminding students not to waste food may not be enough, because oversized portions, unclear menu information, and long queues during peak hours can all lead students to make less-than-ideal choices. A more practical approach is to offer different portion sizes, provide clear information about dishes and prices, and adjust preparation volumes according to demand at different times of day. This gives students more choice while reducing the pressure on canteens to prepare excessive amounts of food in advance when demand is uncertain.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **eliminate something completely** — 彻底消除某事
- **run short of...** — ……不够用 / 快用完
- **a recurring cost** — 反复出现、持续发生的成本
- **simply doing X may not be enough** — 仅仅做 X 可能还不够
- **oversized portions** — 份量过大的餐食
- **during peak hours** — 在高峰时段
- **adjust A according to B** — 根据 B 调整 A
- **preparation volume / amount prepared** — 备餐量
- **in advance** — 提前
- **when demand is uncertain** — 当需求不确定时

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“准备过多食物”**不要机械写成 *prepare too many foods*。一般不可数地说 **prepare too much food**；若强调不同菜品，可用 **prepare excessive quantities of food**。
2. **“防止食物不够”**最自然的是 **avoid running short / avoid shortages**，不是 *prevent food from being insufficient*。
3. **“固定成本”**在这里不是严格会计意义上的 *fixed cost*。中文想表达“长期反复发生的损失”，所以 **recurring cost** 更准确。
4. **“作出并不理想的选择”**可写 **make less-than-ideal choices**，比 *make not very good choices* 更自然。
5. **“根据销量调整备餐数量”**不要堆名词成 *adjust food preparation quantity based on sales amount*；**adjust how much food is prepared according to demand/sales patterns** 更自然。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level — evaluating urban tree-planting programs

先识别逻辑：**合理机制 → 不能只看一个指标 → 地区差异与维护条件 → 更完整的评估设计**。

**许多城市把植树作为缓解夏季高温的一种手段，因为树荫和蒸腾作用能够降低局部地表温度。然而，如果只比较“种树多的地区”和“种树少的地区”的平均温度，就很难判断观察到的差异有多少真正来自新增树木。不同社区在建筑密度、道路材料、收入水平、灌溉条件和原有绿地比例等方面都可能存在系统性差异。此外，新栽种的树木需要多年才能形成足够大的树冠，如果后期维护不足，其降温效果也可能远低于预期。因此，较严格的评估不仅要记录树木数量，还应跟踪树冠覆盖率、存活率、不同时间段的温度变化以及受影响人群的实际暴露情况。只有把这些信息结合起来，研究者才更有可能判断一个项目在什么条件下有效，以及它是否值得在其他地区推广。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Many cities use tree planting as one strategy for reducing summer heat because shade and evapotranspiration can lower local surface temperatures. However, simply comparing the average temperatures of areas with more trees and areas with fewer trees makes it difficult to determine how much of the observed difference is actually attributable to newly planted trees. Neighborhoods may differ systematically in building density, road materials, income levels, irrigation conditions, and the amount of pre-existing green space. In addition, newly planted trees may take years to develop sufficiently large canopies, and their cooling effect may fall well below expectations if long-term maintenance is inadequate. A more rigorous evaluation should therefore track not only the number of trees planted but also canopy coverage, survival rates, temperature changes at different times of day, and the actual heat exposure of affected populations. Combining these measures makes it more likely that researchers can determine the conditions under which a program is effective and whether it is worth expanding to other areas.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **one strategy for doing sth.** — 做某事的一种策略
- **lower local surface temperatures** — 降低局部地表温度
- **be attributable to...** — 可归因于……
- **differ systematically in...** — 在……方面存在系统性差异
- **pre-existing conditions / green space** — 原有条件 / 原有绿地
- **develop a sufficiently large canopy** — 形成足够大的树冠
- **fall below expectations** — 低于预期
- **long-term maintenance** — 长期维护
- **track survival rates / exposure** — 跟踪存活率 / 暴露情况
- **under the conditions in which...** — 在……条件下
- **expand / scale a program** — 扩大项目实施范围

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“缓解高温”**不要直译成 *relieve high temperature*。根据语境用 **reduce heat, mitigate heat, lower temperatures**。
2. **“有多少真正来自新增树木”**可用 **how much ... is attributable to newly planted trees**，这是学术写作里非常高频的因果表达。
3. **“存在系统性差异”**不是 *have systematic differences* 的机械替换；**neighborhoods may differ systematically in...** 更自然。
4. **“形成树冠”**可用 **develop a canopy**；不要写 *form enough crown*。
5. **“推广到其他地区”**若是项目/政策，**expand or scale the program to other areas** 比 *popularize it* 更合适。
6. **“实际暴露情况”**中的 exposure 是环境与公共健康写作里的常用词，表示人群实际经历了多少热、污染物、噪声等，而不仅是某地平均值。

## 3. Precision rewrite — replace vague Chinese-style English

把下面句子改得更自然、更具体。

1. *The school should do more things to reduce food waste.*
2. *More trees can make the city environment better.*
3. *The results are very obvious, so the policy should be promoted everywhere.*
4. *The two communities are different in many aspects, which may influence the result.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. The school can reduce food waste by offering flexible portion sizes and adjusting preparation volumes to actual demand.
> 2. Increasing tree-canopy coverage can reduce heat exposure in some urban settings, although the effect depends on local conditions and maintenance.
> 3. The results are promising, but evidence from one setting is not enough to justify applying the policy everywhere.
> 4. The two communities differ in several potentially relevant characteristics, and these differences may partly account for the observed outcome.

## 4. Micro-drill — choose the most precise expression

Fill each blank with **account for, adjust to, fall below, run short of,** or **be attributable to**.

1. A restaurant may prepare extra food because it is afraid it will ______ popular dishes during the dinner rush.
2. Delivery schedules should ______ predictable changes in demand across the week.
3. Poor maintenance can cause the long-term benefits of a project to ______ initial expectations.
4. Differences in building density may partly ______ the temperature gap between two neighborhoods.
5. Researchers want to estimate how much of the change can reasonably ______ the intervention itself.

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. run short of
> 2. adjust to
> 3. fall below
> 4. account for
> 5. be attributable to

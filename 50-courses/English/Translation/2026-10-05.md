## Exercise 1 — CET-6 level

### Topic: community sports-center booking

Translate the following passage into natural English.

**为了让更多居民能使用有限的场地，一家社区体育中心上线了预约系统。用户可以提前三天预订羽毛球场和乒乓球台，但如果在开始前两小时内取消，系统会记录一次“临时取消”。最初，管理方只限制连续爽约的用户，后来却发现，许多场地虽然显示“已被预订”，实际却长期空置。原因之一是，有些用户担心临时想运动时没有位置，于是先把多个时间段都订下来，再根据当天安排决定是否前往。体育中心因此把评价指标从“预约率”扩展到“实际使用率”和“及时取消率”，并允许候补用户在有人取消后收到通知。新规则的目标不是惩罚偶尔改变计划的人，而是让有限场地尽可能被真正需要的人使用。**

Try to use:

- **book / reserve a court**
- **cancel at short notice**
- **no-show**
- **actual utilization**
- **waiting list**
- **rather than**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> To allow more residents to use its limited facilities, a community sports center introduced an online booking system. Users can reserve badminton courts and table-tennis tables up to three days in advance, but a cancellation made within two hours of the starting time is recorded as a late cancellation. At first, the center restricted only users who repeatedly failed to show up. It later found, however, that many facilities remained empty even though the system showed them as fully booked. One reason was that some users, worried that no space would be available if they decided to exercise at short notice, reserved several time slots first and chose later whether to attend. The center therefore expanded its evaluation from the booking rate alone to actual utilization and timely-cancellation rates, and it allowed people on a waiting list to receive a notification when a slot became available. The aim of the new rule is not to punish people who occasionally change their plans, but to ensure that limited facilities are used by those who actually need them.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **reserve a time slot / reserve a court**
- **up to three days in advance**
- **cancel at short notice**
- **fail to show up / be a no-show**
- **remain empty despite being booked**
- **actual utilization rate**
- **timely cancellation**
- **join / be on a waiting list**
- **a slot becomes available**
- **the aim is not to A, but to B**

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. “预约场地”通常是 **book / reserve a court or facility**，不是 *appoint a place*.
2. “爽约”可写 **fail to show up / no-show**，不要直译成 *break an appointment* in this service context.
3. “临时取消”更自然的是 **late cancellation / cancel at short notice**.
4. “候补”是 **waiting list / waitlist**，不是 *backup people*.
5. “实际使用率”可写 **actual utilization / usage rate**，而不是 *real use percentage*.

## Exercise 2 — TOEFL / academic level

### Topic: evaluating remote-work productivity

Translate the following passage with careful attention to causation, measurement, and scope.

**一些公司在比较远程办公和办公室办公时，会把员工完成的任务数量作为主要生产率指标。如果远程员工平均完成的任务更多，这一结果值得关注，但不能直接说明远程办公本身提高了生产率。首先，不同岗位的任务难度和可量化程度不同：处理二十个简单请求并不一定比完成三个复杂项目产生更多价值。其次，能够远程工作的员工可能在经验、岗位自主性或家庭环境方面与必须到办公室工作的员工不同。更重要的是，如果考核只统计短期产出，员工可能减少培训、协作和长期规划，而这些活动在当周并不会产生明显的“完成任务数”。更可靠的评价应同时考虑工作质量、错误率、项目周期、员工流动、协作负担和客户结果，并尽可能比较相似岗位在制度变化前后的表现。即使远程办公与某些指标改善稳定相关，也应谨慎说明这种关系是否能够推广到其他行业和团队。**

Preserve these distinctions:

- output quantity vs. value;
- association vs. causation;
- short-term measurable output vs. long-term organizational contribution;
- local result vs. generalization.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Some companies compare remote and office-based work primarily by counting the number of tasks employees complete. If remote workers complete more tasks on average, the finding deserves attention, but it does not directly establish that remote work itself increases productivity. First, jobs differ in both task difficulty and measurability: handling twenty simple requests does not necessarily create more value than completing three complex projects. Second, employees who are able to work remotely may differ from employees who must work on site in experience, job autonomy, or home working conditions. More importantly, if an evaluation focuses only on short-term output, employees may reduce time spent on training, collaboration, and long-term planning, activities that may not produce an obvious increase in the week's task count. A more reliable evaluation should consider work quality, error rates, project duration, employee turnover, coordination costs, and customer outcomes, and should compare similar roles before and after a policy change whenever possible. Even if remote work remains consistently associated with improvements in some metrics, researchers should be cautious about whether the relationship generalizes to other industries and teams.

### Academic structures and collocations

- **use X as a proxy for Y**
- **does not directly establish that**
- **differ in task difficulty and measurability**
- **create / generate value**
- **job autonomy**
- **short-term output**
- **long-term organizational contribution**
- **coordination costs / collaboration burden**
- **compare similar roles before and after**
- **generalize to other settings**

### Common transfer errors

1. “生产率指标”常写 **productivity metric / measure**, not *productivity index* unless it is literally an index.
2. “完成任务数量”写 **the number of tasks completed**, not *task finish quantity*.
3. “岗位自主性”是 **job autonomy**, not *work freedom degree*.
4. “员工流动”在组织研究中通常是 **employee turnover**, not *employee flow*.
5. “推广到其他行业”可写 **generalize to other industries**, not *promote to other industries*.
6. “前后比较”可写 **before-and-after comparison**; if a control group is available, specify that separately.

## Precision / rewrite exercise

Rewrite each claim so it is more defensible.

1. Fully booked courts mean the sports center is being used efficiently.
2. Remote workers are more productive because they finish more tasks.
3. A lower error rate proves that the new policy is better.
4. A rule that reduces no-shows has no downside.
5. If two groups differ after a policy change, the policy caused the difference.

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. A high booking rate may indicate strong demand, but actual utilization must be checked before concluding that the facilities are being used efficiently.
> 2. Remote workers may complete more tasks on average, but task counts alone do not establish higher productivity or show that remote work caused the difference.
> 3. A lower error rate is one useful outcome, but a broader evaluation should also consider quality, speed, workload, and other relevant effects.
> 4. A rule may reduce no-shows while also creating costs, such as less flexibility for users who face genuine last-minute changes.
> 5. A difference observed after a policy change may be consistent with an effect of the policy, but alternative explanations and baseline differences should also be considered.

## Spaced review — earlier language

### Sentence 1

**一项服务已经向所有人开放，并不意味着用户采用率会自动提高；如果流程太复杂，实际使用仍可能很低。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Making a service available to everyone does not mean that user uptake will automatically increase; actual use may remain low if the process is too complicated.

### Sentence 2

**如果三个数据源都依赖同一个有偏差的样本，那么看似一致的结果也不应被当作完全独立的佐证。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> If three data sources all rely on the same biased sample, apparently consistent results should not be treated as fully independent corroboration.

## 1. CET-6 level — managing shared-bike parking

先看逻辑：**便利性 → 问题来源 → 为什么单靠处罚不够 → 更可执行的治理方式**。

**共享单车给城市短距离出行带来了很大便利，但在地铁站、商业区和学校周边，车辆集中停放也常常占用人行道。这个问题并不完全是因为用户缺乏规则意识。有些区域本来就缺少足够清晰、足够方便的停车点，如果管理方只是在违规后处罚用户，却没有改善停车位置和提示方式，问题往往会反复出现。更有效的做法可能是根据不同时段的使用量动态调整停车区域，在高需求地点增加临时停车空间，并通过应用程序提前提醒用户附近哪些位置仍有容量。这样既能保留共享单车的便利性，也能减少它对步行空间的影响。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Shared bikes have made short urban trips much more convenient, but large numbers of bikes parked around subway stations, commercial districts, and schools can also obstruct sidewalks. This problem is not entirely caused by users' lack of awareness of the rules. Some areas simply do not provide enough clearly marked and conveniently located parking spaces. If operators only penalize users after improper parking occurs without improving parking locations or guidance, the same problem is likely to recur. A more effective approach may be to adjust parking zones dynamically according to demand at different times of day, provide temporary parking capacity in high-demand areas, and use the app to tell users in advance where nearby spaces are still available. This can preserve the convenience of shared bikes while reducing their impact on pedestrian space.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **short urban trips** — 城市短距离出行
- **obstruct a sidewalk / passage** — 占用、阻碍人行道或通道
- **be entirely caused by...** — 完全由……造成
- **clearly marked parking spaces** — 标识清楚的停车位
- **penalize users for...** — 因……处罚用户
- **the problem is likely to recur** — 问题很可能再次出现
- **adjust dynamically according to demand** — 根据需求动态调整
- **high-demand areas** — 高需求区域
- **available capacity / available spaces** — 剩余容量 / 可用位置
- **preserve convenience while reducing...** — 在保留便利性的同时减少……

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“占用人行道”**不一定要写成 *occupy the sidewalk*。如果强调阻碍通行，**obstruct sidewalks** 更准确；如果只是描述停放位置，**take up sidewalk space** 也自然。
2. **“规则意识不足”**不要机械翻译成 *rule consciousness is low*。自然表达是 **lack awareness of the rules**, **poor compliance**, 或 **be less aware of parking rules**。
3. **“问题反复出现”**可用 **the problem recurs / keeps recurring**，比 *the problem happens again and again* 更简洁。
4. **“动态调整”**在政策与系统语境中常用 **adjust dynamically**，也可以具体写成 **change the size or location of parking zones as demand changes**。
5. **“附近哪些位置仍有容量”**不要写 *which places still have volume*。这里的“容量”是 **capacity / available spaces**。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level — evaluating AI-generated feedback in education

先判断逻辑：**潜在优势 → 不能只看速度或满意度 → 关键混杂因素 → 更完整的评估设计**。

**越来越多的学校开始尝试使用生成式人工智能为学生提供写作反馈。与教师逐篇批改相比，这类系统可以在较短时间内处理大量文本，因此看上去能够显著提高反馈效率。然而，反馈生成得更快，并不等于学生学得更好。如果研究只比较“使用人工智能反馈的学生”和“没有使用的学生”的期末成绩，就很难判断差异究竟来自反馈工具本身，还是来自学生原有水平、使用频率、教师指导方式或课程难度。除此之外，学生是否真正理解并采用建议，也可能比系统给出的建议数量更重要。因此，较严格的评估应同时考察反馈质量、学生修改行为、长期写作表现以及教师投入时间，并尽可能控制两组学生在起点上的差异。只有这样，研究者才更有可能判断这种工具在什么条件下真正有帮助，以及它能否替代部分传统反馈，而不是简单地把“更快”当成“更有效”。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> An increasing number of schools are experimenting with generative AI systems that provide students with feedback on their writing. Compared with teachers reviewing each paper individually, these systems can process large amounts of text in a short period of time and therefore appear capable of greatly increasing feedback efficiency. However, faster feedback does not necessarily mean better learning. If a study simply compares the final grades of students who use AI-generated feedback with those of students who do not, it is difficult to determine whether the observed difference is attributable to the feedback tool itself or to factors such as students' prior ability, frequency of use, the type of teacher guidance they receive, or course difficulty. In addition, whether students actually understand and apply the suggestions may matter more than the sheer number of comments the system produces. A more rigorous evaluation should therefore examine feedback quality, students' revision behavior, long-term writing performance, and the amount of teacher time required, while controlling as far as possible for differences between the groups at baseline. Only then can researchers better determine the conditions under which such tools are genuinely helpful and whether they can replace part of traditional feedback rather than simply treating “faster” as equivalent to “more effective.”

### Useful structures and collocations

- **experiment with a system / approach** — 试用、探索某个系统或方法
- **provide feedback on writing** — 对写作提供反馈
- **process large amounts of text** — 处理大量文本
- **appear capable of doing sth.** — 看上去能够……
- **does not necessarily mean...** — 并不必然意味着……
- **the observed difference** — 观察到的差异
- **be attributable to...** — 可归因于……
- **prior ability / prior attainment** — 原有能力 / 起点水平
- **apply a suggestion** — 采用建议
- **revision behavior** — 修改行为
- **control for differences at baseline** — 控制起点差异
- **under the conditions in which...** — 在……条件下
- **treat A as equivalent to B** — 把 A 当作等同于 B

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“逐篇批改”**不需要逐字翻成 *correct one article after another*。写作课里更自然的是 **review each paper individually / mark each assignment individually**。
2. **“提高反馈效率”**可以写 **increase feedback efficiency**，但若希望更具体，最好说明是 **reduce turnaround time** 或 **provide feedback at greater scale**。
3. **“学得更好”**在学术语境中用 **better learning outcomes / improved learning**，而不是 *study better*。
4. **“原有水平”**常用 **prior ability, prior performance, baseline level**。**original level** 虽然能懂，但不够地道。
5. **“真正理解并采用建议”**可写 **understand and apply the suggestions**。不要机械写 *accept the advice*，因为学生可能接受但没有实际修改。
6. **“控制两组起点差异”**是研究写作中的固定思路：**control for baseline differences between the groups**。
7. **“更快不等于更有效”**最自然的概念表达之一是 **faster is not necessarily more effective**；正式一点可写 **speed should not be treated as equivalent to effectiveness**。

## 3. Precision rewrite — make the claim narrower and more defensible

把下面句子改得更精确。重点不是“高级词汇”，而是避免把相关性写成因果、把一个指标写成全部结果。

1. *Shared-bike users cause sidewalk disorder because they do not follow rules.*
2. *AI feedback improves students' writing because it gives more comments.*
3. *The new policy is effective because complaints decreased after it was introduced.*
4. *Students who used the tool got higher scores, which proves the tool works.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Poor parking behavior can contribute to sidewalk obstruction, but inadequate parking capacity and unclear guidance may also play a role.
> 2. AI-generated feedback may help some students revise more efficiently, but the number of comments alone does not establish that learning has improved.
> 3. Complaints decreased after the policy was introduced, although the change cannot be attributed to the policy without considering other factors.
> 4. Students who used the tool received higher scores, but the comparison does not by itself show that the tool caused the difference.

## 4. Micro-drill — causal precision

Choose from **contribute to, be attributable to, account for, recur, control for**. Change the form if necessary.

1. Poorly located parking areas may ______ congestion around station entrances.
2. Researchers should ______ prior differences between the two groups before comparing outcomes.
3. Some of the improvement may ______ greater teacher support rather than the software itself.
4. A design problem that is not addressed at its source is likely to ______.
5. Baseline ability may ______ part of the score difference between the groups.

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. contribute to
> 2. control for
> 3. be attributable to
> 4. recur
> 5. account for

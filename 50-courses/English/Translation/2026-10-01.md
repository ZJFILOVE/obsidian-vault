## Exercise 1 — CET-6 level

### Topic: bicycle parking on a university campus

Translate the passage before opening the answer.

**某大学过去一直把自行车停车管理理解为“增加车架数量”的问题。然而，学校在新学期调查后发现，最拥挤的区域并不一定是车架总数最少的地方。有些教学楼门口上午八点半之前会迅速停满，但十点以后会空出大量位置；另一些区域虽然全天都有空位，却因为距离主要入口较远，很少有人使用。学校因此决定不再只统计车架数量，而是记录不同时间段的使用率、学生步行到教学楼入口所需的时间，以及不规范停车最容易发生的位置。根据这些数据，学校把部分车架移到需求更高的位置，并在几个入口附近增加了短时停车区。管理人员认为，这种调整比单纯继续增加设施更有效，但他们也指出，一个学期的数据还不足以证明新方案在所有季节都同样有效。**

Try to use: **treat X as a problem of**, **not necessarily**, **rather than simply**, **utilization rate**, **based on these data**, **not sufficient to establish that**.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> A university had long treated bicycle-parking management mainly as a problem of increasing the number of racks. However, a survey conducted at the beginning of the new semester showed that the most crowded areas were not necessarily those with the fewest racks overall. Some spaces outside classroom buildings filled up rapidly before 8:30 a.m. but had many empty spots after 10:00, while other areas had spare capacity throughout the day yet were rarely used because they were farther from the main entrances. The university therefore decided to measure more than the total number of racks. It began recording utilization rates at different times of day, the time students needed to walk from parking areas to building entrances, and the locations where improper parking occurred most frequently. Based on these data, some racks were moved to higher-demand locations and several short-term parking zones were added near major entrances. Administrators believe that this adjustment is more effective than simply adding more facilities, but they also note that data from a single semester are not sufficient to establish that the new arrangement will work equally well in every season.

### Useful structures and transfer errors

**1. 把……理解为……的问题**

Use **treat / frame / view X as a problem of Y**. Do not mechanically use *understand X as a problem to...*.

**2. 并不一定**

Use **not necessarily**. This is weaker and more precise than saying something is definitely not true.

**3. 有空位**

Useful choices include **have spare capacity**, **have available spaces**, and **remain underused**. In transport and facility planning, **capacity** is often more natural than repeatedly saying “empty places.”

**4. 不再只统计**

Use **move beyond simply counting...**, **measure more than...**, or **not rely solely on...**.

**5. 更有效 / 更高效**

- **effective** = achieves the intended result
- **efficient** = achieves it with relatively little time, money, or waste

Do not translate both as the same word automatically.

**6. 不足以证明**

Use **is not sufficient to establish that...** or **does not by itself establish that...**. These are useful academic structures when evidence is limited.

## Exercise 2 — TOEFL / academic level

### Topic: nighttime phone use and next-day concentration

Translate the passage with careful attention to association, causation, and scope.

**一项关于大学生睡前手机使用的研究发现，在调查样本中，报告夜间使用手机时间较长的学生，第二天完成注意力任务时的平均表现较差。这个结果容易被概括成“睡前玩手机会直接降低第二天的注意力”，但这样的表述超过了研究设计本身能够支持的范围。首先，手机使用时间主要来自参与者自我报告，因此可能受到记忆误差影响。其次，使用手机较多的学生也可能同时面临更大的学业压力、更晚的作息或更高的咖啡因摄入，而这些因素本身也可能影响第二天的表现。即使研究者在统计模型中控制了其中一些变量，也不能保证所有相关差异都被完全排除。因此，更谨慎的结论是：在该样本和测量条件下，较长的夜间手机使用与较差的次日注意力表现相关，但要确定因果关系，还需要更严格的实验设计、客观的使用记录以及对睡眠本身的直接测量。**

Before checking the answer, distinguish:

- **association** from **causation**;
- self-reported exposure from objective measurement;
- statistical adjustment from complete elimination of confounding;
- a finding in one sample from a universal claim.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> A study of university students' phone use before sleep found that, within the surveyed sample, students who reported spending more time on their phones at night performed worse on average on an attention task the following day. It would be easy to summarize this result as “using a phone before bed directly reduces next-day attention,” but such a statement goes beyond what the study design can support. First, phone-use duration was based mainly on participants' self-reports and may therefore be subject to recall error. Second, students who used their phones more extensively may also have experienced greater academic stress, kept later schedules, or consumed more caffeine, all of which could independently affect next-day performance. Even if researchers adjusted for some of these variables in a statistical model, this would not guarantee that all relevant differences had been fully eliminated. A more cautious conclusion is that, under the sampling and measurement conditions of this study, longer nighttime phone use was associated with poorer next-day attention performance. Establishing a causal relationship would require a more rigorous experimental design, objective usage records, and direct measurement of sleep itself.

### Useful structures and transfer errors

**1. 在调查样本中**

Keep the scope visible with **within the surveyed sample** or **among participants in the sample**.

**2. 超过研究设计能够支持的范围**

Use **go beyond what the study design can support** or **make a stronger claim than the evidence warrants**.

**3. 受到记忆误差影响**

Use **be subject to recall error**. Compare:
- **recall error** — inaccurate memory;
- **measurement error** — broader error in measurement;
- **reporting bias** — systematic distortion in what is reported.

**4. 控制变量**

Use **adjust for variables** or **control for potential confounders**. In a regression context, **adjust for** is often the natural verb phrase.

**5. 相关**

Use **be associated with** when the design does not establish causation. Avoid casually replacing it with **cause, lead to, or result in**.

**6. 更严格的实验设计**

Use **a more rigorous experimental design**, not *a stricter experiment design*.

## Precision / rewrite exercise

Rewrite each sentence so the claim matches the evidence.

1. **Students park improperly because there are not enough bike racks.**  
   Make lack of convenient parking only one possible explanation.

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrite
> A shortage of conveniently located parking may contribute to improper bicycle parking, but walking distance, time pressure, and enforcement may also matter.

2. **The phone study proved that screen time damages attention.**  
   Rewrite it as an observational finding.

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrite
> The study found an association between longer reported nighttime phone use and poorer next-day attention performance, but the observational design did not by itself establish causation.

3. **Controlling for stress removed the confounding problem.**

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrite
> Adjusting for measured stress reduced one potential source of confounding, but unmeasured or imperfectly measured differences may still remain.

4. **The new bicycle layout works better.**  
   Add scope and time.

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrite
> During the semester observed, the revised layout appeared to match demand more closely at several high-use locations, although its performance in other seasons remains uncertain.

## Spaced review — earlier language

### Review 1

**即使多个网站重复了同一个说法，如果它们都依赖同一份新闻稿，这些网页也不能被视为相互独立的证据。**

Try to reuse **corroborate** or **independent evidence**.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Even if several websites repeat the same claim, they do not constitute independent evidence if they all rely on the same press release, and therefore they do not independently corroborate one another.

### Review 2

**目前的结果与一种相互作用机制一致，但研究设计还不能把这种机制与另一种可能的解释完全区分开。**

Try to reuse **be consistent with** and **disentangle A from B**.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> The current results are consistent with an interaction mechanism, but the study design cannot yet fully disentangle that mechanism from an alternative explanation.

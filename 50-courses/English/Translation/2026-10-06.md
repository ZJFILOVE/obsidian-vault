## Exercise 1 — CET-6 level

### Topic: university parcel lockers

近年来，许多高校在宿舍区附近安装了智能快递柜，希望减少学生排队取件的时间，也减轻快递站在高峰期的压力。这样的设备确实提高了便利性，但“安装了更多柜子”并不等于“取件体验一定更好”。如果柜子离部分宿舍太远，或者大件包裹仍然只能到人工窗口领取，学生可能只是把排队时间换成了更长的步行时间。此外，若系统在晚上经常出现验证码延迟或柜门无法打开的问题，设备数量再多也难以解决核心矛盾。因此，学校在评估效果时，不应只统计新增了多少个柜子，还应观察平均取件时间、故障率、不同宿舍区之间的差异，以及学生在高峰期是否真的少排了队。

Translate the passage before opening the answer.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> In recent years, many universities have installed smart parcel lockers near student dormitories in the hope of shortening pickup lines and reducing pressure on delivery stations during peak hours. Such equipment can certainly make parcel collection more convenient, but installing more lockers does not necessarily mean that the overall pickup experience will improve. If the lockers are too far from some dormitories, or if large packages still have to be collected at a staffed counter, students may simply exchange waiting time for a longer walk. In addition, if verification codes are frequently delayed at night or locker doors fail to open, increasing the number of units will not solve the underlying problem. Therefore, when evaluating the system, universities should not look only at how many new lockers have been added. They should also examine average pickup time, failure rates, differences among dormitory areas, and whether students actually spend less time in queues during peak periods.

## Useful structures from Exercise 1

### 1. in the hope of doing something

Use it for a purpose or intended outcome.

- install lockers **in the hope of reducing** waiting time
- extend opening hours **in the hope of improving** access

### 2. does not necessarily mean that...

A high-value structure for avoiding overclaiming.

> More infrastructure **does not necessarily mean that** the service is more convenient.

### 3. exchange A for B

Useful when one problem is replaced by another.

> Students may **exchange waiting time for a longer walk**.

### 4. solve the underlying problem

More precise than simply saying **solve the problem** when surface symptoms and causes differ.

### 5. examine whether...

Natural academic alternative to repeated **see if**.

## Common Chinese → English transfer errors

1. **“减少排队时间”**  
   Prefer **reduce waiting time / shorten queues / spend less time in queues**. Avoid **reduce the time of lining up**.

2. **“快递柜”**  
   **parcel locker / package locker** is natural. **express cabinet** is not.

3. **“人工窗口”**  
   Use **staffed counter / service counter**, not **artificial window**.

4. **“核心矛盾”**  
   In ordinary English prose, **underlying problem / central problem** is usually better than mechanically translating it as **principal contradiction**.

5. **“体验更好”**  
   Use **improve the user experience / make pickup more convenient**, depending on context.

## Exercise 2 — TOEFL / academic level

### Topic: evaluating an urban tree-planting program

某城市计划在未来五年内大规模增加街道树木，以降低夏季地表温度并改善步行环境。树木能够提供遮阴，也可能通过蒸腾作用降低周围温度，因此这一政策具有合理的物理机制。然而，若仅比较“种树前”和“种树后”的平均温度，就很难判断观察到的变化究竟来自树木本身，还是来自同期天气差异、道路改造或建筑遮挡等因素。不同树种的冠幅、生长速度和耐旱性也并不相同，而新种植的树木往往需要多年才能形成足够的树冠。因此，更严格的评估应同时设置可比路段，记录树冠覆盖率和维护状况，并在多个夏季重复测量。即使最终发现部分街区明显降温，也不能自动推断相同方案在所有地区都会产生同样效果，因为城市形态、气候和养护能力都会改变政策效果。

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> A city plans to substantially increase the number of street trees over the next five years in order to reduce summer surface temperatures and improve conditions for pedestrians. Trees provide shade and may also cool their surroundings through evapotranspiration, so the policy has a plausible physical mechanism. However, simply comparing average temperatures before and after planting would make it difficult to determine whether any observed change was caused by the trees themselves or by differences in weather, road redesign, building shade, or other factors occurring at the same time. Tree species also differ in canopy size, growth rate, and drought tolerance, while newly planted trees often need years to develop substantial crowns. A more rigorous evaluation should therefore include comparable street segments, track canopy coverage and maintenance conditions, and repeat measurements across multiple summers. Even if some neighborhoods ultimately show clear cooling, it would not automatically follow that the same program would produce the same effect everywhere, because urban form, climate, and maintenance capacity can all modify the outcome.

## Useful structures from Exercise 2

### plausible mechanism

A mechanism can be scientifically reasonable before its real-world effect size is known.

> The intervention has a **plausible mechanism**, but its actual impact still needs to be measured.

### determine whether A was caused by B

Better than saying **prove B caused A** when evidence is incomplete.

### factors occurring at the same time

Useful for introducing confounding variables in nontechnical language.

### a more rigorous evaluation should...

A reusable academic frame for improving study design.

### it would not automatically follow that...

Excellent for limiting generalization.

> Success in one district does not mean **it would automatically follow that** the same policy works everywhere.

## Transfer errors to avoid

1. **“大规模增加”**  
   Use **substantially increase / expand on a large scale**, depending on the noun.

2. **“具有合理的物理机制”**  
   Prefer **has a plausible physical mechanism** rather than **has a reasonable physics principle**.

3. **“同期天气差异”**  
   Use **differences in weather over the same period / contemporaneous weather differences**.

4. **“可比路段”**  
   Use **comparable street segments**, not **similar road parts**.

5. **“不能自动推断”**  
   Use **cannot automatically infer / it does not automatically follow that**.

## Precision / rewrite exercise

Rewrite each sentence so that it becomes more precise.

### A

The lockers were installed, so the problem was solved.

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrite
> The lockers increased pickup capacity, but whether they solved the broader service problem depends on location, reliability, and actual waiting times.

### B

The tree-planting program lowered the temperature.

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrite
> Temperatures were lower after the program was introduced, but additional comparisons are needed before attributing the change specifically to tree planting.

### C

The policy worked in one district, so the city should use it everywhere.

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrite
> The result in one district supports further testing, but the effect may vary with local climate, street geometry, tree species, and maintenance.

### D

More technology always means better access.

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrite
> Additional technology can improve access when it removes a real barrier, but availability alone does not guarantee that people can or will use it effectively.

## 1. CET-6 level — improving parcel pickup on campus

先判断逻辑：**需求增长 → 表面解决方案 → 为什么仍可能拥堵 → 更合理的流程设计 → 预期效果但不过度承诺**。

**随着网购数量增加，大学校园里的快递点在中午和傍晚经常出现长时间排队。最直接的办法似乎是增加取件窗口，但如果大量学生仍集中在同一时间到达，单纯增加窗口未必能彻底解决拥堵。一些快递点开始通过短信提醒、分时段取件和自助取件柜来分散人流。这样的措施能否有效，取决于学生是否愿意按照建议时间前往，以及系统是否能够及时更新包裹状态。为了减少无效等待，管理者还应把“包裹已经到站”和“包裹已经可以领取”区分开来，并在高峰期安排工作人员处理异常情况。合理的流程设计不一定能让排队完全消失，但可以降低高峰期压力，并减少学生因信息不准确而反复前往取件点的情况。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> As online shopping has grown, parcel pickup points on university campuses often face long queues around noon and in the evening. The most obvious response may seem to be opening more service counters, but adding counters alone may not eliminate congestion if large numbers of students still arrive at the same time. Some pickup points have begun using text-message reminders, scheduled pickup periods, and self-service lockers to spread demand more evenly throughout the day. Whether these measures work depends partly on whether students are willing to follow the suggested pickup times and whether the system updates parcel status promptly. To reduce unnecessary waiting, managers should also distinguish between a parcel that has arrived at the facility and one that is actually ready for collection, while assigning staff to handle exceptions during peak periods. Better process design may not remove queues completely, but it can reduce peak pressure and prevent students from making repeated trips because of inaccurate information.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **face long queues** — 面临长队
- **the most obvious response may seem to be...** — 最直接的办法似乎是……
- **adding X alone may not...** — 单纯增加 X 未必能……
- **spread demand more evenly** — 更均匀地分散需求
- **scheduled pickup periods** — 分时段取件
- **update parcel status promptly** — 及时更新包裹状态
- **be ready for collection** — 已经可以领取
- **handle exceptions** — 处理异常情况
- **during peak periods** — 在高峰时段
- **reduce unnecessary waiting** — 减少无效等待
- **make repeated trips** — 反复前往

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“增加窗口”**不要机械写成 *increase windows*. 在服务场景中可以写 **open more service counters / add more pickup counters**。
2. **“分散人流”**不宜写 *disperse people flow*. 更自然的是 **spread demand more evenly**, **reduce crowding**, 或 **stagger arrivals**。
3. **“及时更新”**可以写 **update promptly / in a timely manner**；*update timely* 不自然。
4. **“包裹到站”**与 **“可以领取”**要区分。前者可写 **arrive at the facility**，后者是 **be ready for collection / pickup**。
5. **“无效等待”**通常不是 *invalid waiting*. 根据语境写 **unnecessary waiting / avoidable waiting time**。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level — evaluating urban tree planting and heat reduction

先判断逻辑：**政策目标 → 简单相关性的问题 → 基线差异与选择偏差 → 指标与时间尺度 → 更有解释力的评估设计**。

**许多城市把增加树木覆盖率视为缓解夏季高温的一种办法。理论上，树荫可以减少地表受到的太阳辐射，植物蒸腾也可能降低局部温度，因此“树更多的街区更凉”似乎很容易理解。然而，如果研究只是比较树木较多和较少的街区，就很难判断温度差异究竟由树木本身造成，还是与建筑密度、道路宽度、绿地面积、收入水平和临近水体等因素有关。此外，白天平均气温并不是唯一值得关注的指标：行人实际感受到的热暴露、夜间降温速度以及极端高温期间的最高温度都可能具有不同意义。更严格的评估应尽可能记录植树前的基线条件，对相似街区进行比较，并在多个季节持续测量温度、遮阴程度和行人活动。如果研究者还能说明效果在不同街道形态和不同树种之间是否一致，就能更准确地判断植树在什么条件下最有效，而不是把所有降温都简单归因于树木数量。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Many cities view greater tree cover as one way to reduce summer heat. In principle, shade can reduce the amount of solar radiation reaching paved surfaces, while plant transpiration may also lower local temperatures, so the idea that greener neighborhoods tend to be cooler is intuitively appealing. However, a study that simply compares neighborhoods with more and fewer trees cannot easily determine whether the temperature difference is caused by tree cover itself or by other factors such as building density, street width, the amount of open green space, income levels, or proximity to water. Daytime average temperature is also not the only relevant outcome. Pedestrians' actual heat exposure, the rate of nighttime cooling, and peak temperatures during extreme heat events may each matter in different ways. A more rigorous evaluation should document baseline conditions before planting, compare similar neighborhoods where possible, and measure temperature, shade, and pedestrian activity across multiple seasons. If researchers can also determine whether the effects are consistent across different street forms and tree species, they can identify more precisely the conditions under which tree planting is most effective rather than attributing every reduction in heat simply to the number of trees.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **view A as one way to...** — 把 A 视为……的一种办法
- **reduce summer heat** — 缓解夏季高温
- **be intuitively appealing** — 直觉上很有吸引力 / 很容易接受
- **cannot easily determine whether...** — 很难判断是否……
- **be caused by A or by other factors** — 是由 A 还是其他因素造成
- **proximity to water** — 与水体的接近程度
- **relevant outcome** — 值得关注的结果指标
- **heat exposure** — 热暴露
- **document baseline conditions** — 记录基线条件
- **across multiple seasons** — 跨多个季节
- **be consistent across...** — 在不同……中是否一致
- **attribute a change to...** — 把变化归因于……

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“树木覆盖率”**可写 **tree cover / tree canopy cover**。*tree coverage rate* 虽能理解，但不够自然。
2. **“缓解高温”**通常写 **reduce heat / mitigate heat / reduce heat exposure**，不要写 *relieve high temperature*。
3. **“局部温度”**在环境研究里可写 **local temperature**，比 *partial temperature* 正确得多。
4. **“由……造成”**在学术语境中应注意证据强度。**be caused by** 是强因果；如果证据有限，可用 **may reflect / may be partly attributable to**。
5. **“不同街道形态”**可写 **different street forms / urban forms / street configurations**，不要逐字写 *different street shapes*。
6. **“最有效”**最好说明条件：**most effective under particular conditions**，避免无条件的 *the most effective solution*。

## 3. Precision rewrite — separate timing, association, and causation

把下面句子改得更精确。

1. *The new lockers solved the parcel problem because queues became shorter.*
2. *Neighborhoods with more trees are cooler, so trees cause all of the temperature difference.*
3. *A reminder message makes students pick up parcels earlier.*
4. *Tree planting works in cities.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Queues became shorter after the lockers were introduced, but the change may also reflect differences in arrival patterns, staffing, or demand.
> 2. Neighborhoods with more tree cover may be cooler on average, but other differences between neighborhoods must be considered before the temperature gap can be attributed to trees.
> 3. Reminder messages may encourage some students to collect parcels earlier, but their effect depends on timing, message design, and user behavior.
> 4. Tree planting can reduce heat exposure under some conditions, but the size of the effect depends on local climate, urban form, tree species, and the outcome being measured.

## 4. Micro-drill — choose the more precise academic chunk

Choose from **account for, baseline, be attributable to, stagger, prompt, capture, consistent across,** and **rule out**. Change the form if necessary.

1. Researchers should record ______ conditions before a new intervention begins.
2. A single daytime temperature may fail to ______ how uncomfortable a street feels during an extreme heat event.
3. Differences in building density may ______ part of the temperature gap between neighborhoods.
4. Pickup systems can ______ arrivals by assigning different collection periods.
5. A message sent shortly before pickup may ______ users to act, but not everyone will respond.
6. Researchers should test whether the result is ______ different seasons and street types.
7. The observed reduction cannot automatically ______ the new policy.
8. Additional measurements can help ______ some alternative explanations.

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. baseline
> 2. capture
> 3. account for
> 4. stagger
> 5. prompt
> 6. consistent across
> 7. be attributable to
> 8. rule out

## 5. Spaced review

先自己翻译，再展开答案。下面复习的是前几天出现过的语言，不是今天 Daily 的改写。

1. **一个结果与某项措施同时出现，并不能自动说明这个结果是由该措施造成的。**
2. **如果一个指标不能反映长期效果，就不应只凭这个指标判断项目是否成功。**

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. The fact that an outcome appears alongside a measure does not automatically show that the outcome was caused by that measure.
> 2. If a measure fails to capture long-term effects, it should not be used on its own to determine whether a program is successful.

## 6. Short precision exercise — one claim, three strengths

把“新的取件系统减少了排队”分别写成三种证据强度。

### A. Only describe the timing

只写前后变化，不写因果。

### B. Express a plausible contribution

使用 **may have contributed to**。

### C. Make a stronger causal claim with a condition

加入“如果同期包裹量和人员配置没有明显变化”这一限定。

> [!answer]- Possible answers
> A. Average queue length fell after the new pickup system was introduced.
> B. The new pickup system may have contributed to the reduction in average queue length.
> C. If parcel volume and staffing remained broadly unchanged over the same period, the evidence would provide stronger support for the conclusion that the new pickup system reduced queue length.

# Translation — Day 11

## Chinese → English A
越来越多的大学生意识到，保持专注并不只是减少手机的使用。即使没有手机，人们也可能因为疲劳、压力或周围的环境而分心。因此，提高注意力需要学生了解哪些因素会影响自己的学习，并根据实际情况调整学习习惯。

### Reference
An increasing number of university students have realized that staying focused is not simply a matter of reducing phone use. Even without a phone, people may be distracted by fatigue, stress, or their surroundings. Therefore, improving concentration requires students to understand what factors affect their learning and adjust their study habits according to their actual needs.

## Chinese → English B
当人们取得成功时，他们往往把结果归因于自己的能力和努力；而面对失败时，他们有时更容易认为是外部因素造成的。然而，这种判断并不总是准确。为了真正从经验中学习，我们需要认真分析导致结果的各种因素，而不是只选择最容易接受的解释。

### Reference
When people succeed, they tend to attribute the result to their own ability and effort; when they fail, however, they may be more likely to blame external factors. Such judgments are not always accurate. To truly learn from experience, we need to carefully analyze the various factors that contribute to an outcome rather than simply choose the explanation that is easiest to accept.

## English → Chinese
People often assume that having more choices gives them greater freedom. Yet when the number of options becomes extremely large, making a decision can require considerable time and mental effort. Some people may even delay making a choice because they are worried that another option could be better. The problem, therefore, is not necessarily a lack of choice, but the difficulty of evaluating the choices available.

## Exercise 1 — CET-6

**每到学期结束，不少大学生都会把已经用过的教材低价出售，也有人希望用较低的成本购买下学期需要的书。为了减少信息不对称，一些学校尝试建立校内二手书交换平台，让学生可以按课程名称、版本和书况搜索教材。这样的平台确实能提高交易效率，但如果信息长期不更新，买家仍可能联系到已经售出的书；如果只按最低价格排序，也可能鼓励用户忽略版本差异和书籍损坏情况。因此，一个真正好用的平台不仅要让发布和搜索更方便，还应及时标记交易状态，并提醒学生核对教材版本。技术可以降低交易成本，但不能代替基本的信息核实。**

Translate it into natural English. Use at least one if-clause and distinguish **price, edition, condition** precisely.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> At the end of each semester, many university students sell textbooks they no longer need, while others look for cheaper copies required for the next term. To reduce information gaps, some universities have introduced campus second-hand book platforms that allow students to search by course, edition, and condition. These platforms can improve efficiency, but problems remain if listings are not updated. Buyers may still contact sellers about books that have already been sold, and sorting only by the lowest price may encourage users to overlook edition differences or damage. A useful platform should make posting and searching easy while also updating listing status promptly and reminding students to verify the required edition. Technology can reduce transaction costs, but it cannot replace basic verification.

### Structures and collocations

- **information gap / information asymmetry** — 信息不对称
- **book condition** — 书况；not *book situation*
- **required edition / older edition** — 教材版本通常用 **edition**
- **search by course / filter by edition / sort by price**
- **mark an item as sold / update a listing**
- **be no substitute for verification**

### Common transfer errors

1. 周期性事实里的“会”通常不需要 **will**: **Many students sell...**
2. “较低成本”用 **at a lower cost / for a lower price**，不要用 *with a lower cost*。
3. “按价格排序”用 **sort by price**，不要机械翻译成 *according to price*。
4. “核对版本”用 **check / verify the edition**。

## Exercise 2 — TOEFL / academic

**越来越多的博物馆把藏品图像和基本信息上传到网络，这通常被描述为“扩大公众访问”。数字化确实可以让无法亲自到馆的人看到更多材料，但“可以在线查看”并不等于“所有藏品都以同样方式被看见”。博物馆往往优先数字化保存状况较好、已有高质量摄影或更容易吸引公众兴趣的作品，因此线上数据库本身可能带有选择偏差。此外，搜索排序、关键词和页面设计也会影响哪些作品更容易被发现。如果研究者利用线上藏品来判断一个博物馆整体收藏的构成，就需要说明数据库覆盖率、缺失材料以及数字化标准。数字化降低了地理访问门槛，但只有在用户理解数据是如何被选择、描述和排序的情况下，它才能真正提高研究的透明度。**

Translate it into academic English. Distinguish **access** from **representativeness**, express **selection bias** naturally, and use at least two caution structures.

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> An increasing number of museums are putting images and basic information about their collections online, a practice often described as expanding public access. Digitization can indeed allow people who cannot visit in person to examine far more material, but online availability does not mean that every object is equally visible or that the digital collection is fully representative of the museum's holdings. Institutions may prioritize works that are in better condition, already have high-quality photography, or are more likely to attract public interest. The resulting database may therefore contain selection bias. Search rankings, keywords, and interface design can further shape which objects users are most likely to discover. Researchers who use online collections to draw conclusions about a museum's holdings should report the database's coverage, missing material, and digitization criteria. Digitization lowers geographical barriers to access, but it improves research transparency only when users understand how the material has been selected, described, and ranked.

### Academic language

- **expand public access / barriers to access**
- **be representative of the full collection**
- **introduce / contain selection bias**
- **database coverage / known gaps**
- **digitization criteria**
- **draw conclusions about**

Useful caution patterns:

- **X does not necessarily imply Y.**
- **The extent to which X represents Y depends on...**
- **Conclusions should be qualified by...**
- **It would be misleading to treat X as Y.**

### Common transfer errors

1. “扩大访问”写 **expand access**，不是 *enlarge visit*。
2. “代表性”写 **representative / representativeness**。
3. “覆盖率”在这里写 **coverage**，不要写 *cover rate*。
4. “更容易被发现”可写 **more likely to be discovered**。

## Precision / rewrite

Rewrite these claims so they are defensible:

1. Online databases give everyone equal access.
2. Cheap textbooks are always the best choice.
3. More listings automatically make a platform better.
4. Digitization eliminates missing information.

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Online databases can broaden access, but actual access still depends on what has been digitized and how the interface is designed.
> 2. A cheaper textbook is not necessarily the best choice if it is the wrong edition or in poor condition.
> 3. More listings can help, provided that the information is current and searchable.
> 4. Digitization can reduce some access barriers, but it does not eliminate missing information.

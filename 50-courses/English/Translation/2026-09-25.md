## 1. CET-6 level — extending access to campus study spaces

先判断逻辑：**需求存在 → 延长开放时间看似直接 → 实际瓶颈不只在时间 → 更细的管理设计 → 有限但可解释的收益**。

**临近期末时，不少大学的图书馆和自习室会出现座位紧张的问题。延长开放时间看起来是最直接的解决办法，但如果学生仍集中在下午和晚上到达，仅仅把闭馆时间推迟一两个小时，未必能显著改善高峰期的拥挤。更重要的是，学校需要弄清楚学生真正缺少的是“总座位数”，还是“某些时段可以稳定使用的座位”。例如，一些自习区虽然名义上全天开放，却可能因为课程、活动或临时维护频繁关闭；还有一些座位长期被物品占着，但实际没有人使用。学校可以公布各区域的实时开放状态，设置合理的临时离座规则，并在考试周把部分低使用率教室改为自习空间。这样的措施不一定能让每个人随时找到理想座位，但能减少学生反复寻找空间所花的时间，也能让现有设施得到更有效的利用。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Near the end of a semester, libraries and study rooms at many universities become crowded and available seats can be difficult to find. Extending opening hours may seem like the most direct solution, but simply delaying closing time by an hour or two may do little to reduce peak-hour congestion if most students still arrive in the afternoon and evening. More importantly, universities need to determine whether students actually lack total seating capacity or reliable access to seats at particular times. Some study areas may officially remain open all day but close frequently for classes, events, or temporary maintenance. Other seats may be occupied by personal belongings even when no one is using them. Universities can publish real-time information about which areas are available, introduce reasonable rules for temporary absences, and convert underused classrooms into study spaces during exam periods. Such measures may not guarantee an ideal seat for everyone at any time, but they can reduce the time students spend searching for space and make more effective use of existing facilities.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **extend opening hours** — 延长开放时间
- **reduce peak-hour congestion** — 缓解高峰时段拥挤
- **total seating capacity** — 总座位容量
- **reliable access to...** — 稳定获得……的机会
- **be occupied by personal belongings** — 被个人物品占用
- **publish real-time information** — 发布实时信息
- **convert underused classrooms into study spaces** — 把低使用率教室改为自习空间
- **make effective use of existing facilities** — 更有效利用现有设施

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. “座位紧张”不要写成 *seats are tight*；可写 **available seats are limited / seats are difficult to find**。
2. “延长开放时间”是 **extend opening hours**，不是 *prolong the open time*。
3. “某些时段”可写 **at particular times / during certain periods**。
4. “被物品占着”可写 **be occupied by personal belongings**。
5. “不一定能……”常用 **may not guarantee... / does not necessarily ensure...**。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level — evaluating a reusable-container deposit program

先判断逻辑：**政策目标 → 单一指标的诱惑 → 替代成本与系统边界 → 完整评估指标 → 条件化结论**。

**为了减少一次性餐盒，一些校园和商业区开始尝试可重复使用餐盒的押金制度：消费者支付少量押金，使用后把餐盒归还到指定地点，再由运营方集中清洗并重新投入使用。评价这类制度时，最容易统计的是“归还了多少个餐盒”，但归还率并不能单独说明项目是否真正减少了环境负担。如果餐盒需要被长距离运输，清洗过程消耗大量热水和能源，或者大量餐盒因为损坏和丢失而很快被替换，那么高归还率仍可能伴随较高的资源消耗。另一方面，如果只比较项目实施前后一次性餐盒的使用数量，也可能忽略同期客流、菜单结构和外卖比例的变化。更完整的评估应同时记录餐盒平均重复使用次数、回收和运输距离、清洗所需的水和能源、损坏率，以及一次性包装实际减少的数量。只有把这些指标放在同一个系统边界内比较，研究者才能判断这种方案在什么规模和使用条件下更有优势，而不是因为某一个容易测量的数字表现良好，就把整个项目描述为成功。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> To reduce disposable food containers, some campuses and commercial districts have experimented with deposit systems for reusable containers. Customers pay a small deposit, return the container to a designated location after use, and the operator collects, washes, and puts it back into circulation. The easiest indicator to track is often the number or percentage of containers returned, but a high return rate alone does not show that the program has reduced its overall environmental burden. If containers must be transported over long distances, washing requires large amounts of hot water and energy, or many containers are quickly replaced because they are damaged or lost, a high return rate may still coexist with substantial resource use. Likewise, comparing the number of disposable containers used before and after the program may be misleading if customer volume, menu composition, or the share of takeaway orders changed over the same period. A fuller evaluation should track the average number of reuse cycles, collection and transport distance, water and energy used for washing, damage rates, and the actual reduction in disposable packaging. Only by comparing these measures within a consistent system boundary can researchers determine the scale and conditions under which the program offers an advantage rather than declaring it successful because one easy-to-measure indicator looks favorable.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **deposit system for reusable containers** — 可重复使用餐盒押金制度
- **designated return location** — 指定归还地点
- **put something back into circulation** — 重新投入使用
- **return rate** — 归还率
- **overall environmental burden** — 总体环境负担
- **coexist with substantial resource use** — 与较高资源消耗同时存在
- **over the same period** — 在同一时期
- **average number of reuse cycles** — 平均重复使用次数
- **within a consistent system boundary** — 在一致的系统边界内
- **easy-to-measure indicator** — 容易测量的指标

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. “押金制度”是 **deposit system / deposit scheme**，不是 *mortgage system*。
2. “重复使用次数”可写 **number of reuse cycles / number of times each container is reused**。
3. “环境负担”常用 **environmental burden / environmental impact**。
4. “同期变化”可写 **changes over the same period / concurrent changes**。
5. “某个数字表现良好”更自然的是 **an indicator looks favorable / one metric improves**。

## 3. Precision rewrite — narrow the claim

1. *Longer library hours solve the shortage of study space.*
2. *A high return rate proves that reusable containers are environmentally friendly.*
3. *The new study-space policy increased student productivity.*
4. *Reusable packaging is better than disposable packaging.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Longer opening hours may improve access for students who can shift their study time, but they do not necessarily reduce crowding during the busiest periods.
> 2. A high return rate is one useful indicator, but it does not by itself establish that the system has a lower overall environmental burden.
> 3. The policy may have reduced the time students spent searching for seats, but that result should not be treated as direct evidence of higher academic productivity.
> 4. Reusable packaging can offer environmental advantages when it is reused enough times and when collection, transport, and washing are managed efficiently.

## 4. Micro-drill — translate the logic, not the Chinese word order

1. 容易统计的数据不一定是最值得关注的数据。
2. 如果高峰期需求没有变化，延长低峰时段的服务时间可能帮助有限。
3. 一个方案减少了某种浪费，并不意味着它没有把成本转移到别的环节。
4. 在比较两个系统之前，研究者需要先说明哪些过程被纳入计算。

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. The data that are easiest to collect are not necessarily the data that matter most.
> 2. If peak demand remains unchanged, extending service during off-peak hours may have only a limited effect.
> 3. The fact that a system reduces one form of waste does not mean that it has not shifted costs to another part of the process.
> 4. Before comparing two systems, researchers need to specify which processes are included in the analysis.

## 5. Spaced review — earlier language

1. **一个结果在不同条件下仍然成立，通常比只在单一实验设置中出现更有说服力。**
2. **两个类别之间存在重叠，并不意味着这种区分完全没有用。**

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. A result is generally more convincing if it remains robust across different conditions rather than appearing only in a single experimental setting.
> 2. The fact that two categories overlap does not mean that the distinction between them is useless.

## 6. Short precision exercise — one policy, three questions

围绕“延长图书馆开放时间”，分别写一个英文问题。

- **Mechanism:** use **How would... actually reduce...?**
- **Measurement:** use **What outcome should be measured...?**
- **Boundary condition:** use **Under what conditions would...?**

> [!answer]- Possible answers
> 1. How would longer opening hours actually reduce crowding if most students still prefer to study at the same times?
> 2. What outcome should be measured to determine whether longer opening hours improve access to study space?
> 3. Under what conditions would extending opening hours provide enough benefit to justify the additional staffing and operating costs?

## 7. Mini-output — 120–150 words

Choose one: extending library opening hours, a reusable-container deposit program, a campus shuttle route, or a lecture-recording policy.

Answer: **What would count as convincing evidence that the intervention is working?**

Requirements:

- name at least **three measurable outcomes**;
- explain why one easy metric could be misleading;
- use **does not by itself establish**, **under what conditions**, and **compared with**;
- include one limitation or trade-off.

## 1. CET-6 level

先独立翻译，不查参考答案：

**近年来，越来越多的城市开始重新设计公共空间，希望让步行、骑行和公共交通变得更加方便。这样的改变并不只是为了缓解交通拥堵。一个设计良好的街区还可以鼓励居民进行更多日常活动，并为不同年龄和背景的人创造交流的机会。然而，如果规划者忽视当地居民的实际需求，即使投入大量资金，改造项目也未必能够达到预期效果。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> In recent years, a growing number of cities have begun to redesign public spaces in the hope of making walking, cycling, and public transportation more convenient. Such changes are intended not merely to ease traffic congestion. A well-designed neighborhood can also encourage residents to be more active in their daily lives and create opportunities for people of different ages and backgrounds to interact. However, if planners overlook the actual needs of local residents, redevelopment projects may fail to achieve the desired results even when substantial resources are invested.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **a growing number of + plural noun** — 越来越多的……
- **in the hope of doing sth.** — 希望通过……
- **not merely / not only ...** — 不只是……
- **ease traffic congestion** — 缓解交通拥堵
- **create opportunities for sb. to do sth.** — 为某人创造……机会
- **achieve the desired results** — 达到预期效果

### Common transfer errors

- 不要把“越来越多”机械翻成 *more and more cities are starting to...*；它并非错误，但正式写作中 **a growing number of cities** 更紧凑。
- “投入大量资金”重点是资源投入，不必逐字写成 *put a lot of money into it*；学术语域可用 **substantial resources are invested**。
- “未必”通常对应 **may not / may fail to / does not necessarily**，而不是绝对的 *will not*。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level

**科学研究经常要求我们在证据并不完整的情况下作出暂时判断。一个解释能够与现有数据相符，并不意味着它已经得到最终证明；新的实验可能支持它，也可能迫使研究者修改甚至放弃原有模型。因此，科学上的不确定性不应被简单理解为知识的缺陷。相反，明确说明哪些结论较为可靠、哪些问题仍然开放，是严谨研究的重要组成部分。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Scientific research often requires us to make provisional judgments when the available evidence is incomplete. The fact that an explanation is consistent with existing data does not mean that it has been conclusively proven; new experiments may support it, but they may also force researchers to revise or even abandon the original model. Scientific uncertainty, therefore, should not simply be regarded as a defect in our knowledge. On the contrary, clearly distinguishing relatively robust conclusions from questions that remain open is an essential part of rigorous research.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **make a provisional judgment** — 作出暂时判断
- **be consistent with the evidence/data** — 与证据/数据相符
- **does not mean that...** — 并不意味着……
- **revise or abandon a model** — 修改或放弃模型
- **regard A as B** — 将 A 视为 B
- **remain open** — 尚无定论
- **rigorous research** — 严谨的研究

### Common transfer errors

- “证明”在科学语境里不要总写 **prove**；当证据仍有限时，**support**, **be consistent with**, **provide evidence for** 往往更准确。
- “不确定性”是 **uncertainty**，不是 *unsureness*。
- “重要组成部分”可写 **an essential part/component of**，避免中式表达 *an important composition of*。

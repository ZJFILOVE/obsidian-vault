## 1. CET-6 level — improving bus reliability rather than only adding vehicles

先判断逻辑：**表面问题 → 为什么最直观的解决办法未必有效 → 更具体的原因 → 更合理的组合措施**。

**在早晚高峰期间，许多城市公交线路都会出现车辆拥挤和到站时间不稳定的问题。最直观的解决办法似乎是增加车辆，但如果道路本身经常拥堵，单纯增加班次未必能明显改善乘客体验。车辆可能仍然被堵在同一段路上，甚至因为站点附近排队而进一步延误。因此，管理部门需要先判断延误主要发生在哪里，以及它是由道路拥堵、上下客时间过长，还是发车间隔不均造成的。在掌握这些信息之后，可以根据不同时段调整发车频率，在重点路段设置公交优先措施，并通过实时信息减少乘客对等待时间的不确定感。这样的做法不一定能彻底消除延误，但更有可能针对问题真正发生的环节进行改进。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> During the morning and evening rush hours, many urban bus routes experience both overcrowding and unreliable arrival times. The most obvious solution may seem to be adding more vehicles, but simply increasing service frequency may not substantially improve passengers' experience if buses are repeatedly delayed by road congestion. Additional buses can become stuck on the same sections of road and may even create further delays when several vehicles queue near a stop. Transit operators therefore need to identify where delays occur and whether they are mainly caused by traffic congestion, long boarding times, or uneven intervals between departures. Once these patterns are understood, operators can adjust service frequency by time of day, introduce bus-priority measures on critical sections, and provide real-time information to reduce uncertainty about waiting times. These measures may not eliminate delays entirely, but they are more likely to address the points in the system where the problem actually arises.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **during the morning / evening rush hour** — 在早 / 晚高峰期间
- **unreliable arrival times** — 到站时间不稳定
- **increase service frequency** — 增加班次 / 发车频率
- **be delayed by congestion** — 因拥堵而延误
- **queue near a stop** — 在站点附近排队
- **identify where delays occur** — 判断延误发生在哪里
- **uneven intervals between departures** — 发车间隔不均
- **by time of day** — 根据一天中的不同时段
- **bus-priority measures** — 公交优先措施
- **reduce uncertainty about...** — 减少对……的不确定感
- **address the point where the problem arises** — 针对问题真正发生的环节

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“公交线路出现拥挤”**不要直接写成 *the bus line is crowded*. 如果强调车内拥挤，写 **buses become overcrowded**；如果强调线路运行状况，可写 **the route experiences overcrowding**。
2. **“增加车辆”**在公交运营语境中可以写 **add more buses / vehicles**；如果强调班次密度，**increase service frequency** 更精确。
3. **“到站时间不稳定”**不是 *arrival time is unstable*. 更自然的是 **arrival times are unreliable / highly variable**。
4. **“发车间隔不均”**可以写 **uneven headways**，但 *headway* 较专业；普通 CET-6 写作中 **uneven intervals between departures** 更稳妥。
5. **“针对问题发生的环节”**不要写 *aim at the link where the problem happens*. 可写 **address the part of the system where the problem arises**。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level — evaluating a peer-mentoring program

先判断逻辑：**项目目标 → 简单前后比较的问题 → 自我选择与基线差异 → 结果指标不应只看一个 → 更严格的评估设计**。

**大学常常通过朋辈辅导项目帮助新生适应课程、校园生活和学习压力。如果参加项目的学生在第一学期结束时平均成绩更高，学校可能很容易把这种差异解释为项目有效。然而，参加辅导的学生可能本来就更主动、更愿意寻求帮助，或者在入学前已经拥有更好的学习习惯。因此，仅仅比较“参加”和“未参加”两组学生的期末成绩，并不能自动说明项目造成了差异。另一方面，即使成绩变化不大，项目也可能提高学生的归属感、信息获取能力或求助意愿，而这些效果未必能在短期考试成绩中体现。更严格的评估应尽可能记录学生参与项目之前的学习表现和背景差异，比较相似学生，并同时追踪成绩、退课率、持续参与度以及学生对校园支持资源的使用情况。如果研究者能够说明哪些结果发生了变化、哪些没有变化，以及不同类型学生的效果是否一致，就能比一个简单的平均分比较提供更有解释力的结论。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Universities often use peer-mentoring programs to help first-year students adjust to coursework, campus life, and academic pressure. If students who participate in such a program have higher average grades at the end of their first semester, it may be tempting to interpret the difference as evidence that the program is effective. However, students who choose to participate may already be more proactive, more willing to seek help, or better prepared academically before entering university. A simple comparison of final grades between participants and non-participants therefore does not automatically show that the program caused the difference. Moreover, even if grades change little, the program may still improve students' sense of belonging, access to information, or willingness to seek assistance—outcomes that may not be captured by short-term exam performance. A more rigorous evaluation should record relevant baseline characteristics, compare students who are similar where possible, and track multiple outcomes, including grades, course withdrawal, continued participation, and use of campus support services. An evaluation is more informative when it identifies which outcomes changed, which did not, and whether the effects were consistent across different groups of students.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **peer-mentoring program** — 朋辈辅导项目
- **adjust to coursework / campus life** — 适应课程 / 校园生活
- **it may be tempting to interpret... as...** — 很容易 / 很诱人地把……解释为……
- **choose to participate** — 自主选择参加
- **seek help / seek assistance** — 寻求帮助
- **does not automatically show that...** — 并不能自动说明……
- **sense of belonging** — 归属感
- **be captured by a measure** — 被某个指标反映出来
- **baseline characteristics** — 基线特征 / 起点差异
- **track multiple outcomes** — 同时追踪多个结果指标
- **course withdrawal** — 退课 / 退出课程
- **be consistent across groups** — 在不同群体中表现一致
- **provide a more informative conclusion** — 提供更有信息量 / 解释力的结论

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“项目有效”**可以写 **the program is effective**，但学术写作里最好进一步问：**effective at improving what outcome, for whom, and over what period?**
2. **“更主动”**不要机械写成 *more active*. 在这里 **more proactive** 更准确，表示主动采取行动、寻求资源。
3. **“归属感”**的常用表达是 **sense of belonging**，不要写 *belonging feeling*。
4. **“不能自动说明项目造成差异”**可以写 **does not automatically show / establish that the program caused the difference**。注意 **establish** 比 **prove** 更适合学术语境。
5. **“未必能体现”**可以用 **may not be captured by...**，这是讨论测量局限时很实用的结构。
6. **“不同类型学生效果是否一致”**可以写 **whether the effects are consistent across different groups of students**，不要逐字写 *whether different kinds of students have the same effect*。

## 3. Precision rewrite — separate observation from explanation

把下面句子改得更精确，重点是控制因果和范围。

1. *The new bus schedule failed because buses were still late.*
2. *Students in the mentoring program had higher grades, so the program improved academic performance.*
3. *Real-time information solves waiting-time problems.*
4. *The program works for first-year students.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Buses remained late after the new schedule was introduced, but the result does not by itself show whether the schedule was ineffective or whether congestion and other operating conditions limited its impact.
> 2. Students in the mentoring program had higher average grades, but differences in prior preparation or willingness to seek help may partly account for the gap.
> 3. Real-time information cannot eliminate actual delays, but it may reduce uncertainty and help passengers make better travel decisions.
> 4. The program may benefit some first-year students, but its effectiveness should be evaluated across specific outcomes and different student groups.

## 4. Micro-drill — choose the more precise academic chunk

Choose from **account for, baseline, capture, mitigate, constitute, be attributable to**. Change the form if necessary.

1. Researchers should record students' ______ characteristics before comparing outcomes.
2. A single exam score may fail to ______ changes in confidence or help-seeking behavior.
3. Differences in prior preparation may ______ part of the gap between the two groups.
4. Better passenger information may ______ frustration even when it cannot eliminate delays.
5. One improved indicator does not necessarily ______ evidence that the entire program is successful.
6. The observed difference cannot automatically ______ the intervention itself.

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. baseline
> 2. capture
> 3. account for
> 4. mitigate
> 5. constitute
> 6. be attributable to

## 6. Short rewrite exercise — three versions of the same claim

把“新公交方案改善了乘客体验”分别写成三种证据强度。

### A. Only describe what happened after the change

不要写因果。

### B. Express a plausible contribution

用 **may have contributed to**。

### C. Make a stronger causal claim, but state the evidence condition

加入“如果其他关键运行条件基本不变”这一限定。

> [!answer]- Possible answers
> A. Passenger satisfaction increased after the new service plan was introduced.
> B. The new service plan may have contributed to the increase in passenger satisfaction.
> C. If other major operating conditions remained broadly unchanged, the evidence would provide stronger support for the conclusion that the new service plan improved passenger satisfaction.

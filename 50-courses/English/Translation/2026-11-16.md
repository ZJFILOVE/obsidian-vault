## 练习一｜CET-6：社区图书漂流柜

### 中文原文

某社区计划在三个地铁站出口设置图书漂流柜，试运行三个月。居民可以免费取走一本书，但应在两周内归还原书，或放入一本状况良好的替代书。为了减少无人管理带来的混乱，志愿者每周检查一次书柜，清理严重破损的书，并记录借出、归还和新增图书的数量。社区不会仅凭“柜子里总有书”就判断项目成功；它还会调查不同年龄居民是否方便使用，以及志愿者的工作量是否长期可承受。如果试点只吸引了少数经常乘地铁的人，管理方将考虑把部分书柜移到社区服务中心，而不是立即扩大项目。

### 写作要求

- 用 120–150 词完成。
- 明确区分 **规则**、**执行机制**、**评估指标** 和 **调整条件**。
- 至少使用一次 *provided that / as long as*，一次 *rather than*，一次表示“不能仅凭”的结构。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> A neighborhood plans to install book-sharing cabinets near the exits of three metro stations for a three-month trial. Residents may take a book free of charge, provided that they return it within two weeks or contribute another book in good condition. To reduce the disorder that may arise from leaving the cabinets unattended, volunteers will inspect them once a week, remove badly damaged books, and record how many books are borrowed, returned, and added. The community will not judge the project successful merely because books remain available in the cabinets. It will also examine whether residents of different ages can use the service conveniently and whether the volunteers’ workload is sustainable. If the trial attracts only a small group of regular metro users, the organizers will consider moving some cabinets to community service centers rather than expanding the project immediately.

### 结构与搭配

- **run / conduct a three-month trial**：*trial* 强调有限范围试行；不要把所有“试点”都机械译成 *pilot*。
- **provided that + 从句**：把允许行为与条件绑在一起；正式程度高于单纯的 *if*。
- **arise from leaving ... unattended**：*arise from* 表示“由……产生”，*unattended* 是“无人看管”。
- **judge A successful merely because ...**：把“评价对象—判断—证据不足”放在同一结构中。
- **be sustainable**：这里指工作量能否长期维持，不等于“环保”。
- **rather than expanding ... immediately**：*rather than* 后与前面的 *moving* 保持平行。

### 常见中式英语迁移

1. **错误：** *Residents can take books freely.*  
   **问题：** *freely* 容易被理解为“随意、不受限制”，没有表达“免费”。  
   **改为：** *Residents may take a book free of charge, provided that ...*

2. **错误：** *Volunteers check the cabinet every week one time.*  
   **问题：** 频率表达重复。  
   **改为：** *Volunteers inspect the cabinets once a week.*

3. **错误：** *The community will not only see whether there are books.*  
   **问题：** *not only* 需要与 *but also* 配对，而且“仅凭”强调的是证据不足。  
   **改为：** *The community will not evaluate the project solely on the basis of book availability.*

4. **错误：** *If the effect is not good, they will change the place.*  
   **问题：** *effect is not good* 过于空泛。  
   **改为：** *If participation remains concentrated among regular commuters, some cabinets may be relocated.*

### 精确度重写

把下面句子改得更可执行：

> The project will be expanded if people like it.

至少补出：谁来判断、用什么指标、观察多久、扩大前需满足什么条件。

> [!answer]- 一种改写
> After the three-month trial, the community committee will consider expansion if borrowing and return records show regular use across age groups and if the weekly volunteer workload remains manageable.

## 练习二｜TOEFL / Academic：校园安静空间与学习表现

### 中文原文

一项校园研究比较了两栋宿舍楼学生在期中考试周的自习习惯。A 楼新设了预约制安静室，B 楼没有类似空间。研究者发现，A 楼参与调查的学生平均每周自习时间更长，自报的分心次数也更少。然而，这一差异不能直接归因于安静室：两栋楼的年级构成不同，而且选择预约房间的学生可能本来就更重视学习。为了更可靠地评估空间本身的作用，后续研究应在开放安静室之前收集基线数据，使用一致的学习时长记录方法，并比较同一批学生在设施开放前后的变化。即使结果仍然为正，研究者也应报告未参与调查者的比例，并避免把短期考试周的表现推广到整个学期。

### 写作要求

- 用 150–190 词完成。
- 清楚表达：观察到的相关、至少两个替代解释、改进后的研究设计、外推边界。
- 使用 *cannot be attributed to ... alone*、*baseline data*、*even if* 或同等强度表达。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> A campus study compared the study habits of students in two residence halls during midterm week. Hall A had recently introduced reservable quiet rooms, whereas Hall B had no comparable space. Survey respondents in Hall A reported more weekly study time and fewer distractions on average. However, the difference cannot be attributed to the quiet rooms alone. The two halls had different proportions of students from each year, and those who chose to reserve a room may already have been more committed to studying. To estimate the effect of the space more reliably, a follow-up study should collect baseline data before the rooms open, use the same method to record study time, and compare changes among the same students before and after the intervention. Even if the association remains positive, the researchers should report the proportion of students who did not participate and avoid generalizing performance during a short examination period to the entire semester.

### 结构与搭配

- **whereas B had no comparable ...**：用 *whereas* 做对照；*comparable* 比 *same* 更准确，因为设施不必完全相同。
- **on average**：平均差异不代表每个参与者都如此。
- **cannot be attributed to A alone**：拒绝单因果结论，同时不否认 A 可能有作用。
- **may already have been ...**：用完成式表达选择发生前就存在的倾向。
- **collect baseline data before ...**：baseline 是干预前基准，不是普通“背景数据”。
- **compare changes among the same students**：比较“变化量”比只比较两组终点更接近研究问题。
- **generalize A to B**：固定搭配；不要写 *generalize A into B*。

### 常见中式英语迁移

1. **错误：** *Hall A students studied longer, so the quiet room improved their grades.*  
   **问题：** 原文没有成绩数据，且相关不等于因果。  
   **改为：** *Respondents in Hall A reported longer study time, but the observed association does not establish a causal effect.*

2. **错误：** *The grades of the two buildings were different.*  
   **问题：** “年级构成”不是分数。  
   **改为：** *The two halls differed in their distribution of first-, second-, third-, and fourth-year students.*

3. **错误：** *The research should unify the method.*  
   **问题：** *unify* 不自然。  
   **改为：** *The study should use a consistent measurement method.*

4. **错误：** *The result cannot represent the whole semester.*  
   **问题：** 学术英语通常用 *generalize* 或 *be representative of*。  
   **改为：** *The findings from midterm week may not be representative of the semester as a whole.*

### 论证强度阶梯

按证据强度从弱到强排列，并写出每句需要的额外证据：

- Quiet rooms **caused** students to study longer.
- Quiet-room access **was associated with** longer reported study time.
- The results **suggest that** quiet rooms may support longer study sessions.

> [!answer]- 参考判断
> 现有横截面调查最稳妥地支持 *was associated with*。*suggest that ... may support* 可以作为谨慎解释，但仍需明确替代解释。要使用 *caused*，至少需要更强的干预设计、可靠基线、一致测量和对混杂因素的控制。

# Translation Practice — 2026-09-04

## 1. CET-6 level
先自己翻译，不查词：

**许多人把拖延简单地归因于懒惰或缺乏自律，但这种解释并不完整。面对令人不安或困难的任务时，人们有时会优先缓解眼前的不适，而忽视长期目标。因此，减少拖延不仅需要更合理地安排时间，也需要学会处理任务带来的负面情绪。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Many people simply attribute procrastination to laziness or a lack of self-discipline, but this explanation is incomplete. When faced with an unpleasant or difficult task, people may prioritize reducing their immediate discomfort while overlooking their long-term goals. Therefore, reducing procrastination requires not only better time management but also the ability to deal with the negative emotions caused by a task.

### Useful structures
- attribute A to B
- when faced with...
- prioritize A over B
- not only A but also B
- deal with negative emotions

## 2. TOEFL-style level
**技术使人们能够在几秒钟内获得大量信息，但更快地获得答案并不一定能带来更深入的理解。真正重要的是，我们是否会主动检查信息的来源、比较不同解释，并在证据不足时保留判断。换句话说，信息获取能力只有与批判性思考结合起来，才能真正改善我们的决策。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Technology enables people to obtain large amounts of information within seconds, but faster access to answers does not necessarily lead to deeper understanding. What really matters is whether we actively examine the sources of information, compare different explanations, and withhold judgment when the evidence is insufficient. In other words, access to information can genuinely improve our decisions only when it is combined with critical thinking.

### Useful structures
- enable sb. to do sth.
- does not necessarily lead to...
- what really matters is whether...
- withhold judgment
- be combined with...

## 3. Quick output
不看参考译文，翻译这两句：

1. **熟悉一个观点并不一定意味着你真正理解了它。**
2. **与其等待动力出现，不如把任务分成几个可以立即开始的小步骤。**

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. Being familiar with an idea does not necessarily mean that you truly understand it.
> 2. Rather than waiting for motivation, break the task into several small steps that you can start immediately.

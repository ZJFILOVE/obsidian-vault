## 使用方法与原创说明

以下两个情境、中文段落和参考译文均为独立编写，不翻译或改写今日海洋声音材料。六级篇练社区项目中的流程、条件与评价；学术篇练观察研究中的比较、替代解释和结论边界。两者都是虚构教学情境，不是现实机构项目或已经发表的研究。

先完整翻译，再按 **主语—动作—条件—证据—结论强度** 的顺序检查。参考译文不是唯一答案。

## 练习一：CET-6——社区修理角（15–18 分钟）

某社区中心计划每周开放一次“修理角”，让居民带来损坏的小家电，并在志愿者指导下学习基本检查方法。负责人最初打算按修好的物品数量评价活动，但志愿者指出，有些设备因缺少零件或存在安全风险，并不适合当场维修。于是，登记表除了记录结果，还会注明问题类型、需要的材料，以及志愿者为何建议停止操作。参加者在拆开设备前必须听取安全说明；如果电线破损或内部仍有电荷，他们应把设备交给专业人员处理。试行一个月后，中心将分别统计成功维修、获得可靠建议和主动终止危险操作的情况。这样做并不会让每件物品重新工作，却能更准确地反映活动是否帮助居民作出安全而明智的决定。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A community centre plans to open a weekly repair corner where residents can bring broken small appliances and learn basic inspection methods under the guidance of volunteers. At first, the organiser intended to evaluate the programme by counting the number of items repaired. The volunteers, however, pointed out that some devices should not be fixed on the spot because parts are unavailable or the devices present safety risks. The registration form will therefore record not only the outcome but also the type of problem, the materials required and the reason a volunteer recommends stopping. Participants must receive safety instructions before opening a device. If a wire is damaged or a component may still hold an electrical charge, the device should be handled by a professional. After a one-month trial, the centre will separately count successful repairs, reliable advice and cases in which a risky operation was deliberately stopped. This approach will not make every item work again, but it will show more accurately whether the programme helps residents make safe and informed decisions.

### 结构与搭配

- **under the guidance of**：在……指导下；不要逐字写成 *under the guide of*。
- **not only A but also B**：A、B 要保持语法平行；这里都接名词性信息。
- **should not be fixed on the spot**：表达“不适合当场处理”，比一律写 cannot 更准确。
- **make an informed decision**：基于充分信息作决定；不是 *make a wise decide*。
- **a one-month trial**：复合定语中的 month 用单数。

### 常见中式英语

1. “按数量评价”不是 *evaluate according to the quantity* 的唯一写法；**evaluate ... by counting / use the number ... as the measure** 更自然。
2. “停止操作”不应无主语地写成 *stop operating is suggested*；明确谁建议、谁停止。
3. “并不会让每件物品重新工作”是限制语，不要强化成 *the programme is unable to repair anything*。

### 精度改写

把这句改得更可检验：

> The activity was very successful.

可改为：

> During the trial, the centre recorded how many participants repaired an item, received a usable diagnosis or stopped an unsafe procedure.

后一句描述了指标，但仍没有预先宣布“成功”。

## 练习二：TOEFL／academic——夜间照明与候鸟活动（20–25 分钟）

研究团队希望了解校园夜间照明是否与候鸟在迁徙季的活动变化有关。他们在三类地点放置自动录音设备：整夜保持明亮的道路、午夜后调暗的道路，以及远离主要建筑的较暗区域。所有设备在相同日期和时段采集数据，研究人员还记录了降雨、风速与周边植被，因为这些因素也可能影响鸟类活动。初步结果显示，部分明亮地点在午夜后记录到的鸣声较少，但地点之间的差异很大。该模式与照明假设相符，却不能单独证明光线导致了变化：交通噪声、栖息地结构以及不同物种的迁徙时间都可能提供其他解释。下一阶段将扩大地点数量，并在改变灯光设置前后进行重复测量。即使结果保持一致，研究者也应说明样本来自一所校园，不应把结论直接推广到所有城市环境。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> The research team sought to determine whether nighttime lighting on a university campus was associated with changes in migratory-bird activity during the migration season. Automated recorders were placed at three types of sites: roads that remained brightly lit throughout the night, roads where the lights were dimmed after midnight, and darker areas located away from major buildings. All devices collected data on the same dates and during the same time periods. The researchers also recorded rainfall, wind speed and surrounding vegetation because these factors could also influence bird activity. Preliminary results showed that fewer calls were detected after midnight at some brightly lit sites, although the differences among sites were substantial. This pattern was consistent with the lighting hypothesis, but it could not by itself establish that light caused the change. Traffic noise, habitat structure and species-specific migration timing offered alternative explanations. The next phase will include more sites and repeated measurements before and after changes to the lighting settings. Even if the pattern persists, the researchers should state that the sample comes from a single campus and should not generalise the findings directly to all urban environments.

### 结构与学术搭配

- **be associated with**：表示关联，不自动等于因果。
- **be consistent with a hypothesis**：与假设相符；比 prove the hypothesis 克制。
- **by itself establish that ... caused ...**：明确单一观察不足以建立因果。
- **alternative explanations**：替代解释；可与 account for the pattern 连用。
- **generalise the findings to**：把研究结果推广到；英式拼写 generalise 与美式 generalize 选一种保持一致。
- **before and after changes**：前后测量；若没有对照或随机化，也不能仅凭 before-and-after 自动声称因果。

### 常见迁移错误

1. “录到的鸣声较少”是 **fewer calls were detected/recorded**，不是 *less birds were heard*；calls 可数，且录音并不等同于准确统计鸟的个体数。
2. “差异很大”可写 **differences were substantial**；*the difference is very big* 口语且忽略复数地点。
3. “来自一所校园”限制的是外推范围，不是说研究毫无价值。避免从过度肯定跳到 *the result is meaningless*。

### 精度改写

把因果过强的句子：

> Bright lights reduced bird activity.

改为：

> At some sampled sites, brighter nighttime conditions were associated with fewer recorded calls after midnight; the observational design did not isolate lighting from all competing explanations.

## 间隔复习

明天闭卷翻译这两句：

1. 记录一个项目为什么停止，有时与记录它完成了什么同样重要。
2. 与假设相符的结果仍可能有其他解释，尤其是在样本只来自一个地点时。

> [!answer]- 间隔复习参考
> 1. Recording why a project was stopped can sometimes be as important as recording what it completed.
> 2. A result that is consistent with a hypothesis may still have alternative explanations, especially when the sample comes from only one site.

# Translation — Day 2

## Chinese → English
随着互联网的发展，人们获取知识的方式发生了很大变化。如今，大学生可以通过网络课程、电子书和各种学习平台获得大量信息。然而，如何判断信息是否可靠也变得越来越重要。因此，学生不仅需要获取信息，还需要培养独立思考的能力。

## Reference
With the development of the Internet, the way people acquire knowledge has changed significantly. Today, university students can obtain a large amount of information through online courses, e-books and various learning platforms. However, it has also become increasingly important to determine whether information is reliable. Therefore, students need not only to obtain information but also to develop the ability to think independently.

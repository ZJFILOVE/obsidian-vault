## 使用方法与原创说明

两段中文、情境和参考译文均独立编写，不翻译、不概写今日户外原则，也不把其中条目换几个名词后充当新练习。六级篇练服务流程的条件与例外；学术篇练数据处理中的保留、记录与判断。情境均为虚构，不是任何机构的现行规则或已发表研究。

先独立写完整信息，再检查搭配。批改顺序：**执行者 → 时序 → 条件范围 → 信息是否增加或遗漏 → 语言自然度**。参考答案不是唯一版本，保住意思比长句数量重要。

## 练习一：CET-6——社区剧场的取票安排（15–18分钟）

一家社区剧场准备修改周末演出的取票安排。过去，所有观众都在同一个窗口排队，有时只是询问信息的人也会挡住已经订票的观众。新安排将咨询与取票分开，并让提前完成预约的观众通过专门通道入场。观众需要出示预约确认信息，但如果手机无法开机，也可以向工作人员提供预约时使用的姓名，由工作人员查询记录。没有预约的人仍可现场购票，不过是否有票应以当天剩余座位为准。剧场会在入口处张贴说明，并邀请几位第一次来的观众试着按说明完成取票。如果他们仍反复询问下一步做什么，团队就应修改措辞或标识，而不能简单认定观众没有认真阅读。新流程的目的在于减少混乱，而不是让不熟悉线上预约的人更难看演出。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A community theatre plans to revise its ticket collection arrangements for weekend performances. Previously, everyone queued at the same window, and people seeking information sometimes blocked those who had already booked. The new arrangement separates enquiries from ticket collection and provides a dedicated entrance for visitors with advance reservations. Visitors need to show their booking confirmation. If their phone will not turn on, however, they can give a staff member the name used for the reservation so that the record can be checked. People without reservations can still buy tickets at the venue, subject to the availability of seats on the day. The theatre will display instructions at the entrance and ask several first-time visitors to try collecting their tickets by following them. If these visitors repeatedly ask what to do next, the team should revise the wording or signs rather than simply assume that they have not read carefully. The aim of the new procedure is to reduce confusion, not to make it harder for people unfamiliar with online booking to attend a performance.

### 结构与搭配

1. **ticket collection arrangements**：取票安排，不等于 ticket sales（售票）。先区分已经预约的人与尚未购票的人，不能把所有步骤都译成 buy tickets。
2. **separate enquiries from ticket collection**：把两种服务分开。enquiry 在这里是咨询；英式 inquiry 也可能见到，全文保持拼写风格一致。
3. **the name used for the reservation**：过去分词后置限定姓名；不是 the name used the reservation，也不必硬写很长的 which 从句。
4. **so that the record can be checked**：交代提供姓名的目的与工作人员接下来的动作；不要擅自加“所有人都能成功查到”。
5. **subject to the availability of seats**：受剩余座位限制。它修饰现场购票能否实现，不是否定前面全部服务。
6. **by following them**：them 指 instructions。若担心指代不清，可重复 by following the instructions；清楚比避免重复更重要。
7. **rather than simply assume …**：指出不应跳过的判断步骤，而不是说观众永远没有责任。

### 常见中式迁移

- “手机开不了机”用 the phone will not turn on；cannot open the phone 容易变成打开手机外壳或其他含义。
- “以剩余座位为准”不要逐字写 according to the seats is standard；要说明 availability 是条件。
- “第一次来的观众”用 first-time visitors；不是 the first coming people。
- “让某人更难做某事”用 make it harder for someone to do something，形式宾语 it 不丢。

### 精度改写

把 **Visitors without a working phone cannot collect their tickets.** 改成符合中文情境、保留替代办法的一句英语。

> [!answer]- 一个可行版本
> Visitors whose phones will not turn on can ask staff to check the reservation under the name used when booking.

## 练习二：TOEFL／学术——传感器记录中的缺失与异常（18–22分钟）

一个研究小组制定了新的数据处理流程，用于分析教室内传感器的连续记录。研究人员首先应保留原始文件，并为每次处理保存可以重复运行的脚本。若某个时间点没有读数，应将其标为缺失，而不能直接填入零。读数明显偏离其他观测时，也不应仅凭数值大小将它删除；团队需要先检查设备日志和测量条件，再记录保留或排除该读数的理由。为了让结果更透明，报告应同时说明缺失数据的比例、异常值的判断标准，以及不同处理方法是否会改变主要结论。这个虚构项目的试行记录表明，新流程让组员更容易追踪修改，但这并不能证明测量本身更准确。程序的一致性、记录的完整性和传感器的准确性是不同问题，应分别评价。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A research team has established a new data-processing procedure for analysing continuous sensor records from classrooms. Researchers should first retain the original files and save a script that can be rerun for each processing step. If no reading is available at a particular time point, the value should be marked as missing rather than set to zero. Nor should a reading be deleted solely because it differs markedly from the other observations. The team needs to examine the equipment logs and measurement conditions first, then document its reasons for retaining or excluding the reading. To make the results more transparent, the report should state the proportion of missing data, the criteria used to identify outliers, and whether different processing methods change the main conclusion. In this hypothetical project, trial records indicated that the new procedure made it easier for team members to trace revisions. This does not establish that the measurements themselves became more accurate. Consistency in procedures, completeness of records and sensor accuracy are distinct issues and should be assessed separately.

### 学术结构与搭配

- **a script that can be rerun**：可重新执行。它是技术步骤的可重复，不自动等于整个研究结论已经被独立复现。
- **mark a value as missing / set a value to zero**：as 标身份，to 标设定值；两种动作的信息含义不同。此处只练表达，不替真实研究决定插补方法。
- **solely because …**：仅仅因为。保留限制词，不能译成“异常值一概不能删除”。
- **Nor should …**：句首 nor 引出否定补充，助动词置于主语之前。也可以用更稳妥的 A reading should not be deleted …，不为追求倒装牺牲清楚。
- **document the reasons for retaining or excluding …**：document 在此作动词，表示记录；reasons for 后接并列动名词，层次一致。
- **the proportion of missing data**：比例而非总数；criteria 是 criterion 的复数。a criterion / several criteria，不写 a criteria。
- **whether different methods change …**：报告是否改变结论，不能先替分析承诺“不会改变”。
- **make it easier for … to trace …**：形式宾语结构与六级篇呼应，把“方便”具体化为谁更容易完成什么。

### 纠错与范围控制

1. Missing data are zero. → 把“缺失”误作一个已经测得的数值。
2. Every unusual reading must be removed. → 丢掉检查条件，把谨慎处理变成绝对删除。
3. A clearer processing record proves that the sensor is more accurate. → 把记录透明度偷换成测量准确性。
4. The report should explain that all methods give the same answer. → 把待检验的 whether 改成预设为真的 that。

> [!answer]- 原创修正参考
> 1. A missing reading should be labelled as missing rather than treated as an observed zero.
> 2. An unusual reading should be examined before any decision is made to exclude it.
> 3. A clearer processing record improves traceability, but it does not by itself establish greater sensor accuracy.
> 4. The report should explain whether the main conclusion changes when a different processing method is used.

## 1. CET-6 level — public libraries as shared spaces

先不要逐词替换。先判断中文里的三层逻辑：**传统功能 → 新需求 → 为什么仍然重要**。

**很多人把图书馆理解为借书和安静学习的地方，但现代公共图书馆承担的功能正在扩大。除了提供纸质书和电子资源，一些图书馆还开设数字技能课程、为求职者提供电脑和网络，并为儿童、老年人以及新移民组织社区活动。对没有稳定上网条件或缺少安静学习空间的人来说，图书馆尤其重要。衡量图书馆的价值时，如果只统计借出的书有多少，就可能忽视它作为公共空间所提供的其他服务。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Many people think of libraries primarily as places to borrow books and study quietly, but the role of modern public libraries is expanding. In addition to providing printed books and digital resources, some libraries offer digital-skills courses, give job seekers access to computers and the internet, and organize community activities for children, older adults, and newcomers. Libraries are particularly important for people who lack reliable internet access or a quiet place to study. If we assess their value only by counting how many books are borrowed, we may overlook the other services they provide as shared public spaces.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **think of A as B** — 把 A 看作 B
- **the role of ... is expanding** — ……的功能/角色正在扩大
- **in addition to doing sth.** — 除了做某事之外
- **give sb. access to sth.** — 让某人能够使用/获得某物
- **lack reliable access to...** — 缺乏稳定的……条件
- **assess the value of...** — 评估……的价值
- **overlook a service / factor / limitation** — 忽视某项服务、因素或局限
- **a shared public space** — 共享公共空间

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“开设课程”**通常是 **offer/run a course**，不要机械写成 *open a course*。
2. **“提供电脑和网络”**如果重点是“让人能用”，**give/provide access to computers and the internet** 比 *provide computers and network* 更自然。
3. **“没有稳定上网条件”**不要写 *do not have stable internet condition*；优先用 **lack reliable internet access**。
4. **“衡量价值”**可以用 **assess/evaluate the value of...**；*measure the value* 只有在明确量化时更自然。
5. **“借出的书有多少”**可写 **how many books are borrowed / circulation figures**，避免 *how many books are lent out by people*。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level — evaluating automated decision systems

先识别论证关系：**效率优势 ≠ 整体公平；平均表现 ≠ 各群体表现；模型输出 ≠ 无条件的最终决定**。

**越来越多的机构使用自动化系统来筛选申请、识别风险或分配有限资源。这些系统能够在短时间内处理大量信息，因此常被认为比完全依赖人工判断更高效。然而，速度和一致性本身并不能保证决策公平。如果训练数据反映了过去的不平等，或者模型在某些群体上的错误率明显更高，那么一个总体准确率看似不错的系统仍可能产生不均衡的后果。因此，对自动化决策系统的评估不应只关注平均准确率，还应考察错误如何分布、受影响的人能否提出申诉，以及人工监督能否真正纠正系统性问题。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> An increasing number of institutions use automated systems to screen applications, identify risks, or allocate scarce resources. Because these systems can process large amounts of information quickly, they are often regarded as more efficient than relying entirely on human judgment. However, speed and consistency do not in themselves guarantee fair decisions. If the training data reflect past inequalities, or if a model has substantially higher error rates for certain groups, a system with apparently strong overall accuracy may still produce uneven outcomes. Evaluating an automated decision system should therefore involve more than examining average accuracy; it should also consider how errors are distributed, whether affected individuals can appeal a decision, and whether human oversight can meaningfully correct systematic problems.

### Useful structures and collocations

- **screen applications** — 筛选申请
- **allocate scarce resources** — 分配稀缺资源
- **rely entirely on human judgment** — 完全依赖人工判断
- **do not in themselves guarantee...** — 其本身并不能保证……
- **reflect past inequalities** — 反映过去的不平等
- **error rate** — 错误率
- **overall accuracy** — 总体准确率
- **produce uneven outcomes** — 产生不均衡结果
- **appeal a decision** — 对决定提出申诉
- **human oversight** — 人工监督
- **systematic problem** — 系统性问题

### Common Chinese-English transfer errors

1. **“识别风险”**通常是 **identify/assess risk**，而不是 *recognize danger*。
2. **“一致性”**在决策系统里是 **consistency**，不等于“所有结果相同”。它强调相似输入被相似处理。
3. **“总体准确率看似不错”**可以写 **apparently strong overall accuracy** 或 **a high aggregate accuracy rate**；不要写 *the total correct rate looks good*。
4. **“不均衡的后果”**根据语境可用 **uneven/disparate outcomes**。**unequal** 更直接，但学术语境里要确保你确实在陈述不平等，而非仅仅差异。
5. **“人工监督”**是 **human oversight**；*human supervision* 也可理解，但更像对人的管理。
6. **“纠正系统性问题”**中的“系统性”是 **systematic/systemic**，不要误写成 *system problems*。

## 3. Precision rewrite

把下面四句改得更自然，并控制因果强度。

1. *Libraries are useful because they have many functions.*
2. *The algorithm is consistent, so it is fair.*
3. *A high accuracy proves the system has no bias.*
4. *People should have a way to complain about a machine decision.*

> [!answer]- Suggested rewrites
> 1. Libraries provide value through a range of services that extend beyond lending books.
> 2. Consistent decision-making may improve reliability, but consistency alone does not establish fairness.
> 3. High overall accuracy does not rule out substantial differences in error rates across groups.
> 4. People affected by an automated decision should have access to a meaningful appeal process.

## 4. Micro-drill — choose the more precise verb

Fill each blank with **cause, contribute to, facilitate, reinforce,** or **reflect**. More than one answer may be grammatically possible, but choose the one that makes the intended claim most defensible.

1. Limited internet access can ______ educational inequality without being its only cause.
2. A digital application system may ______ faster processing by reducing repetitive manual work.
3. Historical data can ______ patterns that already existed in earlier decisions.
4. Poorly designed incentives may ______ an undesirable behavior that is already common.

> [!answer]- Suggested answers
> 1. contribute to
> 2. facilitate
> 3. reflect
> 4. reinforce

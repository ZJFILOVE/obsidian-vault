## 1. CET-6 level

先完整翻译，再看提示：

**学习一种乐器的价值并不只在于掌握演奏技巧。长期练习要求学习者反复协调听觉、视觉和动作，并及时根据错误调整自己的表现。这种过程提醒我们，真正有效的学习往往不是被动接收信息，而是在反馈中不断修正和巩固已有的能力。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> The value of learning a musical instrument lies not only in acquiring performance skills. Long-term practice requires learners to repeatedly coordinate auditory, visual, and motor processes and to adjust their performance promptly in response to mistakes. This process reminds us that effective learning often involves not the passive reception of information, but the continuous refinement and consolidation of existing abilities through feedback.

### Structures & collocations
- **lie not only in...**：价值“不仅在于……”；不要机械写成 *the value is not only...*。
- **in response to** feedback / mistakes / changes：回应、针对。
- **passive reception of information**：比 *passively receive information* 更适合较正式论述。
- **refine and consolidate** skills/knowledge：改进并巩固。

### Transfer warning
中文的“要求学习者……”不一定译成 *ask learners to*。描述客观需求时，**require sb. to do sth.** 更准确。

## 2. TOEFL / academic-English level

**城市公共空间的质量很难只用面积或投资规模来衡量。一个设计精良的广场如果让行人感到不安全，或者无法适应老人、儿童和残障人士的需求，就未必能真正改善城市生活。因此，评估公共空间时，不仅要关注建筑本身，还应考察人们如何使用它、不同群体能否平等进入，以及这种空间是否能够随着社区需求的变化而调整。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> The quality of urban public space is difficult to measure solely in terms of area or investment. Even a well-designed square may fail to improve urban life if pedestrians feel unsafe there or if it cannot accommodate the needs of older people, children, and people with disabilities. Therefore, evaluating public space requires attention not only to the physical design itself, but also to how people use it, whether different groups have equitable access to it, and whether the space can adapt as community needs change.

### Structures & collocations
- **measure A in terms of B**
- **fail to do sth.**：并非“尝试失败”，也可表示没有产生预期效果。
- **accommodate the needs of...**：满足/适应不同需求。
- **equitable access to...**：强调公平可及性；不要把 *access* 当可数名词。
- **adapt as needs change**：比逐字翻译“随着……变化而调整”自然。

### Common transfer errors
- “并不只/不仅”不要每次都写 *not only...but also...*；可用 **not solely**, **go beyond**, **involve more than**。
- “考察”在学术语境常是 **examine / assess / consider**，通常不是 *investigate*。

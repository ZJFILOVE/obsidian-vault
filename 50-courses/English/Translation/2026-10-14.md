> [!answer]- 参考译文
> A strong classification framework not only summarizes known phenomena but also marks the boundaries of its own explanatory power. Although the model has successfully predicted the existence of multiple elementary particles and the strength of their interactions, it neither explains the nature of dark matter nor incorporates gravity into the same mathematical framework. Physicists therefore treat the theory as our most reliable working description rather than a final answer.

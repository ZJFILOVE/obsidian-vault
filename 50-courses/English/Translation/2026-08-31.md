# Translation — Day 13

## A · CET-4+
如今，越来越多的大学生通过短视频获取新闻和知识。短视频能够在较短时间内提供大量信息，因此使用起来十分方便。然而，如果人们习惯于只接触简短的内容，他们可能会逐渐失去长时间集中注意力的习惯。因此，在享受新技术带来的便利时，学生也应该有意识地培养深度阅读的习惯。

### Reference
Nowadays, an increasing number of university students obtain news and knowledge through short videos. Short videos can provide a large amount of information within a relatively short period, making them highly convenient. However, if people become accustomed to being exposed only to brief content, they may gradually lose the habit of concentrating for long periods. Therefore, while enjoying the convenience brought by new technologies, students should also consciously develop the habit of reading in depth.

## B · CET-6
研究发现，经常锻炼的人通常具有更好的睡眠质量。然而，这种相关性本身并不能证明锻炼是造成这一差异的唯一原因。例如，经常锻炼的人也可能拥有更健康的饮食习惯或更规律的生活方式。因此，在解释研究结果时，我们需要考虑其他可能影响结果的因素，而不能仅仅根据两个现象之间的相关性得出因果结论。

### Reference
Studies have found that people who exercise regularly generally have better sleep quality. However, this association alone does not prove that exercise is the only cause of the difference. For example, people who exercise regularly may also have healthier diets or more regular lifestyles. Therefore, when interpreting research findings, we need to consider other factors that may affect the outcome rather than draw a causal conclusion simply from an association between two phenomena.

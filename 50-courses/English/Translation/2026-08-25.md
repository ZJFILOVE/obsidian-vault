# Translation — Day 9

## A · CET-4+
如今，许多大学生会利用人工智能工具辅助学习。这些工具可以帮助学生快速查找资料、总结文章，甚至发现写作中的错误。然而，过度依赖人工智能可能会削弱学生独立解决问题的能力。因此，如何合理使用这些工具已经成为一个值得关注的问题。

### Reference
Today, many university students use artificial intelligence tools to assist their learning. These tools can help students quickly search for information, summarize articles, and even identify errors in their writing. However, excessive dependence on AI may weaken students' ability to solve problems independently. Therefore, how to use these tools properly has become an issue that deserves attention.

## B · CET-6
在信息极其丰富的时代，真正的困难往往不是找到信息，而是判断哪些信息值得相信。即使一篇文章看起来十分专业，其中的观点也不一定有可靠的证据支持。因此，在接受一个结论之前，我们应该考虑信息的来源，比较不同的观点，并判断作者提供的证据是否充分。

### Reference
In an age of abundant information, the real challenge is often not finding information, but determining which information can be trusted. Even if an article appears highly professional, the views it presents are not necessarily supported by reliable evidence. Therefore, before accepting a conclusion, we should consider the source of the information, compare different viewpoints, and determine whether the evidence provided by the author is sufficient.

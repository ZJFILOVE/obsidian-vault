## 练习一｜CET-6：社区工具借用点

### 中译英

一些社区正在建立工具借用点，让居民不必为偶尔一次的维修购买电钻、梯子等设备。这类服务不仅能减少闲置物品，还可能降低独居老人和新搬入居民寻求帮助的门槛。然而，便利并不会自动带来公平：如果登记流程过于复杂、开放时间只覆盖工作日白天，最需要服务的人反而可能被排除在外。因此，管理者应定期收集使用数据，并与没有使用该服务的居民交流。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> Some communities are setting up tool-lending hubs so that residents do not have to buy equipment such as drills or ladders for an occasional repair. Such services can reduce the number of items sitting idle and may also lower the barrier for older people living alone and newly arrived residents to ask for help. Convenience, however, does not automatically ensure equitable access. If registration is cumbersome and opening hours cover only weekday daytime, the people who need the service most may be left out. Managers should therefore collect usage data regularly and speak with residents who have not used the service.

### 结构与搭配

- **set up a tool-lending hub**：*set up* 表示建立可运行的服务；仅写 *build* 容易让人误以为只建造物理空间。
- **equipment sitting idle**：*idle* 描述资源未被使用；比 *useless equipment* 准确，因为物品本身仍有用。
- **lower the barrier for somebody to do something**：说明谁面对什么门槛，避免只写抽象的 *make it convenient*。
- **ensure equitable access / be left out**：前者是制度目标，后者写实际排除结果。

### 常见迁移错误

1. “偶尔一次的维修”不宜逐字写成 *one occasional repair once*；用 *for an occasional repair* 即可。
2. “最需要的人”写成 *the people who need the service most*，不要用不自然的 *the most needed people*。
3. “与没有使用的人交流”不是 *communicate the residents*；应为 *speak with / consult residents*。

### 精确改写

把 *The service is convenient, but some people cannot use it.* 改写为一句包含条件和结果的句子：

> If registration remains cumbersome, the service may appear convenient while still excluding residents with limited time or digital access.

## 练习二｜TOEFL / Academic：延长图书馆开放时间的评估

### 中译英

一所大学计划在考试季延长图书馆开放时间，并准备以夜间入馆人数的增加来证明政策有效。但仅有更高的使用量并不能说明学生的学习质量有所改善：原本就经常使用图书馆的学生，可能只是把学习时间推迟到了深夜。较可靠的评估应在政策实施前确定比较指标，同时考察睡眠、课程压力和替代学习空间。即使结果显示相关性，研究者也应避免把所有变化都归因于开放时间。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> A university plans to extend library hours during the examination period and intends to treat an increase in late-night entries as evidence that the policy works. Higher usage alone, however, does not show that the quality of students’ learning has improved: students who already use the library frequently may simply shift their study time later into the night. A more reliable evaluation should define comparison measures before implementation and examine sleep, course workload, and alternative study spaces. Even if the results reveal an association, researchers should avoid attributing every change to the extended hours.

### 结构与搭配

- **treat A as evidence that ...**：A 是证据，不等于 A 已经证明结论；比 *prove by A* 更符合学术语气。
- **usage alone does not show that ...**：用 *alone* 明确单一指标不足。
- **shift study time later into the night**：*shift* 同时保留“时间重新分配”而非“总量增加”。
- **define comparison measures before implementation**：突出事前设计，避免看到结果后再挑指标。
- **attribute a change to a policy**：*attribute A to B* 的方向不要写反。

### 常见迁移错误

1. “政策有效”不是任何情况下都用 *the policy is effective*；这里要说明对什么结果有效，如 *improves access* 或 *improves learning outcomes*。
2. “考察睡眠”不要写成 *investigate students to sleep*；可用 *examine sleep duration / sleep patterns*。
3. *even if* 引出让步条件，不能与 *but* 在同一主句重复堆叠。

### 精度训练

比较下面三句的证据强度：

1. *Late-night entries increased after the policy.*（时间顺序）
2. *The policy was associated with more late-night entries.*（相关）
3. *The policy caused students to study more effectively.*（因果与效果，证据要求最高）

用 2 改写一遍，再补一句说明为什么暂时不能写 3。

## 间隔复习句（明后天回译）

2. 使用量上升与结果改善相关，并不意味着前者必然造成后者。

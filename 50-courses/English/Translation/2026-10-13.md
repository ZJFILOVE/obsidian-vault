### 中文原文

研究团队在低温真空腔体中捕获了数百个反氢原子，并测量它们在激光照射下的能级跃迁。尽管实验设备极其精密，单次测量仍受限于粒子存留时间过短与背景噪声。研究人员不会仅凭少数几次跃迁频率的微小偏移就宣布对称性被打破；他们还需要与普通氢原子在完全相同的磁场条件下进行反复比对，并在误差范围内排除系统偏差。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> The research team trapped several hundred antihydrogen atoms in a cryogenic vacuum chamber and measured their energy-level transitions under laser illumination. Although the apparatus is highly sophisticated, individual measurements remain constrained by short confinement times and background noise. Researchers will not declare that symmetry has been broken merely on the basis of minor shifts in a few transition frequencies; they must repeatedly compare the results with ordinary hydrogen under identical magnetic-field conditions and rule out systematic bias within experimental uncertainty.

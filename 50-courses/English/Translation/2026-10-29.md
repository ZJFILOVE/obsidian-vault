## 使用方法与原创说明

以下两篇中文材料、情境和参考译文均为独立编写，不翻译或改写今日天气文章。六级篇练公共设施试运行的规则、例外与反馈；托福／学术篇练数字阅读实验中的结果变量、替代解释与外推边界。两个项目均为虚构教学材料。

先独立翻译，再检查：**谁执行规则、时间条件是否清楚、名词是否保持一致、结论有没有超过设计能够支持的范围**。折叠答案只是一种可行写法。

## 练习一：CET-6——社区自行车库试运行（15–18 分钟）

某社区将在三栋住宅楼旁试运行夜间自行车库。居民可凭门禁卡在晚上六点至次日早上八点进入，但每户只能登记两辆自行车。车库提供固定车架和照明，不负责保管车篮内的个人物品。若门禁失灵，居民应通过物业应用报告，不要让陌生人尾随进入。试运行持续八周；物业将记录使用次数、设备故障和车位不足报告，并在第四周邀请使用者和未使用者分别说明原因。若需求明显高于容量，物业会先调整登记规则，而不是立即扩建。试运行旨在判断服务是否值得长期保留，并不保证盗窃事件会完全消失。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A residential community will pilot overnight bicycle storage beside three apartment buildings. Residents may enter with their access cards between 6 p.m. and 8 a.m. the next morning, but each household may register no more than two bicycles. The facilities provide fixed racks and lighting but do not take responsibility for personal belongings left in bicycle baskets. If the access system fails, residents should report the problem through the property-management app rather than allow strangers to follow them inside. The pilot will run for eight weeks. The management team will record the number of uses, equipment failures and reports of insufficient space, and in the fourth week it will invite both users and non-users to explain their decisions. If demand clearly exceeds capacity, the team will first adjust the registration rules instead of expanding the facilities immediately. The pilot is intended to determine whether the service should be retained in the long term; it does not guarantee that bicycle theft will disappear completely.

### 结构与搭配

- **pilot a service / run an eight-week pilot**：试运行服务／开展八周试点。
- **no more than two**：至多两辆；不要写 not more than only two。
- **take responsibility for**：对……承担责任；不要把“不保管”扩大成“任何情况都免责”。
- **rather than allow...**：规则中的替代动作；rather than 后可接动词原形。
- **demand exceeds capacity**：需求超过容量；capacity 在此不是 ability。
- **be retained in the long term**：长期保留；与 permanent expansion 不同。

### 常见中式英语与精度修正

1. “门禁卡”是 **access card**，不是 entrance card。
2. “尾随进入”可写 **follow someone inside / tailgate through the door**；正式通知优先前者。
3. “使用者和未使用者”要保持平行：**users and non-users**。
4. 把 *The garage will prevent theft* 改为：**Secure racks may reduce some opportunities for theft, but the pilot cannot guarantee that all incidents will be prevented.**

## 练习二：TOEFL／academic——纸质阅读与屏幕阅读（20–25 分钟）

一所大学计划研究阅读媒介是否影响学生识别论证结构的能力。研究者将招募一百二十名学生，随机分到纸质文本组和屏幕文本组。两组阅读同一篇陌生文章，字体大小、阅读时间和测试题保持一致；屏幕组关闭通知，但允许正常滚动。主要结果是学生正确排列“主张—证据—限制”三类句子的比例，次要结果包括阅读时间和自报认知负荷。随机分组有助于减少组间原有差异，但一次短文测试不能代表长期学习。若纸质组得分更高，研究者还应检查滚动方式、设备熟悉度或记笔记习惯是否解释了部分差异，并报告置信区间，而不只报告平均分。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A university plans to investigate whether the reading medium affects students' ability to identify argumentative structure. Researchers will recruit 120 students and randomly assign them to a print-text group or a screen-text group. Both groups will read the same unfamiliar article, with font size, reading time and test questions held constant. Notifications will be disabled for the screen group, although normal scrolling will be allowed. The primary outcome will be the proportion of sentences that students correctly classify as claims, evidence or limitations. Secondary outcomes will include reading time and self-reported cognitive load. Random assignment should reduce pre-existing differences between the groups, but a single short-text task cannot represent long-term learning. If the print group scores higher, researchers should also examine whether scrolling patterns, familiarity with the device or note-taking habits explain part of the difference, and they should report confidence intervals rather than average scores alone.

### 结构与学术搭配

- **investigate whether A affects B**：研究是否影响，不预设方向。
- **randomly assign participants to groups**：把参与者随机分组；assign 后要有 to。
- **hold X constant**：控制 X 一致；不等于现实中 X 永远不变。
- **primary / secondary outcome**：主要／次要结果，避免混成同一终点。
- **pre-existing differences**：干预前已经存在的差异。
- **explain part of the difference**：解释部分差异，保留多重机制空间。
- **confidence interval**：置信区间；不是 confidence range 的随意替代。

### 因果与外推边界

1. 随机分组提高组间可比性，但样本来源仍影响能否外推到其他人群。
2. 关闭通知控制了一种干扰，却没有让纸面和屏幕体验完全相同。
3. 一次正确率差异不是长期记忆、学习习惯或学业成绩的直接证据。
4. 自报认知负荷是主观指标，不能替代行为结果。

## 精度改写

### A. 把服务承诺降到可执行范围

原句：**The new storage facility will make every bicycle safe.**

> [!answer]- 参考改写
> The facility provides controlled access and fixed racks, which may reduce some risks, but it cannot guarantee the security of every bicycle or personal item.

### B. 把一次实验改成有限结论

原句：**The experiment proved that paper is better for learning.**

> [!answer]- 参考改写
> In this short task, the print group identified argumentative roles more accurately on average; further evidence would be needed to determine whether the difference extends to long-term learning or other kinds of text.

## 中英迁移提醒

- 中文“研究……是否”不要先写成 **prove that**；优先 **investigate / examine whether**。
- “保持一致”根据语境选 **hold constant / keep the same**，不要写 keep consistency。
- “解释差异”是 **explain the difference**，不是 explain why the difference happened unless the mechanism has evidence。
- “分别说明原因”要让 both groups 可见，避免只收集使用者的正面反馈。
- “长期”可作 **in the long term**；形容词形式是 **long-term learning**。

## 间隔复习句

1. 试点可以揭示容量和使用障碍，但不能保证所有风险都会消失。
2. 即使两组平均分不同，研究者仍应报告不确定性，并限制结论的外推范围。

> [!answer]- 间隔复习参考
> 1. The pilot can reveal capacity constraints and barriers to use, but it cannot guarantee that every risk will disappear.
> 2. Even if the group averages differ, researchers should report uncertainty and limit how far the conclusion is generalized.

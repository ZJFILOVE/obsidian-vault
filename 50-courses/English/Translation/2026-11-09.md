## 练习一｜CET-6：校园失物招领系统

### 中文原文

某高校准备把分散在宿舍、食堂和教学楼的失物招领信息整合到一个线上平台。过去，学生必须逐个询问不同值班室，而工作人员也很难判断一件物品是否已在其他地点登记。新平台将允许用户按时间、地点和物品类别搜索，但不会公开证件号码、联系方式或能直接识别失主的照片。认领者仍需回答核验问题，并在领取时出示有效证明。学校计划先在三个区域试运行八周，记录成功归还率、误领报告和平均处理时间。如果平台只增加浏览量，却没有提高归还率，管理人员将调整登记流程，而不是立即扩大使用范围。便利性很重要，但不能以泄露个人信息为代价。

### 写作要求

- 140–180 词；
- 使用 **integrate ... into**、**while / whereas**、**rather than**；
- 写清“可搜索”与“公开敏感信息”之间的区别；
- 保留八周试点和扩大范围的条件性。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> A university plans to integrate lost-and-found information from dormitories, cafeterias, and teaching buildings into a single online platform. In the past, students had to contact several duty offices one by one, while staff could not easily tell whether an item had already been registered elsewhere. The platform will allow users to search by time, location, and item category, but it will not display ID numbers, contact details, or photographs that could directly identify the owner. Claimants will still have to answer verification questions and present valid evidence when collecting an item. The university will first run an eight-week pilot in three areas and record the successful return rate, reports of mistaken claims, and average processing time. If the platform merely increases page views without improving returns, administrators will revise the registration process rather than expand it immediately. Convenience matters, but it should not come at the cost of personal privacy.

### 结构与搭配

1. **integrate A into B**：把分散系统整合进统一平台；不是 *integrate A to B*。
2. **could not easily tell whether ...**：表达信息不对称；whether 后接完整判断。
3. **allow users to search by ...**：search by time / location / category；不要写 *search according to time*。
4. **present valid evidence**：出示有效证明；若特指身份证件可用 **present valid identification**。
5. **merely ... without ...**：只增加前一指标而没有改善目标结果。
6. **come at the cost of**：以……为代价，适合表达权衡。

### 常见中式迁移错误

- ❌ *combine the informations* → ✅ **integrate the information**；information 不可数。
- ❌ *open the ID number to public* → ✅ **make the ID number public / display it publicly**。
- ❌ *prove that the item is his* → ✅ **provide evidence that the item belongs to them**；中性单数可用 them。
- ❌ *if the click rate is high, it is useful*：浏览量不是归还效果；应保留实际结果指标。

### 精确度重写

把过强句子改得符合试点证据：

> The new platform will solve the university's lost-property problem.

推荐：

> The pilot will test whether a shared platform can improve return rates without increasing mistaken claims or exposing personal information.

## 练习二｜TOEFL / academic：公交到站信息试点

### 中文原文

一项城市研究评估了实时公交到站屏是否能缩短乘客“感知到的”等候时间。研究者在十二个站点安装显示屏，并选择十二个客流量和线路结构相近的站点作为比较组。六周内，调查员在不同时段询问乘客实际等候了多久、主观上觉得等了多久，以及他们是否在等待期间改变出行计划。安装显示屏的站点报告了更低的主观等候时间，但车辆定位记录显示，两组的实际等候时间变化不大。这个差异与“信息降低不确定性”的解释一致，却不能单独证明显示屏改善了公交运行效率。研究还可能受到天气、问卷应答偏差和站点之间既有差异的影响。后续试验应随机安排安装顺序，并预先规定主要指标。

### 写作要求

- 170–220 词；
- 使用 **matched comparison sites**、**be consistent with**、**cannot by itself establish**；
- 区分 perceived waiting time 与 actual waiting time；
- 写出至少三项限制和一个更强的后续设计。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> An urban study evaluated whether real-time bus arrival displays could reduce passengers' perceived waiting time. Researchers installed displays at twelve stops and selected twelve stops with similar passenger volumes and route structures as matched comparison sites. Over six weeks, surveyors asked passengers at different times of day how long they had actually waited, how long the wait had felt, and whether they had changed their travel plans while waiting. Stops with displays reported shorter perceived waits, whereas vehicle-location records showed little change in actual waiting time in either group. This difference is consistent with the explanation that information reduces uncertainty, but it cannot by itself establish that the displays improved operational efficiency. The findings may also have been affected by weather, survey-response bias, and pre-existing differences between stops. A follow-up trial should randomize the order in which displays are installed and specify the primary outcomes in advance.

### 结构与搭配

1. **evaluate whether ...**：评估某条件是否成立；比 *prove that* 更适合研究目的。
2. **matched comparison sites**：按关键变量匹配的比较站点，不自动等于随机对照组。
3. **whereas**：把主观等待与车辆记录形成明确对照。
4. **be consistent with an explanation**：结果与解释相符，但不是排他性证明。
5. **cannot by itself establish that ...**：单凭当前结果不能确立更强结论。
6. **pre-existing differences**：干预前已经存在的差异。
7. **specify outcomes in advance**：预先规定结果指标，减少事后挑选有利指标。

### 常见中式迁移错误

- ❌ *feeling waiting time* → ✅ **perceived waiting time / how long the wait felt**。
- ❌ *the real waiting time did not have obvious change* → ✅ **actual waiting time changed little**。
- ❌ *the result accords to the explanation* → ✅ **the result is consistent with the explanation**。
- ❌ *randomly install the order* → ✅ **randomize the installation order**。

### 论证强度阶梯

把下列句子从强到弱排序：

1. The displays improved bus operations.
2. The displays caused passengers to feel that the wait was shorter.
3. Passengers at display-equipped stops reported shorter perceived waits.

本研究最直接支持 **3**。若安装并非随机，**2** 仍需排除站点差异；**1** 与车辆记录不符。

### 精确度重写

原句：

> Real-time information makes buses faster.

推荐：

> In this six-week comparison, real-time information was associated with shorter perceived waits, while recorded waiting times changed little.

## 间隔复习｜24 小时后先译后看

1. 试点应同时衡量便利性、误领风险和隐私成本，而不是只统计页面浏览量。
2. 结果与一种机制相符，并不意味着其他解释已经被排除。

> [!answer]- 复习参考
> 1. The pilot should measure convenience, the risk of mistaken claims, and privacy costs together rather than count page views alone.
> 2. A result can be consistent with one mechanism without ruling out alternative explanations.

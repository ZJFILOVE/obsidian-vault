### 中文原文

当观测结果与现有理论预期出现明显缺口时，研究者通常不会立刻推翻全部既有规律，而是先检验是否存在未被直接探测到的隐藏变量。由于这类因素既不发光也不反射信号，直接成像往往难以奏效。相比之下，通过精确测量可见部分的运动轨迹与能量守恒偏差，团队仍能为未知成分的质量范围划定边界。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> When observations diverge sharply from theoretical expectations, researchers rarely discard established laws immediately; instead, they first test whether an undetected hidden variable is at play. Because such factors neither emit nor reflect signals, direct imaging is often ineffective. By contrast, precisely measuring the trajectories of visible components and deviations from energy conservation still allows teams to place bounds on the mass range of the unknown component.

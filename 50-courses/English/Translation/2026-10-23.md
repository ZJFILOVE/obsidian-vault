## 使用方法与原创说明

以下两组情境、中文段落和参考译文均为独立编写，不翻译或改写今日 NIST 计量术语文章。六级篇练公共服务中的规则、责任和例外；学术篇练自然实验、混杂因素与谨慎外推。两者均为虚构教学材料，不对应真实项目或已发表研究。

先限时完成，再按 **信息主语—动作—条件—例外—结论强度** 检查。参考译文提供一种可行版本，不是唯一标准答案。

## 练习一：CET-6——图书馆的工具借用服务（15–18 分钟）

市图书馆准备试办工具借用服务，居民可凭借阅证免费借用电钻、缝纫机和简单园艺工具。项目负责人认为，共享低频使用的物品既能减少家庭开支，也能避免仍可使用的设备被过早丢弃。不过，工具不会像普通图书那样直接上架。首次借用者要先观看安全演示，并在工作人员面前完成基本操作；带有锋利刀片或高温部件的设备只能在馆内指定区域使用。为防止押金把低收入居民排除在外，图书馆不收现金押金，而是记录归还时间和设备状况。若物品因正常磨损而损坏，借用者无需赔偿；若工作人员发现明显的不当使用，则会先与借用者核实情况，再决定是否暂停其借用资格。三个月后，图书馆将根据预约等待时间、设备故障和安全事件调整清单，而不会只用借出次数判断项目是否成功。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> The city library is preparing to pilot a tool-lending service through which residents can borrow electric drills, sewing machines and basic gardening tools free of charge with a library card. The project team believes that sharing items used only occasionally can both reduce household expenses and prevent usable equipment from being discarded too early. The tools, however, will not simply be placed on shelves like ordinary books. First-time borrowers must watch a safety demonstration and perform the basic operations in front of a staff member. Equipment with sharp blades or heated parts may be used only in designated areas of the library. To prevent a deposit requirement from excluding low-income residents, the library will not charge a cash deposit; instead, it will record the return time and the condition of each item. Borrowers will not be held responsible for damage caused by normal wear and tear. If staff members find signs of clear misuse, they will first confirm what happened with the borrower before deciding whether to suspend the borrowing privilege. After three months, the library will revise the inventory on the basis of reservation wait times, equipment failures and safety incidents rather than judging the programme solely by the number of loans.

### 结构与搭配

- **pilot a service / run a pilot programme**：试办服务；pilot 可作动词。
- **free of charge**：免费；若写 for free，语气更口语。
- **prevent A from doing B**：防止 A 做 B；from 不要漏。
- **be held responsible for**：被要求对……负责；比一律用 pay for 更完整。
- **normal wear and tear**：正常损耗，常用于租赁与设备管理。
- **on the basis of / rather than**：明确评价依据，并与被排除的单一指标形成对照。

### 常见中式英语

1. “凭借阅证”不是 *borrow by a card*；可写 **with / on presentation of a library card**。
2. “完成基本操作”在此指展示会用，不是 *finish the operation forever*；用 **perform basic operations**。
3. “暂停资格”不是永久取消，避免擅自强化成 **ban the borrower permanently**。
4. “低频使用”不要机械写 *low-frequency tools*，那会像物理术语；改成 **items used only occasionally**。

### 精度改写

把模糊句：

> The programme will help everyone and protect the environment.

改为：

> The pilot is intended to lower access costs and extend the useful life of shared equipment, but the library will evaluate who uses the service and whether safety incidents remain manageable.

后一句说明目标和检查条件，没有预先把愿望写成已证实效果。

## 练习二：TOEFL／academic——树荫与候车行为（20–25 分钟）

一项校园观察研究拟检验：在炎热天气中，公交站旁的树荫是否与学生更愿意步行到较远站点有关。研究人员选择了十二个线路和班次相近的站点，其中六个在下午大部分时间被树冠遮挡，另外六个几乎没有自然遮蔽。他们连续四周记录气温、湿度、候车人数和乘客到达站点的方向，并通过匿名问卷询问步行距离与选站原因。若有树荫的站点吸引了更多从较远建筑步行而来的乘客，这一结果将支持“热舒适度影响选站”的解释。然而，站点附近的餐饮设施、座椅数量和实时到站屏也可能影响选择。由于研究没有随机分配树木，观察到的差异不能被直接归因于树荫。研究团队计划在分析中控制可记录的站点特征，并在学期不同时段重复观察；即便结果稳定，结论也应限于气候、线路密度和校园布局相似的环境。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A campus observational study is designed to examine whether shade near bus stops is associated with students’ willingness to walk to more distant stops during hot weather. The researchers selected twelve stops with broadly comparable routes and service frequencies. Six were covered by tree canopies for most of the afternoon, whereas the other six had little natural shelter. For four consecutive weeks, the team recorded temperature, humidity, the number of waiting passengers and the directions from which passengers approached the stops. An anonymous questionnaire also asked about walking distance and reasons for choosing a particular stop. If shaded stops attract more passengers who have walked from distant buildings, the result would support the explanation that thermal comfort influences stop choice. However, nearby food outlets, the number of seats and real-time arrival displays may also shape that choice. Because trees were not randomly assigned to the sites, any observed difference cannot be attributed directly to shade. The team plans to control for observable stop characteristics in the analysis and repeat the observations at different points in the semester. Even if the pattern remains stable, the conclusion should be limited to settings with similar climates, route densities and campus layouts.

### 结构与学术搭配

- **be associated with**：表示相关，不自动宣称因果。
- **broadly comparable**：大体可比；不要写成 identical，实际站点不可能完全相同。
- **whereas**：在同一维度上形成对照。
- **support an explanation**：支持某种解释，弱于 prove。
- **attribute A to B**：把 A 归因于 B；被动式是 A be attributed to B。
- **control for observable characteristics**：在分析中控制可观测特征，不代表所有混杂都已消除。
- **limit the conclusion to**：主动说明外推边界。

### 常见迁移错误

1. “线路和班次相近”应写 **comparable routes and service frequencies**，不要写 *the bus times are almost same*。
2. “从较远建筑步行而来”是到达方向与距离信息，不等于追踪了个人全部行程；避免写 **proved that they chose the stop because of shade**。
3. “匿名问卷”是 **an anonymous questionnaire**；anonymous 描述不记录身份，不等于 respondents 的回答一定无偏。
4. “不能直接归因”不是“完全没有关系”，应保持 **cannot be attributed directly** 的证据边界。

### 精度改写

把过强句子：

> Planting trees makes students walk farther to buses.

改为：

> In the sampled campus, shaded stops may attract passengers from a wider walking area, but the observational design cannot separate shade from every difference among the stops.

## 间隔复习

明天闭卷翻译：

1. 一个公共服务不能只按使用次数评价，因为安全、可及性和设备状况同样重要。
2. 即使某种模式在多次观察中保持稳定，研究者仍应说明哪些替代解释尚未排除。

> [!answer]- 间隔复习参考
> 1. A public service should not be evaluated solely by how often it is used, because safety, accessibility and equipment condition also matter.
> 2. Even if a pattern remains stable across repeated observations, researchers should still state which alternative explanations have not been ruled out.

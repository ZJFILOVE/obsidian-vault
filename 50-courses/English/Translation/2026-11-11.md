## 练习一｜CET-6：社区共享冰箱

### 中译英

一些社区设置了共享冰箱，让商店和居民把仍可安全食用的食物留给有需要的人。这类项目可以减少浪费，但前提是食品来源、存放时间和冷藏温度都得到清楚记录。如果志愿者只负责补充食物，却没有人定期检查标签和清洁设备，便利措施反而可能带来健康风险。因此，项目负责人既要降低求助门槛，也要建立明确、可执行的安全规则。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> Some communities have set up shared refrigerators where shops and residents can leave food that is still safe to eat for people who need it. Such projects can reduce waste, provided that the source of the food, storage time, and refrigeration temperature are clearly recorded. If volunteers only restock the refrigerator while no one checks the labels or cleans the equipment regularly, a convenient service may instead create health risks. Project coordinators should therefore lower the barrier to seeking help while also establishing clear and enforceable safety rules.

### 结构与搭配

- **set up a shared refrigerator**：表示建立并运行一项设施；*put a refrigerator* 只描述摆放动作。
- **food that is still safe to eat**：*still* 保留“尚在安全期限内”，比笼统的 *good food* 精确。
- **provided that ...**：表示必要条件，不等于较弱的 *if possible*。
- **restock / check labels / clean equipment**：三个并列动作分别对应补充、核验和维护。
- **enforceable safety rules**：规则不仅存在，还必须能够执行。

### 常见迁移错误

1. “有需要的人”通常写 *people in need* 或 *people who need it*，不要逐字写 *people who have needs*。
2. “食品来源”是 *the source of the food*，不是 *food resources*。
3. “反而可能”可用 *may instead*，不要把 *on the contrary* 塞进同一句造成逻辑跳跃。

### 精确改写

把 *The refrigerator is useful if it is clean.* 改写成同时包含记录、责任人和结果的句子：

> The refrigerator can remain useful only if storage conditions are recorded and a designated person checks the equipment regularly.

## 练习二｜TOEFL / Academic：城市鸟类调查中的“未发现”

### 中译英

一项城市鸟类调查发现，安装隔音屏障后，研究区域内记录到的鸟种数量有所下降。然而，记录减少并不必然意味着真实种群缩小。屏障可能改变声音传播，使观察者更难通过鸣声识别远处的鸟。为了区分生态变化与探测能力变化，研究者应在多个时段重复调查，并同时记录天气、背景噪声和观察距离。在估计探测概率之前，把“没有记录到”直接解释为“并不存在”会夸大证据的确定性。

> [!answer]- 参考译文
> An urban bird survey found that fewer species were recorded in the study area after noise barriers were installed. A decline in recorded observations, however, does not necessarily mean that the actual population became smaller. The barriers may have altered how sound travelled, making it harder for observers to identify distant birds by their calls. To distinguish ecological change from a change in detection ability, researchers should repeat surveys at different times and record weather, background noise, and observation distance. Until detection probability has been estimated, interpreting “not recorded” as “absent” would overstate the certainty of the evidence.

### 结构与搭配

- **fewer species were recorded**：这是观测结果；不要提前升级成 *fewer species existed*。
- **does not necessarily mean that ...**：否定必然推论，同时保留该解释仍有可能。
- **make it harder for somebody to do something**：*it* 是形式宾语，后面接真正任务。
- **distinguish A from B**：区分生态变化和探测能力变化，不能写成 *distinguish between A from B*。
- **overstate the certainty of the evidence**：批评的是结论强度，不是否认全部数据价值。

### 常见迁移错误

1. “记录到的鸟种数量”要保留观测层级，避免写成 *the number of birds*，后者把“物种数”误成“个体数”。
2. “在多个时段”可写 *at different times / across multiple time periods*，不是 *in many times*。
3. *absence of evidence* 与 *evidence of absence* 不能互换；前者可能来自探测不足。

### 论证强度训练

比较并排序：

1. *No birds were present behind the barrier.*
2. *No birds were recorded during the survey.*
3. *The survey detected fewer species after the barrier was installed.*

先选出最忠实于原始观测的一句，再补一个条件，说明什么额外证据才能支持句 1。

## 间隔复习句（明后天回译）

1. 只要责任明确且记录完整，共享设施就能在提高便利性的同时控制风险。
2. 观测次数减少可能来自真实变化，也可能来自探测能力下降。

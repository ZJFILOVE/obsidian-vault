## 使用方法与原创说明

以下两篇中文材料、情境和参考译文均为独立编写，不翻译或改写今日国家公园动物研究。六级篇练循环餐盒项目的规则、押金与评价指标；托福／学术篇练校园遮阳设施的准实验比较、替代解释和外推边界。两个项目均为虚构教学材料。

先独立翻译，再检查五件事：**谁执行、规则何时触发、比较组是否清楚、观察指标是什么、结论有没有超过设计能够支持的范围**。折叠答案只是可行版本之一。

## 练习一：CET-6——食堂循环餐盒试点（15–18 分钟）

一所大学将在两个食堂试行可重复使用的餐盒。学生点餐时可以支付二十元押金领取餐盒，并在七天内把清洗过的餐盒退回任一指定回收柜；系统确认归还后，押金会原路退回。若餐盒严重损坏或超过期限仍未归还，平台会先发送提醒，三天后才扣除押金。食堂负责高温消毒，但学生仍需倒掉剩余食物并简单冲洗。试点持续十周，项目组将记录领取量、按时归还率、清洗成本和一次性包装的减少量，还会分别询问使用者与未使用者遇到的困难。项目旨在判断这套服务是否方便且可持续，并不保证短期内完全取消一次性餐盒。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> A university will pilot reusable takeaway containers in two dining halls. When ordering food, students may pay a deposit of 20 yuan to receive a container and return the rinsed container to any designated collection locker within seven days. Once the system confirms the return, the deposit will be refunded through the original payment method. If a container is seriously damaged or has not been returned by the deadline, the platform will send a reminder first and deduct the deposit only after another three days. The dining halls are responsible for high-temperature sanitization, but students must still empty any leftover food and rinse the containers briefly. During the ten-week pilot, the project team will record the number of containers issued, the on-time return rate, cleaning costs and the reduction in disposable packaging. It will also ask users and non-users separately about the difficulties they have encountered. The project is intended to determine whether the service is convenient and sustainable; it does not guarantee that disposable containers will be eliminated in the short term.

### 结构与搭配

- **pilot a service in two dining halls**：pilot 作动词，表示小范围试行。
- **pay a deposit / refund a deposit / deduct the deposit**：押金的支付、退回和扣除要用不同动词。
- **within seven days**：包括七天这个时间窗；by the deadline 强调截止点。
- **through the original payment method**：比 return to the original way of payment 自然。
- **be responsible for sanitization**：负责制度动作；不要误写成 be responsible to。
- **users and non-users separately**：把两个群体都写出来，避免只收集使用者意见。
- **is intended to determine whether...**：目的不是结果；不能提前写成 will prove。

### 常见中英迁移错误

1. “原路退回”不是 return by the same road，而是 **be refunded through the original payment method**。
2. “超过期限仍未归还”要写清对象：**has not been returned by the deadline**。
3. “一次性包装的减少量”可写 **the reduction in disposable packaging**，不要写 decrease quantity。
4. “并不保证”用 **does not guarantee**，不要把试点目的写成必然结果。

### 精度改写

把下面过强或含糊的句子改准确：

1. The program will remove disposable packaging.
2. Students must return it soon.
3. The team will ask people whether they like it.

> [!answer]- 精度改写参考
> 1. The pilot will measure how much disposable packaging is reduced; it does not assume that such packaging can be eliminated immediately.
> 2. Students must return the rinsed container to a designated locker within seven days.
> 3. The team will ask users and non-users separately about convenience, barriers to participation and reasons for not using the service.

## 练习二：TOEFL／学术——遮阳步道与午间出行（20–25 分钟）

某大学在主校区两条步行路线安装了遮阳棚，并希望评估这些设施是否会改变学生午间出行。研究人员在安装前四周和安装后四周记录每条路线的行人数量、地表温度和平均停留时间，同时选择两条长度相近但没有新增遮阳设施的路线作为比较组。初步结果显示，新增遮阳的路线在晴天中午吸引了更多行人，但在阴天没有明显差异。研究人员不能仅凭这一结果断言遮阳棚导致了全部增长，因为新学期课程安排、附近商店促销和测量周的天气也可能影响路线选择。他们计划在下一个学期继续收集数据，并检验这种变化是否在不同年级和行动不便的学生中同样明显。即使结果再次出现，结论也只适用于相似气候和校园布局，不能直接推广到所有城市空间。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／学术（原创）
> A university installed shade structures along two walking routes on its main campus and sought to evaluate whether the facilities would change students' midday travel. For four weeks before and four weeks after the installation, the researchers recorded pedestrian counts, surface temperature and average dwell time on each route. They also selected two routes of similar length that received no new shade structures as comparison sites. Preliminary results showed that the newly shaded routes attracted more pedestrians at noon on sunny days, whereas no clear difference appeared on cloudy days. The researchers cannot conclude from this result alone that the shade structures caused the entire increase, because the new semester's class schedule, promotions at nearby shops and weather during the measurement weeks may also have influenced route choice. They plan to continue collecting data in the following semester and examine whether the change is similarly pronounced among students in different year groups and students with mobility limitations. Even if the pattern is observed again, the conclusion would apply only to settings with comparable climates and campus layouts and should not be generalized directly to all urban spaces.

### 结构与搭配

- **evaluate whether X would change Y**：研究问题；would 不是对结果打包票。
- **for four weeks before and four weeks after**：把两个观察窗写对称。
- **routes of similar length**：比较组只说明长度相近，不暗示所有条件都完全一致。
- **whereas**：连接晴天与阴天的对照，比分句堆叠清楚。
- **cannot conclude from this result alone that...**：限制证据强度。
- **may also have influenced**：对替代解释使用情态完成体。
- **be similarly pronounced among...**：比较不同子群的效应强弱。
- **apply to / generalize to**：适用于某范围；推广到另一范围。

### 常见中英迁移错误

1. “安装前后”不要只写 before and after installation 而省略观察时长。
2. “没有明显差异”是 **no clear / statistically detectable difference**；没有统计信息时不要擅自写 statistically significant。
3. “导致全部增长”不能弱化成 was related to the increase；原文是在限制完整因果归因。
4. “行动不便的学生”可写 **students with mobility limitations**，避免把人等同于限制。
5. “不能直接推广”用 **should not be generalized directly to**，不是 cannot promote。

### 论证强度阶梯

按证据强度从低到高比较：

- **The increase coincided with the installation.** 只写时间对应。
- **The comparison is consistent with a possible effect of shade.** 与某种效应相符。
- **The design strengthens the case that shade contributed to the increase.** 比较组增强解释，但仍保留其他因素。
- **The shade structures caused the increase in every setting.** 过强；当前设计与范围都不支持。

### 精度改写

把 “The shaded routes became more popular” 改成三层：

> [!answer]- 改写参考
> 1. **描述层：** Pedestrian counts increased on the two newly shaded routes during sunny midday periods after installation.
> 2. **比较层：** The increase was larger than that observed on two routes of similar length without new shade structures.
> 3. **边界层：** The comparison is consistent with an effect of shade, but schedule changes, local promotions and weather may also have influenced route choice.

## 中英迁移提醒

- 中文省略主语时，英语必须决定是 **the platform, the dining halls, students** 还是 **the research team**。
- “是否”通常对应 **whether**，而不是先写 prove that。
- “分别”要在句法上体现：**ask users and non-users separately**。
- “再次出现”是可重复性线索，不等于自动获得全球外推资格。
- 观察到更多人使用某路线，不等于已经测量了满意度、健康收益或长期习惯。

## 间隔复习句

1. 试点会记录归还率和清洗成本，但不会把减少一次性包装当成已经实现的结果。
2. 比较组能加强因果解释，却不能消除同时发生的所有变化。

> [!answer]- 间隔复习参考
> 1. The pilot will record return rates and cleaning costs, but it will not treat a reduction in disposable packaging as an outcome that has already been achieved.
> 2. A comparison group can strengthen a causal explanation, but it cannot eliminate every change occurring at the same time.

## 使用方法与原创说明

以下两段中文、情境与参考译文均独立编写，不翻译或改写今日博物馆文章。六级篇练校园设备借还中的条件、责任与替代流程；学术篇练城市降温项目的比较、混杂因素与结论边界。两者都是虚构练习，不是现实机构规则或已完成研究。

先整段翻译，再按 **执行者—动作—条件—证据强度—搭配** 的顺序修改。参考译文不是唯一答案；比起堆长句，更重要的是没有删掉例外，也没有把计划写成已经实现的结果。

## 练习一：CET-6——校园设备借还（15–18分钟）

学校准备在图书馆入口设置一个小型设备借用点，为学生提供充电器、转接头和计算器。学生可以凭校园卡免费借用，但每次只能选择一件设备，并应在当天闭馆前归还。工作人员会在借出前记录设备编号，而不会保留与借用无关的个人信息。如果设备在使用中出现故障，学生应停止使用并把问题告诉值班人员；他们不应自行拆开设备，也不会仅仅因为报告了故障就被认定造成了损坏。若系统暂时无法读取校园卡，工作人员可以先登记姓名和联系方式，并在系统恢复后补录。学校计划试行两周，然后根据归还记录、故障报告和学生反馈决定是否扩大服务。试行的目的在于发现流程中的问题，而不是证明所有设备需求已经得到满足。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：CET-6（原创）
> The university plans to set up a small equipment-lending desk near the library entrance, offering students chargers, adapters and calculators. Students may borrow an item free of charge with their campus card, but they may select only one item at a time and should return it before the library closes that day. Staff will record the equipment number before an item is lent out, without retaining personal information unrelated to the loan. If a device malfunctions during use, the student should stop using it and report the problem to the staff member on duty. Students should not open the device themselves, nor will they be assumed to have caused the damage merely because they reported a fault. If the system is temporarily unable to read a campus card, staff may first record the student's name and contact details and enter the loan into the system once service is restored. The university plans to run the trial for two weeks and then decide whether to expand the service on the basis of return records, fault reports and student feedback. The purpose of the trial is to identify problems in the procedure, not to prove that every need for equipment has already been met.

### 结构与搭配

1. **set up an equipment-lending desk**：set up 表示设立；equipment 通常不可数，不写 equipments。复合修饰可用 equipment-lending。
2. **one item at a time**：每次一件，不能只写 one time one equipment。
3. **unrelated to the loan**：过去分词短语限定 information；不要把“无关”扩大成“不保留任何信息”。
4. **malfunction during use / report a fault**：前者描述设备失灵，后者描述报告的内容。report about the problem 不一定错，但 report the problem 更简洁。
5. **merely because …**：保留“仅仅因为”的限制。若漏掉 merely，责任判断的范围会变强。
6. **once service is restored**：系统恢复后；once 在此是连词，不是“一次”。
7. **on the basis of …**：说明决定依据，不等于三个来源必然得出一致结论。
8. **identify problems, not prove …**：试行可以帮助发现问题；不能从两周试行自动推出需求已全部满足。

### 常见中式迁移

- “借设备”是 **borrow a device**；工作人员把设备借出是 **lend out a device**。主语不同，动词不能随意互换。
- “补录”可具体译成 **enter the loan into the system later**，不要硬造 re-record the information unless 语境真是重复记录。
- “值班人员”是 **the staff member on duty**；on duty 是状态，不写 in duty。
- “认定造成损坏”需要被动或判断结构；不要把 **be assumed to have caused** 简化成 **be caused**。

### 精度改写

把 **Anyone who reports a fault is responsible for the damage.** 改成符合中文情境、保留判断边界的一句。

> [!answer]- 一个可行版本
> A student should not be assumed to have caused the damage solely because they reported the fault.

## 练习二：TOEFL／学术——评估街道树荫与体感温度（18–22分钟）

一个城市团队希望评估新种植的行道树是否改善了夏季步行环境。研究人员计划在有新树和没有新树的街段记录气温、地表温度、湿度与风速，并在一天中的相近时段重复测量。两类街段还可能在建筑高度、路面材料和交通量上存在差异，因此不能把任何温度差都直接归因于树木。团队应在分析前说明选择街段的标准，保留原始记录，并报告传感器缺失或位置改变的情况。问卷可以补充行人的体感，但自我报告不能替代环境测量；同样，较低的地表温度也不必然意味着每位行人都感到更舒适。如果不同指标给出不一致的结果，报告应解释这种差异，而不是只保留最符合预期的一项。即使试点地区出现改善，也需要在其他季节和街区重复观察，才能判断结果是否适用于更广泛的城市环境。

> [!answer]- 参考译文：TOEFL／academic（原创）
> A municipal team aims to assess whether newly planted street trees have improved the summer walking environment. Researchers plan to record air temperature, surface temperature, humidity and wind speed on street segments with and without the new trees, repeating the measurements at comparable times of day. The two groups of streets may also differ in building height, paving materials and traffic volume, so any temperature difference cannot be attributed directly to the trees. Before analysing the data, the team should state its criteria for selecting street segments, retain the original records, and report missing sensor data or changes in sensor location. Surveys can supplement environmental measurements by describing pedestrians' perceived comfort, but self-reports cannot replace those measurements. Likewise, a lower surface temperature does not necessarily mean that every pedestrian feels more comfortable. If the indicators produce inconsistent results, the report should explain the discrepancy rather than retain only the measure that best matches expectations. Even if conditions improve in the pilot area, observations should be repeated in other seasons and neighbourhoods before the findings are assumed to apply to the wider urban environment.

### 学术结构与搭配

- **assess whether …**：评估是否，而不是预先写成 demonstrate that。whether 保留了可能成立也可能不成立。
- **street segments with and without …**：建立比较框架；with/without 不是随机分配，后文仍需处理组间差异。
- **at comparable times of day**：可比不等于完全相同。comparable 强调比较有意义，identical 强调完全一致。
- **attribute A to B**：把A归因于B。被动形式 **A is attributed to B** 常见，但在证据不足时应保留 cannot be attributed directly。
- **criteria for selecting …**：criterion 的复数是 criteria。写清选择标准，有助于读者判断样本范围。
- **supplement, not replace**：补充而非替代。问卷与传感器回答的问题不同，不应互相冒充。
- **does not necessarily mean …**：不必然意味着，不是否定所有关联。不要把审慎限制译成 has nothing to do with。
- **before the findings are assumed to apply …**：在推广结论前还需重复观察；apply to 指适用于某范围。

### 纠错与范围控制

1. The streets with trees were cooler, so the trees caused the entire difference. → 忽略建筑、路面和交通等差异。
2. Survey answers are the same as temperature measurements. → 把主观体感与环境量混为同一指标。
3. The team removed every result that did not meet its expectation. → 选择性保留，不是解释不一致。
4. A positive result in one area proves the policy will work everywhere. → 从一个试点直接推广到所有季节与街区。

> [!answer]- 原创修正参考
> 1. The observed difference may be associated with the trees, but other differences between the street segments must also be considered.
> 2. Survey responses can describe perceived comfort, whereas sensors measure environmental conditions.
> 3. The team should report and investigate inconsistent indicators rather than discard them for failing to match expectations.
> 4. A positive result in one pilot area should be tested in other seasons and neighbourhoods before wider application.

## 间隔复习

1. 说明文字可以帮助新生完成借用流程，但发布说明本身不能证明每个人都理解了它。
2. 如果两组样本来自不同条件，增加样本数量也不会自动消除偏差。

> [!answer]- 复习参考
> 1. Instructions can help new students complete the borrowing procedure, but publishing them does not by itself show that everyone understands the process.
> 2. If the two groups of samples come from different conditions, increasing the sample size will not automatically remove the bias.

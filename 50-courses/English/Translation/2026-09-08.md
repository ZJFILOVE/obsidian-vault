## 1. CET-6 level — Learning and memory
先自己翻译，不查词典：

**在信息随手可得的时代，人们很容易把“看过”误认为“学会了”。然而，仅仅重复阅读笔记往往只能带来熟悉感，而不能保证我们在需要时真正想起其中的内容。相比之下，主动回忆、间隔复习以及用自己的话解释一个概念，虽然更费力，却更有助于形成长期记忆。因此，有效学习的关键并不是花更多时间盯着材料，而是让大脑反复练习提取信息。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> In an age when information is readily available, it is easy to mistake having seen something for having learned it. However, simply rereading notes often creates a sense of familiarity without ensuring that we can actually recall the content when we need it. By contrast, active recall, spaced review, and explaining a concept in our own words require more effort but are more effective in building long-term memory. Therefore, the key to effective learning is not spending more time staring at the material, but repeatedly making the brain practice retrieving information.

### Structures & collocations
- **mistake A for B** — 把 A 误认为 B
- **a sense of familiarity** — 熟悉感
- **ensure that...** — 确保……；比 *make sure* 更适合正式写作
- **by contrast** — 相比之下
- **active recall / spaced review** — 主动回忆 / 间隔复习
- **the key is not A, but B** — 重点不在 A，而在 B

### Common transfer errors
- 不要把“学会了”机械翻成 *have studied it*；这里强调掌握，可用 *have learned it*。
- “在需要时想起”自然表达是 **recall it when needed**，不是 *remember it out*。
- “更费力”可用 **require more effort**，通常比 *cost more energy* 更自然。

## 2. TOEFL / academic level — Cities and public space

**一座城市的公共空间并不只是建筑之间剩余的空地。设计良好的公园、广场和步行区域能够创造偶遇的机会，使不同年龄和背景的人在没有明确目的的情况下共享同一环境。这种低强度但持续发生的社会接触未必会立刻形成亲密关系，却可能增强居民对社区的熟悉感和信任。因此，在评估城市基础设施时，决策者不仅应考虑交通效率和经济回报，还应考虑空间如何影响人与人之间的联系。**

> [!answer]- Reference translation
> Public space in a city is not merely the leftover space between buildings. Well-designed parks, squares, and pedestrian areas can create opportunities for casual encounters, allowing people of different ages and backgrounds to share the same environment without a specific purpose. Such low-intensity but recurring social contact may not immediately produce close relationships, but it can strengthen residents' sense of familiarity with and trust in their community. Therefore, when evaluating urban infrastructure, policymakers should consider not only transportation efficiency and economic returns but also how physical space shapes connections between people.

### Structures & collocations
- **not merely...** — 不仅仅是……
- **create opportunities for casual encounters** — 创造偶遇机会
- **people of different ages and backgrounds** — 不同年龄和背景的人
- **recurring social contact** — 持续/反复发生的社会接触
- **urban infrastructure** — 城市基础设施
- **shape connections between people** — 塑造人与人之间的联系

### Common transfer errors
- “公共空间”固定表达是 **public space**，不要写成 *public place* 来泛指城市规划概念。
- “经济回报”在政策语境中用 **economic returns**，比 *economic feedback* 正确。
- 中文连续使用“使……”时，英文可用 **allowing...** 等分词结构避免多个 *make*。

# Translation — Day 6

## Chinese → English
制定目标对于大学生十分重要，但仅仅有目标并不能保证成功。为了取得长期进步，学生还需要养成良好的学习习惯。例如，每天阅读一段时间、定期复习所学内容以及及时发现自己的不足，都有助于提高学习效率。与短期的努力相比，持续的行动往往更加重要。

## Reference
Setting goals is important for university students, but having goals alone cannot guarantee success. To make long-term progress, students also need to develop good study habits. For example, reading for a certain period every day, regularly reviewing what they have learned, and identifying their weaknesses in time can all contribute to greater learning efficiency. Compared with short-term effort, consistent action is often more important.
